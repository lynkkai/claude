What October says to do in November

Ordered by expected clicks per hour of work, not by how interesting the work is.

1

Rewrite titles and intros for the 28 striking-distance queries

These already rank between positions 4 and 20 and took zero clicks on — impressions. Lead with the answer in the first 40 words, put the query in the title, and link each page from the homepage or its feature page. No new content needed.

2

Fix mobile performance before anything else technical

Mobile LCP is 4.7 seconds against a 2.5 second threshold, and time to interactive is 10.1 seconds. Trimming the 202 KB of unused JavaScript and removing the redirect chain is the single highest leverage engineering task on this list.

3

Start link acquisition, because authority is the ceiling

Five referring domains and zero follow links explain the deep average position better than any content gap. Target product directories, the comparison and alternatives pages already published, and the tool-submission copy in the brand memory file. Aim for 20 to 30 referring domains by the end of Q1.

4

Build out the conversational and AI-surface answers

120 queries of ten words or more averaged position 7.8 and returned no clicks. They ask about SSO and SCIM, transcript retention, which third-party models see call data, volume discounts and source-linked search. Each is a short, factual page or an FAQ block, and each maps onto something Lynkk already does. This is also the traffic least likely to click, so treat it as brand presence inside answers.

5

Push the commercial pages, not just the blog

Comparison, use-case and integration pages took one click between them on 672 impressions. Competitor pricing queries (granola pricing, fathom pricing) already rank on page two and are the cheapest route from a competitor search to a Lynkk comparison page.

6

Pick one cluster to own instead of spreading thin

Dictation and voice-to-text is the largest cluster by impressions and the worst by position, which means it is competitive and Lynkk is not winning it. Google Meet is the opposite: strong average position, real volume, a near miss. Concentrate November on Google Meet and Zoom and park the generic voice-to-text articles.

7

Clear the small crawl issues

Four thin pages, six short titles, one page blocked by a meta robots tag, one 302 redirect and five unfriendly URLs. An afternoon of work, and it keeps the health score from drifting as the site grows.

Three caveats worth keeping in mind. Search Console caps the query export at 1,000 rows, so the keyword totals here are a floor, not a full count, and they do not add up to the site totals. Query, page, country and device tables each use Google's own anonymisation, so their sums differ slightly from the daily totals.
