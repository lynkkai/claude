# Video Script: "The Deal That Almost Slipped"

**Format:** Story-driven explainer, about 90 seconds
**Structure:** Act 1 shows the problem through one rep's week. Act 2 replays the same week with Lynkk.
**Characters:**
- **Maya**, account executive
- **Raj**, buyer at Brightline Logistics (fictional company)
- **Tom**, Maya's sales manager

**CTA:** Start free at lynkk.ai

---

## ACT 1: THE WEEK WITHOUT LYNKK

### Scene 1: Monday, coffee shop [0:00 - 0:10]

**Visual:** Maya and Raj laugh over coffee. Warm, relaxed. No laptop, no notes.

**RAJ:** "Our budget gets approved on the 15th. Send me pricing before then, and we're in."

**MAYA:** (smiling) "Perfect. You'll have it."

**On-screen text:** Monday. In person.

### Scene 2: Tuesday, in the car [0:10 - 0:18]

**Visual:** Maya's phone buzzes. A WhatsApp call from Raj. She answers on the go.

**RAJ (on phone):** "One more thing. Please loop in Priya, our CFO. She signs off on this."

**MAYA:** "Got it. Priya. Noted."

**Visual:** On her laptop at home, a meeting bot sits idle. It never knew this call happened.

**On-screen text:** Tuesday. WhatsApp. No bot can join.

### Scene 3: Wednesday to Thursday, back-to-back [0:18 - 0:28]

**Visual:** Fast montage. Call after call. The CRM tab stays open in the corner. Maya looks at it, sighs: "Later." The tab gets buried under other windows.

**NARRATOR:** "Updating the CRM always takes more effort than skipping it. So reps skip it. 40% of reps still update fields by hand."

**Visual:** The Brightline deal record. Next step: blank. Close date: blank. Key contact: blank.

**On-screen text:** 40% of reps still update CRM fields by hand (Source: Cotera, Revenue.io)

### Scene 4: Friday, forecast meeting [0:28 - 0:38]

**Visual:** Tom scrolls the pipeline on a big screen. He stops on Brightline.

**TOM:** "Brightline. Is this real?"

**MAYA:** (hesitating) "I think so. They said something about a budget date? And someone in finance..."

**Visual:** Tom quietly drags the deal into the "Maybe" column.

**NARRATOR:** "The details were never written down. They lived in Maya's memory. And memory isn't a forecast."

### Scene 5: The 16th [0:38 - 0:45]

**Visual:** An email lands in Maya's inbox. She reads it, and her face drops.

**On-screen email from Raj:** "Hi Maya, we didn't get pricing in time, so the budget went to another project. Maybe next quarter."

**NARRATOR:** "The deal wasn't lost on a call. It was lost after the call."

**Visual:** Screen fades to black. A rewind sound.

**On-screen text:** Now let's run that week again. With Lynkk.

## ACT 2: THE SAME WEEK WITH LYNKK

### Scene 6: Monday, coffee shop [0:45 - 0:55]

**Visual:** Same coffee shop, same table. This time Maya opens Lynkk on her MacBook and sets it aside. A small "Recording" dot glows.

**RAJ:** "Our budget gets approved on the 15th. Send me pricing before then, and we're in."

**Visual:** As they say goodbye, Lynkk notes appear on screen:
- Budget approved on the 15th
- Action item for Maya: send pricing before the 15th

**NARRATOR:** "Lynkk records in-person conversations too. Not just video calls."

### Scene 7: Tuesday, WhatsApp call [0:55 - 1:03]

**Visual:** Raj calls on WhatsApp. Maya takes it on her Mac. A notification slides in at the top of the screen: "Lynkk detected a WhatsApp call."

**RAJ:** "Please loop in Priya, our CFO."

**Visual:** A new line appears in the notes: Key contact: Priya, CFO, final sign-off.

**NARRATOR:** "The Lynkk Mac app detects WhatsApp and FaceTime calls, so nothing slips past."

### Scene 8: The CRM fills itself [1:03 - 1:12]

**Visual:** The Brightline record in Salesforce. Fields fill in one by one, like magic: Next step: send pricing. Close date: the 15th. Key contact: Priya, CFO. Maya leans back with her coffee. She didn't type a thing.

**NARRATOR:** "Lynkk updates Salesforce or HubSpot from what was actually said on the call, not from what someone remembers on Friday."

**On-screen text:** Filled from the conversation. Not from memory.

### Scene 9: Friday, forecast meeting [1:12 - 1:20]

**Visual:** Same meeting room. Tom stops on Brightline. Everything is there.

**TOM:** "Pricing went out on the 12th, the CFO is looped in... Brightline is committed."

**Visual:** Tom drags the deal into "Commit." He smiles at Maya.

**TOM:** "Now that's a forecast I can trust."

### Scene 10: The 15th [1:20 - 1:25]

**Visual:** A new email from Raj. Maya grins.

**On-screen email from Raj:** "Budget approved. Let's sign."

### Production notes

- Act 1 and Act 2 use the same locations, framing, and lines on purpose. The audience should feel "I've seen this before, but now it works."
- Act 1 color grade: slightly cool and flat. Act 2: warmer and brighter.
- Scene 6 shows Lynkk on a MacBook because Web and Mac are the confirmed platforms. If an iOS app is confirmed, a phone on the table reads more naturally.
