## Why are US teams looking for Otter.ai alternatives in 2026?

Three limits send people looking at Otter AI alternatives: a 30-minute cap per conversation on the free plan, only the 25 most recent conversations kept on Basic, and 20 AI chat queries a month. Teams that meet all day outgrow that within a week, usually right after the first long client call gets cut off before anyone agreed on next steps.

The other reasons we hear from teams:

- **Price at team scale.** Otter Business is $30 per user per month on monthly billing, or $19.99 billed yearly. A 10-person team pays $2,400 a year on the annual plan.
- **Notes stop at the transcript.** Otter writes a summary, but turning decisions into Jira tickets or follow-ups still takes manual work.
- **In-person meetings.** Many teams now want one tool for Zoom calls and for the conversation in the conference room.
- **Bot fatigue.** Some clients now ask to remove meeting bots, which pushes people toward bot-free capture.

## Is Otter AI really free?

Yes, Otter has a free Basic plan, but it allows 300 minutes a month, 30 minutes per conversation, 3 lifetime file imports and only your 25 most recent conversations. For occasional short calls it works. For daily client meetings it runs out fast.

## Is there a secure or local alternative to Otter AI?

Yes. Several secure alternatives to Otter AI are built for privacy first. Look for 3 things: bot-free capture, a choice of data region, and a clear no-training policy.

- **Bot-free capture:** Granola, Jamie and Lynkk's Mac app and Chrome extension record without a visible bot.
- **Data region:** Lynkk lets a workspace pin processing to the US, EU, UK, India, Singapore or Australia. Jamie hosts in the EU.
- **Local and open source:** local-first tools such as Meetily keep audio on your machine, at the cost of setup time and fewer integrations.

Meetily's Community edition is free for individuals, with Pro from $10 a user a month, so "open source" does not always mean free for a team. Data is encrypted with AES-256 in transit and at rest on Lynkk. Otter has a bot-free route too: its [desktop app records without a bot](https://otter.ai/desktop) on Zoom, Google Meet, Slack huddles and in person, so a privacy switch is not the only reason to leave. Whatever you pick, check whether the vendor trains models on your data before you sign.

## How do you switch from Otter without losing your notes?

Export first, then cancel. Otter Basic exports MP3 and TXT, and Pro adds PDF, DOCX and SRT, with bulk export from Business up. Follow these 4 steps:

1. **Export** your most important conversations from Otter before your plan changes.
2. **Connect your calendar** in the new tool so it joins upcoming meetings automatically.
3. **Run both tools for 1 week** on a few meetings and compare the notes side by side.
4. **Remove Otter's calendar access** so its bot stops joining calls.

## FAQ

### What is the best free alternative to Otter AI?

Fathom has the most generous free recording, with unlimited recordings and transcripts. Lynkk's free plan is smaller at 5 notes a month, but each note includes tasks with owners and Jira send, which Otter Basic does not offer. Fathom's free plan has no monthly cap.

### Is Otter AI worth it in 2026?

Otter is worth it if you need fast English transcription and live captions. It is less worth it if you need notes turned into tasks, in-person capture in the same tool, or meetings longer than 30 minutes on a free plan. Weigh those three gaps first.

### Why is Otter AI so expensive for teams?

Otter Business costs $30 per user per month on monthly billing, or $19.99 billed yearly. For a 10-person team that is up to $3,600 a year, which is why teams compare Fireflies, Fathom and Lynkk. Annual billing lowers the cost, but many teams still find cheaper options with similar features.

### Which Otter alternative works without a bot?

Granola and Jamie are built around bot-free capture. Lynkk offers both: a bot for Zoom, Meet and Teams, plus a Mac app and Chrome extension that record without one. Bot-free capture still records the meeting, so tell participants at the start, especially on client calls.

### Is there an Otter alternative that supports Hindi?

Yes. Lynkk supports Hindi and has a [Hinglish setting](https://lynkk.ai/features/hinglish-transcription) for calls that switch between Hindi and English mid-sentence. It covers 17 languages plus auto-detect in total. Tamil, Telugu, Marathi and Bengali are in beta, and teams can keep data processing in India.

### Can I import my Otter transcripts into another tool?

Most tools accept audio uploads rather than Otter's own files, so export your transcripts as TXT or DOCX from Otter and keep them in your docs, then let the new tool capture meetings going forward. Run both for a week. Compare.
