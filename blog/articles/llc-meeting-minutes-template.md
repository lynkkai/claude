# LLC Meeting Minutes Template for US Owners in 2026

*By [Samarth Wagh](https://lynkk.ai/blog/authors/samarth-wagh), Co-founder, Lynkk.ai · Last updated 17 October 2026*

> **Quick answer:** This LLC meeting minutes template records 6 items: **LLC name**, **meeting type, date and place**, **members or managers present**, **quorum**, **each resolution and vote**, and **signatures**. Most US states **do not require** LLCs to keep minutes, but your **operating agreement may**, and minutes help prove major decisions to banks, investors and courts. Hold at least **one annual meeting** and record it.

*Disclosure: Lynkk publishes this guide and makes an AI meeting assistant. This is general information, not legal advice; check your state law and operating agreement, or ask an attorney.*

LLC owners search for an LLC meeting minutes template for 2 reasons. Either a bank, lender or accountant asked for minutes, or they read that keeping them helps protect their personal liability. Both justify getting the basics right.

Covered here: whether LLCs need minutes, what to include, and copy-ready templates for annual meetings, first meetings and single-member LLCs.

## Are meeting minutes required for an LLC?

In most US states, the law does not require an LLC to hold annual meetings or keep minutes, unlike corporations. Your operating agreement can, though. If it does, you must follow it. Some lenders, investors and banks also ask for minutes or resolutions before acting.

Why keep them anyway:

- **Proof of decisions.** Minutes show who approved a loan, a new member or a large purchase.
- **Liability protection.** Treating the LLC as a separate business, with records of major decisions, supports the separation between you and the company if it is ever challenged. Skipping minutes alone rarely removes liability protection; courts look at the whole picture, such as mixing personal and business money.
- **Smoother transactions.** Banks and buyers often ask for a resolution authorizing an action.
- **Fewer disputes.** Multi-member LLCs avoid arguments about what was agreed.

## How often should an LLC have meeting minutes?

Hold and record at least 1 meeting a year, and record any major decision when it happens. Got a schedule in your operating agreement? Follow that.

Record minutes or a written resolution for decisions such as:

1. Admitting or removing a member
2. Changing ownership percentages or distributions
3. Taking on significant debt or signing a major contract
4. Buying or selling major assets or real estate
5. Opening bank accounts or changing signers
6. Amending the operating agreement
7. Electing or changing managers

## What does an LLC meeting minutes template look like?

Use this LLC meeting minutes template for annual or special meetings of a multi-member LLC.

```
[LLC NAME], LLC
MINUTES OF THE [ANNUAL / SPECIAL] MEETING OF THE MEMBERS

Date: [ ]   Time: [ ]   Location: [Address / video link]

CALL TO ORDER
The meeting was called to order at [time] by [Name], who acted
as chair. [Name] recorded the minutes.

MEMBERS PRESENT
[Name], [ownership %]
[Name], [ownership %]
Members absent: [Name]
Members holding [X]% of membership interests were present,
constituting a quorum under the Operating Agreement.

APPROVAL OF PRIOR MINUTES
The minutes of the meeting held on [date] were approved.

REPORTS
[Manager / member] presented [financial results, operations update].

RESOLUTIONS
1. RESOLVED, that [action, e.g. the Company open a business line of
   credit with Bank of ___ for up to $___ ].
   Vote: [For / Against / Abstain, by % or by member]. Approved.

2. RESOLVED, that [action].
   Vote: [ ]. Approved.

OTHER BUSINESS
[ ]

ADJOURNMENT
There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at [time].

_________________________   _________________________
[Name], Chair                [Name], Secretary / Recorder
Date: [ ]
```

## How do single-member LLCs record minutes?

A single-member LLC has 1 owner and no one else to meet with, so the usual approach is a written consent or resolution signed by the sole member. That records the decision the same way minutes would.

```
[LLC NAME], LLC
WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE SOLE MEMBER

The undersigned, being the sole member of [LLC Name], LLC,
a [State] limited liability company, adopts the following
resolutions effective [date]:

1. RESOLVED, that [action, e.g. the Company's financial statements
   for [year] are approved].

2. RESOLVED, that [action, e.g. the Company enter into a lease for
   office space at ___ for ___ months].

_________________________
[Name], Sole Member
Date: [ ]
```

Many solo owners sign one annual consent covering the year's major decisions, plus separate consents for big one-off actions.

## What goes in LLC first meeting minutes?

Usually held within weeks of filing, the first meeting (sometimes called the organizational meeting) records the setup decisions after you form the LLC. Typical resolutions include:

- Adopting the operating agreement
- Confirming the members and their ownership percentages
- Recording initial capital contributions
- Naming managers, if manager-managed
- Approving the EIN application and opening a business bank account
- Choosing the fiscal year and accountant
- Authorizing payment of formation costs

List these as numbered resolutions in the main template above. Registered agent services such as [Northwest Registered Agent](https://www.northwestregisteredagent.com/legal-forms/llc/first-meeting-minutes) also publish free first-meeting forms.

## Do manager-managed LLCs need different minutes?

Yes, slightly. Member-managed LLCs record meetings of their members. A manager-managed LLC usually records 2 kinds of meetings: manager meetings for day-to-day decisions, and member meetings for the decisions the operating agreement reserves to members, such as admitting new members or selling the business.

| | Member-managed LLC | Manager-managed LLC |
|---|---|---|
| Who decides most things | Members | Managers |
| Typical minutes | Member meetings | Manager meetings and member meetings |
| Member vote needed for | Most major decisions | Only reserved decisions |

Check your operating agreement for which decisions need a member vote. Record those as member resolutions even if managers proposed them.

## What should LLC meeting minutes include?

LLC meeting minutes should include 8 items:

| Item | Example |
|---|---|
| LLC name and meeting type | "Annual meeting of the members" |
| Date, time, place | Including video link if remote |
| Who chaired and who recorded | Names |
| Members or managers present | With ownership percentages |
| Quorum statement | Per the operating agreement |
| Resolutions | Exact wording of each decision |
| Votes | For, against, abstain |
| Signatures | Chair and recorder, with date |

Keep discussion summaries short. What matters later is the resolutions and votes.

## Where should you keep LLC minutes?

Store LLC minutes with your other company records (the articles of organization, operating agreement, member list and tax filings), ideally as signed PDFs in a dedicated folder, physical or digital. Some states require LLCs to keep certain records at their principal office, so check your state's rules.

How long? For the life of the company. Storage costs nothing, and minutes can matter years later during a sale, audit or dispute.

## FAQ

### Does an LLC need meeting minutes?

Most states do not legally require LLCs to keep minutes, but your operating agreement may, and minutes help document major decisions and support liability protection. Banks, lenders and buyers often ask for minutes or resolutions before approving a loan or a sale. Keeping them saves time later.

### How often should an LLC hold meetings?

Hold at least one annual meeting and record it, plus log major decisions as they happen. Follow any schedule in your operating agreement. Single-member LLCs can sign one annual written consent for the year's major decisions, with separate ones for big one-off actions.

### What is a single-member LLC meeting minutes template?

Usually it is a written consent signed by the sole member, listing resolutions for the year or for a specific decision. That document captures the decision just as minutes would. Banks often ask to see it before opening an account or approving credit.

### Who signs LLC meeting minutes?

Usually the person who chaired the meeting and the person who recorded the minutes sign. Single-member LLC? The sole member signs a written consent. Use a printed name, signature and date for each signer, and file the signed copy alongside the operating agreement and other company records.

### What decisions should be recorded in LLC minutes?

Admitting members, changing ownership, taking significant debt, buying or selling major assets, opening bank accounts, amending the operating agreement and electing managers. Record each one as a resolution with exact wording and the vote. Why the precision? Banks, buyers and courts will rely on that wording later.

### Can LLC minutes be kept digitally?

Yes, in most cases. Signed PDFs stored securely with your other company records work well; just check any record-keeping rules in your state and operating agreement. Hold on to minutes for the life of the company, since they cost nothing to store and can matter years later during a sale, audit or dispute.
