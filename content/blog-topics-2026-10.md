# Lynkk Blog Topic Plan (October 2026, v4: TRUTHS.md aligned)

Sources: Ubersuggest (US and India), Google Search Console export (2026-10-12), the 32 articles already written
(content/blog-inventory-2026-10-12.md), every live lynkk.ai page, and `lynkk-post-kit/docs/TRUTHS.md`
(branch `claude/eager-tesla-cvuh6f`, last checked against the product 2026-09-29).

## Selection rules (v4)

1. **TRUTHS.md only.** Every topic must be served by something TRUTHS.md says Lynkk does. No phone or mobile app,
   no file uploads, no speaking bot, no CRM, no regions, no accuracy numbers, no prices.
2. **No named-competitor topics.** TRUTHS.md says do not name competitors unless the Lynkk team supplies sourced facts.
   Built-in platform tools (macOS, Microsoft Teams, Microsoft Word, Google Docs) may appear as how-to steps, as in the
   existing drafts.
3. **No repeats.** Not already written (live or draft) and not targeted by a live lynkk.ai page.
4. **High volume, low competition:** primary keyword 1,000+ searches a month, SEO difficulty (SD) 38 or lower.

## Competition notes from SERP checks

- **#2 Hindi voice typing (India):** top results are small tool sites (DA 4 to 20). Very winnable.
- **#3 Tamil voice typing (India):** a Play Store app ranks first, then sites with DA 3 to 42. Winnable.
- **#1 Meeting agenda template (US):** Microsoft, Canva and Zoom (DA 88 to 93) hold the top 5, but sites with DA 21
  and 37 rank in the top 10. Page 1 is realistic; the top 3 will take links.

## Must confirm with the Lynkk team before writing

1. **#3, #9 and the optional Telugu/Bengali post:** TRUTHS.md confirms Tamil, Marathi, Telugu and Bengali
   *transcription* (beta). It says Mac cloud dictation hears 18 languages but does not list them. Until the team
   confirms, these posts must sell transcription only, not voice typing in those languages.
2. **#2 and #3 searcher intent:** many searchers want a phone keyboard or an online tool. Lynkk has no phone app and
   no browser dictation, so these posts should show the platform options honestly and pitch the Mac app.
3. **#4:** Lynkk records audio, not the screen. The post teaches screen recording with macOS tools and positions
   Lynkk for call audio and notes only.
