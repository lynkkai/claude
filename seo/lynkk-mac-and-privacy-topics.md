## Seeds that came back empty

Reporting these so nobody re-researches them:

- **`on device transcription`**: zero volume on the head term and every variant (`mac on device
  transcription`, `apple on device transcription`, `best on device transcription` all 0). The concept
  has no search demand yet, despite being a real product capability.
- **`gdpr compliant transcription`**: zero volume, head term and all variants.
- **`apple notes alternative`**: 70/mo on the head term and then nothing. Every platform variant
  (`for windows`, `for android`, `for linux`, `open source`, `self hosted`) returns 0 to 10. M5 is
  viable only as a small post, not a cluster.
- **`macos note app`** and **`mac transcription app`**: 10/mo each. The volume in this space attaches
  to "note taking app for mac", not to "macos" or "mac app" phrasing. Worth knowing for how you write
  titles.
- **`zero knowledge encryption`** (210, SD 16) looked promising but the SERP intent is password
  managers and cloud storage, not notes. Wrong audience, skip it.
- **`local llm`** is 4,400 at SD 45, with `best local llm` 1,600 at SD 31 and `local llm mac` 90. I
  am not recommending it despite the volume: the intent is developers and hobbyists choosing models
  and GPUs, not buyers choosing a meeting tool. It would bring traffic that never converts.
