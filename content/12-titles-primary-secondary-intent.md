## What the live SERPs told me about title format

I pulled the actual ranking pages before writing these titles. Three findings changed the wording:

1. **Personal testing in the title outranks domain authority.**
   For `best ai note taker`, position 2 is thebusinessdive.com (DA **34**) with
   *"Best 9 AI Note-Takers | My Top Picks After +2 Years"*, taking an estimated **652 clicks**.
   Zapier (DA 81) sits at position 3 with 92 clicks. The tested-personally framing wins.

2. **Medium already ranks in this exact market.**
   For `ai note taker for in person meetings`, position 3 is a Medium post:
   *"Best AI Meeting Note-Takers in 2026: I Tested 14 Over 90 Days, Here's the Honest Ranking"*.
   It is sitting above plaud.ai, read.ai, otter.ai and notta.ai. This is direct proof that the
   Medium strategy works for Lynkk, and it sets the format: a number, a time period, and the
   word "honest" or "tested".

3. **For `mcp server`, the SERP wants a definition, not a tutorial.**
   Position 2 is Stack Overflow's *"No Dumb Questions: What is an MCP server and why do I..."*
   at an estimated **17,871 clicks**, then the official docs, then Reddit. Every top result is
   explanatory. So the Hashnode title must lead with the explainer promise and deliver the build
   second, or it will not match intent and will not rank.

4. **For `otter ai alternative`, low-DA sites win with an angle in the title.**
   DA 34 at position 2, DA 43 at 4, DA 14 at 8 with *"Choose by the Wall You Hit"*. There is no
   authority moat here. The differentiator is the organising idea, which is why M4 below is cut
   by workflow rather than by feature.

## One claims problem to resolve before S3, M1 or M3 go out

The Ubersuggest project's business summary for lynkk.ai is returning product claims that are
**not** in `memory/lynkk-brand.md`, specifically **97% transcription accuracy**, **AES-256
encryption** and **GDPR**. Someone on the team entered those, or the tool inferred them. I do not
know which, and I will not publish an accuracy figure or a compliance claim on either basis.

Before S3 (and before M1 or M3 quote any accuracy number), confirm from the team:

- Is 97% a measured number? Measured on what audio, against what reference, by whom?
- Is AES-256 at rest, in transit, or both?
- GDPR: self-assessed, DPA available, or audited? SOC 2 status?
- Which data regions are actually live?
- Is customer audio used for model training, ever?

These are the same items still open in the "To confirm" list in `memory/lynkk-brand.md`.
