# Read AI Alternatives US Teams Are Switching To in 2026

*By Samarth Wagh, Co-founder, Lynkk.ai · Last updated 17 October 2026 · Pricing checked 17 October 2026*

> **Quick answer:** The best Read AI alternative depends on what you need after the call. **Lynkk** turns meetings into tasks and Jira tickets (free for 5 notes, $15 Pro). **Fathom** gives unlimited free recordings. **Fireflies** syncs to CRMs from $10 a seat billed yearly. **Granola** and **Jamie** skip the bot. **Teams Copilot** suits Microsoft-only companies.

*Disclosure: Lynkk publishes this guide and is one of the tools reviewed. Prices come from vendor pricing pages.*

Teams usually start looking for a Read AI alternative for one of three reasons. The free plan stops at 5 meetings a month. The bot joins calls people did not expect it in. Or the summaries are good, but nobody acts on them.

Read AI is a capable product. It summarizes meetings on Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, scores engagement and talk time, and coaches you on how you ran the call. If those metrics are what you value, stay. If you want something else from your meetings, the seven tools below are where US teams are moving.

## Why do teams look for a Read AI alternative?

The most common trigger is the free plan limit: 5 meeting transcripts, 5 reports and 5 live-note sessions a month, each capped at 1 hour. After that, Pro costs $19.75 per user per month, or $15 billed yearly.

Other reasons we hear from US teams:

- **Auto-join surprises.** Once one person connects a calendar, the notetaker can show up in meetings where other attendees did not expect it. IT teams often ask how to control this.
- **Metrics over action.** Engagement scores are interesting, but teams want the follow-up email written and the ticket filed.
- **Seat minimums.** Read AI's Enterprise tier requires 5 or more licenses.
- **One place for online and in-person.** Many teams want the same tool in the conference room and on Zoom.

## Which Read AI alternatives are worth switching to?

We judged each Read AI alternative on 4 things: free limits, price per user, privacy controls, and what happens after the meeting ends.

### 1. Lynkk (our product): best for turning meetings into work

Lynkk records online meetings through a bot on Zoom, Google Meet and Teams, and records in-person conversations or bot-free calls through its Mac app and Chrome extension. Where Read AI measures the meeting, Lynkk acts on it.

- **Meeting Brief** before each call, with what is still open from past meetings.
- **Cue** answers questions during the call (Pro).
- **Tasks with owners and dates**, a decision log and one-click Jira tickets after the call.
- **Ask Lynkk** searches all your meetings and shows the exact source.

It supports 17 languages plus auto-detect, including Hindi and Hinglish, and lets teams pin data to the US, EU, UK, India, Singapore or Australia. Free covers 5 notes a month. Pro is $15 a month.

**Trade-off:** no Windows or mobile apps yet, and CRM sync is listed as coming soon.

### 2. Otter.ai: best for live English transcription

Otter shows a live transcript during the call and answers questions about it afterward. Its free plan gives 300 minutes a month, but each conversation stops at 30 minutes. Pro is $8.33 a month billed yearly. See [Read AI vs Otter AI](https://lynkk.ai/blog/read-ai-vs-otter-ai) for a full comparison.

### 3. Fireflies.ai: best for sales teams on a CRM

Fireflies pushes call notes into CRMs and offers search across every call. Pro is $10 per seat billed yearly and Business is $19. Lower tiers have storage limits, and some AI features use credits.

### 4. Fathom: best free Read AI alternative

Fathom's free plan includes unlimited recordings and transcripts, which makes it the strongest free Read AI alternative. Premium is $16 a month billed yearly, and Business adds CRM field sync at $25.

### 5. Granola: best for notes without a bot

Granola captures audio from your device, so no bot ever joins. It turns your rough typed notes into full ones. Business costs $14 per user per month. Our [Granola AI alternatives guide](https://lynkk.ai/blog/granola-ai-alternatives) covers its limits.

### 6. Jamie: best for EU data hosting

Jamie records without a bot, hosts data in the EU and lists 99+ languages. Free gives 10 notes a month up to 30 minutes each. Team costs $37 per seat billed yearly.

### 7. Microsoft Teams Copilot: the Microsoft alternative to Read AI

If your company runs on Microsoft 365, Teams' AI recap features come with a Microsoft 365 Copilot license, so you may already pay for them. They stay inside Teams, so Zoom calls and in-person meetings are not covered. Our [Teams AI note takers guide](https://lynkk.ai/blog/ai-note-taker-microsoft-teams) compares the options.

## Is there a free Read AI alternative?

Yes. Fathom is the most generous free Read AI alternative, with unlimited recordings and transcripts. Otter gives 300 free minutes a month, Jamie gives 10 notes, and Lynkk gives 5 notes a month with tasks and Jira send included.

Read AI's own free plan stops at 5 meetings, so even the smallest free plans above are comparable. The real question is which free plan matches your meeting length. Otter's 30-minute cap and Jamie's 30-minute cap rule out hour-long calls. Lynkk allows up to 2 hours per note.

## Is Read AI safe and private?

Read AI's top tier, Enterprise+ ($29.75 billed yearly), adds SAML, SCIM, domain capture, HIPAA compliance and custom retention. The common concern is less about security and more about control: who can send the notetaker into a meeting.

If privacy is your reason for switching, compare these 3 controls:

1. **Bot-free capture:** Granola and Jamie never use a bot. Lynkk's Mac app and Chrome extension record without one.
2. **Data location:** Lynkk pins processing to one of 6 regions per workspace. Jamie hosts in the EU.
3. **Training on your data:** Granola lets every plan opt out of model training. Jamie states it does not train on your data.

## Which Read AI alternative is best for teams?

For teams of 5 to 50, the best Read AI alternatives are Fireflies (CRM sync), Fathom Team ($15 per user billed yearly, 2-user minimum) and Lynkk (Jira tasks and shared meeting memory). Each removes the 5-seat minimum that Read AI's Enterprise tier carries.

Match the tool to the team:

- **Sales:** Fireflies or Fathom Business, for CRM fields and call review.
- **Product and engineering:** Lynkk, for decisions and Jira tickets. See [how product teams turn minutes into Jira issues](https://lynkk.ai/blog/meeting-minutes-to-jira).
- **Consultants and founders:** Granola or Lynkk, for private notes and prep before back-to-back calls.

## How do you switch from Read AI cleanly?

Switching takes about 15 minutes. Follow these 4 steps so the old bot stops joining calls:

1. **Export** the reports and transcripts you need from Read AI.
2. **Disconnect** Read AI from your calendar and turn off auto-join in its settings.
3. **Connect** your calendar to the new tool and test it on 2 or 3 internal meetings.
4. **Tell your team** which tool now records, so nobody is surprised by a new bot.

## FAQ

### What is the best Read AI alternative?

The best Read AI alternative for most teams is the one that matches their follow-up work. Lynkk suits teams that want tasks and Jira tickets, Fireflies suits CRM-driven sales teams, and Fathom suits anyone who wants free unlimited recording.

### Is there a free alternative to Read AI?

Yes. Fathom offers unlimited free recordings and transcripts. Otter, Jamie and Lynkk also have free plans, with limits of 300 minutes, 10 notes and 5 notes a month respectively. Read AI's own free plan stops at 5 meetings a month, so even the smallest free plans above are comparable for light use.

### What is the Microsoft alternative to Read AI?

Microsoft's alternative is the AI recap in Teams, which comes with a Microsoft 365 Copilot license. It works well inside Teams but does not cover Zoom, Google Meet or in-person meetings. If your company does not license Copilot, a third-party tool that joins Teams calls is usually the more affordable route.

### Who are Read AI's main competitors?

Read AI's main competitors are Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, Fathom, Granola, tl;dv, Jamie and Lynkk. Each takes a different approach, from live transcription to bot-free notes to automated follow-up. Compare them on free limits, price per user, privacy controls and what happens after the meeting.

### Can I use a Read AI alternative without a meeting bot?

Yes. Granola and Jamie are bot-free by design. Lynkk gives you both options: a bot for Zoom, Meet and Teams, or bot-free recording through its Mac app and Chrome extension. Bot-free capture still records, so announce it at the start of each call, especially with clients.

### Does any Read AI alternative support Hindi?

Lynkk supports Hindi and has a Hinglish setting for conversations that mix Hindi and English. Read AI lists 20+ languages, so check its list against the languages your team uses. Test any tool on a real meeting in each language your team uses before you roll it out.
