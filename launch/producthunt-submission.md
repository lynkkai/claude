## Maker's first comment

> Hey Product Hunt 👋
>
> We built Lynkk because most AI notetakers only work for one kind of meeting: a video call, with a bot in it, in English. Real work doesn't look like that. Some calls are on Zoom, some on Teams, some at a customer's office, and plenty of them switch between two languages mid-sentence.
>
> Lynkk records all of it and turns each conversation into a note:
>
> **Record your way.** Send a meeting bot to Zoom, Google Meet or Teams (it joins from your Google Calendar and shows up under your name). Or use the Mac app or Chrome extension with no bot in the call. For a meeting in the room, press record on your Mac or in the browser and leave the laptop on the table.
>
> **Notes you can trust.** Every note has a transcript with speaker labels, a summary, action items with owners and due dates, and decisions, each linked to the moment it was said, so you can click and hear it.
>
> **Work that goes somewhere.** Send action items to Jira as issues. On Pro, ask questions across every meeting ("what did we promise Acme last time?") and get answers with numbered sources, in the app or in Slack.
>
> A few things we care about:
> - **17 languages plus Hinglish**, for teams who mix Hindi and English in the same sentence.
> - **No AI training on your conversations.**
> - **Free that doesn't run out**: unlimited notes on the free plan, with your first 5 hours of AI summaries included.
> - **Cue (beta)**: help on your screen during the call in the Mac app, by invite.
>
> Honest list of what's not there yet: no CRM sync (you can copy any brief into Salesforce or HubSpot), no Windows or phone app, no file upload, and Outlook calendar isn't connected yet. Tell us which of those you want first.
>
> We're here all day for questions, feature requests and hard ones. 🙏
