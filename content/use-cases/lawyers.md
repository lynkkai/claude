### Block 2. How it works

## Client meetings, written down for you

**Record: with the client's consent.** Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or in the room with the Mac app.

**Read: facts, decisions, next steps.** Each with the moment it was said, so you can hear it again.

**Find: any detail later.** On Pro, ask "What did the client say about the notice date?" across the matter's meetings.

### Block 4. Features

## AI for law firms that keeps client data private

**Private by default**: notes are yours until you share them, and share links can be revoked.

**Choose your data region**: US, EU, UK, India, Singapore or Australia.

**In-person consultations**: record the room with the Mac app, even with no signal.

**Ask across the matter**: answers cite the exact meeting and moment. Pro.

### Block 6. FAQ

## Questions about AI for lawyers

**Is Lynkk a legal transcription service for court?**

No. Lynkk writes notes and transcripts for your own work. It isn't a certified court transcript. See our guide to
[legal transcription services](/blog/legal-transcription-service).

**Is client data used to train AI models?**

No. Your conversations are never used to train models.

**Where is the data processed?**

In the region you choose for your workspace: US, EU, UK, India, Singapore or Australia.

**Do I need the client's consent to record?**

Yes, get consent first. Rules differ by country and by bar, so check your professional obligations before recording.
