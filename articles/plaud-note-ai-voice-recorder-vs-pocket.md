## What is the Plaud Note AI voice recorder and how does it work?

The Plaud Note AI voice recorder is a card-sized device that records conversations and sends the audio to Plaud's app for transcription and AI summaries. The current Plaud Note Pro has 4 MEMS microphones, a pickup range of 16.4 feet, up to 50 hours of recording and 60 days of standby.

You press a button to record, then sync the file to your phone or computer. Plaud transcribes it in up to 112 languages and writes a summary using templates for meetings, interviews, lectures and more.

The appeal of the Plaud Note AI voice recorder is simple: it captures conversations away from your laptop, such as site visits, client lunches and interviews, without taking out your phone.

## Can you use Plaud without the device or a subscription?

Yes, in 2 ways. The free Starter plan gives 300 minutes a month with no subscription, and Plaud Desktop is a bot-free app for macOS and Windows that records Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Webex and Slack calls into your Plaud account.

Pocket works the other way around: its core features need no plan, and Pocket Pro is optional at $19.99 a month, with the first 30 days free.

The Plaud Desktop point matters most. If you mostly want online calls recorded, you are no longer choosing a device at all; you are choosing between apps, and that is where Lynkk fits.

## Do you need an AI voice recorder device or just an app?

You need a device if more than 50% of your important conversations happen away from a laptop: on site, in a car, at lunch or walking between meetings. For calls and desk meetings, an app records the same conversation with 0 extra hardware to buy, charge or carry.

The [Chrome extension](https://lynkk.ai/features/chrome-extension) records Meet, Zoom (web) and Teams in a browser tab, and the [free Lynkk meeting bot](https://lynkk.ai/features/meeting-bot) joins from your calendar. ## Is it legal to record in-person conversations with an AI voice recorder?

It depends on the state: 1 party consent is enough under federal law, but some states require everyone in the conversation to agree. Federal law ([18 U.S.C. § 2511](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title18-section2511)) allows 1 party consent, while California ([Penal Code § 632](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=632)) requires all parties to consent to recording a confidential conversation.

A small device on the table is easy to miss, so say out loud that you are recording, whichever tool you use. The [NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework/privacy-framework) is a useful checklist for reviewing any vendor. Lynkk keeps recordings private to your account and never trains on your conversations.

## FAQ

### Is Plaud better than Pocket?
Plaud is better for long recording days, with up to 50 hours of recording and 4 microphones on the Note Pro, as listed on September 30, 2026. Pocket is better if you want a 52 g MagSafe clip-on with no required plan. If your meetings are mostly online, Lynkk records them with no device at all.

### How much does the Plaud subscription cost?
Plaud's Pro plan costs $99.99 a year or $17.99 a month, and Unlimited costs $239.99 a year or $29.99 a month, as checked on September 30, 2026. The free Starter plan includes 300 minutes a month. The Plaud Note Pro device itself costs $189 on top of any plan.

### Do I need a device to record in-person meetings?
No, not if you have a laptop in the room. Lynkk records in-person meetings by recording your microphone in the browser at lynkk.ai, then writes a Meeting Brief with 7 parts. A device such as Plaud or Pocket is better when you record away from a laptop or for many hours a day.

### Can a laptop app replace an AI voice recorder?
For calls and desk meetings, yes. Lynkk's Mac app records Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Slack, WhatsApp, FaceTime and Webex calls with no bot, and the browser records in-person meetings, with a 2 hour cap per recording. For site visits and long days on the move, a recorder is still the better tool.

### Does Plaud record online meetings?
Yes. Plaud Desktop is a bot-free app for macOS and Windows that records Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Webex and Slack calls into your Plaud account, as listed on September 30, 2026. Lynkk records the same 5 platforms on the Mac and adds Jira send and a morning brief before meetings.
