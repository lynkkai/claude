# Lynkk Conversational Brain: 40-Second Video Script

**Format:** Short-form video (Reels / Shorts / LinkedIn / X)
**Length:** ~40 seconds (~105 words of voiceover)
**Tone:** Relatable, a little chaotic, then calm and confident once Lynkk shows up

---

## 0-5s | HOOK

**Visual:** A video call grid. The manager smiles: "So... what did we decide last week?" Six faces freeze. One person (Mark) slowly mimes a frozen screen.

**VO:** "Ever been in a meeting where nobody remembers the last meeting?"

---

## 5-13s | THE PROBLEM

**Visual:** Quick cuts: a sticky note that just says "follow up???", a notebook page with a cat doodle and zero notes, a Slack search for "that thing from Tuesday" returning nothing.

**VO:** "Decisions vanish. Action items disappear. And the only record is 'follow up' with three question marks... next to a cat."

---

## 13-30s | THE SOLUTION

**Visual:** Lynkk logo pops in. A knowledge graph animates: people, topics, and decisions connecting across past meetings.

**VO:** "Meet Lynkk's conversational brain. It remembers every meeting, online or in person, and connects who said what, what was decided, and what's still open."

**Visual:** Back on the same call. The manager says out loud: "Lynkk, what did we promise the client?"

**Lynkk (voice):** "Delivery by the 15th, and Mark owes them the pricing deck."

**Visual:** Mark's "frozen screen" instantly unfreezes.

---

## 30-36s | THE PAYOFF

**Visual:** Call ends. Notes, action items, CRM fields, and Jira tickets fly into place on screen.

**VO:** "Thirty seconds after the call: notes, action items, CRM updated, Jira tickets created."

**Mark:** "Sorry, my Wi-Fi died. What did I miss?"
**Lynkk:** "Nothing. It's all in your notes."

---

## 36-40s | CTA

**Visual:** Lynkk logo + URL on screen.

**VO:** "Lynkk. Your meetings finally have a memory. Start free at lynkk.ai."

---

### Production notes
- Keep the VO brisk (about 2.7 words per second) to land at 40 seconds.
- The "frozen screen" gag is the running joke: set it up in the hook, pay it off in the solution and end beats.
- On-screen captions recommended; most short-form views are on mute.
- Avoid naming specific meeting platforms or CRMs until confirmed (see `memory/lynkk-brand.md`).
