# Lynkk.ai: All Blog Titles, USA, Ubersuggest Volume Only

Prepared 2026-11-03. Every title carries a US monthly search volume from Ubersuggest (locId 2840).
No GSC-derived topics. No zero-volume topics. Fresh discovery run across 12 new seed sets
(`meeting summary`, `meeting recap`, `meeting minutes`, `interview transcription`,
`sales call recording`, `call recording software`, `note taking app`, `transcribe audio to text`
and others) on top of the earlier work.

**Vol** = sum of US monthly volume for every keyword variant in the cluster.
**SD** = Ubersuggest search difficulty of the entry keyword (the easiest viable variant), not the head
term. **Risk** = cannibalization against the 54 existing lynkk.ai blog posts.

### Notes on the Tier 1 entries that matter most

**#1, AI note taking apps, 12,650 volume at SD 26.** The largest low-difficulty cluster anywhere on
this map, and I did not find it until this run. Entry keyword `ai note taking app` is **4,400/mo at
SD 26**, with `best ai note taking app` 2,400 (SD 35), `ai powered note taking app` 3,600 (SD 45),
`best ai note taking app for meetings` 480 (SD 33), `best free ai note taking app` 390 (SD 27).
**High cannibalization risk**: `/blog/best-ai-note-takers` already exists at position 36.65 with 948
impressions, and `/blog/best-free-ai-note-takers` too. My recommendation is **not** to write a new
post. Re-optimise `best-ai-note-takers` to target "note taking app" phrasing alongside "note taker",
because "app" and "taker" are the same intent and Google will treat two pages as duplicates. That
single re-optimisation addresses more volume than any new post in this document.

**#2, Mac note taking app, 5,000 volume, and `mac note taking app` carries SD 9.** An SD of 9 at
1,300 monthly searches is the best volume-to-difficulty ratio I found. Lynkk ships a Mac desktop app
with cursor-level dictation, so the product fit is direct, and no existing post targets it.
`/blog/dictation-on-mac` is adjacent but narrow (35 impressions), so keep dictation phrasing there.

**#3 and #4, the meeting minutes clusters.** Two distinct intents worth separating. #3 is software
and tools (`app for meeting minutes` 1,000 at SD 24, `software for meeting minutes` 1,600 at
**$31.09 CPC**, `board meeting minutes software` 140 at SD 24 and $28.16). #4 is the how-to
(`how to do meeting minutes` 1,600, `how to take meeting minutes` 1,000 at SD 30, `taking meeting
minutes` 720 at SD 25). **#4 is high risk** against `/blog/meeting-minutes-sample` (235 impressions,
position 16.4) and `/blog/meeting-notes-vs-minutes` (142 impressions). Either re-scope the existing
sample post into the full how-to, or keep all "sample" and "example" phrasing there and all "how to"
phrasing in #4. Do not publish #4 without making that call first.

**#5, interview transcription, 3,600 volume with entry SD 17.** `interview transcription` is 1,300 at
SD 25, `interview transcription format` 260 at **SD 17**, `interview transcription company` 260 at
SD 18, `software for interview transcription` 260 at SD 21. Medium risk against
`/blog/recruiting-interview-transcription` (584 impressions, position 29.6), which targets recruiting
specifically. Keep #5 on research and qualitative framing, and link the two.

**#10 contains the highest CPC on the entire map.** `teams copilot meeting summary` is 90/mo at SD 30
with a **$70.78 CPC**. Small volume, extraordinary commercial value. `zoom ai companion meeting
summary` adds 390 at SD 26.

**#11, privacy-first note apps, has the lowest difficulty scores I found.** `privacy focused note
taking app` **SD 5**, `local note taking app` **SD 7**, `note taking app offline` SD 26. The bigger
variants are harder (`secure note taking app` 480 at SD 44, `end to end encrypted` 320 at SD 43), so
enter on the SD 5 to 26 terms. Direct fit for Lynkk's offline-first positioning.

### One caveat that still applies

These are Google volumes, not traffic forecasts. lynkk.ai is DA 1 with zero follow backlinks and a
0.08% non-brand CTR, so a low SD score means "winnable eventually", not "winnable this month". The
four Tier 2 entries are the only ones where I verified by live SERP that sub-DA-35 domains actually
rank. For everything in Tier 1 and Tier 3, the SD score is Ubersuggest's estimate and I have not
SERP-checked it. If you want that check before committing a writer, say which titles and I will run
