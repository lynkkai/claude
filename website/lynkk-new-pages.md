#### Sales Follow-Up Email: 12 Templates to Send After a Sales Call

**Quick answer:** Send a sales follow-up email within 24 hours of the call. Thank the buyer for one specific thing they said, recap their problem in their words, confirm the next step with a date, and attach only what you promised. Keep it under 150 words. The 12 templates below cover discovery calls, demos, pricing calls and proposals that got no reply.

##### When to send a follow-up email after a sales call

Send it the same day if you can, and always within 24 hours. The buyer still remembers the call, and your email becomes the written record their team forwards around. If you agreed on a next meeting during the call, send the calendar invite first and the email right after, so both arrive together.

| Situation | When to send |
|---|---|
| After a discovery call | Same day |
| After a demo | Within 2 hours, while questions are fresh |
| After a pricing or negotiation call | Same day, with the numbers in writing |
| No reply to a proposal | 3 business days later, then 7, then 14 |
| Buyer went quiet after a good call | 5 business days later |

##### What to include in a sales follow-up email

Every good sales follow-up email has five parts. Skip any of them and the buyer has to work out what happens next, which is when deals stall.

1. **A specific thank-you.** Name something they said, not "thanks for your time".
2. **Their problem, in their words.** One or two sentences that prove you listened.
3. **What you agreed.** Decisions and open questions from the call.
4. **The next step, with a date.** Who does what, by when.
5. **One attachment or link.** Only what you promised on the call.

#### How to Record a Teams Meeting

**Quick answer:** To record a Microsoft Teams meeting, join the meeting, select **More actions (...)** in the meeting controls, then **Record and transcribe** and **Start recording**. Everyone in the meeting sees that it is being recorded. When the meeting ends, the recording appears in the meeting chat and is saved to OneDrive, or to SharePoint for channel meetings. You need a Microsoft 365 work or school license with recording turned on, and you must be the organizer or a presenter from the same organization.

##### How to record a Teams meeting on desktop

1. Join the meeting in the Teams desktop app.
2. Select **More actions (...)** in the meeting controls at the top.
3. Select **Record and transcribe**, then **Start recording**.
4. To stop, select **More actions (...)** again, then **Record and transcribe**, then **Stop recording**.

Recording also stops when everyone leaves. Teams starts the transcript at the same time as the recording, unless your admin has turned transcription off.

The steps are the same in Teams on the web (in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome): **More actions (...)**, **Record and transcribe**, **Start recording**. If the option is missing in the browser, try the desktop app, which supports every meeting feature.

Where Teams saves a recording depends on the meeting type:

| Meeting type | Where the recording is saved |
|---|---|
| Regular (non-channel) meeting | The OneDrive of the person who started the recording, in a Recordings folder |
| Channel meeting | The channel's SharePoint site, in a Recordings folder |
| Any meeting | A link in the meeting chat and on the meeting's Recap tab |

To find a recording later, open the meeting chat, or go to the meeting in your Teams calendar and select **Recap**. People invited to the meeting can watch it; external guests need the recording shared with them.

##### How to download a Teams recording

Open the recording from the meeting chat or Recap tab, select **Open in OneDrive** (or SharePoint), then select **Download**. The file downloads as an .mp4. If Download is missing, the owner or your admin has blocked it: ask the person who recorded the meeting to share a copy.

##### FAQ

###### What is the best Gong alternative for small teams?

For coaching and deal insight, Avoma. For call notes and follow-ups on a small budget, Lynkk at $4 a month or Fireflies at $10 per seat. For free call recording, Fathom.

###### Is there a cheaper alternative to Gong?

Yes. Every tool in this list publishes lower prices or has a free plan except Clari Copilot and Chorus, which are quoted. Lynkk Pro ($4 a month), Otter Pro ($8.33) and Fireflies Pro ($10) are the lowest paid options.

###### Does Gong have a free trial?

Gong does not offer a self-serve free trial. You see the product in a demo and buy through its sales team.

###### Can a Gong alternative sync to Salesforce or HubSpot?

Avoma, Clari Copilot, Fireflies and Fathom's paid plans all sync to common CRMs. Lynkk does not sync to a CRM yet.

Gong is built for large sales teams with forecasting needs and the budget for an annual contract. Smaller teams usually get what they need from Avoma, Fireflies or Fathom, and Lynkk covers call notes and follow-up emails for $4 a month. [Get Lynkk Pro](/pricing).
