## 2. Key findings from competitors

   "personal knowledge graph" across meetings, email and chat, but its Search Copilot
   article ranks only for "read.ai vs copilot" (position 33). The meeting-memory angle is open.

5. **Skip the head term "knowledge graph"** (9,900, SD 44). The SERP is Google, IBM, Wikipedia,
   and the searchers want a definition, not software. Use it naturally on the page,
   but don't make it the target.

## 4. Page outline

**URL:** keep `/features/knowledge-graph`.

**Title tag (39 chars):** `AI Knowledge Graph for Meetings | Lynkk`
Alt (53 chars): `Meeting Knowledge Graph: Your AI Second Brain | Lynkk`

**Meta description (155 chars):**
Lynkk turns every meeting into an AI knowledge graph of people, decisions and tasks. Search it, ask it anything, and never lose team knowledge. Start free.

**H1:** The AI knowledge graph for every meeting you've ever had

**Intro (put "AI knowledge graph" in the first 100 words):**
Lynkk builds an AI knowledge graph from your meetings, online and in person. Every person,
company, project, decision and action item gets connected across calls, so you can search
everything you've discussed, see what's still open, and walk into your next meeting
already prepared.

**H2 sections (target keyword in brackets):**

1. **What is a meeting knowledge graph?** [ai knowledge graph, what is a knowledge graph in ai]
   Open with a 40 to 60 word definition. That's the length Google tends to pull into
   featured snippets and AI Overviews.
2. **How Lynkk builds your knowledge graph automatically** [knowledge graph software]
   Three steps: capture (online + in person, multilingual) → extract (people, companies,
   topics, decisions, tasks) → connect (link across every meeting, update after each call).
   - **Every decision, logged automatically** [decision log, decision log project management]
   - **Action items tracked across every meeting** [action item tracking, action item tracker, rolling action item list]
   - **Walk into every meeting prepared** [meeting preparation]
   (Three H2s added after competitor placement research; see `seo/knowledge-graph-keyword-placement.md`.)
3. **Ask your meetings anything** [ai second brain]
   Natural-language search, answers with links to the exact moment, and live voice answers
   during a meeting.
4. **A second brain for your whole team** [ai second brain, personal knowledge graph]
   Personal and team views. Context before every call: what was said, what's open, who said it.
5. **Stop losing institutional knowledge** [knowledge management ai]
   Decisions don't vanish when people leave or skip a meeting. Link to the institutional
   knowledge post.
6. **Knowledge graph vs. a folder of transcripts** [knowledge base vs knowledge graph]
   Comparison table: transcripts / notes app / Lynkk knowledge graph, covering search,
   cross-meeting links, follow-up tracking and live answers.
7. **Connect your knowledge graph to any AI tool via MCP** [knowledge graph mcp] *(only if true)*
8. **Built for sales, product and founders**
   One short use case each: CRM fields, Jira tickets, back-to-back meetings.
9. **Private by design: choose your data region**
10. **FAQ** (add FAQPage schema)
    - What is an AI knowledge graph?
    - How is a knowledge graph different from meeting transcripts or a knowledge base?
    - Does Lynkk build the knowledge graph automatically?
    - Can I search across all my past meetings?
    - Does it work for in-person meetings and other languages?
    - Where is my data stored and processed?
    - Is the knowledge graph free?
    - Is this a knowledge graph or a context graph?
    - Can Lynkk track action items and decisions across meetings?
