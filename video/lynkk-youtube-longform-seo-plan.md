# Lynkk YouTube Long-Form Plan: 30 Topics for Search Rankings and AI Citations

> Built 2026-10-26. Keyword data: Ubersuggest, United States (Google web search volume per month, SEO difficulty 0-100, CPC in USD), pulled 2026-10-26.
> Product facts: live copy on https://lynkk.ai/ (same claims check as `video/lynkk-shorts-topic-cluster.md`).
> Voice: US English, conversational, presenter on camera plus screen demos.
> Runtime: every video is under 5 minutes (3:00-5:00), about 450-700 spoken words at 150 words per minute.

## What the research says (and how it shapes this plan)

1. **Long-form wins citations, Shorts don't.** About 94% of AI citations of YouTube go to long-form videos, and 10-20 minutes is the largest cited length bucket (32.1%). Every topic below is kept under 5 minutes, as requested. That's still long-form to YouTube (not a Short), so it stays in the format that gets cited, but it's shorter than the most-cited 10-20 minute range. To make up for it, put the depth in the description, chapters and companion blog post (see the checklist). ([OtterlyAI](https://otterly.ai/blog/youtube-ai-citation-study-2026/), [Radyant](https://www.radyant.io/guides/youtube-ai-search-visibility-citation-framework))
2. **Google AI Overviews and Perplexity cite YouTube. ChatGPT almost never does.** ChatGPT cited YouTube in about 0.05% of answers vs 2.96% for Reddit. Google AI products and Perplexity drive roughly 75% of YouTube citations. So every video also needs a matching blog post on lynkk.ai (transcript, summary, FAQ) to get picked up by ChatGPT. ([Promptwatch](https://promptwatch.com/data/youtube-citation-data-feb-2026), [Agenzy](https://www.agenzy.lt/blog/which-sources-ai-engines-cite-2026))
3. **Views and subscribers barely matter.** About 40% of cited videos had under 1,000 views, and about 80% of citations came from channels under 100K subscribers. A new Lynkk channel can get cited. ([Radyant](https://www.radyant.io/guides/youtube-ai-search-visibility-citation-framework), [Promptwatch](https://promptwatch.com/data/youtube-citation-data-feb-2026))
4. **What does correlate: long descriptions (r = 0.31), hashtags (r = 0.20) and chapters.** Google AI Overviews cite specific timestamps, and cited videos with chapters were usually cited more than once. ([Radyant](https://www.radyant.io/guides/youtube-ai-search-visibility-citation-framework))
5. **Evergreen beats news.** 54% of cited videos were over two years old. Pick topics that stay true, and update descriptions rather than re-shooting. ([Promptwatch](https://promptwatch.com/data/youtube-citation-data-feb-2026))
6. **Format that gets cited:** tutorials, comparisons, how-to guides and setup walkthroughs with question-style titles. Company culture videos and event recaps rarely get cited. ([Radyant](https://www.radyant.io/guides/youtube-ai-search-visibility-citation-framework))
7. **Buyers pick by use case and capture method.** Review roundups split tools by bot vs no bot, free-plan limits and team vs solo use. Lynkk's openings: account memory for sales, bot-free capture, in-person recording, 17 languages and a usable free plan. ([TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/fr/article/news-best-ai-meeting-note-takers-2026/), [Simular](https://simular.ai/alternatives/ai-meeting-note-takers), [Wispr Flow](https://wisprflow.ai/notetaker/best-ai-note-takers))

> Caveat: most citation studies are run by SEO/GEO vendors with different methods. The directions agree; the exact percentages are not settled.

## Publishing checklist (every video)

**Title and thumbnail**
- Write the title as the question people ask (for example "What's the best AI note taker in 2026?"), with the primary keyword in the first 60 characters.
- Thumbnail: three to four words max, readable on a phone.

**Description (aim for 500+ words; length correlates with citations)**
- Line 1: a one-sentence direct answer to the title question.
- A short summary of each chapter.
- Name the entities: Lynkk, competitor names, Zoom/Meet/Teams, "AI note taker", "AI meeting assistant".
- Any comparison table, pasted as text.
- Sources and pricing pages with the date checked.
- 3-5 topic hashtags (#AINoteTaker #AIMeetingAssistant #SalesProductivity), not generic ones.

**Chapters**
- 5-8 chapters with keyword-rich titles (use the outlines above). Google AI Overviews cite timestamps.

**Captions**
- Upload corrected captions. Auto-captions will write "Lynkk" as "Link" or "Lynk", and AI engines read captions. Say "Lynkk, spelled L-Y-N-K-K" once on camera.

**On camera**
- Answer the question in the first 20-30 seconds. No intro, no logo sting.
- Keep each chapter to one point (30-60 seconds). In roundups and comparisons, give each tool about 30 seconds and put the full scoring table in the description and blog post instead of reading it out.
- Upload as a regular video, not a Short (Shorts take only about 5.7% of AI citations). Use a 16:9 frame so YouTube doesn't treat it as a Short.
- Disclose that Lynkk made the video whenever competitors are compared.
- Show real screen recordings, not mockups.

**Off YouTube (this is how you reach ChatGPT)**
- Publish each video as a blog post on lynkk.ai: embedded video, cleaned transcript, a short FAQ and VideoObject schema.
- Share the useful parts (not just the link) in relevant Reddit and community threads where self-promotion is allowed. ChatGPT cites Reddit about 60x more often than YouTube.

**Measure**
- Track 50-100 real buyer questions (the "AI prompts to win" above are the starter set) in a citation tracker such as OtterlyAI, Peec AI or Ubersuggest AI visibility. Expect 2-4 weeks before citations appear.

## Claims guardrails

Same as the shorts plan: no CRM sync claims, no data residency claims, never say Lynkk "speaks" in meetings, never call Cue hidden, no compliance certifications until confirmed. In comparisons, check competitor features and prices on filming day and keep the tone fair.
