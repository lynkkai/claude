# Best Otter.ai Alternatives in the US for 2026

*By Samarth Wagh, Co-founder, Lynkk.ai · Last updated 17 October 2026 · Pricing checked 17 October 2026*

*Disclosure: Lynkk publishes this guide and is one of the tools reviewed. We checked every price on the vendor's own pricing page.*

Most people searching for otter ai alternatives hit the same wall. Otter's free Basic plan caps you at 300 transcription minutes a month and 30 minutes per conversation, so a single hour-long client call gets cut in half. Paid Pro costs $16.99 a month on monthly billing. That pushes a lot of US teams to compare options.

This guide compares eight tools on the things that actually change your week: free limits, real prices, who the tool suits, and what happens after the meeting ends.

## Why are US teams looking for Otter.ai alternatives in 2026?

Three limits drive most switches: a 30-minute cap per conversation on the free plan, only the 25 most recent conversations kept on Basic, and 20 AI chat queries a month. Teams that meet all day outgrow that in a week.

The other reasons we hear from teams:

- **Price at team scale.** Otter Business is $30 per user per month on monthly billing, or $19.99 billed yearly. A 10-person team pays $2,400 a year on the annual plan.
- **Notes stop at the transcript.** Otter writes a summary, but turning decisions into Jira tickets or follow-ups still takes manual work.
- **In-person meetings.** Many teams now want one tool for Zoom calls and for the conversation in the conference room.
- **Bot fatigue.** Some clients now ask to remove meeting bots, which pushes people toward bot-free capture.

## Is Otter AI really free?

Yes, Otter has a free Basic plan, but it allows 300 minutes a month, 30 minutes per conversation, 3 lifetime file imports and only your 25 most recent conversations. For occasional short calls it works. For daily client meetings it runs out fast.

## Is there a secure or local alternative to Otter AI?

Yes. Several Otter AI alternatives are built for privacy. Look for 3 things: bot-free capture, a choice of data region, and a clear no-training policy.

- **Bot-free capture:** Granola, Jamie and Lynkk's Mac app and Chrome extension record without a visible bot.
- **Data region:** Lynkk lets a workspace pin processing to the US, EU, UK, India, Singapore or Australia. Jamie hosts in the EU.
- **Local and open source:** local-first tools such as Meetily keep audio on your machine, at the cost of setup time and fewer integrations.

Lynkk encrypts data with AES-256 in transit and at rest. Always check whether a vendor trains models on your data before you sign.

## How do you switch from Otter without losing your notes?

Export first, then cancel. Otter Basic exports TXT, and Business adds PDF, DOCX and SRT. Follow these 4 steps:

1. **Export** your most important conversations from Otter before your plan changes.
2. **Connect your calendar** in the new tool so it joins upcoming meetings automatically.
3. **Run both tools for 1 week** on a few meetings and compare the notes side by side.
4. **Remove Otter's calendar access** so its bot stops joining calls.

## FAQ

### What is the best free alternative to Otter AI?

Fathom has the most generous free recording, with unlimited recordings and transcripts. Lynkk's free plan is smaller at 5 notes a month, but each note includes tasks with owners and Jira send, which Otter Basic does not offer.

### Is Otter AI worth it in 2026?

Otter is worth it if you need fast English transcription and live captions. It is less worth it if you need notes turned into tasks, in-person capture in the same tool, or meetings longer than 30 minutes on a free plan.

### Why is Otter AI so expensive for teams?

Otter Business costs $30 per user per month on monthly billing, or $19.99 billed yearly. For a 10-person team that is up to $3,600 a year, which is why teams compare Fireflies, Fathom and Lynkk.

### Which Otter alternative works without a bot?

Granola and Jamie are built around bot-free capture. Lynkk offers both: a bot for Zoom, Meet and Teams, plus a Mac app and Chrome extension that record without one.

### Is there an Otter alternative that supports Hindi?

Yes. Lynkk supports Hindi and has a Hinglish setting for calls that switch between Hindi and English mid-sentence. It covers 17 languages plus auto-detect in total.

### Can I import my Otter transcripts into another tool?

Most tools accept audio uploads rather than Otter's own files. Export your transcripts as TXT or DOCX from Otter and keep them in your docs. Then let the new tool capture meetings going forward.
