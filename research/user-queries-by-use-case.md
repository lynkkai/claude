## Cross-cutting patterns (the main insights)

1. **Bot fatigue is real.** "without bot", "no bot", "doesn't join meetings", "how to block ai notetakers",
   "remove ai note taker from teams" appear across every meeting use case. If Lynkk's Mac app records
   without a visible bot, lead with it. If Lynkk joins as a participant, sell the upside: it can *speak*
   and answer questions, which a silent bot cannot.
2. **Privacy, legal and region concerns show up in every use case:** consent, GDPR, Europe, "EU alternative",
   local, offline, HIPAA. Lynkk's data residency + offline-first is a direct answer. Build one trust
   page and link to it from every landing page.
3. **The platform's built-in tool is the real competitor.** Many queries are "does Teams / Zoom / Meet
   already have this?" and "Copilot license / Teams Premium". Pages explaining *built-in vs Lynkk* catch
   people right as they hit the native tool's limits.
4. **Price sensitivity:** "free", "no subscription", "lifetime", "unlimited minutes", "cheaper" appear
   everywhere. Make the free plan limits explicit on the site.
5. **"Can X do Y?" questions are comparison gold.** "Can Granola / Fathom / Otter record in-person meetings"
   and "Can ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini transcribe meetings" can each be answered by one page that ranks for many queries.
6. **Avoid these intents:** "undetectable / invisible ai meeting assistant", "interview copilot" (Cluely,
   LockedIn) and "record a meeting without people knowing". These are cheating and covert-recording
   searches, which conflict with Lynkk's consent and compliance positioning. Use them as ad negatives.
