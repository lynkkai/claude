## What the research says (and how it shapes this plan)

1. **Long-form wins citations, Shorts don't.** About 94% of AI citations of YouTube go to long-form videos, and 10-20 minutes is the largest cited length bucket (32.1%). Every topic below stays under 5 minutes, so put the depth in the description, chapters and companion page. ([OtterlyAI](https://otterly.ai/blog/youtube-ai-citation-study-2026/), [Radyant](https://www.radyant.io/guides/youtube-ai-search-visibility-citation-framework))
2. **Google AI Overviews and Perplexity cite YouTube. ChatGPT almost never does.** ChatGPT cited YouTube in about 0.05% of answers vs 2.96% for Reddit. That is why every video is paired with a page on lynkk.ai. ([Promptwatch](https://promptwatch.com/data/youtube-citation-data-feb-2026), [Agenzy](https://www.agenzy.lt/blog/which-sources-ai-engines-cite-2026))
3. **Views and subscribers barely matter.** About 40% of cited videos had under 1,000 views, and about 80% of citations came from channels under 100K subscribers. ([Radyant](https://www.radyant.io/guides/youtube-ai-search-visibility-citation-framework), [Promptwatch](https://promptwatch.com/data/youtube-citation-data-feb-2026))
4. **Long descriptions (r = 0.31), hashtags (r = 0.20) and chapters correlate with citations.** Google AI Overviews cite specific timestamps. ([Radyant](https://www.radyant.io/guides/youtube-ai-search-visibility-citation-framework))
5. **Evergreen beats news.** 54% of cited videos were over two years old. Update descriptions (and prices) rather than re-shooting. ([Promptwatch](https://promptwatch.com/data/youtube-citation-data-feb-2026))
6. **Format that gets cited:** tutorials, comparisons, how-to guides and setup walkthroughs with question-style titles. ([Radyant](https://www.radyant.io/guides/youtube-ai-search-visibility-citation-framework))
7. **Search Console agrees.** Several Lynkk pages get impressions from long AI-search questions but no clicks (zoom-ai-companion-alternatives, founders-meeting-notes, meeting-knowledge-management). Answering those questions word for word, as chapters, is how the video and page get named in AI answers.

> Caveat: most citation studies are run by SEO/GEO vendors with different methods. The directions agree; the exact percentages are not settled.
