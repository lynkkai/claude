## What is Fathom AI, and why look for an alternative?

Fathom AI is a meeting note taker that joins 3 platforms, Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, to record, transcribe and summarize calls. The Fathom note taker is best known for its free plan with unlimited recordings and for sales features such as CRM sync on paid plans.

People look for Fathom alternatives for 3 common reasons, based on the questions they search most:

1. **The notetaker joins every call.** "Fathom AI without bot" and "does Fathom AI join meetings" are frequent searches.
2. **Free summaries have a limit.** Advanced summaries cover your first 5 calls a month.
3. **Team costs.** New Team and Business plans need a paid seat for every member.

## How much does Fathom AI cost in 2026?

Fathom AI is free for unlimited recordings, transcripts and AI summaries, and paid plans start at $15 per user per month. New team plans no longer include free seats.

| Plan | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Unlimited recordings; advanced summaries for 5 calls a month |
| Paid plans | From $15 per user per month | More AI features, CRM sync on higher tiers |
| Team and Business | Paid seat for every member | No free seats on new plans |

*Source: Fathom pricing page, checked September 30, 2026.*

For a 10-person team, that means at least $150 a month on paid Fathom AI plans.

## 1. Lynkk: best Fathom AI alternative overall

Lynkk is the best Fathom AI alternative because it keeps a free meeting bot like Fathom's and adds 3 things Fathom does not focus on: bot-free recording on the Mac, a brief before each meeting and owned tasks with Jira send.

### How does Lynkk record meetings?

Lynkk records in 4 ways, and 2 of them use no bot. The [Lynkk Mac app](https://lynkk.ai/download) notices calls in Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Slack huddles, WhatsApp, FaceTime and Webex and records both sides on 2 channels, so your side is labeled "You".

The [Chrome extension](https://lynkk.ai/features/chrome-extension) records Meet, Zoom (web) and Teams in a browser tab with no extra participant. When a bot is fine, the [free Lynkk meeting bot](https://lynkk.ai/features/meeting-bot) joins from your calendar, audio only. For in-person meetings, you record your microphone in the browser.

### What does every Lynkk note include?

Every recording becomes a Meeting Brief with 7 parts: title, abstract, key takeaways with timestamps, topics, action items, decisions with context and open questions. Each [action item](https://lynkk.ai/features/action-items) has an owner, a due date and a priority, and you can send it to Jira from the note, 1 issue per item.

With Google Calendar connected, a morning briefing email shows who you are meeting, your latest note with them and how many action items are still open. On Pro, [Ask Lynkk](https://lynkk.ai/features/knowledge-graph) answers from your own notes with citations.

### What could be better?

Lynkk has 3 limits compared with Fathom. It does not sync with a CRM, it has no sales coaching or call scoring, and each recording has a 2 hour cap.

### How much does Lynkk cost?

Lynkk starts free, with recording, transcription in 17 languages, the meeting bot (audio only), the Meeting Brief, tasks and Jira send. See [Lynkk pricing](https://lynkk.ai/pricing) for what Pro adds.

## 2. Fireflies: best for a searchable call library

Fireflies includes unlimited transcription and AI summaries on all 4 plans, and Pro costs $10 per seat per month billed annually, $5 less than Fathom's paid entry price. Its bot, Fred, joins Zoom, Google Meet and Teams.

**Best for:** sales teams that search past calls and push notes into a CRM.

**What we liked:** AskFred answers questions across meetings, and the integration list is long.

**What could be better:** free storage is limited to 400 minutes per team, and recordings are capped at 2 hours on Free and Pro.

**Where Lynkk goes further:** bot-free recording on the Mac and a morning brief before each meeting.

*Source: Fireflies pricing page, checked September 30, 2026.*

## 3. Otter: best for live transcripts and phone apps

Otter's free plan includes 300 transcription minutes a month, and Pro costs $8.33 per user per month billed annually, the lowest paid price in this list. Its bot shows a live transcript during calls.

**Best for:** people who read along during calls and record on a phone.

**What we liked:** the live transcript and iPhone and Android apps.

**What could be better:** the free minutes run out quickly, and online calls rely mainly on its bot.

**Where Lynkk goes further:** bot-free call recording on the Mac, owners and due dates, and Jira send.

*Source: Otter pricing page, checked September 30, 2026.*

## 4. Read AI: best for meeting reports

Read AI's free plan covers 5 meetings a month, and Pro starts at $15 per user per month billed annually, the same entry price as Fathom. It adds reports on talk time and engagement.

**Best for:** managers who want data on meeting habits alongside notes.

**What we liked:** paid plans store an unlimited number of meetings.

**What could be better:** 5 free meetings a month is a short trial.

**Where Lynkk goes further:** owned action items and Jira send from each note.

*Source: Read AI plans and pricing page, checked September 30, 2026.*

## 5. Granola: best bot-free notepad

Granola listens to your microphone and computer audio with 0 bots in the call, then fills in the rough notes you type. It is free with 30 days of history, and Business costs $14 per user per month.

**Best for:** people who want no notetaker in the call and like typing their own notes.

**What we liked:** no bot ever joins, 29 templates, and apps for Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android.

**What could be better:** the notes depend on what you type, and free users only see the last 30 days.

**Where Lynkk goes further:** Lynkk writes the whole note for you, and offers a free bot for calls you miss.

*Source: Granola pricing page and help center, checked October 1, 2026.*

## 6. Jamie: best bot-free option with EU hosting

Jamie records your computer's audio with no bot on macOS and Windows, and in person on iOS and Android. The free plan includes 10 meetings a month at up to 30 minutes each, and Plus costs €25 a month.

**Best for:** European teams that want EU hosting and no notetaker in calls.

**What we liked:** no bot and apps on every major platform.

**What could be better:** the 30 minute free limit is short, and prices are in euros.

**Where Lynkk goes further:** Jira send, a morning brief and a free meeting bot when you want one.

*Source: Jamie website and pricing page, checked October 1, 2026.*

## 7. Zoom AI Companion: best for paid Zoom customers

Zoom AI Companion costs $0 extra with paid Zoom Workplace plans and writes meeting summaries, live notes and next steps. Licensed users can also invite it to Teams and Google Meet meetings.

**Best for:** companies that already pay for Zoom and want summaries without another tool.

**What we liked:** no extra cost, and admins can turn features on or off centrally.

**What could be better:** it needs a paid Zoom plan for every user.

**Where Lynkk goes further:** Lynkk starts free with no Zoom plan needed, and sends action items to Jira.

*Source: Zoom help center, checked September 30, 2026.*

## Is Fathom AI safe to use on client calls?

Fathom AI and every tool in this list are only safe if you check 2 things: how the vendor handles your data, and whether everyone on the call agreed to be recorded. The [NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework/privacy-framework) is a practical checklist for the first.

For consent, federal law ([18 U.S.C. § 2511](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title18-section2511)) allows 1 party consent, while California ([Penal Code § 632](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=632)) requires all parties to agree. Lynkk keeps recordings private to your account and never trains on your conversations.
