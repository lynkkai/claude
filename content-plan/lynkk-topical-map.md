# Lynkk Content Plan v3: 35 Buying-Intent Blogs + 20 Medium/Substack Posts

Updated 2026-10-17. v3 keeps the v2 cannibalization and SERP audit and re-orders the plan around **buying intent**: posts whose readers are choosing, paying for or switching tools come first. Every post now has an SEO title tag, an H1, the reason a reader would sign up, and its main CTA. How to write them so readers click through to sign-up: `blog-conversion-playbook.md`.

## 1. Funnel mix

| Stage | What the reader is doing | Posts | How it converts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BOFU** (buying intent) | Comparing, pricing or picking a tool | 18 | Direct: comparison table with Lynkk first, CTA to /sign-up |
| **MOFU** (problem now) | Stuck on a task a tool solves (cannot record, removing a bot, using ChatGPT) | 7 | Solve the task, then offer Lynkk as the easier way |
| **TOFU** (learning) | Wants a template or a how-to | 10 | Give the template, then 'or let Lynkk do it for you' |

TOFU posts stay because they build the topical authority that helps the BOFU posts rank, and they feed readers into BOFU posts through internal links.

## 2. Volume, difficulty and time: how to read them

- **Volume** is for the **exact primary keyword** in its own column, not for the title. Cluster volume adds the separate volumes of the other keywords the post covers. Ubersuggest volumes are rounded estimates (read 480 as roughly 400-600).
- **SD** is Ubersuggest's 0-100 score. **Real difficulty** comes from the sites actually in Google's top 10 today, which is more reliable for a new domain.
- **Time to page 1** assumes about 3 posts a week, the internal links listed, and a few backlinks. It is an estimate, not a promise.
