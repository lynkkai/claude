## Long description

**Primary (about 1,100 characters)**
```
Lynkk is one AI across the entire meeting lifecycle, not just the part after the call.

Before a meeting, it surfaces context from past conversations: what was discussed, what is still open, and who said what, so you start ahead instead of cold.

During the meeting, it joins and speaks when you need it. Ask for context from an earlier call, pull up a document, or get an answer out loud without breaking the flow of the conversation.

Within about 30 seconds after, you get structured notes, action items assigned to the right people, updated CRM fields, created Jira tickets, and a knowledge graph that makes every meeting you have ever had searchable.

Lynkk records online meetings and in-person conversations, works in multiple languages, and is offline-first, so it keeps up on a plane or in a room with no dial-in. You choose the region your data is processed in, and every model behind Lynkk answers from that region. It connects to your calendar, Jira, Calendly, Cal.com and CRM, or to anything else via MCP. Web app and Mac desktop app, including dictation that types at your cursor in any app.

Start free.
```

## Notes for whoever pastes this

- Keep the "before, during, after" structure. That is the differentiator against Otter, Fireflies and Granola, which are all after-the-call tools.
- The region choice is worth keeping in any listing aimed at regulated industries or the EU.
- No dashes in Lynkk copy. Use commas, colons or parentheses.
- Unconfirmed items (paid tier prices, supported meeting platforms, which CRMs, number of
  languages, available regions) are deliberately left out. See the "To confirm" list in
  `memory/lynkk-brand.md` before adding specifics.
