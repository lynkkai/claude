## Product description

Primary (855 characters):
```
Lynkk is an AI meeting assistant that works across the entire meeting lifecycle: before, during and after every call.

Before a meeting, Lynkk surfaces context from past conversations: what was discussed, what's still open, and who said what. During the meeting, it joins and speaks when you need it, so you can ask for past context, pull up documents and get answers live by voice. Within about 30 seconds after, Lynkk delivers structured notes, assigns action items, updates CRM fields and creates Jira tickets, with every meeting searchable in a knowledge graph.

Lynkk records online meetings and in-person conversations, is multilingual and offline-first, and lets you choose the region where your data is processed. It connects to your calendar, Jira, Calendly, Cal.com and your CRM, plus anything else via MCP. Available on web and Mac. Start free.
```

## Answers for G2's "comparison" and Q&A sections

**Who is Lynkk for?**
Sales and customer-facing teams, product and engineering teams, founders,
managers and consultants with back-to-back meetings, teams in regulated
industries that need data residency, and multilingual or global teams.

**What problem does Lynkk solve?**
Meetings create work that nobody has time to do: writing notes, chasing action
items, updating the CRM and filing tickets. And people walk into the next
meeting without remembering the last one. Lynkk preps you before the call,
helps during it and does the follow-up after.

**How is Lynkk different from other AI note takers?**
Most AI note takers only work after the call. Lynkk also brings context before
the meeting and acts as a live voice assistant during it. It records in-person
conversations too, works offline, and lets you choose where your data is
processed.
