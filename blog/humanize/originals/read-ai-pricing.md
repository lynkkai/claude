# Read AI Pricing in 2026: What US Teams Pay for Free, Pro, Enterprise and Enterprise+

*By [Samarth Wagh](https://lynkk.ai/blog/authors/samarth-wagh), Co-founder, Lynkk.ai · Last updated 17 October 2026 · Pricing checked 17 October 2026*

> **Quick answer:** Read AI pricing in 2026 starts at **$0** for 5 meetings a month. **Pro** costs **$15 per user per month billed yearly** or $19.75 monthly. **Enterprise** costs $22.50 yearly or $29.75 monthly. **Enterprise+** costs $29.75 yearly or $39.75 monthly, needs 5+ licenses and adds HIPAA, SAML and SCIM.

*Disclosure: Lynkk publishes this guide and makes a competing AI meeting assistant. Prices come from [Read AI's plans and pricing page](https://www.read.ai/plans-pricing), checked 17 October 2026.*

Read AI is an AI meeting assistant that joins Zoom, Google Meet and Teams calls to write transcripts, summaries and coaching reports. Read AI pricing is easy to read and easy to underestimate. Free looks like a full product, then stops at 5 meetings. Annual billing knocks off 25%, which matters a lot at team scale. And the Enterprise+ tier has a 5-license minimum.

This guide walks through the Read AI pricing plans one by one: what each costs, what it includes, and what a real team will pay in a year.

## How much does Read AI cost per month?

Read AI costs $0 on Free, $19.75 per user per month on Pro with monthly billing, and $15 per user per month on Pro with annual billing. Above that, Enterprise and Enterprise+ cost more and add security and admin features.

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing (per month) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | 5 meetings a month |
| Pro | $19.75 | $15 | Unlimited transcripts |
| Enterprise | $29.75 | $22.50 | Audio and video playback |
| Enterprise+ | $39.75 | $29.75 | HIPAA, SAML, SCIM; requires 5+ licenses |

Prices are in USD, per user per month. Pay yearly and every paid plan drops 25%, which is the single biggest lever in Read AI pricing.

## Is Read AI free?

Yes, Read AI has a free plan, but it covers only 5 meeting transcripts, 5 meeting reports and 5 live-note sessions a month, each up to 1 hour. Uploading files? Not on Free.

What the free plan does include:

- Summaries, topics and a personal meeting coach
- Unlimited enterprise search and basic integrations
- Apps for Windows, macOS, Android and iOS
- Support for 20+ languages

Five meetings a month suits someone testing Read AI on their most important calls. Daily meetings? You will hit the limit in the first week, so compare it with other [free AI note takers with higher caps](https://lynkk.ai/blog/best-free-ai-note-takers). One quirk: the plan card says 20+ languages, while the comparison table lists 16+.

## What does Read AI Pro include?

At $15 billed yearly or $19.75 monthly, Read AI Pro removes the meeting cap and adds 4 things: unlimited transcripts, priority report processing, 100 file upload credits a month, and meetings up to 4 hours long.

Pro also adds premium integrations:

- Notion and Confluence
- Salesforce and HubSpot
- Jira
- Zapier and webhooks

For most individuals and small teams, Pro is the point in Read AI pricing where the tool becomes usable every day.

## What is the difference between Read AI Enterprise and Enterprise+?

Enterprise ($22.50 yearly, $29.75 monthly) adds audio and video playback, video highlights, 200 file upload credits a month and premium support. There is no license minimum on this tier.

On top of that, Enterprise+ ($29.75 yearly, $39.75 monthly, minimum 5 licenses) adds compliance and identity features:

- HIPAA compliance
- SAML single sign-on and SCIM provisioning
- Domain capture
- Custom data retention policy
- 300 file upload credits a month
- Workspace onboarding

Meeting length differs too. The comparison table caps Enterprise at 4 hours, the same as Pro, and raises Enterprise+ to 8 hours. Long board meetings or workshops? Only Enterprise+ covers a full day.

## What does Read AI cost for a team?

A 10-person team pays $1,800 a year on Pro with annual billing, or $2,370 on monthly billing. For the same team, Enterprise costs $2,700 a year billed yearly. Here is how Read AI pricing scales with headcount.

| Team size | Pro yearly | Enterprise yearly | Enterprise+ yearly |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 user | $180 | $270 | Not available (5+ licenses) |
| 5 users | $900 | $1,350 | $1,785 |
| 10 users | $1,800 | $2,700 | $3,570 |
| 25 users | $4,500 | $6,750 | $8,925 |

Small team that needs HIPAA? Enterprise+ starts at 5 licenses, so a team of 1 to 4 still pays for 5 seats, or $1,785 a year billed yearly.

## Which Read AI plan should you choose?

Most people should choose between 2 options: Free if you record 5 or fewer meetings a month, and Pro at $15 billed yearly if you record more. The Enterprise tiers? Only when a buyer, IT team or compliance rule requires them.

| If you are... | Pick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Trying Read AI on key calls | Free | 5 meetings a month, 1 hour each |
| A manager or seller with daily calls | Pro | Unlimited transcripts, 4-hour meetings |
| A team that wants playback and highlights | Enterprise | Audio and video playback |
| In healthcare or a regulated field | Enterprise+ | HIPAA, SAML, SCIM, custom retention |

On the fence between monthly and annual Pro? Start monthly for 1 or 2 months, then switch to annual once you know the team uses it and let the 25% saving kick in.

## Are there hidden costs in Read AI pricing?

There are no hidden fees on the pricing page. Still, 3 details raise the real cost for many teams: the 5-license minimum on Enterprise+, the 25% discount you give up on monthly billing, and upload credits that cap imported recordings.

1. **License minimums.** A team of 3 that wants Enterprise+ features still pays for 5 seats, or $1,785 a year billed yearly.
2. **Monthly premium.** Pro on monthly billing costs $237 a year per user, versus $180 billed yearly.
3. **Upload credits.** Pro includes 100 file upload credits a month, Enterprise 200 and Enterprise+ 300. Teams that import many recorded interviews or phone calls should check these limits.

Upload credits are counted in minutes: the comparison table lists 100, 200 and 300 minutes a month. Count the time spent after each meeting, too. When people still write follow-up emails and [turn meeting minutes into Jira tickets](https://lynkk.ai/blog/meeting-minutes-to-jira) by hand, the tool's price is only part of the cost.

## What does Read AI cost in real-world scenarios?

Real-world Read AI pricing depends on team size, billing cycle and whether you need playback or compliance. These 3 scenarios use list prices with annual billing.

|---|---|---|---|
| Small team that just needs notes | Pro | 8 | $1,440 |
| Sales team that reviews call video | Enterprise | 25 | $6,750 |
| Healthcare organization needing HIPAA | Enterprise+ | 100 | $35,700 |

**Small team on Pro.** Eight people on Pro billed yearly pay $1,440. If only 4 of them record meetings regularly, ask whether everyone needs a seat.

**Sales team on Enterprise.** Twenty-five reps on Enterprise pay $6,750 a year, and get audio and video playback for reviewing calls, the core of [sales conversation intelligence](https://lynkk.ai/blog/sales-conversation-intelligence). Coaching-heavy teams often weigh this against Fathom Business at $25 per user billed yearly (see [a side-by-side with Fathom](https://lynkk.ai/compare/fathom)).

**Regulated organization on Enterprise+.** One hundred users on Enterprise+ pay $35,700 a year at list price for HIPAA compliance, SAML, SCIM and custom retention. Read AI's FAQ offers 10% off at 100+ annual licenses, which brings that to $32,130.

## Why does Read AI join meetings you did not expect?

Calendar access is how Read AI joins meetings. When any invited Read user has auto-join on, the notetaker can join that meeting, including meetings other people organized, and turning off your own "Add Read?" toggle does not stop it. A settings question, not a hidden cost. Still, it affects how many seats teams end up buying.

To keep control, agree on 3 rules before rollout:

1. **Who may connect a calendar** to Read AI.
2. **Which meetings are auto-joined**, for example internal meetings only.
3. **How attendees are told** that a meeting is being recorded and summarized.

Notetaker joining calls you organize without an invite? Read AI's help center says the host can deny its request to be admitted, or remove it like any participant; on Zoom, the request may not appear if the host already connected Read.

## Does Read AI offer education, nonprofit or India pricing?

Schools and universities get a separate [educational pricing page](https://www.read.ai/edu-pricing): EDU costs $5 a month ($60 a year) and EDU + Video $8.33 a month ($100 a year), both billed annually with a .edu email. As of 17 October 2026, the main pricing page lists no nonprofit discount and no India-specific prices; everything is shown in USD.

Nonprofit, or based outside the US? Ask Read AI's sales team before buying, since the public page shows only USD list prices.

## Is Read AI worth the price?

Read AI is worth the price if you value meeting analytics (engagement, talking speed, coaching) and want one tool across Zoom, Google Meet and Teams with apps on 4 platforms. Less so if you mainly need follow-up work done, where other [AI meeting assistants](https://lynkk.ai/blog/ai-powered-meeting-assistants) focus, or only meet a few times a week.

Ask 3 questions before you upgrade:

1. **Do you hold more than 5 meetings a month you want recorded?** If not, Free is enough.
2. **Do you need meetings longer than 1 hour?** Free stops at 1 hour; Pro allows 4.
3. **Do you need HIPAA or SSO?** Only Enterprise+ includes both.

## FAQ

### How much does Read AI cost per month?

Read AI Pro costs $19.75 per user per month on monthly billing, or $15 billed yearly, so one user pays $180 a year instead of $237. Already on Zoom One Pro, Business or Business Plus? Read AI says Zoom Essential Apps include its premium features at no extra cost, so check that before paying.

### Is Read AI free to use?

Yes, with no credit card required, but Free is strictly single-user. Read AI's FAQ says free users cannot be part of a Workspace, so an admin cannot manage free teammates or share their reports centrally. Anyone who wants team controls needs a paid license per person, starting at $15 billed yearly.

### Does Read AI cost money for teams?

Yes, and Read AI does not allow mixed plans. Every license in a Workspace must be on the same plan, so 2 people needing playback means Enterprise for all 10, or $2,700 a year instead of $1,800 on Pro. There are no admin-only licenses either; the account owner needs a seat too.

### What is Read AI Enterprise pricing?

Read AI Enterprise costs $22.50 per user per month billed yearly or $29.75 monthly, with no seat minimum. Large buyers get volume discounts on annual licenses: 10% at 100+, 15% at 500+ and 20% at 1,000+, according to Read AI's pricing FAQ. Enterprise+ adds a 5-license minimum.

### Is Read AI cheaper than Otter?

No. Otter Pro costs $8.33 a month billed yearly, compared with $15 for Read AI Pro. Otter Pro caps you at 1,200 minutes a month and 90 minutes per meeting, though, while Read AI Pro has unlimited transcripts up to 4 hours each. Heavy users may find Read AI cheaper per meeting.

### Does Read AI offer a discount for annual billing?

Yes, 25% on every paid plan, but the commitment is real. Read AI's FAQ says it does not offer refunds: you can cancel any time before renewal and keep access until the period ends. Paying yearly locks in 12 months, so test monthly for 1 or 2 months first.
