It's hard to write and follow a lecture at the same time, and the part you missed is the part you need. Record lectures

### Block 2. How it works

## Record lectures, then read the note

**Record: in the room.** Use the Mac app or the web app. Each recording runs up to 2 hours.

**Read: takeaways and topics.** A title, a short abstract, the key takeaways and the topics, so revision starts from a list.

**Replay: any minute.** Click a timestamp and the recording plays from that moment.

### Block 4. Features

## A note taking app for students that listens for you

**Replay any minute**: every takeaway has a timestamp that plays the recording from that point.

**Weak signal is fine**: the Mac app saves the recording and uploads it when you're back online.

**Ask about a lecture**: ask a question about one lecture, or across all of them with Ask Lynkk. Pro.

**Up to 2 hours**: long class? Start a second recording after the break.

### Block 5. Recording permission

Ask your lecturer before you record, even if the notes are only for you. Many schools have their own rules about
recording classes, so check those too, especially before you share a recording.

### Block 6. FAQ

## Questions about recording lectures

**Can I record a lecture on my laptop?**

Yes, on a Mac with the Mac app, or in any browser with the web app. The browser needs a connection while it records.

**Is Lynkk an AI note taker for lectures?**

Yes. It records the lecture and writes the note for you: takeaways with timestamps, topics and the full transcript.

**Does Lynkk capture the slides?**

No. Lynkk records the audio and writes the note from what was said. It doesn't capture slides or make flashcards.

**Is it free?**

Yes. Recording lectures and getting the note works on the free plan. Asking questions about a lecture is part of Pro.
