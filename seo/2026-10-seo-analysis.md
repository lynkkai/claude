# Lynkk: SEO analysis (October 2026)

Sources: InfraNodus knowledge graphs of Google results and related searches (US, English, 5 seed
queries), Ubersuggest keyword and domain data (US), Google Search Console export for lynkk.ai
(AI features only, last 3 months to 2026-10-23), and a crawl of lynkk.ai pages.

Seed queries: ai meeting assistant, ai note taker, ai meeting notes, meeting transcription
software, conversation intelligence.

## Executive Summary

lynkk.ai is a brand new domain to Google (domain authority 1, 3 referring domains, one ranking
keyword at position 63), but its October blog posts are already being picked up by Google's AI
features: impressions went from about 20 a week to about 790 a week within two weeks of
publishing. The fastest wins are low-difficulty, high-intent keywords that match Lynkk's real
differentiators (in-person meetings, no device, multilingual), plus a homepage whose crawlable
text is currently dominated by demo names instead of the words people search for.

### Who ranks today (informational supply)

The top results are owned by Otter, Granola, tl;dv, Krisp, Read AI, Fathom, Plaud and
Fireflies, plus Reddit threads, app store listings and Zoom, Microsoft and Google help pages.

InfraNodus topical clusters in the current results:

1. **Meeting Assistant** (ai, meeting, note, notetaker, assistant): 65% of the network's
   bridging power. This is the core pillar every page must cover.
2. **Conversation Tech** (conversation intelligence, call, software, customer, sales): the
   sales-tool cluster (Gong, Salesloft, Dialpad, HubSpot).
3. **Real-time Transcription** (transcribe, summarize, record, capture, automatically)
4. **Voice Bot** (app, bot, store, free, voice)
5. **Instant Summary** (transcript, action, summary, follow up)
6. **Team Collaboration** (Zoom, team, Google Meet, Microsoft, joining, person)

Structural gaps in what ranks (topics that are rarely connected in one page):

- Meeting Assistant and Team Collaboration: generic "AI meeting assistant" pages rarely go deep
  on a specific platform or on in-person meetings.
- Meeting Assistant and Instant Summary: few pages connect the assistant to concrete outputs
  (action items, follow-up emails, tickets).
- Conversation Tech and Team Collaboration: sales conversation intelligence content rarely
  covers the everyday Zoom/Meet/Teams workflow.

Lynkk's positioning (one AI before, during and after; online and in person) sits exactly in
these gaps.

### Demand that current results do not satisfy (InfraNodus gap analysis)

InfraNodus found 427 related searches that the top results do not cover well. The useful
groups for Lynkk:

1. **Free, download, trial, extension**: "otter ai free plan limits", "otter ai chrome
   extension", "read ai chrome extension", "free app to record and transcribe meeting minutes",
   "meeting transcription software free". Lynkk has a free plan, a free audio-only meeting bot
   and a Chrome extension page (/features/chrome-extension) that gets almost no impressions.
2. **Wearables and devices**: "wearable ai note taker", "ai note taking device", "best
   wearable ai pendant", "bee ai wearable", "limitless ai pendant", "plaud note price". Lynkk's
   angle: record in-person conversations without buying a device. Plaud alternative has SD 10.
3. **Teams how-to problems**: "how to enable ai notes in teams", "turn off read ai meeting
   notes in teams", "teams ai note taker copilot", "how to generate meeting notes in teams using
   copilot". These extend the Teams post that is already working.
4. **Reddit opinions**: "best free ai meeting note taker reddit", "ai note taker for teams
   reddit". The existing posts already have "What do Reddit users say" sections; keep them.
5. **HubSpot and CRM**: "conversation intelligence hubspot", "hubspot call transcription",
   "hubspot ai call summary". Only worth a page if the HubSpot integration is live (the
   homepage mentions HubSpot once; to confirm).

## Recommendations

Ranked by expected impact for the effort.

### 1. Build an "AI note taker for in-person meetings" landing page (highest priority)

480 searches a month (720 at peak), SD 40, transactional intent, no Lynkk page, and it is the
one thing most competitors (Otter, Fireflies, Fathom, Read AI) do not do well. Suggested
structure:

- URL: `/use-cases/in-person-meetings` (or `/features/in-person-meetings`)
- Title (55 chars): `AI Note Taker for In-Person Meetings, No Device | Lynkk`
  (full draft: `seo/drafts/in-person-meetings.md`)
- Meta description: `Record in-person meetings, interviews and client visits on your
  laptop. Lynkk writes notes and action items in 17 languages. Start free.`
- H1: `The AI note taker for in-person meetings`
- H2s, written as questions people ask:
  - Can an AI note taker record in-person meetings?
  - How do you record an in-person meeting with Lynkk?
  - Do you need a device like Plaud or a wearable pendant?
  - Does it work offline and in other languages?
  - Who uses it (sales visits, interviews, lectures, field research)?
  - FAQ
- Markup: `SoftwareApplication`, `HowTo` for the recording steps, `FAQPage`,
  `BreadcrumbList`. Link to it from the homepage, /compare/plaud, /use-cases/field-interviews
  and /blog/best-ai-note-takers.

### 2. Put the target keyword in the title and H1 of the compare pages

The compare pages already use "alternative" in the H1 but not in the title. Suggested titles:

| /compare/plaud | Lynkk vs Plaud: Features and Pricing Compared \| Lynkk | Plaud Alternative With No Device: Lynkk vs Plaud \| Lynkk |

Each compare page should have a real HTML `<table>` (with `<thead>`) of features and prices,
so Google and AI Overviews can lift it.

### 3. Fix what Google reads on the homepage

- Keep the revenue headline as the visual hook, but make the H1 (or a line directly under it)
  carry the category: for example `Lynkk is the AI meeting assistant and note taker for online
  and in-person meetings.`
- Consider a title that matches demand better. "Conversation intelligence" searches are mostly
  about a book, HubSpot, Twilio and Salesforce. Option (60 chars): `Lynkk: AI Meeting Assistant
  & Note Taker, Online and In Person`.
- Add an eyebrow or subline to the key H2 sections with the plain feature name (Meeting notes,
  Action items, Ask your meetings, Integrations, Pricing).
- Add a short plain-language section (150 to 250 words) covering: AI note taker, meeting
  assistant, Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, in-person, 17 languages, Hinglish, offline,
  action items, Jira, CRM, knowledge graph.
- Wrap the demo UI blocks (Corvane, Priya, Kelso) in `data-nosnippet` so they cannot be quoted
  in snippets or AI Overviews, or render them as images with descriptive alt text. Do not hide
  them with CSS; just make sure the real copy outweighs them.
- Verify the `AggregateRating` in the homepage structured data comes from real, visible
  reviews. If it does not, remove it, because it can trigger a manual action.

### 4. Extend the content that AI features already cite

The Google Meet and Teams posts produce 38% of page-level AI-feature impressions. Add sibling
how-to posts that link back to them:

- How to turn on AI meeting notes in Microsoft Teams (with and without Copilot)
- How to turn off Read AI (or other bots) in Teams and Google Meet
- Otter.ai free plan limits in 2026 (and what to use instead)
- Best wearable AI note takers vs apps (Plaud, Bee, Limitless, Lynkk)

### 5. Make the Chrome extension and free plan findable

"otter ai chrome extension" and "read ai chrome extension" show steady demand. The current title
(`Record Meet, Zoom and Teams Without a Bot`) never says "Chrome extension". Try
`AI Note Taker Chrome Extension, No Bot: Meet, Zoom, Teams | Lynkk` and link it from the
Google Meet posts. Make "free" visible in titles where it is true
(the meeting bot is free, audio only).

### 6. Use the India signal

India is the second biggest country (10% of impressions), and Lynkk has Hindi and Hinglish
transcription pages. Run the same InfraNodus analysis for country IN to find India-specific
queries (Hinglish meeting notes, Hindi transcription for Zoom) before writing more.
