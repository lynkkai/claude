## What you get from each recording

- Speaker-labelled transcript
- **Meeting Brief:** title, abstract, key takeaways with seekable timestamps, topics, action items (owner, due date, priority), decisions with context, open questions
- Auto-tags plus extracted people, companies and projects (links notes together)
- **Summary Shapes** (Pro): e.g. Sales Call, Daily Stand-up, Customer Interview
- Decision log, action tracker, weekly digest (Pro)
- **Cue** (Pro): answering during the call (privacy policy also mentions "voice answers")
- Timing: site says "in 30 seconds" / "under 30 seconds" on how-it-works, and "shortly after the recording finishes uploading" in the FAQ. Safer wording: **"notes within seconds of the recording uploading."**

## Privacy / security / compliance

- Notes private by default; encrypted in transit and at rest with **AES-256**; integration credentials encrypted with per-deployment keys.
- **Never trains models on your conversations.** Export or delete everything anytime.
- Audio retention you control: keep forever, or auto-delete after 30 days, 90 days or a year (FAQ also lists "wipe after upload").
- **Data residency:** model calls pinned per workspace to the **US, EU, UK, India, Singapore or Australia** (set up with the Lynkk team, not a self-serve toggle). Covers transcription, summaries, assistant and search embeddings.
- "Built to comply with" **GDPR, UK GDPR and India's DPDP Act**, with a consent flow for multi-party recording. **No certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001) are claimed on the site.**
- Bot only joins calls you authorize and is always visible in the participant list.
- Sub-processors (privacy policy): Deepgram and AssemblyAI (speech-to-text), Anthropic (summaries, extraction, chat), Cohere (search embeddings).
