# Lynkk.ai: Spotter and Integration Topics

Prepared 2026-11-03. I read `/features/spotter` and `/integrations` first rather than guessing, since
neither is in the brand memory file. US volumes from Ubersuggest, locId 2840.

**What I found, in short:** Spotter opens two large low-difficulty clusters, one of which comes with
a serious reputational caveat. Integrations are a weak SEO play at the keyword level, and I would
rather tell you that than invent topics. Details below.

---

## What Spotter actually is (for the record)

A real-time AI assistant inside the Lynkk Mac app. Press ⌘↩ and it reads the text in your front
window plus the call audio, then suggests what to say. Microphone is "Me", Mac audio is "Them". Seven
modes: General, Meeting, Sales, Support, Lecture, Interview prep, Mock interview. Requires Apple
silicon and macOS 14.2+. Early access, Pro accounts.

**Two details that matter for content strategy:**

1. **It appears in screen shares and recordings by design, and it does not join the call.** That is a
   deliberate product choice and it turns out to be a positioning asset. See the caveat on S1 below.
2. **It does not know your past meetings.** The page points to Ask Lynkk for that. So Spotter content
   must not promise meeting recall, or it will misrepresent the product.

### S1, AI interview assistant, 4,300 at entry SD 16, and read the caveat

The cluster: `ai interview assistant` **1,900 (SD 36)**, `real time ai interview assistant` **1,600
(SD 23)**, `best ai interview assistant` 210 (SD 25), `final round ai interview assistant` 210
(**SD 5**), `interview sidekick ai interview assistant` 70 (**SD 5**), `free ai interview assistant`
70 (SD 32), `live ai interview assistant` 50 (SD 33), `ai interview assistant free` 50 (SD 28),
`ai interview assistant reddit` 50 (SD 30), `ai interview assistant real time` 40 (**SD 16**),
`sensei ai interview assistant` 40 (SD 12), `parakeet ai interview assistant` 30 (SD 20).

**The caveat, which I am not going to bury.** The long tail of this cluster is overwhelmingly about
cheating in job interviews: `undetectable ai interview assistant` (many variants),
`can zoom detect ai interview assistant`, `ai interview assistant invisible on screen share`,
`ai interview assistant cheat`. That is what most of the 4,300 searches want.

Spotter is the opposite of that by design: it shows up in screen shares and does not hide. So Lynkk
can write the honest version of this page, and the visibility-by-design choice is the differentiator
that makes it credible. But be clear-eyed about two things:

- A large share of the traffic will bounce, because they wanted an undetectable tool and Lynkk is not
  one. The effective volume is well below 4,300.
- Do not chase the "undetectable", "invisible" or "can Zoom detect" terms. They would bring the wrong
  users, misrepresent the product, and attach Lynkk's brand to interview fraud.

Written as "here is what these tools do, here is why visible-by-design is the defensible choice, and
here is what Spotter's Interview prep and Mock interview modes are actually for", this is a strong
page. Written as a bid for the cheating traffic, it is a liability. Your call, but I would only
commission the first version.

Fifty searches total. Write it as a short companion to S2, not on its own.

## Integration direction: the honest answer

**Direct "meeting notes to CRM" keywords do not exist.** I tested the obvious phrasings and they
return nothing:

| log calls to salesforce | **0** |

**The conclusion: integrations are a product-page play, not a blog play.** Individual integration
keywords run 20 to 170 searches a month. That does not justify blog posts, and it is exactly why your
existing `/integrations/*` pages draw 1 to 15 impressions each. Those pages are right to exist for
conversion; they will not produce traffic.

**One real opportunity inside this, though:**

`fathom hubspot integration` (SD 21), `fireflies hubspot integration` (SD 22), `gong hubspot
integration` (SD 22), `granola hubspot integration` (SD 28) and the "and" variants total about 210
searches at SD 21 to 28. These are people already evaluating a competitor's CRM sync, which is late
buying intent at genuinely low difficulty. One comparison post covers all four.

**And one gap in your own pages worth fixing.** `/integrations` names HubSpot, Pipedrive and Zoho CRM
as supported, but the sitemap has no page for any of them, while it does have pages for Attio and
Salesforce. Three supported CRMs with no landing page is a conversion gap, not an SEO one, and it is
cheap to close.

**Four topics, about 7,960 US monthly searches.**

### What I would do

1. **S2, Objection Handling.** Highest CPC in the entire programme, SD 16 entry, exactly what Spotter
   does, no ethical caveat. Clear first pick.
2. **I1, AI notetakers and HubSpot.** Small but late-funnel, SD 21, and it also gives you the page to
   link your missing HubSpot integration page from.
3. **S1, AI interview assistants**, only as the honest version. It is the largest cluster here and
   the one I would want a decision on before writing, not after.
4. **S3** as a companion to S2.

### Two things I did not find

- **No integration keyword cluster worth blogging about.** Reported rather than padded.
- **Nothing for the Slack, Google Calendar, WhatsApp or FaceTime integrations.** All return single-digit
  or zero volume. They are product-page material only.

Standing caveat: these are Ubersuggest difficulty estimates, not live SERP checks. S2 is the one
worth verifying, since a $102 CPC keyword at SD 16 is unusual enough to deserve a second look before
you commission it.
