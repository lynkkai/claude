# Jamie AI Note Taker Review 2026: Bot-Free Meeting Notes Tested for US Teams

Jamie is an AI note taker that records meetings on your own device instead of sending a bot into the call. It also puts Jamie in a small group of bot-free [AI note takers](https://lynkk.ai/blog/best-ai-note-takers).

This review covers how Jamie works, what Jamie AI note taker pricing adds up to, how it handles privacy and languages, and which alternatives fit better for specific teams.

## What is the Jamie AI note taker?

Jamie is an AI note taker that captures meeting audio on your computer or phone rather than sending a bot into the call. After the meeting it writes notes, detects tasks and identifies speakers, and per Jamie's pricing page it works with "all online platforms" and in-person meetings.

What Jamie does on every plan:

- **Bot-free capture** from your device
- **Speaker recognition** and task detection
- **Notes and transcripts in 99+ languages**
- **Ask Jamie**, a chat over your meeting notes
- **Unlimited storage**, EU hosting and GDPR compliance
- **No model training** on your data

## How does Jamie work without a bot?

Like a person with a recorder, Jamie records the audio playing on your device and your microphone. That is why it works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex or a phone call, and why nobody sees a participant called "Jamie" join, the same approach a bot-free [Zoom note taker](https://lynkk.ai/blog/zoom-note-taker) takes.

The trade-offs of device capture:

1. **You must start it.** If Jamie is not running, the meeting is not captured.
2. **One device, one recording.** Your colleagues need their own Jamie to capture meetings they host.
3. **Consent is still required.** No bot does not mean no recording. [Federal law](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/2511) allows one party to consent, several states want everyone to agree, and Jamie includes a consent email on every plan.

## Is the Jamie AI note taker free?

Yes. Jamie's free plan gives 10 AI notes a month, each up to 30 minutes, with 1 custom template, 50 custom vocabulary terms, Ask Jamie chat, and exports to Google Docs, Notion and OneNote.

That 30-minute cap is the real limit. A 45-minute client call gets cut off. Short check-ins or a trial suit the free plan; daily users will need Plus or Pro. Other [free AI note takers](https://lynkk.ai/blog/best-free-ai-note-takers) allow longer single meetings.

## What do you get on Jamie Plus, Pro and Team?

Moving from Free to Plus doubles your notes to 20 a month and raises the meeting cap to 2 hours. Pro removes the note cap, allows 5-hour meetings and adds CRM and AI-tool integrations, while Team adds shared workspaces and admin controls.

- **Plus ($24):** 20 notes a month, 2-hour meetings, 3 custom templates.
- **Pro ($46 billed yearly):** unlimited notes, 5-hour meetings, unlimited templates, Salesforce, HubSpot, Asana, Attio, Dynamics 365, Pipedrive, API and webhooks, plus an MCP connection for AI tools.
- **Team ($37 per seat billed yearly):** shared templates, shared workspace and tags, user management, 100 vocabulary terms, centralized billing.
- **Enterprise (custom):** SAML SSO, SCIM, audit logs, IP allowlist, data processing agreements, custom retention, 250 vocabulary terms, volume discounts and a dedicated account manager.

## Is Jamie safe and private?

Privacy is Jamie's strongest feature: bot-free capture, EU hosting, GDPR compliance and a stated policy of no model training on your data, on all 5 plans. ISO 27001 and DORA are listed on its page. SOC 2 Type II? Marked as pending.

US teams have 2 questions worth asking Jamie's sales team:

- **US data residency.** EU hosting suits European clients. Some US companies prefer or require US hosting.
- **SOC 2 status.** Many US security reviews require a completed SOC 2 Type II report, and Jamie's page still shows it as pending. Customized consent notifications are Enterprise-only, which matters if your legal team wants its own wording.

## Does Jamie have an Android app?

Yes. [Jamie's documentation](https://docs.meetjamie.ai/enterprise/users/mobile-app), last updated 3 September 2026, lists the mobile app on both the App Store and Google Play. Jamie AI note taker Android capture covers in-person meetings, shows your meetings and tasks, and answers questions on the go, with everything synced to desktop. Desktop apps handle online meetings.

One mismatch to note: the pricing page footer offers downloads for Mac, Windows and iPhone only, so Android users go through Google Play. If in-person capture is your main use, test the mobile app on a real meeting first. Room noise and distance from the phone affect any tool's accuracy.

## FAQ

### Is the Jamie AI note taker free?

Yes: 10 AI notes a month, 30 minutes each. Two things the free plan keeps that rivals charge for: unlimited storage and exports to Google Docs, Notion and OneNote. It also allows 1 custom template and 50 custom vocabulary terms, so names and jargon can be taught early.

### How much does Jamie AI cost?

Billed annually, Jamie Plus costs $24, Pro $46 and Team $37 per seat, each per month, and Enterprise is quoted. The toggle on its pricing page puts the annual saving at 17%, so monthly billing costs more. Pro is the first plan with unlimited notes and 5-hour meetings.

### Does Jamie join meetings as a bot?

No, it records your device audio, so nothing appears in Zoom, Google Meet or Teams. Jamie covers the disclosure gap with a consent email on every plan, while a customized consent notification is Enterprise-only. You still have to start capture, so missed starts mean missed notes.

### Does Jamie work on Android?

Yes. Jamie's docs list the mobile app on Google Play alongside the App Store, and recordings sync to desktop instantly. Its pricing page footer, though, offers only Mac, Windows and iPhone downloads, so install Android from Play. Mobile records in-person meetings and runs Ask AI.

### Which languages does Jamie support?

Jamie lists notes and transcripts in 99+ languages on every plan, speaker recognition included. Custom vocabulary is where plans diverge: 50 terms on Free, Plus and Pro, 100 on Team and 250 on Enterprise, which matters for names, drug names or product codes in any language.

### What is a good alternative to Jamie?

Granola costs $14 a month for bot-free notes, Fathom records unlimited calls free, and Lynkk pairs bot and bot-free capture with Jira actions for $15 a month. Jamie keeps 2 advantages over all of them: EU-only hosting and 99+ languages. Weigh those before switching.
