### AI for sales calls: record every call, get the next steps written for you.

Lynkk records your sales calls on Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams or in person, then writes the next steps with
owners and dates, the decisions and the open questions. No call recording software to set up, and no bot needed.

#### What does Lynkk do on a sales call?

Lynkk listens to your sales call and writes the notes for you. After the call you get a short list: what you promised, who will do it, and by when. You don't need to type anything during the call.

**Who it's for**
- Sales reps who run many calls a day
- Founders who do their own selling
- Account managers who need to remember every client

#### Your AI sales assistant, before, during and after the call

**Before: start ahead.** Ask Lynkk what's still open with an account and get the answer from past calls.

**During: answers in the call.** Cue answers questions during the call, like what was agreed on pricing last time.

**After: the note is written.** Next steps, decisions and open questions, ready before you're back in your inbox.

*Ask Lynkk and Cue are part of Pro.*

#### Conversation intelligence for every sales call

**Next steps with owners**: every action item gets an owner, a due date and a priority.

**Ask across calls**: "What did Acme say about pricing?" Each answer shows which call it came from.

**Record in person, even offline**: use the Mac app or web app. It uploads when you're back online.

**Send to Jira**: create one Jira issue per action item, from the note.

#### Questions about AI for sales calls

**Can Lynkk record without a bot joining?**

Yes. The Mac app and Chrome extension record from your computer. The bot is optional.

**Does Lynkk sync with my CRM?**

No. You can send action items to Jira and share notes with a link.

**How is Lynkk different from other AI sales tools?**

Lynkk focuses on the conversation: notes, next steps and answers across calls, online or in person. Revenue platforms
add CRM sync and forecasting.
