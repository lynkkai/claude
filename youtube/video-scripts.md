## Production notes

- **Brand and writing:** say and spell "Lynkk" (double k). No em or en dashes in titles, descriptions or captions.
- **Hooks:** first 3 seconds of every Short must show the problem on screen (mic cutting out, greyed-out
  button, crossed-out REC). Text on screen is mandatory, since most Shorts are watched muted.
- **Shorts format:** 9:16, captions burned in, under 60 s, end on the product or a link to the long video.
- **Long videos:** put the keyword in the first line of the title, the first sentence of the description,
  and say it out loud in the first 30 seconds (YouTube indexes captions). Add chapters as above.
- **Playlists:** "Mac voice & dictation" (Videos 2, 3) and "AI meeting notes" (Videos 1, 4). Cross-link with end screens.
- **Pinned comment:** link to the matching blog post + "What should we cover next?" to collect topic ideas.
- **Demo account:** prepare a clean Lynkk account with a sample meeting, and Jira/CRM connected only if you'll show it.
- **Before recording:** clear every `[CHECK]` item. Menus, shortcuts and plan requirements change, and a
  wrong step is the fastest way to collect "this doesn't work" comments.
