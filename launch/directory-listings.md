### Long description (about 1,000 characters)
```
Lynkk is meeting memory for sales and revenue teams. The call ends, but the opportunity shouldn't, so Lynkk remembers every customer conversation and turns it into follow-ups, action items and your next best move.

Record any conversation: a meeting bot for Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, the Mac app (which also picks up WhatsApp and FaceTime calls), the Chrome extension, or in the room. Every call becomes a note with a summary, decisions and action items, in 17 languages plus Hinglish.

Every call with an account sits together, so your fifth call starts five calls ahead. Ask Lynkk "What did we promise them?" or "What's blocking the deal?" and get answers across all your calls, each with its source. Draft a follow-up grounded in what was said, send the recap to everyone on the call, push action items to Jira and get answers in Slack. Cue (beta) gives you live help on your Mac screen during the call.

Free forever for unlimited notes. Pro is $15 a month.
```

### Key features (bullet list)
```
- Records Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams with a meeting bot, with calendar auto-join
- Mac app records any call, including WhatsApp and FaceTime, plus in-person conversations
- Chrome extension and web recorder for Windows and Linux users
- 17 languages plus Hinglish, with speaker detection
- AI notes with key takeaways, decisions and action items
- Summary Shapes: Sales Call, General Meeting, Customer Interview and more
- Ask Lynkk: answers across all your calls, each with its source
- Follow-up drafts grounded in what was actually said; you review and send
- Email the summary to everyone on the call
- Tasks board, action items to Jira, answers in Slack
- Cue (beta): live help on your Mac screen during the call
- Weekly digest every Monday, dictation on Mac
- No AI training on your conversations; export everything as a zip
```
