# Read AI Pricing in 2026: Plans and Costs for US Teams

*By Samarth Wagh, Co-founder, Lynkk.ai · Last updated 17 October 2026 · Pricing checked 17 October 2026*

> **Quick answer:** Read AI pricing in 2026 starts at **$0** for 5 meetings a month. **Pro** costs **$15 per user per month billed yearly** or $19.75 monthly. **Enterprise** costs $22.50 yearly or $29.75 monthly and needs 5+ licenses. **Enterprise+** costs $29.75 yearly or $39.75 monthly and adds HIPAA, SAML and SCIM.

*Disclosure: Lynkk publishes this guide and makes a competing AI meeting assistant. Prices come from [Read AI's plans and pricing page](https://www.read.ai/plans-pricing), checked 17 October 2026.*

Read AI pricing is easy to read and easy to underestimate. The free plan looks like a full product, then stops at 5 meetings. The annual discount is 25%, which matters a lot at team scale. And the Enterprise tier has a 5-license minimum.

This Read AI pricing guide breaks down what each plan costs, what it includes, and what a real team will pay in a year.

## How much does Read AI cost per month?

Read AI costs $0 on Free, $19.75 per user per month on Pro with monthly billing, and $15 per user per month on Pro with annual billing. Enterprise and Enterprise+ cost more and add security and admin features.

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing (per month) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | 5 meetings a month |
| Pro | $19.75 | $15 | Unlimited transcripts |
| Enterprise | $29.75 | $22.50 | Requires 5+ licenses |
| Enterprise+ | $39.75 | $29.75 | HIPAA, SAML, SCIM |

All prices are in USD, per user per month. Annual billing saves 25% on every paid plan.

## Is Read AI free?

Yes, Read AI has a free plan, but it covers only 5 meeting transcripts, 5 meeting reports and 5 live-note sessions a month, each up to 1 hour. File uploads are not included on Free.

What the free plan does include:

- Summaries, topics and a personal meeting coach
- Unlimited enterprise search and basic integrations
- Apps for Windows, macOS, Android and iOS
- Support for 20+ languages

Five meetings a month suits someone who wants to test Read AI on their most important calls. Anyone with daily meetings will hit the limit in the first week.

## What does Read AI Pro include?

Read AI Pro, at $15 billed yearly or $19.75 monthly, removes the meeting cap and adds 4 things: unlimited transcripts, priority report processing, 100 file upload credits a month, and meetings up to 4 hours long.

Pro also unlocks premium integrations:

- Notion and Confluence
- Salesforce and HubSpot
- Jira
- Zapier and webhooks

For most individuals and small teams, Pro is the plan that makes Read AI usable every day.

## What is the difference between Read AI Enterprise and Enterprise+?

Enterprise ($22.50 yearly, $29.75 monthly) adds audio and video playback, video highlights, 200 file upload credits a month and premium support. It requires at least 5 licenses.

Enterprise+ ($29.75 yearly, $39.75 monthly) adds compliance and identity features on top:

- HIPAA compliance
- SAML single sign-on and SCIM provisioning
- Domain capture
- Custom data retention policy
- 300 file upload credits a month
- Workspace onboarding

Read AI's page lists different maximum meeting lengths for Enterprise in different places (4 hours in the table, 8 hours in the plan card). If long meetings matter to you, confirm the limit with Read AI's sales team.

## What does Read AI cost for a team?

A 10-person team pays $1,800 a year on Pro with annual billing, or $2,370 on monthly billing. Enterprise for the same team costs $2,700 a year billed yearly. Here is how Read AI pricing scales.

| Team size | Pro yearly | Enterprise yearly | Enterprise+ yearly |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 user | $180 | Not available (5+ licenses) | Ask Read AI |
| 5 users | $900 | $1,350 | $1,785 |
| 10 users | $1,800 | $2,700 | $3,570 |
| 25 users | $4,500 | $6,750 | $8,925 |

Enterprise+ for 1 to 4 users is not listed as a separate case on the page; check with Read AI if you are a small team that needs HIPAA.

## Which Read AI plan should you choose?

Most people should choose between 2 options: Free if you record 5 or fewer meetings a month, and Pro at $15 billed yearly if you record more. Enterprise tiers only make sense when a buyer, IT team or compliance rule requires them.

| If you are... | Pick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Trying Read AI on key calls | Free | 5 meetings a month, 1 hour each |
| A manager or seller with daily calls | Pro | Unlimited transcripts, 4-hour meetings |
| A team of 5+ that wants playback and highlights | Enterprise | Audio and video playback |
| In healthcare or a regulated field | Enterprise+ | HIPAA, SAML, SCIM, custom retention |

If you are on the fence between monthly and annual Pro, start monthly for 1 or 2 months. Switch to annual once you know the team uses it, and the 25% saving kicks in.

## Are there hidden costs in Read AI pricing?

There are no hidden fees on the pricing page, but 3 details raise the real cost for many teams: the 5-license minimum on Enterprise, the 25% premium for monthly billing, and file upload credits that cap how many recordings you can import.

1. **License minimums.** A team of 3 that wants Enterprise features still pays for 5 seats, or $1,350 a year billed yearly.
2. **Monthly premium.** Pro on monthly billing costs $237 a year per user, versus $180 billed yearly.
3. **Upload credits.** Pro includes 100 file upload credits a month, Enterprise 200 and Enterprise+ 300. Teams that import many recorded interviews or phone calls should check these limits.

Also count the time spent after each meeting. If people still write follow-up emails and tickets by hand, the tool's price is only part of the cost.

## Does Read AI offer education, nonprofit or India pricing?

Read AI has a separate [educational pricing page](https://www.read.ai/edu-pricing) for schools and universities. Its main pricing page does not list a nonprofit discount or India-specific prices as of 17 October 2026; prices are shown in USD.

If you are a nonprofit or based outside the US, ask Read AI's sales team before buying. Some vendors adjust prices at checkout based on region or card.

## Is Read AI worth the price?

Read AI is worth the price if you value meeting analytics (engagement, talk time, coaching) and want one tool across Zoom, Google Meet and Teams with apps on every platform. It is less worth it if you mainly need follow-up work done, or if you only meet a few times a week.

Ask 3 questions before you upgrade:

1. **Do you hold more than 5 meetings a month you want recorded?** If not, Free is enough.
2. **Do you need meetings longer than 1 hour?** Free stops at 1 hour; Pro allows 4.
3. **Do you need HIPAA or SSO?** Only Enterprise+ includes both.
