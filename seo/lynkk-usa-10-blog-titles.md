## Why each one made the cut

**1. HIPAA-compliant AI note takers for therapists.** The only verified near-term US organic traffic
play in the whole set. Live US SERP: therapro.ai ranks page one at **DA 7**, autonotes.ai at DA 21,
upheal.io at DA 23, getfreed.ai at DA 35. Four of seven organic results under DA 43. Highest CPC on
the map at $31 to $57 across the cluster. You also already appear at position 53 for "what is the
best ai tool for healthcare transcription", so Google associates you with the topic faintly already.
**Condition: only run this if Lynkk can sign a BAA and publish its subprocessor list.** If not,
delete this row and promote A2 (bot-free, 370 vol, SD 30) in its place as a links-permitting bet.

**2. Who can see your meeting transcripts.** 172 impressions at position 3.6 on queries like "which
third-party llm providers get to see prospect call transcripts" and "configurable transcript
retention and an audit trail". No competitor publishes this honestly, which is exactly why it is
citable. Region-pinned processing is Lynkk's own differentiator and `/security` currently draws 22
impressions, so the claim has no page carrying it.

**3. Enterprise AI notetakers: SSO, SCIM, audit logs and a 300-seat rollout.** This merges what were
B3 and A14 in the earlier list. I merged them deliberately: separately they would have cannibalized
each other on the same SSO and SCIM phrasing. Combined they carry **193 impressions and a best
position of 1.0**, the largest AI topic in the set. The language ("300 reps", "Okta or Microsoft
Entra", "SCIM deprovisioning for leavers") is unambiguously US enterprise procurement.

**4. Source-linked answers across past meetings.** 124 impressions, position 2.3. Maps directly to
the knowledge graph, which competitors cannot claim back quickly, since Fireflies and Otter return
search results rather than cited answers.

**5. From summary to CRM.** Position 3.0 on **your single highest-impression prompt query** (102
impressions for the Zoom AI Companion CRM-gap question). Strong US sales-ops intent.

**6. Volume discounts and rollout pricing.** 66 impressions at position 4.0. Pairs with #3 as the
commercial half of the same procurement conversation, without overlapping its keywords.

**7. Alternatives to the OpenAI note taker.** 31 impressions and **position 1.0** on one variant. New
enough that almost nobody has written it, and you have 12 compare pages with no OpenAI entry.

**8. AI note takers for Webex, Slack and Discord.** SD 24, the lowest-difficulty Google play that is
not already occupied by Microsoft, Google or a DA 80+ incumbent. 140 searches is modest, but it is
cheap coverage of the platform rows every competitor's grid leaves blank.

**9. AI note takers for financial advisors.** Only 30 searches, included for two reasons: $28.10 CPC,
and it repeats the vertical pattern that #1 proves works at low DA. You already have
`/use-cases/financial-advisors` to link to. Treat it as a test of whether the vertical play
generalises beyond healthcare.

**10. AI notetakers that work with MCP.** Thinnest evidence in the set at 6 impressions, and I am
including it anyway: position 2.5, essentially zero competing content, and MCP is in Lynkk's product
and in no competitor's marketing. This is a cheap first-mover land grab, not a traffic forecast. If
you want a tenth title with harder numbers instead, substitute A2 (AI note takers that do not join
your meeting, 370 vol, SD 30, $21.61 CPC) once link building is underway.

## Two caveats to keep attached to this list

1. **None of these will convert at the modelled rate until CTR is fixed.** Non-brand performance
   across your top-1,000 queries is 10,777 impressions and 9 clicks, a 0.08% CTR. The retitle work
   in `lynkk-low-hanging-fruit.md` still comes first.
2. **The seven AI posts have no reliable click forecast.** Position 3 in an AI-mode answer does not
   generate clicks the way position 3 in classic search does, because the answer is synthesised above
   the link. Their value is influencing what the engine says about Lynkk and earning the citation.
   I can give you their impressions and positions, which are real and measured, but I cannot give you
   a credible click number and will not invent one.
