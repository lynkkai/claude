# What Should a Project Kickoff Meeting Agenda Include? Template, Checklist and Examples

*Last updated: 24 November 2026*

> **Quick answer:** A good agenda for a project kickoff meeting has 8 blocks and fits in about 60 minutes: introductions, project purpose, goals and success metrics, scope, roles, timeline, risks and next steps. Share it 2 to 3 days before the meeting, and end with every action assigned to a named owner with a date.

A **project kickoff meeting** is the first meeting of a project. The sponsor, project manager and core team agree on why the project exists, what it will deliver and how they will work together. A clear agenda turns that conversation into decisions instead of a slide tour.

Below you will find a copyable 60-minute agenda, a pre-meeting checklist and 3 agenda examples. You also get a slide outline, an invite email and a plan for the 24 hours after the meeting.

## What is a project kickoff meeting?

A project kickoff meeting is a 45 to 90 minute session held after the project is approved and before work starts. It gets the sponsor, project manager, core team and key stakeholders to agree on goals, scope, roles and timeline in one room.

It usually happens at the end of the initiation phase, once a charter or statement of work exists. Some teams hold two: an internal kickoff with the delivery team, then an external kickoff with the client.

### What is the purpose of a project kickoff meeting?

Its purpose is to create one shared picture of the project before real work starts. Flowtrace found that [72% of professionals say clear objectives are the key to an effective meeting](https://www.flowtrace.co/collaboration-blog/50-meeting-statistics). A kickoff is where those objectives get set for months of work.

A kickoff should leave everyone able to answer 5 questions:

- Why are we doing this project, and why now?
- What does success look like, and how will we measure it?
- What is in scope, and what is explicitly out?
- Who owns what, and who makes the final call?
- What are the first milestones and the next 2 weeks of work?

## Who should attend a project kickoff meeting?

Invite 5 to 12 people: the sponsor, the project manager, the core team and leads from teams you depend on. Larger groups turn into presentations. Send a recording or summary to everyone else.

| Role | Why they attend |
|---|---|
| Project sponsor | Explains the business reason and has final say on scope |
| Project manager | Runs the meeting and owns the plan |
| Core team members | Do the work and raise risks early |
| Dependent team leads | Confirm hand-offs and timing |
| Client contact (external kickoff) | Confirms goals, approvals and how to communicate |

## What should a project kickoff meeting agenda include?

A project kickoff meeting agenda should include 8 blocks: introductions, project background, goals, scope, roles, timeline, risks, and next steps. Put the goals and scope blocks early, because every later decision depends on them.

### Project kickoff meeting agenda template (60 minutes)

Copy this kick off meeting agenda template and adjust the times. The same project kickoff meeting template works for internal and client kickoffs:

| Time | Agenda item | Owner | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 min | Welcome and introductions | Project manager | Everyone knows names and roles |
| 5 to 12 min | Background: why this project, why now | Sponsor | Shared understanding of the problem |
| 12 to 20 min | Goals and success metrics | Sponsor + PM | 2 to 4 measurable goals agreed |
| 20 to 30 min | Scope: in, out, and open questions | PM | Scope boundaries written down |
| 30 to 38 min | Roles, decision rights and RACI | PM | One accountable owner per deliverable |
| 38 to 46 min | Timeline and milestones | PM + team | First 3 milestones dated |
| 46 to 53 min | Risks, dependencies and assumptions | Team | Top 5 risks with owners |
| 53 to 60 min | Ways of working and next steps | PM | Meeting cadence, tools, action items |

If you only have 30 minutes, keep goals, scope, roles and next steps, and move background and risks into a pre-read.

### What should be discussed in each block?

Each block answers one question the team will otherwise ask again in week 3:

1. **Introductions.** Name, role on this project, and one thing each person needs from the others.
2. **Background.** The business problem, the customer or user affected, and what happens if nothing changes.
3. **Goals and success metrics.** 2 to 4 outcomes with a number and a date, for example "cut onboarding time from 10 days to 5 by Q2."
4. **Scope.** A 3-column list: in scope, out of scope, and still undecided.
5. **Roles and decision rights.** Who is responsible, who approves, who is consulted and who is kept informed.
6. **Timeline and milestones.** The first 3 dated milestones, not the full Gantt chart.
7. **Risks and dependencies.** Anything that could slip the timeline, each with an owner.
8. **Ways of working.** Status meeting cadence, where documents live, how decisions are recorded, and the first action items.

## How do you prepare for a project kickoff meeting?

To prepare, finish 6 tasks at least 2 days ahead: confirm the charter, draft goals and scope, book the room, send the agenda, share a pre-read, and brief the sponsor. Most of a kickoff's value is decided before anyone joins the call.

### Project kickoff meeting checklist

- [ ] Project charter or statement of work approved
- [ ] Draft goals, scope and success metrics written
- [ ] Attendee list confirmed with the sponsor
- [ ] Agenda and pre-read sent 2 to 3 days ahead
- [ ] Draft roles (RACI) and first milestones prepared
- [ ] Project workspace and document folder created
- [ ] Note taker or AI meeting assistant set up
- [ ] Sponsor briefed on what they will present

For a general pre-meeting routine, see our [meeting preparation checklist](https://lynkk.ai/blog/meeting-preparation).

## How long should a project kickoff meeting be?

Plan for 60 minutes for most internal projects and 90 minutes for large or client-facing projects. Flowtrace estimates that people spend about [392 hours a year in meetings](https://www.flowtrace.co/collaboration-blog/50-meeting-statistics), so a kickoff that runs long without decisions costs real time.

Timebox each agenda block and park off-topic discussions in a "parking lot" list you review at the end.

## What are some project kickoff meeting agenda examples?

The right agenda example depends on who is in the room: an internal team, a client, or an agile team starting a new product. Here are 3 versions you can adapt.

### Internal project kickoff meeting agenda

1. Introductions and project roles (5 min)
2. Business case from the sponsor (10 min)
3. Goals, metrics and scope (15 min)
4. RACI and decision rights (10 min)
5. Milestones and dependencies (10 min)
6. Next steps and first-week tasks (10 min)

### Client project kickoff meeting agenda

1. Introductions from both teams (10 min)
2. Client goals and definition of success (15 min)
3. Scope, deliverables and change process (15 min)
4. Communication plan, approvals and points of contact (10 min)
5. Timeline and first deliverable date (10 min)
6. Questions and next steps (10 min)

### Agile or IT project kickoff meeting agenda

1. Product vision and target users (10 min)
2. Initial backlog and must-have features (15 min)
3. Team roles, sprint length and ceremonies (10 min)
4. Definition of done and tooling (10 min)
5. Technical risks and dependencies (10 min)
6. Sprint 1 plan and next steps (5 min)

For construction or other regulated projects, add safety, permits and site access as a separate block.

## What should a project kickoff presentation include?

A project kickoff presentation should be 8 to 10 slides, one per agenda block, plus a title and a closing slide. Keep each slide to one decision or question so the meeting stays a discussion.

| Slide | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Project name, sponsor, date |
| 2 | The problem and why now |
| 3 | Goals and success metrics |
| 4 | In scope, out of scope, open questions |
| 5 | Team and roles (RACI) |
| 6 | Timeline with the first 3 milestones |
| 7 | Top risks and dependencies |
| 8 | Ways of working: cadence, tools, where decisions live |
| 9 | Action items and owners |
| 10 | Questions |

Send the deck as a pre-read 2 to 3 days ahead. In the meeting, spend under 2 minutes per slide and the rest on questions. If a slide needs more than 5 bullet points, it belongs in an appendix.

### What are the requirements for a project kickoff meeting?

The 3 minimum requirements are an approved project charter, a sponsor who can attend, and a draft of goals and scope. Without these, postpone the kickoff by a week rather than hold a meeting that cannot make decisions.

## How do you run a project kickoff meeting?

To run the meeting well, start on time, state the 3 outcomes you need by the end, and keep the project manager in the facilitator seat. The sponsor sets the "why," and the team owns the "how."

- **Open with the outcomes.** "By the end of this hour we will agree on goals, scope and the first 3 milestones."
- **Show, then ask.** Present each draft section in 2 minutes, then ask "what is missing or wrong?"
- **Write decisions live.** Put agreed goals, scope lines and owners in a shared document as you go.
- **Name an owner for every action.** An action without an owner and a date will not happen.
- **Close with a recap.** Read back the decisions and actions in the last 5 minutes.

## What should you do after a project kickoff meeting?

After the kickoff, send a written recap within 24 hours that lists decisions, owners, dates and open questions. Then move every action item into the tool where the team already tracks work.

A strong follow-up includes:

- Final goals, scope and success metrics
- The RACI or owner list
- First 3 milestones with dates
- Risks with owners
- Action items with owners and due dates
- Date of the first status meeting

## Project kickoff meeting email template

Send the invite 2 to 3 days before the meeting so attendees can read the pre-read. Keep it under 120 words.

>
> Hi team,
>
> We're kicking off [Project name] on [date] at [time]. In 60 minutes we'll agree on goals, scope, roles and the first 3 milestones.
>

> 4. Scope

> 8. Next steps

> [Your name]

## What are common project kickoff meeting mistakes?

The 5 most common project kickoff mistakes are inviting too many people, presenting slides without discussion, skipping scope boundaries, leaving roles vague, and sending no recap. Each one shows up later as rework or missed deadlines.

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| 20+ attendees | Keep the core group to 5 to 12 and share a recording |
| A 40-slide deck | Send slides as a pre-read and use the meeting for decisions |
| No "out of scope" list | Write 3 to 5 things the project will not do |
| "The team" owns everything | One accountable owner per deliverable |
| No recap | Send decisions and actions within 24 hours |

## FAQ

### What is the difference between a kickoff meeting and a planning meeting?

A kickoff meeting aligns everyone on goals, scope and roles at the start of a project. Planning meetings come after it and break the work into tasks, estimates and sprints over 1 to 2 sessions.

### What phase is the project kickoff meeting in?

It happens at the end of the initiation phase, which is phase 1 of the 5 classic project phases. It comes after approval and before planning and execution begin.

### Should the client attend the project kickoff meeting?

The client should attend a separate external kickoff, usually 1 to 2 weeks after the internal one. The internal kickoff lets the team agree on risks and staffing before presenting a plan to the client.

### Is it kickoff or kick-off?

Both of the 2 spellings are correct. "Kickoff" (one word) is more common in US business writing, and "kick-off" in British English. Pick one and use it consistently.

### What is a project kickoff meeting checklist?

A project kickoff meeting checklist is a list of 6 to 10 tasks to finish before the meeting, such as approving the charter, drafting goals and scope, confirming attendees, and sending the agenda 2 to 3 days ahead.
