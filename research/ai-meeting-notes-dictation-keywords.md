**Where to focus:** high volume *and* high buying intent is the core "AI note taker / AI meeting
assistant" cluster, but it is hard to rank for. The best ratio of intent to effort is
**sales/conversation intelligence** (highest CPCs, almost no ad competition), **platform-specific
note takers** (Teams/Meet/Zoom, low SD) and **competitor alternative pages**. **Mac AI dictation**
is the fastest-rising opportunity (Wispr Flow searches went from 12K to 90K/mo in under a year) with
the easiest SEO in the whole set.
