# How to Record a Zoom Meeting as the Host or a Participant, on Desktop and Mobile

*Last updated: 24 November 2026*

> **Quick answer:** To record a Zoom meeting, click **Record** in the meeting toolbar and choose **Record on this Computer** (local) or **Record to the Cloud**. Local recording is available on free and paid accounts in the Zoom desktop app. Cloud recording needs a paid account. Participants can record only after the host clicks **Allow Record**.

This guide shows exactly how to record a Zoom meeting in 2 ways (local and cloud), as the host or a participant, on a laptop, Mac, iPhone or iPad. It also covers where Zoom saves recordings and what to do if the Record button is missing.

## How do you record a Zoom meeting as the host?

As the host, you record a Zoom meeting in 3 clicks: start the meeting, click **Record** in the toolbar, and choose where to save it. Zoom's help center confirms that [computer recording is available to Basic (free) accounts and above](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0076922).

1. Open the Zoom desktop app and start the meeting as the host.
2. Click **Record** in the meeting controls toolbar. On a narrow window it may sit under **More**.
3. Choose **Record on this Computer** or **Record to the Cloud** (cloud appears only on paid accounts).
4. A "Recording..." label appears in the top-left corner, and participants see a recording notice.
5. Click **Pause** or **Stop** in the toolbar, or end the meeting to stop automatically.

When you stop a local recording, Zoom converts the file to MP4 after the meeting ends. Keep the app open until conversion finishes.

### What is the difference between local and cloud recording?

Local recording saves an MP4 file to your computer and works on free accounts. Cloud recording saves to your Zoom account online and needs a paid plan. Choose cloud recording if you need to share a link, record from a phone, or keep files off your laptop.

| | Local (computer) recording | Cloud recording |
|---|---|---|
| Plans | Free and paid | Paid only |
| Devices | Desktop app (Windows, macOS, Linux) | Desktop, web, iOS, Android |
| Where it is saved | Your computer, Documents > Zoom by default | Recordings page in the Zoom web portal |
| Sharing | Upload the file yourself | Share a link from the portal |

## How do you record a Zoom meeting as a participant?

To record a Zoom meeting as a participant, you need the host's permission first. Ask in chat or out loud, and once the host allows it, the **Record** button becomes active in your toolbar.

The host grants permission in 3 steps:

1. Click **Participants** in the meeting toolbar.
2. Hover over the participant's name and click **More**.
3. Click **Allow Record**.

Participants can record only to their own computer, not to the host's cloud. If the host leaves or revokes permission, the recording stops.

### Can you record a Zoom meeting if you are not the host?

Yes, but only with the host's permission through **Allow Record**, or if the host makes you a co-host on an account that allows co-hosts to record. Recording without the host's knowledge using other tools may break your company policy or recording laws. In the US, the federal wiretap law ([18 U.S.C. § 2511](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/2511)) allows recording when 1 party consents, but many states require every party's consent. See our guide on [whether it is legal to record a meeting](https://lynkk.ai/blog/is-it-legal-to-record-a-meeting) before you do.

## How do you record a Zoom meeting on iPhone or iPad?

You can record a Zoom meeting on iPhone or iPad only with cloud recording, which requires a paid account. Zoom's help center points mobile users to [cloud recording for paid accounts](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0063640), because local recording is not available in the mobile apps.

1. Join or start the meeting in the Zoom app as host (or with recording permission).
2. Tap **More** (the 3 dots) in the meeting controls.
3. Tap **Record to the Cloud**.
4. To stop, tap **More**, then **Stop Recording** and confirm.

After the meeting ends, the recording processes and appears under **Recordings** in the Zoom web portal. The same steps work on Android phones and tablets.

## How do you record a Zoom meeting on a laptop or Mac?

On a Windows laptop, MacBook or Linux computer, you record a Zoom meeting with the desktop app by clicking **Record** and choosing **Record on this Computer**. The steps are identical on all 3 systems.

By default, Zoom saves local recordings to a "Zoom" folder inside Documents:

- **Windows:** C:\Users\[Your name]\Documents\Zoom
- **macOS:** /Users/[Your name]/Documents/Zoom

You can change the folder in the desktop app under **Settings > Recording**. On a Chromebook, use cloud recording if your plan includes it, since local recording requires the desktop app.

### How do you record a Zoom meeting with video and audio?

Local and cloud recordings both capture video and audio by default. To get separate audio files for each speaker, which helps with editing or transcription, turn on **Record a separate audio file for each participant** in **Settings > Recording** before the meeting.

## Where do Zoom recordings go?

Zoom recordings go to 1 of 2 places: local recordings save to the Documents > Zoom folder on your computer, and cloud recordings save to the **Recordings** page of the Zoom web portal. Zoom can email the host when a cloud recording finishes processing.

If a cloud recording is still processing after 48 hours, Zoom's help center lists troubleshooting steps. Local files only appear after conversion completes, so do not close the app or shut down your laptop right after the meeting.

## Why can't I record a Zoom meeting?

You usually cannot record a Zoom meeting for 1 of 4 reasons: you are not the host, recording is turned off in settings, you are on a mobile device with a free account, or your admin has blocked recording.

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| No Record button | Ask the host for Allow Record, or check the **More** menu |
| Record is grayed out | Turn on local or cloud recording in web portal Settings > Recording |
| Only "Record to the Cloud" missing | Cloud recording needs a paid account |
| Recording blocked on a company account | Ask your Zoom admin; recording may be locked by policy |

## How do you record a Zoom meeting without using Zoom's recorder?

You can record a Zoom meeting without Zoom's built-in recorder in 2 ways: use your computer's screen recorder, or use an AI meeting assistant that records and transcribes the call. Both still require you to tell participants they are being recorded.

**Screen recording.** On a Mac, press Command + Shift + 5 and choose **Record Entire Screen**. On Windows 11, open the Snipping Tool and choose **Record**. Built-in screen recorders may not capture the other participants' audio without extra setup; see our guide to [recording audio on a Mac, including system audio](https://lynkk.ai/blog/record-audio-on-mac).

About 30 seconds after the call, it gives you structured notes and action items, not just a video file. If you mainly want a searchable record, this saves rewatching a 60-minute recording.

## FAQ

### Can you record a Zoom meeting for free?

Yes. Local recording on the Zoom desktop app is free on Basic accounts. Cloud recording, and recording from a phone or tablet, requires 1 of Zoom's paid plans.

### Does Zoom notify participants when you record?

Yes. Zoom shows a recording indicator in the top-left corner and plays or displays a notice to participants when recording starts. People who join later also see the notice.

### Can more than one person record a Zoom meeting?

Yes. The host can give **Allow Record** to 2 or more participants, and each records a separate local copy on their own computer.

### Are Zoom meetings recorded automatically?

No, unless the host or admin turns on automatic recording in Settings > Recording. When it is on, recording starts as soon as the meeting begins, either locally or to the cloud.

### Can recorded Zoom meetings be transcribed?

Yes. Cloud recordings on 1 of Zoom's paid plans can generate an audio transcript, and AI meeting assistants can transcribe and summarize meetings automatically. See [how to transcribe Zoom meetings](https://lynkk.ai/blog/how-to-transcribe-zoom-meetings) for the steps.
