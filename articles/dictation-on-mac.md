## What is dictation on Mac?

Dictation on Mac is Apple's built-in speech to text Mac feature, and it costs $0 on every current Mac. You speak, and macOS types your words wherever the cursor sits: an email, a note, a document or a chat box.

It is built for short, live typing. It does not record audio for later, save a separate file or write meeting notes. That is why many people pair it with voice dictation software for Mac once they need more, which we cover below.

## How do you turn on dictation on Mac?

You can enable dictation on Mac in 3 clicks from System Settings, the steps people look for when they search "enable dictation Mac". Apple lists this as the first check when dictation is missing, so start here.

1. **Open System Settings** from the Apple menu.
2. **Click Keyboard** in the sidebar.
3. **Turn on Dictation** and confirm when macOS asks.

In the same Dictation panel you can pick your language, choose a microphone and change the shortcut. If you use more than 1 language, add each one there so macOS recognizes them.

## How do you use dictation on Mac?

Here is how to use dictation on Mac in 4 steps, taken from Apple Support. If you searched how to do dictation on Mac, these are the only steps you need.

1. **Click where you want the text** in any app, such as Mail, Notes or Pages.
2. **Start dictation:** press the Microphone key if your keyboard has one, press Fn twice, or choose Edit > Start Dictation.
3. **Speak normally.** Say punctuation such as "comma" or "period" where you want it.
4. **Stop dictation** with the Escape key, the Microphone key or the same shortcut.

Speak at a normal volume and keep the microphone clear. Apple notes that clothing, your body or background noise can block or confuse the microphone.

## Where is the dictation button on Mac?

Most people start dictation on Mac with 1 key: the Fn key, pressed twice. That default dictation Mac shortcut works in every app, and newer keyboards also carry a Microphone key in the top row.

| Way to start | Where to find it |
|---|---|
| Microphone key | Top row of newer Mac keyboards |
| Keyboard shortcut | Fn twice by default |
| Menu | Edit > Start Dictation in most apps |
| Custom shortcut | System Settings > Keyboard > Dictation > Shortcut |

To change the shortcut, open the Shortcut pop-up menu in Dictation settings, then pick a preset or choose Customize and press the keys you want.

## Why is dictation on Mac not working?

Dictation on Mac usually fails for 1 of 6 reasons, and Apple's own troubleshooting page covers each of them. Work down this table in order; most people are fixed by the first 3 rows.

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Dictation is off | Turn it on in Keyboard settings |
| Wrong language or region | Choose the language you speak in Dictation settings |
| Wrong microphone | Select the right one in Sound or Keyboard settings |
| Mac does not hear you | Raise the input volume in Sound settings |
| Muffled or noisy audio | Keep the mic clear and move away from noise |
| No connection | Some dictation may need an internet connection |

If dictation Mac not working problems continue after all 6 checks, restart the Mac and test in a simple app like Notes. If it works there, the problem is the other app, not dictation itself.

## Does Mac dictation work in Google Docs?

Yes. Dictation on Mac types anywhere you can type text, so it works in a Google Docs file in 2 steps: click inside the document, then press Fn twice and start speaking. It works the same in Safari, Chrome or another browser.

Google Docs also has its own voice typing under Tools > Voice typing. You only need 1 of the 2. Mac dictation has the advantage of working the same way in every other app too.

## FAQ

### Where is dictation saved on Mac?
Dictation on Mac saves 0 separate files. The text goes straight into the app where your cursor sits, such as Notes, Mail or Pages, and it is saved like anything you type there. To keep audio and a transcript of a conversation, use a recording app like Lynkk, which stores both with notes.

### Can you dictate on Mac in Pages?
Yes. Pages works with dictation on Mac in 2 steps, like any other app where you can type: click inside the document, then press Fn twice or choose Edit > Start Dictation and speak. Say punctuation such as "comma" and "period" out loud, and press Escape when you finish dictating to stop listening.

### How do I stop dictation on Mac?
You can stop dictation on Mac in 3 ways: press the Escape key, press the Microphone key if your keyboard has one, or press the dictation shortcut again, which is Fn twice by default. Keep your cursor in the document while you speak so the text lands in the right place.

### Can I change the dictation shortcut on Mac?
Yes. Open System Settings, click Keyboard, and find the Shortcut pop-up menu in the Dictation section. Pick 1 of the preset shortcuts or choose Customize and press the keys you want. A custom shortcut helps if the Fn key already does something else on your keyboard or in your apps.

### Does Mac dictation need an internet connection?
Sometimes. Apple's troubleshooting page says you may need an internet connection for dictation to work, so connection is 1 of the 6 checks when it fails. If dictation stops on a weak network, reconnect and try again, or test in Notes to rule out the app you were using.
