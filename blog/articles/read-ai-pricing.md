# Read AI Pricing in 2026: Plans and Costs for US Teams

*By [Samarth Wagh](https://lynkk.ai/blog/authors/samarth-wagh), Co-founder, Lynkk.ai · Last updated 17 October 2026 · Pricing checked 17 October 2026*

> **Quick answer:** Read AI pricing in 2026 starts at **$0** for 5 meetings a month. **Pro** costs **$15 per user per month billed yearly** or $19.75 monthly. **Enterprise** costs $22.50 yearly or $29.75 monthly and needs 5+ licenses. **Enterprise+** costs $29.75 yearly or $39.75 monthly and adds HIPAA, SAML and SCIM.

*Disclosure: Lynkk publishes this guide and makes a competing AI meeting assistant. Prices come from [Read AI's plans and pricing page](https://www.read.ai/plans-pricing), checked 17 October 2026.*

Read AI pricing is easy to read and easy to underestimate. Free looks like a full product, then stops at 5 meetings. Annual billing knocks off 25%, which matters a lot at team scale. And the Enterprise tier has a 5-license minimum.

In this Read AI pricing guide: what each plan costs, what it includes, and what a real team will pay in a year.

## How much does Read AI cost per month?

Read AI costs $0 on Free, $19.75 per user per month on Pro with monthly billing, and $15 per user per month on Pro with annual billing. Above that, Enterprise and Enterprise+ cost more and add security and admin features.

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing (per month) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | 5 meetings a month |
| Pro | $19.75 | $15 | Unlimited transcripts |
| Enterprise | $29.75 | $22.50 | Requires 5+ licenses |
| Enterprise+ | $39.75 | $29.75 | HIPAA, SAML, SCIM |

Prices are in USD, per user per month. Pay yearly and every paid plan drops 25%.

## Is Read AI free?

Yes, Read AI has a free plan, but it covers only 5 meeting transcripts, 5 meeting reports and 5 live-note sessions a month, each up to 1 hour. Uploading files? Not on Free.

What the free plan does include:

- Summaries, topics and a personal meeting coach
- Unlimited enterprise search and basic integrations
- Apps for Windows, macOS, Android and iOS
- Support for 20+ languages

Five meetings a month suits someone testing Read AI on their most important calls. Daily meetings? You will hit the limit in the first week.

## What does Read AI Pro include?

At $15 billed yearly or $19.75 monthly, Read AI Pro removes the meeting cap and adds 4 things: unlimited transcripts, priority report processing, 100 file upload credits a month, and meetings up to 4 hours long.

Pro also adds premium integrations:

- Notion and Confluence
- Salesforce and HubSpot
- Jira
- Zapier and webhooks

For most individuals and small teams, Pro is the plan that makes Read AI usable every day.

## What is the difference between Read AI Enterprise and Enterprise+?

Enterprise ($22.50 yearly, $29.75 monthly) adds audio and video playback, video highlights, 200 file upload credits a month and premium support. Minimum: 5 licenses.

On top of that, Enterprise+ ($29.75 yearly, $39.75 monthly) adds compliance and identity features:

- HIPAA compliance
- SAML single sign-on and SCIM provisioning
- Domain capture
- Custom data retention policy
- 300 file upload credits a month
- Workspace onboarding

One oddity: the pricing page lists different maximum meeting lengths for Enterprise in different places (4 hours in the table, 8 hours in the plan card). Long meetings matter to you? Confirm the limit with Read AI's sales team.

A 10-person team pays $1,800 a year on Pro with annual billing, or $2,370 on monthly billing. For the same team, Enterprise costs $2,700 a year billed yearly. Here is how Read AI pricing scales.

Small team that needs HIPAA? Check with Read AI, because Enterprise+ for 1 to 4 users is not listed as a separate case on the page.

## Which Read AI plan should you choose?

Most people should choose between 2 options: Free if you record 5 or fewer meetings a month, and Pro at $15 billed yearly if you record more. The Enterprise tiers? Only when a buyer, IT team or compliance rule requires them.

| If you are... | Pick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Trying Read AI on key calls | Free | 5 meetings a month, 1 hour each |
| A manager or seller with daily calls | Pro | Unlimited transcripts, 4-hour meetings |
| A team of 5+ that wants playback and highlights | Enterprise | Audio and video playback |
| In healthcare or a regulated field | Enterprise+ | HIPAA, SAML, SCIM, custom retention |

On the fence between monthly and annual Pro? Start monthly for 1 or 2 months, then switch to annual once you know the team uses it and let the 25% saving kick in.

## Are there hidden costs in Read AI pricing?

There are no hidden fees on the pricing page. Still, 3 details raise the real cost for many teams: the 5-license minimum on Enterprise, the 25% premium for monthly billing, and upload credits that cap imported recordings.

1. **License minimums.** A team of 3 that wants Enterprise features still pays for 5 seats, or $1,350 a year billed yearly.
2. **Monthly premium.** Pro on monthly billing costs $237 a year per user, versus $180 billed yearly.
3. **Upload credits.** Pro includes 100 file upload credits a month, Enterprise 200 and Enterprise+ 300. Teams that import many recorded interviews or phone calls should check these limits.

Count the time spent after each meeting, too. When people still write follow-up emails and tickets by hand, the tool's price is only part of the cost.

## What does Read AI cost in real-world scenarios?

Real Read AI costs depend on team size, billing cycle and whether you need playback or compliance. These 3 scenarios use list prices with annual billing.

| Scenario | Plan | Seats | Yearly cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small team that just needs notes | Pro | 8 | $1,440 |
| Sales team that reviews call video | Enterprise | 25 | $6,750 |
| Healthcare organization needing HIPAA | Enterprise+ | 100 | $35,700 |

**Small team on Pro.** Eight people on Pro billed yearly pay $1,440. If only 4 of them record meetings regularly, ask whether everyone needs a seat.

**Sales team on Enterprise.** Twenty-five reps on Enterprise pay $6,750 a year, and get audio and video playback for reviewing calls. Coaching-heavy teams often compare this with Fathom Business at $25 per user billed yearly.

**Regulated organization on Enterprise+.** One hundred users on Enterprise+ pay $35,700 a year for HIPAA compliance, SAML, SCIM and custom retention. At this size, ask Read AI's sales team about volume pricing.

## Why does Read AI join meetings you did not expect?

Calendar access is how Read AI joins meetings. When someone connects their calendar and auto-join is on, the notetaker can join meetings on that calendar, including meetings other people organized. A settings question, not a hidden cost. Still, it affects how many seats teams end up buying.

To keep control, agree on 3 rules before rollout:

1. **Who may connect a calendar** to Read AI.
2. **Which meetings are auto-joined**, for example internal meetings only.
3. **How attendees are told** that a meeting is being recorded and summarized.

Notetaker joining calls you organize without an invite? Ask the bot's owner to change their auto-join settings, or remove it from the participant list.

## Does Read AI offer education, nonprofit or India pricing?

Schools and universities get a separate [educational pricing page](https://www.read.ai/edu-pricing). As of 17 October 2026, the main pricing page lists no nonprofit discount and no India-specific prices; everything is shown in USD.

Nonprofit, or based outside the US? Ask Read AI's sales team before buying. Some vendors adjust prices at checkout based on region or card.

## Is Read AI worth the price?

Read AI is worth the price if you value meeting analytics (engagement, talk time, coaching) and want one tool across Zoom, Google Meet and Teams with apps on every platform. Less so if you mainly need follow-up work done, or only meet a few times a week.

Ask 3 questions before you upgrade:

1. **Do you hold more than 5 meetings a month you want recorded?** If not, Free is enough.
2. **Do you need meetings longer than 1 hour?** Free stops at 1 hour; Pro allows 4.
3. **Do you need HIPAA or SSO?** Only Enterprise+ includes both.
