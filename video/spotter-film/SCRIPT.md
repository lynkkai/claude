**The idea:** "Know what to say next." The film opens on the Mac keyboard in the dark, close enough to
read the keys. A thumb's worth of light, one press of ⌘↩, and the screen crashes in with Spotter already
answering the question on the call. Each act after that is one stunt: the doc read as text, the two sides
heard, the bar sitting in every screen share, the asks dissolving to nothing.
**Music:** 120 BPM, groove from the keypress at 2.0 s to the end; drop on 26.0 s (the recap); final chord
on 30.0 s, when Remi lands on the end card's button.
