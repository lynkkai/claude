# Fireflies AI Pricing, Features and Limits Explained, Plus How Lynkk Compares for Your Team

*Last updated: October 1, 2026 · Fireflies details checked on Fireflies' own pricing and help pages on September 30, 2026*

> **Quick answer:** Fireflies AI pricing has 4 plans: Free, Pro at $10 per seat per month, Business at $19 and Enterprise at $39, all billed annually, with recordings capped at 2 hours on Free and Pro. **Lynkk is the better choice for teams that want the follow-up done**: bot-free recording on the Mac, a Meeting Brief with owners and due dates, Jira send and a morning brief before meetings, and it starts free.

> **Disclosure:** This guide is published by Lynkk, and Lynkk is one of the 2 tools compared. Fireflies details come from Fireflies' own pricing and help pages, cited in plain text with the date we checked them.

## What is Fireflies AI and how does it work?

Fireflies AI is an AI meeting assistant whose bot, Fred, joins 3 platforms, Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, from your calendar to record, transcribe and summarize them. After the call, the Fireflies AI note taker produces a transcript with speaker labels, a summary and action items.

Fireflies stores every call in a searchable library. Its AI assistant, AskFred, answers questions across your meetings and drafts follow-up emails, and its sales features push notes into CRMs such as Salesforce and HubSpot.

If you want a single place to search past calls, the Fireflies AI note taker does that well. Teams that care more about owned tasks and prep before the next call often compare it with Lynkk.

## How much does Fireflies.ai cost in 2026?

Fireflies AI pricing starts at $0 and runs to $39 per seat per month for Enterprise, billed annually. Monthly billing costs more: Pro is $18 a month and Business $29 a month. Prices below were checked on September 30, 2026.

Every Fireflies plan includes unlimited transcription and AI summaries. Fireflies also meters some newer AI features through AI credits, so check how many credits a plan includes if you rely on those features.

## Is Fireflies AI free to use?

Yes, Fireflies has a free plan with unlimited transcription and AI summaries, but storage is limited to 400 minutes per team and each recording is capped at 2 hours. That is enough to try it on 5 or 6 calls, not to run a team on it.

Lynkk also starts free. The Free plan includes recording and transcription, the meeting bot (audio only), the Meeting Brief, tasks and Jira send, with a 2 hour cap per recording. See [Lynkk pricing](https://lynkk.ai/pricing) for what Pro adds.

### How do they record meetings?

Lynkk gives you 4 ways to record, and 2 of them use no bot. The [Lynkk Mac app](https://lynkk.ai/download) notices calls in Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Slack huddles, WhatsApp, FaceTime and Webex and records both sides on 2 channels.

The [Lynkk Chrome extension](https://lynkk.ai/features/chrome-extension) records Meet, Zoom (web) and Teams in a browser tab, and the [free Lynkk meeting bot](https://lynkk.ai/features/meeting-bot) joins from your calendar when a bot is fine. Fireflies relies mainly on Fred, which clients and guests see in the participant list.

### What do the notes contain?

Every Lynkk recording becomes a Meeting Brief with 7 parts: title, abstract, key takeaways with timestamps, topics, action items, decisions with context and open questions. Each action item has an owner, a due date and a priority.

Fireflies summaries include an overview, keywords and action items, and they are easy to scan. The difference is structure: Lynkk separates decisions and open questions, which is what people search for later.

### What happens before and after the call?

Lynkk sends a morning briefing email on days with meetings, covering 3 things per meeting once Google Calendar is connected: who you are meeting, your latest note with them and how many action items are still open. After the call, tasks land on a board and you can send them to Jira from the note.

On Pro, [Ask Lynkk](https://lynkk.ai/features/knowledge-graph) answers questions from your own notes with citations that open the note at the right moment. AskFred covers similar ground inside Fireflies.

## Where is Fireflies.ai stronger than Lynkk?

Fireflies is stronger in 4 areas, and it is fair to say so. If any of these is your main need, Fireflies is a sound choice.

1. **CRM sync.** Fireflies pushes notes to Salesforce, HubSpot and other CRMs. Lynkk does not sync with a CRM.
2. **Integrations.** Fireflies connects to a long list of apps. Lynkk connects to Jira, Google Calendar and Slack.
3. **Languages.** Fireflies lists 100+ transcription languages. Lynkk transcribes 17, with 4 in beta.
4. **Platforms.** Fireflies has apps beyond the Mac. Lynkk records from a Mac, a Chrome tab or a browser.

## Is Fireflies AI safe to use in meetings?

Fireflies AI is widely used by businesses, but any AI note taker is safe only if you check 2 things: how the vendor handles your data and whether everyone on the call has agreed to be recorded. The [NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework/privacy-framework) is a practical checklist for the first.

For consent, federal law ([18 U.S.C. § 2511](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title18-section2511)) allows 1 party consent, while California ([Penal Code § 632](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=632)) requires all parties to agree. Lynkk keeps recordings private to your account and never trains on your conversations.

*This is general information, not legal advice.*
