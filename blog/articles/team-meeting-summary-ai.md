*Disclosure: Lynkk publishes this guide and is one of the tools reviewed. Prices come from vendor pages, checked 18 October 2026.*

Team meeting summary AI is a type of software that listens to a call and writes up the decisions, action items and key points once it ends. Microsoft Teams, Zoom and Google Meet all ship one on eligible plans. So the real question has shifted. Not whether AI can summarize a meeting, but which summary your colleagues will actually read and act on.

Below: the 3 built-in summarizers in Teams, Zoom and Google Meet. Then 5 cross-platform tools, plus the setup steps people search for most.

## Can AI summarize a meeting accurately?

Yes. AI can summarize a meeting accurately enough to replace manual notes for most internal calls, typically within a few minutes of the call ending. Quality slips, though. Crosstalk, poor microphones, heavy jargon and mixed languages all drag accuracy down.

A good team meeting summary contains 4 parts:

1. **Decisions** made in the meeting
2. **Action items** with an owner and a due date, like these [action item examples](https://lynkk.ai/blog/action-items-examples-template)
3. **Key discussion points** by topic
4. **Open questions** to carry into the next meeting

Before anything leaves the team, check names, numbers and dates. Team meeting summary AI trips on those details most often.

## How do you get an AI summary from a Microsoft Teams meeting?

To get an AI summary from a Teams meeting, you need two things: a Teams Premium or Microsoft 365 Copilot license, and a meeting that was recorded or transcribed. Afterward, open the meeting chat and select Recap. The AI notes sit inside it.

So how to get an AI summary from a Teams meeting without paying more? You mostly can't. Microsoft's recap documentation says intelligent recap comes with Teams Premium, an add-on license, and with the Microsoft 365 Copilot license. Unlicensed? You can still open the recording, transcript and shared files in the basic recap ([Microsoft Teams recap documentation](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/teams/meetings/recap-in-microsoft-teams)).

No Copilot licenses at your company? Then the practical route is [a meeting bot that joins Teams calls](https://lynkk.ai/features/meeting-bot), and our roundup of [Teams AI note takers](https://lynkk.ai/blog/ai-note-taker-microsoft-teams) lists options.

## Is there a free Teams meeting summary AI?

Not from Microsoft. Teams' own AI summary needs a Teams Premium or Copilot license. Want Teams meeting summary AI free of license costs? Fathom joins Teams calls and records without a cap, and Otter's free plan allows 300 minutes a month at 30 minutes per call. Read AI gives 5 meetings a month, 1 hour each, and Lynkk gives 5 notes a month at up to 2 hours each.

Where these free tiers really differ is meeting length. A 1-hour weekly team meeting fits inside Fathom, Read AI (right at its 1-hour cap) and the [free and Pro plans](https://lynkk.ai/pricing) we offer, while Otter's free plan cuts it off at 30 minutes. Halfway through.

## Where is the AI summary in Zoom?

Zoom's meeting summary. That's the source of Zoom's AI summary, and Zoom dropped the AI Companion name for it on 24 June 2026 ([Zoom blog](https://www.zoom.com/en/blog/zoom-ai-companion-zoommate/)). Once the host starts the meeting summary mid-call, Zoom generates a recap afterward and shares it with the host automatically.

A few things to know:

- **Who can turn it on:** the meeting host.
- **Who gets it:** the host by default; the host can share it with participants.
- **What it costs:** included with eligible paid Zoom Workplace plans; free Basic accounts get summaries for 3 hosted meetings a month.
- **What it covers:** Zoom meetings. Zoom's separate My Notes feature reaches other platforms, with 3 free uses a month.

For cross-platform picks, see our [Zoom AI Companion alternatives](https://lynkk.ai/blog/zoom-ai-companion-alternatives) guide.

## How does Google Meet summarize meetings?

Google Meet summarizes meetings with Gemini's "Take notes for me", which writes notes into a Google Doc saved in the organizer's Drive. Eligibility matters: you need a qualifying Google Workspace edition or Google AI plan. Language support covers 8 languages, one per meeting ([Google Meet help](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/14754931)).

Three switches exist: the calendar event, the greenroom before joining, or the "Take notes for me" button mid-meeting. The notes are separate from Meet's own transcript, which our [Google Meet transcript guide](https://lynkk.ai/blog/google-meet-transcript) covers. Hindi isn't on the supported list. That matters for anyone choosing team meeting summary AI for India-based colleagues.

## FAQ

### Can AI summarize a Teams meeting?

Yes, with a Teams Premium or Microsoft 365 Copilot license, but watch two edge cases. Microsoft says recap notes and content aren't currently available for ad-hoc events, and AI summaries only appear for events transcribed in a supported language. Record without transcription and no AI summary appears.

### Is there a free AI meeting summarizer?

Yes. Fathom records and summarizes unlimited calls at no cost, and its free plan now offers bot-free capture as a Mac beta. The catch with other free tiers is uploads: Otter's free plan allows only 3 audio or video file imports in total, so summarizing old recordings quickly pushes you to a paid plan.

### Where do I find the AI summary in Zoom?

The host gets it automatically once the summary finishes processing, and can share it with participants by email or Zoom Chat. Chat sharing only works if continuous meeting chat is on for that meeting. Participants see nothing unless the host shares it, so ask the host rather than searching your own account.

### Does Google Meet summarize meetings automatically?

Not by default for most users, though a Workspace admin can set Take notes for me to switch on automatically for certain meetings. Google recommends it for meetings of 15 minutes to 8 hours. If the organizer shares notes with invited guests, Google means everyone on the invite, not only attendees, so check the list before sensitive calls.

### How long does an AI meeting summary take?

Usually a few minutes. Google says Meet's notes document is generated shortly after the meeting ends, and Zoom shares its summary with the host once processing finishes. Very short calls are the exception: Teams only produces AI summaries for events of 5 minutes or longer. Review names, numbers and owners before sharing.

### Can AI summarize meetings in Hindi?

Some tools can. Lynkk supports Hindi, plus a Hinglish setting for speakers who mix Hindi and English, and Read AI lists Hindi among its report languages. Google Meet's Take notes for me leaves Hindi off its 8 languages. Best test: a real meeting in your team's actual language mix, watching how names and numbers come through.
