# Lynkk website changes

Every recommended change to lynkk.ai in one place: what to change, why, and the new copy.
Compiled 15 November 2026, updated 16 November 2026, from the Search Console export (14 Aug to 13 Nov), a crawl of all 126 sitemap URLs, Ubersuggest data and the live plan table.
**Nothing here has been applied to the site yet.** Full copy for the 14 new pages is in section 11. The pricing page and the legal pages (terms, refund) are left out on purpose: they will be handled separately.

Pricing used throughout: **Pro** $4 a month (₹250 in India), **Enterprise** custom. Users must pay before they can use Lynkk: there is no free plan, free tier or free trial.

##### When to send a follow-up email after a sales call

Send it the same day if you can, and always within 24 hours. The buyer still remembers the call, and your email becomes the written record their team forwards around. If you agreed on a next meeting during the call, send the calendar invite first and the email right after, so both arrive together.

##### What to include in a sales follow-up email

1. **A specific thank-you.** Name something they said, not "thanks for your time".
2. **Their problem, in their words.** One or two sentences that prove you listened.
3. **What you agreed.** Decisions and open questions from the call.
4. **The next step, with a date.** Who does what, by when.
5. **One attachment or link.** Only what you promised on the call.

##### 1. Avoma: closest all-round Gong alternative

Avoma records and transcribes calls, scores them for coaching, tracks deals and syncs with the CRM, with public per-seat pricing and a 14-day trial. After the trial, accounts that do not pay drop to a viewer role that cannot record. See [Lynkk vs Avoma](/compare/avoma).

**Best for:** sales teams of 10 to 100 that want coaching and deal insight without Gong's contract.

##### 2. Clari Copilot: coaching with forecasting

Clari Copilot (formerly Wingman) records calls, gives live cues and coaching scorecards, and connects to Clari's forecasting. Pricing is by quote. See [Lynkk vs Clari Copilot](/compare/clari-copilot).

**Best for:** teams that also want Clari for forecasting.

##### 3. Fireflies: affordable call library with CRM sync

Fireflies records Zoom, Meet and Teams calls into a searchable library, with CRM integrations and basic conversation analytics. Pro is $10 per seat per month billed yearly. See [Lynkk vs Fireflies](/compare/fireflies).

**Best for:** small teams that want call search and CRM logging for little money.

##### 4. Fathom: free recording, CRM sync on paid plans

Fathom's free plan records unlimited calls with summaries. Paid team plans, from $15 per user per month, add CRM sync and team features. See [Lynkk vs Fathom](/compare/fathom).

**Best for:** founders and small teams starting out with call recording.

##### 5. Otter: lowest-cost transcripts

Otter records and transcribes calls with a bot or its app, with a live transcript. Pro is $8.33 per user per month billed yearly. It has no coaching or deal features.

**Best for:** reps who just want transcripts.

##### 6. Lynkk: call notes and follow-up emails for $4

Lynkk records sales calls with a [meeting bot](/features/meeting-bot), with no bot from the [Chrome extension](/features/chrome-extension), or in person on a Mac. After each call it writes the notes, lists the buyer's problems and next steps as action items with owners, and drafts the follow-up email. Before your next call, Ask Lynkk answers "what did this customer say about pricing?" across every past call, with sources.

**Price:** Lynkk Pro is $4 a month (₹250 in India), with no contract and no sales call.

**Where Gong is better:** Lynkk has no call scoring, forecasting or deal boards, and no CRM sync yet. See [Lynkk for sales calls](/use-cases/sales-calls) and [Lynkk vs Gong](/compare/gong).

**Best for:** small sales teams, founders and consultants who need good call notes and fast follow-ups.

##### 7. Chorus by ZoomInfo: for ZoomInfo customers

Chorus is ZoomInfo's conversation intelligence tool, with call recording, coaching and deal insights. It is sold with ZoomInfo, by quote.

**Best for:** teams already paying for ZoomInfo.

##### Which Gong alternative should you choose?

- **Need coaching and deal insight:** Avoma or Clari Copilot.
- **Need CRM logging on a budget:** Fireflies or Fathom's paid plans.
- **Need notes and follow-up emails fast:** Lynkk ($4 a month).
- **Need forecasting at scale:** stay with Gong, or look at Clari.

##### FAQ

###### What is the best Gong alternative for small teams?

For coaching and deal insight, Avoma. For call notes and follow-ups on a small budget, Lynkk at $4 a month or Fireflies at $10 per seat. For free call recording, Fathom.

###### Is there a cheaper alternative to Gong?

Yes. Every tool in this list publishes lower prices or has a free plan except Clari Copilot and Chorus, which are quoted. Lynkk Pro ($4 a month), Otter Pro ($8.33) and Fireflies Pro ($10) are the lowest paid options.

###### Does Gong have a free trial?

Gong does not offer a self-serve free trial. You see the product in a demo and buy through its sales team.

###### Can a Gong alternative sync to Salesforce or HubSpot?

Avoma, Clari Copilot, Fireflies and Fathom's paid plans all sync to common CRMs. Lynkk does not sync to a CRM yet.

##### Conclusion

Gong is built for large sales teams with forecasting needs and the budget for an annual contract. Smaller teams usually get what they need from Avoma, Fireflies or Fathom, and Lynkk covers call notes and follow-up emails for $4 a month. [Get Lynkk Pro](/pricing).
