## Does Microsoft Word have dictation?

Yes. Word has a built-in Dictate feature, and it is included with 1 thing: a Microsoft 365 subscription. Microsoft describes it as speech to text for writing documents, and it needs a microphone and a reliable internet connection.

Before you pay for Microsoft Word dictation software, try Dictate first, since it costs nothing extra on a subscription. For letters, reports and notes typed by voice, it is often all you need.

## How do you dictate in Word?

Here is how to dictate in Word in 4 steps, taken from Microsoft Support. The steps are the same in spirit on Windows and Mac; only the shortcut changes.

1. **Sign in to Word** with your Microsoft 365 account and open a new or existing document.
2. **Go to Home > Dictate**, or press Alt + ` (backquote) on Windows or Option + F1 on Mac.
3. **Wait for the microphone to turn on**, then speak normally.
4. **Say punctuation and commands** as you go, and select Dictate again to stop.

If Word asks for microphone access the first time, allow it, because Dictate cannot hear you without it. No extra Microsoft Word dictation software is needed for these steps.

## Where is Dictate in Word 365?

In Word for Microsoft 365, the Dictate button sits in 1 place: the right side of the Home tab, shown as a microphone icon. If you do not see it, the most common reason is that you are not signed in with a subscription account.

| Word version | How to open Dictate |
|---|---|
| Word for Windows (Microsoft 365) | Home > Dictate, or Alt + ` |
| Word for Mac (Microsoft 365) | Home > Dictate, or Option + F1 |
| Word for the web | Home > Dictate |

## How do you use Microsoft Word dictation commands?

Word's Dictate understands spoken punctuation and at least 5 editing commands that Microsoft lists by name. Using them turns dictation from plain typing into real editing by voice.

- **"New line"** starts a new line.
- **"Delete"** removes the last word or selection.
- **"Bold that"** bolds what you just said.
- **"Underline last sentence"** underlines the previous sentence.
- **"Start list"** begins a bulleted list.

Say punctuation such as "comma", "period" and "question mark" out loud. Speak at a steady pace and pause briefly before each command.

## Why is Microsoft Word dictation not working?

Word dictation fails for 1 of 4 reasons in most cases, all tied to what Microsoft lists as requirements. Check them in this order before you reinstall anything.

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Dictate button missing or grey | Sign in with a Microsoft 365 subscription account |
| Mic turns on but no text appears | Allow Word to use the microphone in your system settings |
| Text stops or lags | Check your internet connection; Dictate needs a reliable one |
| Wrong words | Pick the language you speak in Dictate settings, and move away from noise |

If none of these fixes work, test your microphone in another app. If the mic works there, sign out of Word, sign back in and try again. If Dictate still fails, Microsoft Word dictation software that runs outside Word, such as Lynkk on a Mac, is a reliable fallback.

## Can Microsoft Word transcribe audio?

Yes. Besides Dictate, Word for Microsoft 365 has a separate Transcribe feature that turns recorded audio into text, with 300 minutes a month included. It accepts .wav, .mp4, .m4a and .mp3 files, and Microsoft 365 Copilot users get 30,000 minutes.

Dictate is for live typing, and Transcribe is for recordings. ## FAQ

### Is Word dictation free?
Word dictation is included with 1 thing you may already pay for: a Microsoft 365 subscription. Microsoft says Dictate is only available to subscribers, so there is no extra charge if you have one. For $0 options, Windows voice typing (Windows logo key + H) and Mac Dictation (Fn twice) also type into Word documents.

### Where is dictation on Microsoft Word?
Dictation on Microsoft Word is on the Home tab, as a Dictate button with a microphone icon, in Word for Windows, Mac and the web. You can also press Alt + ` (backquote) on Windows or Option + F1 on Mac. If the button is missing, sign in with a Microsoft 365 subscription account first.

### Which Word version has Dictate?
Dictate is available in 3 versions of Word for Microsoft 365 subscribers: Word for Windows, Word for Mac and Word for the web. Microsoft says the feature is only available to subscribers and needs a microphone plus a reliable internet connection. Older one-time purchase versions may not show the Dictate button.

### Is Microsoft Word dictation good?
Word's Dictate is good for 1 job: typing documents by voice inside Word, with spoken punctuation and editing commands. It is not built for recording meetings or dictating in other apps. For dictation in every Mac app plus meeting transcripts and notes, Lynkk is the stronger choice for busy professionals.

### What is a good voice to text app for Word?

### Can I dictate in Word without internet?
Microsoft lists 3 requirements for Word's Dictate, and 1 is a reliable internet connection, so it will not work offline. If your connection drops, dictation stops or lags. Check your network first when text stops appearing, then turn Dictate off and on again. The other 2 requirements are a microphone and a Microsoft 365 subscription.
