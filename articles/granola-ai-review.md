## What is Granola AI and how does it work?

Granola AI is an AI notepad that listens to 2 audio sources during a meeting, your microphone and your computer's audio, with no bot joining the call. You type a few rough bullets while people talk, and after the meeting Granola merges them with the transcript into a full set of notes.

That design is why Granola became popular fast. Nobody sees "notetaker has joined" in the participant list, and the notes read like your own, because they start from your own bullets.

Granola runs on Mac and Windows, with apps for iPhone and Android, and it works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and other calling apps because it captures audio on your device.

## What do Granola AI notes look like?

Granola AI notes start from what you typed and are expanded by AI, using 1 of 29 built-in templates or a template you create. Templates set the structure, such as a sales call, a 1:1 or a stand-up, and "Recipes" are saved prompts you run on a note afterward.

The result is personal notes in your own voice. That is a strength for people who like to stay in control of what gets written down, and a weakness if you want the notes written without typing anything during the call.

## Is Granola AI free?

Yes, Granola has a free Basic plan, but it only shows notes from the last 30 days in the app. Older notes are kept, and they become visible again if you upgrade.

Granola's notes depend on the bullets you type. Some people love that, because it keeps them engaged; others find typing during a client call distracting and want the notes done for them.

### What happens before and after the call?

Lynkk sends a morning briefing email on days with meetings, covering 3 things for each meeting once Google Calendar is connected: who you are meeting, your latest note with them and how many action items are still open. After the call, tasks go to a board and you can send them to Jira from the note.

On Pro, [Ask Lynkk](https://lynkk.ai/features/knowledge-graph) answers questions from your own notes with citations that open the note at the right moment. Granola also lets you chat with your meetings, and its Business plan posts summaries to Slack and attaches notes in HubSpot.

## Is Granola AI legal to use without telling people?

Recording with no bot is still recording, so consent rules apply to Granola and Lynkk alike. Federal law ([18 U.S.C. § 2511](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title18-section2511)) allows 1 party consent, while California ([Penal Code § 632](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=632)) and several other states require every party to agree.

The simple habit is to say at the start of each call that you are taking AI notes. To review any vendor's data practices, the [NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework/privacy-framework) is a useful checklist.

*This is general information, not legal advice.*
