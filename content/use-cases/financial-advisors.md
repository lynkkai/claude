### Block 2. In simple words

## How do financial advisors use AI notes?

Financial advisors meet clients to talk about goals, money and life changes. These meetings are long and full of details. With the client's consent, Lynkk records the meeting and writes the goals, the changes and the action items, so the next review starts where the last one ended.

**Who it's for**
- Independent financial advisors
- Wealth managers
- Advisors who meet clients in person

### Block 3. How it works

## Client reviews without the note-taking

**Record: with client consent.** Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or in your office with the Mac app.

**Read: goals and changes.** What the client wants, what changed since last time, and what you agreed to do.

**Prepare: for the next review.** On Pro, ask "What did the Shahs say about retirement last year?" before the meeting.

### Block 8. FAQ

## Questions about AI for financial advisors

**Does Lynkk sync with my CRM?**

No. Lynkk doesn't connect to a CRM. You can share notes by link and send action items to Jira.

**Is Lynkk a compliance archive?**

No. Lynkk writes meeting notes for your own work. Check your firm's recordkeeping rules with your compliance team.

**What's the best AI for financial advisors?**

It depends on what you need. If CRM sync and compliance archiving matter most, look at advisor-specific tools. If you
want private, accurate notes from every client meeting, online or in person, try Lynkk free.

**Do I need the client's consent to record?**

Yes. Ask before you record. Rules differ by country and state.
