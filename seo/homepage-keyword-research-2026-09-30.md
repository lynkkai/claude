### Patterns [JUDGMENT, based on the tables above]

1. **Brand first, then one fixed category noun.** Fathom = "AI Notetaker", tl;dv = "AI Meeting Notetaker", Granola = "AI Notepad". They repeat the same pairing everywhere, so people search the pair.
2. **A second head term follows** ("Transcription", "Insights", platforms) or a single benefit ("Never Take Notes Again").
3. **Some own a category word** (Otter "Meeting Agent", Fireflies "AI Teammate", Granola "AI Notepad") to stand out while keeping "notetaker" elsewhere.
4. **The brand shows up off-site on the brand SERP too:** App Store, Chrome Web Store, Zoom Marketplace, LinkedIn (Granola, Fathom, tl;dv SERPs). [DATA]
5. **Read AI is the exception.** Its homepage title is pure category terms, and it ranks #2 for "ai powered meeting assistant" (40,500) and #4 for "ai notes taker" (27,100). But it has DA 47 behind it. [DATA]
