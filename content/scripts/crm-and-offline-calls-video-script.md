# Video Script: Incomplete CRM Data and Unrecorded Calls

**Format:** Short explainer / social video, about 65 seconds
**Structure:** Problem, then the Lynkk fix, for each of the two issues
**CTA:** Start free at lynkk.ai

## Part 1: CRM data is incomplete, so forecasts are guesswork

### [0:00 - 0:05] Hook

**Visual:** A sales manager looks at a forecast dashboard. Half the deal fields are blank.

**VO:** "Your forecast is only as good as your CRM. And right now, your CRM is mostly empty."

**On-screen text:** Incomplete CRM = forecast guesswork

### [0:05 - 0:16] The problem

**Visual:** A rep finishes a call. The CRM tab is open. They glance at it, close it, and jump into the next call.

**VO:** "Reps skip CRM updates because updating takes more effort than skipping. One report found 40% of reps still update fields by hand. So next steps, budgets, and deal stages live in someone's memory, not in your pipeline."

**On-screen text:** 40% of reps still update CRM fields by hand

**Source footnote:** Cotera, Revenue.io

### [0:16 - 0:28] The Lynkk fix

**Visual:** The same call ends. Lynkk notes appear, then Salesforce and HubSpot fields fill in one by one.

**VO:** "With Lynkk, the call ends and your CRM is already up to date. Lynkk fills in Salesforce or HubSpot from what was actually said on the call, not from what the rep remembers an hour later."

**On-screen text:** Filled from the conversation. Not from memory.

## Part 2: Calls outside Zoom, and in-person meetings, go unrecorded

### [0:28 - 0:33] Hook

**Visual:** A rep's phone lights up with a WhatsApp call from a buyer.

**VO:** "But not every deal happens on Zoom."

### [0:33 - 0:43] The problem

**Visual:** Quick cuts: a WhatsApp call, a FaceTime call, two people talking over coffee. A bot notetaker icon tries to join each one and gets blocked.

**VO:** "Buyers call on WhatsApp. They FaceTime. They meet you in person. Bot notetakers can't join any of those, so some of your most important conversations never get recorded."

**On-screen text:** WhatsApp. FaceTime. In person. Unrecorded.

### [0:43 - 0:57] The Lynkk fix

**Visual:** A Mac notification slides in: "Lynkk detected a FaceTime call." Cut to a coffee shop table with Lynkk recording the conversation.

**VO:** "Lynkk's Mac app detects WhatsApp and FaceTime calls. And Lynkk records in-person conversations too. Every conversation turns into notes, tasks, and CRM updates, wherever it happens."

**On-screen text:** Every call. Every room. Captured.
