- Competitor brand queries (Otter, Granola, Plaud, Read AI) carry most of the new volume. Keep these posts factual and fair; do not make claims about competitors that cannot be sourced.

## How to use the Reddit queries

1. Add a "What Reddit users say" or FAQ section to each post answering the listed queries in plain language.
2. Search each query on Reddit, note the top recurring complaints (price, privacy, bots joining calls, accuracy) and address them directly.
3. Do not paste Reddit usernames or full quotes without permission; summarize the sentiment instead.

## Before publishing (from memory/lynkk-brand.md "To confirm")

These claims appear in several posts and must be confirmed first:
- Free-tier limits and paid pricing (#3, #13, #25)
- HIPAA / BAA status, SOC 2 and other certifications (#5)
- Supported CRMs (#26), Windows/iOS/Android availability (#9, #20, #21)
- Supported meeting platforms (Zoom / Meet / Teams) (#15, #17, #18, #23, #24)
- Accuracy and language claims ("~97%", "15+ languages") (#22)
