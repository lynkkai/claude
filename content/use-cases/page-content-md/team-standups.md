#### What is a standup meeting?

A standup is a short daily team meeting. Each person shares what they did yesterday, what they'll do today, and what is blocking them. Lynkk joins the meeting, writes this down, and turns each task into an action item with an owner.

**Who it's for**
- Project managers
- Engineering and product teams
- Team leads who can't join every standup

#### Your daily standup meeting, without anyone taking notes

**Joins: from your calendar.** Turn on auto-join and the meeting bot goes to the recurring standup every day.

**Writes: yesterday, today, blockers.** The note sorts the standup the way your team runs it, with timestamps.

**Tracks: Tasks board and Jira.** Action items land on the Tasks board with owners. Send them to Jira when you're ready.

#### Questions about AI standup notes

**Does everyone on the team need Lynkk?**

No. One person records, or the bot joins from their calendar, and they share the note with a link.

**Do action items sync to Jira automatically?**

No. They go to the Tasks board on their own. Sending them to Jira is one step from the note, with one issue per action item.

**Does it work for a scrum meeting or a standup in the office?**

Yes. Any recurring meeting on your calendar works, and in the room you record with the Mac app or the web app.

**How is Lynkk different from AI project management tools?**

Lynkk doesn't replace your project tool. It captures what was said in the meeting and turns it into action items you can
send to Jira.
