Patterns across all ten

  • BOFU is mostly brand. Every competitor ranks #1-2 for its own name, pricing and download. Lynkk cannot take those, but it can answer them from the side with pricing, alternatives and vs pages.
  • MOFU is where low-DA sites win. tldv, bluedot and meetgeek (DA 34-44) rank top 3 for "best ai meeting note takers", "otter ai alternative" and competitor brand names through reviews.
  • TOFU volume comes from tools and platform how-tos (Otter's audio-to-text pages, Notta's language pages, read.ai's "how to record on zoom"). Off-topic TOFU (Fireflies' YouTube to MP3) brings visits but not users.

What ranks fast and what takes time

Quick win 1-3 months

  • "Tool A vs Tool B" pages (SD 11-21). Proof: page 1 of "granola vs otter ai" includes mrktcorrect.com (DA 9) and zackproser.com (DA 22).
  • Platform how-tos with a precise answer. The Google Meet transcript page reached #9 despite SD 53. Google rewards the exact answer more than authority here.
  • Alternatives and reviews for weak-domain brands (Jamie DA 13: "jamie ai note taker" 1,300/mo, SD 17).
  • Long-tail templates ("how to write meeting notes template" SD 9, India "meeting minutes format pdf" SD 6).
  • Pages already at #10-20 (pricing posts, Zoom note taker): on-page fixes alone can move them.

Medium 3-6 months

  • Teams and Zoom AI note taker pages (SD 26-33). Commercial SERPs with DA 40-60 listicles.
  • "sales call follow up email" (SD 26), "granola alternative" (SD 28), "gong alternative" (SD 28, $64 CPC).
  • "ai meeting assistant" (SD 11, but brand-heavy commercial SERP).
  • These need 5-15 internal links each and a few relevant backlinks.

Long-term 6-12+ months

  • Head terms: "ai note taker" (22,200, SD 49), "meeting notes template" (22,200, SD 49), "best ai note taker" (SD 36, paid difficulty 100).
  • "transcribe audio to text" (22,200): owned by tool pages on DA 51-66 domains.
  • "meeting transcription software" (SD 67), "ai meeting note taker" (SD 60).
  • These need referring domains first: Lynkk has 6, and the competitors have 12K-355K backlinks.

Funnel path to a sign-up

  • TOFU (how-tos, templates): a CTA in context, such as "No Zoom transcript? Lynkk records Zoom on any plan". Goal: Starter sign-up (5 free hours) or Chrome extension install.
  • MOFU (vs, best-of, note taker for X): a comparison table with Lynkk as a row, then a link to /compare/[tool]. Goal: Starter sign-up.
  • BOFU (pricing, alternatives, /compare, /use-cases): price and switching proof, ending in "Try 5 hours on us". Goal: sign-up, then Pro at $4.
