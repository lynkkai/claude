## What is Fireflies AI, and why look for an alternative?

Fireflies AI is a meeting assistant whose bot, Fred, joins 3 platforms, Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, to record, transcribe and summarize calls. The Fireflies AI note taker stores every call in a searchable library, and AskFred answers questions across your meetings.

People look for Fireflies alternatives for 3 common reasons, based on the questions they search most:

1. **The bot in every call.** Clients and guests see Fred in the participant list.
2. **Limits on the cheaper plans.** Free storage is 400 minutes per team, and recordings are capped at 2 hours on Free and Pro.
3. **Follow-up and prep.** Teams want owned tasks, Jira issues and context before the next call, not just a library.

## How much does Fireflies.ai cost in 2026?

Fireflies AI pricing starts at $0 and runs to $39 per seat per month for Enterprise on annual billing. Monthly billing costs more: Pro is $18 and Business $29 per seat per month.

Every Fireflies plan includes unlimited transcription and AI summaries, and some newer AI features use a separate pool of AI credits. When you compare the Fireflies AI price with the tools below, check both the seat price and those limits.

## 1. Lynkk: best Fireflies AI alternative overall

Lynkk is the best Fireflies AI alternative because it covers 3 stages of a meeting: a brief before, recording with or without a bot during, and owned tasks with Jira send after. It starts free.

### How does Lynkk record meetings?

Lynkk records in 4 ways, and 2 of them use no bot. The [Lynkk Mac app](https://lynkk.ai/download) notices calls in Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Slack huddles, WhatsApp, FaceTime and Webex and records both sides on 2 channels, so your side is labeled "You".

The [Chrome extension](https://lynkk.ai/features/chrome-extension) records Meet, Zoom (web) and Teams in a browser tab with no extra participant. When a bot is fine, the [free Lynkk meeting bot](https://lynkk.ai/features/meeting-bot) joins from your calendar, audio only. For in-person meetings, you record your microphone in the browser.

### What does every Lynkk note include?

Every recording becomes a Meeting Brief with 7 parts: title, abstract, key takeaways with timestamps, topics, action items, decisions with context and open questions. Each [action item](https://lynkk.ai/features/action-items) has an owner, a due date and a priority, and you can send it to Jira from the note, 1 issue per item.

With Google Calendar connected, a morning briefing email shows who you are meeting, your latest note with them and how many action items are still open. On Pro, [Ask Lynkk](https://lynkk.ai/features/knowledge-graph) answers from your own notes with citations, and Slack answers come to your channels.

### What could be better?

Lynkk has 3 limits compared with Fireflies. It does not sync with a CRM, it connects to fewer tools (Jira, Google Calendar and Slack), and it transcribes 17 languages, 4 in beta, against Fireflies' 100+.

### How much does Lynkk cost?

Lynkk starts free, with recording, transcription in 17 languages, the meeting bot (audio only), the Meeting Brief, tasks and Jira send. See [Lynkk pricing](https://lynkk.ai/pricing) for what Pro adds.

## 2. Fathom: best free Fireflies alternative

Fathom's free plan includes unlimited recordings, transcripts and AI summaries, with advanced summaries for your first 5 calls a month. Paid plans start at $15 per user per month.

**Best for:** people who want unlimited recordings without paying, and are fine with a notetaker in the call.

**What we liked:** the most generous free plan in this list for recording volume, plus a draft follow-up email after each call.

**What could be better:** new Team and Business plans need a paid seat for every member.

**Where Lynkk goes further:** bot-free recording on the Mac, owners and due dates on every action item, and a brief before meetings.

*Source: Fathom pricing page, checked September 30, 2026.*

## 3. Otter: best for live transcripts and phone apps

Otter's free plan includes 300 transcription minutes a month, and Pro costs $8.33 per user per month billed annually, the lowest paid price in this list. Its bot, OtterPilot, shows a live transcript during calls.

**Best for:** people who read along during calls and record on an iPhone or Android phone.

**What we liked:** the live transcript and Otter's mobile apps.

**What could be better:** the free minutes run out quickly, and online calls rely mainly on its bot.

**Where Lynkk goes further:** bot-free call recording on the Mac, Jira send and a morning brief.

*Source: Otter pricing page, checked September 30, 2026.*

## 4. Read AI: best for meeting reports

Read AI's free plan covers 5 meetings a month, and Pro starts at $15 per user per month billed annually. It adds reports on how each meeting went, such as talk time and engagement.

**Best for:** managers who want data on meeting habits as well as notes.

**What we liked:** paid plans store an unlimited number of meetings, and Enterprise+ adds data retention policies, domain capture and SAML.

**What could be better:** 5 free meetings a month is a short trial for a team.

**Where Lynkk goes further:** owned action items with due dates and Jira send from each note.

*Source: Read AI plans and pricing page, checked September 30, 2026.*

## 5. Granola: best bot-free notepad

Granola listens to your microphone and computer audio with 0 bots in the call, then fills in the rough notes you type. It is free with 30 days of history, and Business costs $14 per user per month.

**Best for:** people who like typing their own notes during calls.

**What we liked:** no bot ever joins, and Business connects to HubSpot, Notion, Slack and Zapier.

**What could be better:** the notes depend on what you type, and free users only see the last 30 days.

**Where Lynkk goes further:** Lynkk writes the whole note for you, and offers a free meeting bot for calls you cannot attend.

*Source: Granola pricing page and help center, checked October 1, 2026.*

## 6. Jamie: best bot-free option with EU hosting

Jamie records your computer's audio with no bot on macOS and Windows, and in person on iOS and Android. The free plan includes 10 meetings a month at up to 30 minutes each, and Plus costs €25 a month.

**Best for:** European teams that want EU hosting and bot-free notes.

**What we liked:** no bot, apps on every major platform and in-person recording on phones.

**What could be better:** the 30 minute free limit is short, and prices are in euros.

**Where Lynkk goes further:** Jira send, a morning brief and a free meeting bot when you want one.

*Source: Jamie website and pricing page, checked October 1, 2026.*

## 7. Gemini in Google Meet: best for Google Workspace companies

Gemini's "Take notes for me" captures Google Meet notes in real time and saves them to a Google Doc with suggested next steps. Since January 15, 2025, it is included in Google Workspace Business and Enterprise plans.

**Best for:** companies that run most meetings in Google Meet.

**What we liked:** no extra tool or bot, and notes land in Google Docs.

**What could be better:** it covers Google Meet, not Zoom, Teams or in-person meetings.

**Where Lynkk goes further:** 1 place for every platform, with owned tasks and Jira send.

*Source: Google Workspace help pages, checked September 30, 2026.*

You can switch in 4 steps and run both tools side by side while you compare. Fireflies can stay connected until you are sure.

1. **Sign up for Lynkk free** and connect Google Calendar.
2. **Install the Mac app or Chrome extension** for bot-free recording, or turn on the meeting bot.

## Is Fireflies AI safe to use on client calls?

Fireflies AI and every tool in this list are only safe if you check 2 things: how the vendor handles your data, and whether everyone on the call agreed to be recorded. The [NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework/privacy-framework) is a practical checklist for the first.

For consent, federal law ([18 U.S.C. § 2511](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title18-section2511)) allows 1 party consent, while California ([Penal Code § 632](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=632)) requires all parties to agree. Lynkk keeps recordings private to your account and never trains on your conversations.

### What is the best Fireflies AI alternative?
Lynkk is the best Fireflies AI alternative in 2026. It records with no bot on the Mac, writes a Meeting Brief with 7 parts including owners and due dates, sends action items to Jira and briefs you each morning before your meetings. Fathom is the best free pick for unlimited recordings.

### What is Fireflies AI used for?
Fireflies AI is used to record, transcribe and summarize meetings on 3 platforms, Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, through its bot, Fred. Teams use it to search past calls, ask AskFred questions and push notes into CRMs such as Salesforce and HubSpot. Lynkk focuses on owned tasks, Jira send and prep.

Fireflies.ai costs $10 per seat per month for Pro, $19 for Business and $39 for Enterprise on annual billing, as checked on September 30, 2026. Monthly billing is $18 for Pro and $29 for Business. The free plan costs $0 but limits storage to 400 minutes per team.

Lynkk's Free plan includes recording, transcription, the meeting bot (audio only), the Meeting Brief and Jira send.

### Is Lynkk better than Fireflies.ai?
For teams whose meetings end in tasks and tickets, yes. Lynkk adds 3 things Fireflies does not focus on: bot-free recording on the Mac, a morning brief before meetings and action items with owners and due dates in every note. Fireflies is stronger for CRM sync, integrations and language count.

### Which Fireflies alternative records without a bot?
Lynkk, Granola and Jamie all record without a bot. Lynkk's Mac app records Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Slack, WhatsApp, FaceTime and Webex calls on 2 channels, and its Chrome extension records in a browser tab. Granola and Jamie capture your computer's audio on your own device.
