# Lynkk Instagram and LinkedIn Content Plan

> Built 2026-11-16. Same product facts and pricing as `video/lynkk-youtube-longform-seo-plan.md` and the November website changes doc.
> Generated from `social/social_data.py` by `social/build_social.py`; edit the data file, then rebuild.
> Each platform gets its own content types. Topics reuse the YouTube research so one idea becomes a long video, a Reel, a carousel and a LinkedIn post.

## What the research says

1. **Instagram: Reels for reach, carousels for saves.** Reels get about 36% more reach than carousels, and carousels about 12% more engagement than Reels (Buffer, 4M+ posts). Carousels now take up to 20 slides. ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources//instagram-algorithms/), [ContentStudio](https://contentstudio.io/blog/instagram-algorithm))
2. **Instagram: sends decide who sees you next.** Instagram's head, Adam Mosseri, names watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach as the top signals. Sends matter most for reaching people who don't follow you yet, so posts are built to be forwarded to a colleague. ([Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/instagram-algorithm), [Smash Balloon](https://smashballoon.com/how-the-instagram-algorithm-works/))
3. **Instagram: captions are search.** Keywords in the first line of the caption, the bio and the display name feed Instagram search, and public posts can show up on Google. So every caption opens with the plain search phrase. ([Later](https://later.com/blog/instagram-seo/amp/), [True Future Media](https://www.truefuturemedia.com/articles/instagram-reach-2026-algorithm-reels-carousels-caption-seo))
4. **Instagram: 3-5 good Reels a week.** The most common guidance is 3-5 quality Reels a week with a steady cadence; originality and watch time count more than volume. ([ListenFirst](https://www.listenfirstmedia.com/the-state-of-instagram-in-2026-reach-is-not-a-posting-strategy/), [Metadata Reactor](https://metadatareactor.com/blog/instagram-growth-strategy-2026/))
5. **LinkedIn: people beat pages.** Every study agrees personal profiles out-reach company pages; the gap ranges from under 2x to about 7x depending on who measured it. The founder's profile carries reach; the company page carries proof and assets. ([Ordinal](https://www.tryordinal.com/blog/linkedin-content-strategy), [AuthoredUp](https://authoredup.com/blog/how-to-use-linkedin-for-business), [Blck Alpaca](https://blckalpaca.at/en/knowledge-base/social-media/social-media-algorithms-distribution/linkedin-company-page-or-personal-profile-reach))
6. **LinkedIn: documents hold attention.** Document (PDF) carousels lead on dwell time and saves in most 2026 reports; native video under 90 seconds is growing (views up 36% year over year). Strong text posts still win on comments. ([Dataslayer](https://dataslayer.ai/blog/linkedin-algorithm-february-2026-whats-working-now), [DSMN8](https://dsmn8.com/blog/whats-working-on-linkedin-in-2026-report-key-findings/))
7. **LinkedIn: 2-5 posts a week, early comments, careful with links.** Buffer's 2M+ post study puts the sweet spot at 2-5 posts a week. Early engagement drives distribution. Studies disagree on how much an outside link hurts, so links go in the first comment and the team tests both. ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-linkedin/), [Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/linkedin-algorithm-february-2026-whats-working-now))

> Caveat: Most of these figures come from social media tool vendors with different methods. The directions agree; the exact numbers don't. Check Instagram Insights and LinkedIn analytics after 4 weeks and shift the mix toward what works for Lynkk.

## Content types by platform

### Instagram

| Type | Job | What it is | How often |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reel: meeting life | Reach and sends | Relatable 10-20s skits about meetings, built to be forwarded to a colleague. Product appears in the last beat only. | 2 a week |
| Reel: how-to or product in 30 seconds | Reach and search | Screen-recorded answers to one search question (Google Meet transcript, record a Teams meeting) or one Lynkk workflow. | 1-2 a week |
| Carousel: template or comparison | Saves | Copy-ready templates and fair comparisons, 6-10 slides, keyword on the cover. The format people save for later. | 2 a week |
| Stories | Keep followers warm | Daily polls, quizzes and reposts; the one place a link sticker works. Pin highlights for evergreen sets. | Daily |
| Trial Reels | Test hooks safely | Try two hooks for the same Reel with non-followers before sharing to the main feed. | As needed |

### LinkedIn company page

| Type | Job | What it is | How often |
|---|---|---|---|
| Document (PDF carousel) | Dwell time and saves | Playbooks, templates and buyer guides, 8-12 pages, one idea per page. | 2 a week |
| Native video | Show the product | 60-90s square video with burned-in captions, hook in the first 3 seconds. | 1 a week |
| Image or text: company news | Proof | Pricing, launches and milestones. Short, factual, no hype. | When there's news |

### LinkedIn founder and team

| Type | Job | What it is | How often |
|---|---|---|---|
| Text post | Reach and comments | 150-250 words: stories from building Lynkk, sales craft, honest opinions about the category. No link in the body. | 3 a week |
| Reshare with a comment | Amplify the page | Team members reshare company documents with one line of their own, within the first hour. | Each company post |

## Before posting

- Publish after the website changes go live. The site still says "Start for free"; posts that say $4 a month would contradict it.
- Confirm the Instagram handle and LinkedIn page URL (still open in the brand memory) and update bios: "AI meeting assistant for Zoom, Meet, Teams and in-person meetings. Lynkk Pro $4/mo."
- Instagram: set up comment-to-DM automation (for example ManyChat) before the "Comment MOM" and "Comment ACTION" posts, or change those CTAs to "link in bio".
- Check every competitor price on posting day and show the date on the slide.
- Founder posts are drafts for the founder to rewrite in their own voice. Don't post them as written.

## Claims guardrails

- Lynkk Pro is $4 a month (₹250 in India), Enterprise is custom. Never "start free", "free plan" or "free AI note taker" about Lynkk. "Free" is fine for templates and competitors' plans.
- No CRM sync, no phone app, no public API, Google Calendar only. No data residency or certification claims (SOC 2, HIPAA, GDPR).
- Spotter is in early access and is never called hidden. Never say Lynkk "speaks" in meetings.
- Disclose that Lynkk made any comparison and include where competitors win.
- Spell it Lynkk (two k's) everywhere, including captions and alt text. No em or en dashes in copy.
