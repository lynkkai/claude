## Caption (LinkedIn)

Record the call. Lynkk writes the rest.

Four ways to record:
- Mac app: notices the call in Zoom, Meet, Teams and more. No bot joins.
- Chrome extension: a capsule above the call controls. Press Record.
- Meeting bot: paste a link, or turn on calendar auto-join.
- Web: record your mic at lynkk.ai for a meeting in the room.

After every recording you get a Meeting Brief: decisions, open questions, and action items with owners and due dates. Transcripts in 17 languages (four in beta). Send tasks to Jira, one issue per action item.

With Pro, Ask Lynkk answers only from your own notes, with citations that play the recording from that moment. It also answers in Slack.

Lynkk never trains on your conversations. Start free: https://lynkk.ai
