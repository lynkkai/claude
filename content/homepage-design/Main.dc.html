Frequently asked questions

What is Lynkk?

Lynkk is an AI note taker and meeting assistant. It records your online and in-person meetings, joins calls to answer questions by voice, and turns every conversation into notes, action items, CRM updates, and Jira tickets within about 30 seconds.

How is Lynkk different from other AI notetakers?

Most AI notetakers only write a summary after the meeting. Lynkk works across the whole meeting: it brings context from past calls before you start, speaks when you need it during the call, and does the follow-up work after.

Does Lynkk work for in-person meetings?

Yes. Lynkk is an AI meeting recorder for both online calls and in-person conversations, including interviews, classes, and client visits.

Which apps does Lynkk connect to?

Lynkk connects to your calendar, Jira, Calendly, Cal.com, your CRM, and Slack, and you can connect anything else via MCP.

Where is my meeting data processed?

You choose the region. Every model behind Lynkk processes and answers from the region you pick.

Does Lynkk work offline?

Yes. Lynkk is offline-first, so recording keeps working without an internet connection.

Is Lynkk free?

You can start free. See pricing for paid plans.
