## Meet Laura

Laura is a lawyer at Clearwater Legal LLP. Today a new client comes to her office. The client's landlord wants them to
leave, and the client is worried.

## Laura's problem

The client talks fast and shares many details: dates, emails, payments. Laura needs every fact.

So she writes while the client talks. She looks at her notepad more than at the client. Later, she can't read half of
what she wrote. She tried meeting transcription once, but she got pages of text and still had to dig out the facts. Was the notice sent on the 2nd or the 12th?

## How Lynkk helps Laura in client meetings

Laura starts using Lynkk, a simple AI for lawyers. Here is what changes.

**Before the meeting, Laura asks the client.**
"May I record this, so I don't miss anything?" The client agrees. Laura records with the Lynkk Mac app on her desk.

**During the meeting, Laura just listens.**
She looks at her client. She asks better questions. She notices what the client is worried about.

**After the meeting, the note is ready.**
Lynkk writes the key facts, the documents to collect, and the next steps with names and dates.

### Here is the note Lynkk wrote for Laura (example)

**Client meeting, lease dispute · In the office · 38 min**

**Key facts:** Lease signed in March 2024. Landlord sent a notice by email on 2 September.

**Documents to collect:** The signed lease. The notice email. Rent receipts for the last 6 months.

**Next step:** Send the engagement letter. Who: Laura. When: Friday.

Laura can click any line to hear exactly what the client said.

## So, what is Lynkk?

Lynkk is AI for law firms that turns client meetings into clear notes. It records the conversation, writes it down, and
keeps it private.

With Lynkk, you get three things:
- **Clear notes:** key facts, documents to collect and next steps.
- **Every word:** the full text, so you can check exactly what was said.
- **Memory:** ask "What did the client say about the notice date?" across all meetings on the case (Pro).

## Questions people ask

**Is Lynkk a legal transcription service for court?**

No. Lynkk writes notes and text for your own work. It isn't a certified court transcript. See our guide to
[legal transcription services](/blog/legal-transcription-service).

**Is client data used to train AI?**

No. Your meetings are never used to train AI.

**Where is my data stored?**

In the region you choose: US, EU, UK, India, Singapore or Australia.

**Do I need the client's permission to record?**

Yes. Always ask first. Rules differ by country and by bar, so check your professional rules too.

**Can I record a meeting in my office?**

Yes. Use the Mac app on your desk, even without internet.
