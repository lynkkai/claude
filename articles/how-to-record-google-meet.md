# How to Record Google Meet Calls, Even When the Record Button Is Missing

*Last updated: October 1, 2026 · Steps and plan details checked against Google Meet Help on October 1, 2026*

> **Quick answer:** Here is how to record Google Meet with Google's own tool: you need an eligible Google Workspace edition or a Google One plan with 2 TB or more, and you must be the host or have permission. Click Activities (or Meeting tools), choose Recording, then Start recording. If the Record button is missing, the Lynkk Chrome extension records the call from your side with no bot, and the Lynkk meeting bot is free.

## Can you record Google Meet calls?

Yes, you can record Google Meet calls, but Google's built-in recording is limited to about 10 eligible plans and to people with permission in the meeting. Free personal Gmail accounts cannot use it, so how to record Google Meet depends first on your plan.

According to Google Meet Help, checked on October 1, 2026, recording is available on these plans:

| Plan type | Eligible plans |
|---|---|
| Google Workspace business | Business Standard, Business Plus |
| Google Workspace enterprise | Enterprise Starter, Standard, Plus and Essentials |
| Education | Education Plus, Teaching and Learning Upgrade |
| Personal | Workspace Individual, Google One with 2 TB or more |

If your account is on a plan that is not listed, the Record option will not appear, however long you look for it.

## How to record Google Meet calls step by step

If you have an eligible plan and permission, Google's own recording takes 4 clicks from a computer. This is how to record Google Meet calls with the built-in tool:

1. **Join the meeting** in Google Meet on your computer.
2. **Click Activities or Meeting tools** at the bottom right of the screen.
3. **Click Recording, then Start recording.** Everyone in the meeting is notified that it is being recorded.
4. **To stop, click Recording again, then Stop recording.** Recording also stops when everyone leaves.

The recording is saved to the meeting organizer's **Meet Recordings** folder in Google Drive, and the organizer and the person who started it get an email with the link once it is processed.

## Who is allowed to record a Google Meet?

Google lets 4 kinds of people start a recording, as long as the meeting's plan allows recording at all. You can record if you are:

- **The meeting host.**
- **In the host's organization**, when Host Management is turned off.
- **From outside the host's organization**, if the host promotes you to co-host.
- **A teacher or co-teacher** in a meeting created through Google Classroom.

If you are a guest from another company, you usually cannot record unless the host makes you a co-host.

## Why am I not allowed to record Google Meet meetings?

You are usually not allowed to record Google Meet meetings for 1 of 4 reasons: your plan, your role, your admin's settings or your device. Work through this table to find which one applies.

| Reason | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Your plan | Free Gmail and some Workspace plans have no recording | Upgrade, or use a tool that records from your side |
| Your role | Only hosts, co-hosts or same-org members can record | Ask the host to record or make you a co-host |
| Admin settings | Your Workspace admin turned recording off | Ask your admin to turn it on |
| Your device | Recording is not available everywhere | Join from a computer |

If none of these can change in time for your meeting, the next section shows how to record Google Meet from your own side.

## Is it legal to record a Google Meet call?

Recording a Google Meet call is legal in most of the US with at least 1 participant's consent, but some states require everyone on the call to agree. Federal law ([18 U.S.C. § 2511](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title18-section2511)) sets the 1 party rule, while California ([Penal Code § 632](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=632)) requires all parties to consent.

Google notifies participants when its own recording starts. When you record from your side with an extension, that notice is your job: say it out loud and in the chat. The [NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework/privacy-framework) is a useful checklist for reviewing any recording tool.

## FAQ

### Can you record Google Meet on a free Gmail account?
No. Google's built-in recording needs an eligible plan, such as Workspace Business Standard or a Google One plan with 2 TB or more, as listed on October 1, 2026. On a free account, you can record from your side with the Lynkk Chrome extension or invite the free Lynkk meeting bot.

### Where are Google Meet recordings saved?
Google saves recordings to 1 place, the meeting organizer's Meet Recordings folder in Google Drive, and emails the link to the organizer and the person who started the recording. Lynkk saves its recordings to your Lynkk account, with a Meeting Brief of 7 parts and a transcript with speaker labels.

### Why is the Google Meet record button missing?
The Record button is missing for 1 of 4 reasons: your plan does not include recording, you are not the host or a co-host, your Workspace admin turned recording off, or you joined from an unsupported device. Ask the host or admin, or record from your side with a Chrome extension.

### Can participants record a Google Meet without the host?
With Google's tool, only if they are in the host's organization and Host Management is off, or the host made them a co-host. A Chrome extension like Lynkk's records from your own browser tab on 2 channels, but you should still tell the host and everyone else that you are recording.

### Does Google Meet notify people when you record?
Yes. When someone starts Google's built-in recording, all participants are notified, as described in Google Meet Help on October 1, 2026. Extensions and bots handle this differently, so whichever tool you use, announce the recording at the start of the call and follow your state's consent rules.
