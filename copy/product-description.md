# Lynkk: Product description

Ready-to-use product copy in three lengths. Everything here comes from
`memory/lynkk-brand.md`. Nothing unconfirmed (pricing tiers, specific meeting
platforms, CRMs, language counts) is claimed, so it is safe to publish as is.

---

## Headline
```
Lynkk: one AI for every meeting, before, during and after
```

## Short (about 50 words)
For directory listings, app store subtitles, social bios and email footers.

> Lynkk is the AI meeting assistant that works the whole meeting, not just the
> recap. It brings context from past calls before you join, answers by voice
> while you talk, and within 30 seconds after turns the conversation into notes,
> action items, CRM updates and Jira tickets. Online or in person. Start free.

## Standard (about 150 words)
For website sections, marketplace listings and partner pages.

> Most AI notetakers show up after the meeting is over. Lynkk is there for all
> of it.
>
> **Before** the call, Lynkk surfaces what matters from past meetings: what was
> discussed, what's still open, and who said what. You start ahead, not cold.
>
> **During** the call, Lynkk joins and speaks when you need it. Ask for past
> context, pull up a document, or get an answer out loud without leaving the
> conversation.
>
> **After** the call, within about 30 seconds, you get structured notes,
> assigned action items, updated CRM fields and new Jira tickets, all
> searchable in a knowledge graph of every meeting you've had.
>
> Lynkk records online meetings and in-person conversations, works across
> languages, and is offline-first. Choose the region where your data is
> processed, and connect your calendar, Jira, Calendly, Cal.com, your CRM, or
> anything else via MCP.
>
> One AI for the entire meeting lifecycle. Start free at lynkk.ai.

## Long (about 300 words)
For a landing page, press kit or "About the product" section.

> ### Meet Lynkk, your AI teammate for every meeting
>
> Meetings don't end when the call does. Someone still has to write the notes,
> chase the action items, update the CRM and file the tickets, and then walk
> into the next meeting remembering what was promised in the last one. Lynkk
> takes care of all of it.
>
> Lynkk is one AI across the entire meeting lifecycle.
>
> **Start ahead, not cold.** Before every call, Lynkk pulls context from your
> past meetings: what was discussed, what's still open, and who said what.
>
> **Get answers live, by voice.** Lynkk joins your meeting and speaks when you
> need it. Ask it for context from a previous conversation, pull up a document,
> or get a quick answer without breaking the flow. Meetings are detected and
> recorded automatically.
>
> **Let the follow-up do itself.** Within about 30 seconds of hanging up, Lynkk
> delivers structured notes, assigns action items, updates CRM fields and
> creates Jira tickets. Every meeting becomes part of a searchable knowledge
> graph, so nothing said is ever lost.
>
> **Built for how teams actually meet.** Lynkk captures online meetings and
> in-person conversations alike. It is multilingual, offline-first, and
> available on the web and as a Mac desktop app, which also includes dictation
> that types wherever your cursor is.
>
> **Your data, your region.** Pick a region and every model behind Lynkk
> processes and answers from that region, so legal and compliance teams can say
> yes.
>
> **Fits your stack.** Connect your calendar, Jira, Calendly, Cal.com and your
> CRM, or connect anything else via MCP.
>
> Whether you're in sales, product, engineering, consulting, or simply running
> back-to-back meetings, Lynkk makes sure every conversation turns into work
> that gets done.
>
> **Try Lynkk free at [lynkk.ai](https://lynkk.ai).**
