# Hinglish transcription for meetings that mix Hindi and English

In plenty of Indian workplaces, nobody sticks to one language. A sentence starts in Hindi,
picks up an English product name, and finishes in Hindi again. Lynkk's Hinglish mode is built
for exactly that kind of meeting: the transcript follows the conversation the way people
actually spoke it, labels who said what, and becomes a note you can search.

**[Button]** Get started free

---

## Cards

### One sentence stays one sentence

Many tools force a call into one language and garble the rest. Hinglish mode listens for Hindi
and English together, so a sentence that switches halfway through comes out as one sentence,
not two broken halves.

### Names on every line

Every transcript is split by speaker. When the meeting bot records, the labels use the names
people have in the meeting, and the Mac app marks your own voice as You.

### On from day one

Hinglish is the setting every new account starts with. There is nothing to switch on, and it
covers the meeting bot, the Mac app, the Chrome extension and the browser alike.

---

## The kind of meeting it's built for

Picture a project check-in like this one:

> **Priya:** Okay, toh client ne kal call pe bola ki they need the deck by Friday.
>
> **Rahul:** Friday tak? Theek hai, main pricing slide update kar deta hoon, but legal review
> abhi pending hai.
>
> **Priya:** Legal ko main aaj ping kar dungi.

Three lines, two languages, and two tasks. Hinglish mode keeps each sentence whole and each
line with the right speaker, so the note can show that Rahul owns the pricing slide and Priya
is pinging legal today.

## How it works

From the recording to the note, a Hinglish meeting goes through five steps.

1. Go to Settings, then Transcription, and make sure Hinglish is selected. New accounts start
   on it.
2. Record however suits the meeting: the meeting bot, the Mac app, the Chrome extension or the
   browser.
3. Once the recording uploads, Lynkk sends it for transcription in Hinglish mode, with speaker
   labels on.
4. You get the transcript back split by speaker, along with a summary, action items and
   decisions written from it.
5. Search the note later by meaning or by the exact words people used.

## Hinglish, Hindi or auto-detect

Three settings cover Hindi speakers, and the right one depends on how your calls sound. When
people mix Hindi and English inside sentences, keep Hinglish. When a call is in Hindi from
start to finish, you can pick Hindi. When every call sticks to one language but that language
varies, auto-detect works out the main language of each recording. Your calls go beyond Hindi
and English? Lynkk also covers
[17 languages plus auto-detect](/features/multilingual-transcription), and you can switch at
any time.

---

## Speaker labels when the room switches languages

Switching languages doesn't confuse who is talking. Speaker labels work in Hinglish exactly as
they do in any other language, so every action item and decision lands with the right person.
Bot recordings take names from the meeting itself; on the Mac app your voice sits on its own
channel, labelled You.

---

## What to expect, and the limits

Clear audio gives the best Hinglish transcript. Before you rely on it, know that:

- Auto-detect only picks one main language per recording, so it isn't built for mid-sentence
  switching. For mixed Hindi and English, stay on Hinglish.
- Hinglish mode doesn't cover English mixed with Tamil, Telugu, Marathi or Bengali. Those four
  languages are in beta: pick the main language of the call.
- Indian names, places and product terms can come out spelled the way they sound. The
  dictionary in Settings helps Mac dictation, not meeting transcripts.
- Recordings can be up to 2 hours long.
- Transcription happens in the cloud. If your connection drops, the Mac app keeps recording and
  uploads once you are back online.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Hinglish transcription?

A written transcript of speech that mixes Hindi and English, often within one sentence. Lynkk's Hinglish mode listens for both languages at once, so a sentence that switches halfway through is kept whole.

### Is Hinglish the default?

Yes. Every new account starts on Hinglish. To change it, go to Settings, then Transcription; the new setting applies to recordings from then on.

### Should I pick Hinglish, Hindi or auto-detect?

Hinglish when people mix Hindi and English in the same sentence. Hindi when the whole call is in Hindi. Auto-detect when each call sticks to one language but that language changes from call to call.

### Does Hinglish work with the meeting bot?

Yes. The meeting bot records the call for you, and for in-person meetings or calls without a bot you can use the Mac app or the Chrome extension. The Hinglish setting covers all of them.

### Can it handle Tamil, Telugu, Marathi or Bengali mixed with English?

Not in the way it handles Hinglish. Mid-sentence switching is built for Hindi and English. Tamil, Telugu, Marathi and Bengali are available as single languages, in beta.

### Are speakers labelled in Hinglish transcripts?

Yes, every transcript is split by speaker. Meeting bot recordings use the names people have in the meeting, and the Mac app labels your own voice as You.

### Is Hinglish transcription free?

Yes, on every plan. Free transcribes unlimited meetings and includes your first 5 hours of AI summaries; Pro, at $15 a month, makes summaries unlimited.
