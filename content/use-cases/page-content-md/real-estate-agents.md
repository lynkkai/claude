#### How do real estate agents use AI?

Real estate agents talk to many buyers and sellers every week. Each one has a budget, a wish list and questions. Lynkk records your client calls and meetings and writes down each client's needs and your next steps, so you never mix up clients.

#### Questions about AI for real estate agents

**Does Lynkk sync with my real estate CRM?**

No. Lynkk doesn't connect to a CRM. You can share notes by link and send action items to Jira.

**Can I record a property viewing?**

Lynkk records on a Mac or in a browser. For viewings on the move, check whether that works for you before relying on it.

**Is Lynkk one of the real estate AI tools that writes listings?**

No. Lynkk records conversations and writes notes. It doesn't write listing descriptions.

**Do I need the client's permission?**

Yes. Ask before you record. Rules differ by country and state.
