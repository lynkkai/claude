Follower counts above are from third-party snapshots and may be out of date; they are directional only.

### 1.2 What this tells us

1. **People follow people, not logos.** Granola (founder), tl;dv (team personalities), Fathom and Read AI (CEOs) get reach through personal accounts. The company page is the archive, the founder is the megaphone.
2. **Creators beat owned channels for reach.** Fireflies grows through creators even though its own TikTok is tiny. Granola's TikTok presence is mostly creator testimonials.
3. **Humor works in this category.** Everyone has suffered a bad meeting. tl;dv proved meeting comedy builds a B2B audience.
4. **Speed wins attention.** Fireflies wrote about the Fathom acquisition within days. Lynkk can do the same, respectfully.
5. **Nobody owns these on social yet:** the "AI speaks up in the meeting" moment shown in a relatable way, in-person and offline meetings, data residency and consent explained simply, and "meetings that do the work" (Jira tickets, CRM updates) as a visual before/after.

### 1.3 Platform benchmarks to plan around (2026)

| Format (LinkedIn) | Avg. engagement | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| Document carousel (PDF) | ~7.0% (highest) | Guides, checklists, data |
| Native video | ~3.5-5.8% (top quartile 8-15%) | Demos, skits |
| Polls | ~3.0-5.5% | Research + engagement |
| Image | ~2.8-4.5% | Memes, quotes, stats |
| Text-only | Strong reach, feels personal | Founder stories, opinions |
| Posts with external links | ~0.8-1.8% (lowest) | Avoid. Put links in the first comment |

Median B2B company page engagement is ~5.7% (top 25%: ~12.8%). Posting at least 2-5 times a week is the threshold where LinkedIn treats you as an active creator; brands posting 2+ times a week see ~40% more brand searches than monthly posters.

## Part 2: Lynkk social strategy

### 2.1 Channels and cadence (for the 30 days)

| Channel | Role | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn company page** | Main daily post, the archive of everything | **Daily** (1 post/day, 30 posts) |
| **Founder LinkedIn** | Reach, trust, opinions, build story | 3x/week (Mon, Wed, Fri) + reshare company posts with a personal line on other days |
| **X / Twitter** | Fast takes, demos, MCP/dev crowd, newsjacking | **Daily**, 1-2 posts (adapt the day's main post, plus replies to AI/productivity threads) |
| **Short video** (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn native video) | Show the product moment; discovery | 3x/week (Tue, Thu, Sat) |
| **YouTube long-form** | Search-friendly demos that AI tools cite | 1x/week (Thursday) |
| **Reddit and communities** | Helpful answers, not posts (see audit section 7.4) | Daily: 1 genuinely helpful answer, disclosed |

Total for 30 days: 30 company posts, ~13 founder posts, ~40 X posts, ~13 short videos, 4 YouTube videos, ~30 Reddit/community answers.

### 2.2 Content pillars and mix

| Pillar | Share | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Product proof** (before, during, after) | 30% | "Ask Lynkk" clips, "30 seconds after" screen recordings |
| **Meeting smarts** (useful advice) | 20% | Prep checklists, follow-up templates, carousel guides |
| **Trust** (consent, privacy, data region) | 15% | Consent rules, "where your data lives" explainers |
| **Founder and behind the scenes** | 15% | Build log, why decisions were made, team |
| **Humor** (relatable meeting moments) | 10% | Memes, short skits, weekend posts |
| **Community** (polls, questions, reposts) | 10% | Polls, "worst meeting" stories, user shoutouts |

### 2.3 Recurring series (makes daily posting sustainable)

| Series | Day | Format | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Meeting Math** | Monday | Carousel or stat image | One number about meetings, with a source and what to do about it |
| **Ask Lynkk** | Tuesday | 20-40 sec video | Someone asks Lynkk a question out loud mid-meeting and it answers |
| **Founder Take** | Wednesday | Founder text post | An opinion or lesson, no product pitch |
| **30 Seconds After** | Thursday | Screen recording | Meeting ends, timer runs, notes + action items + Jira tickets + CRM fields appear |
| **Build Log** | Friday | Founder text + image | What shipped, what broke, what's next |
| **Meetings, Honestly** | Saturday | Meme or skit | Relatable meeting humor |
| **Sunday Question** | Sunday | Text or poll | Community question, replies become next week's content |

### 2.4 Voice and rules

- **Voice:** warm, clear, a little witty. Like a teammate who took great notes, not a sales deck.
- **Say "Lynkk, the AI meeting assistant"** in bios and the first mention in videos (name confusion with Lynk/Lynq is real).
- **Never bash competitors.** Talk about trends and choices, not "X is bad." Honesty about our own gaps builds trust.
- **Show, don't claim.** Every product claim has a clip or screenshot behind it.
- **Consent on camera.** Everyone in a demo knows they are recorded. Show the "Lynkk is taking notes" disclosure in demos.
- **Links go in the first comment** on LinkedIn. Use UTM tags (`utm_source=linkedin&utm_campaign=30day`).
- **Max 3 hashtags** on LinkedIn (e.g. #meetings #AI #productivity). On X, 0-1.
- **No em dashes or en dashes** in any copy.

### 2.5 Best posting times (start here, then adjust from data)

| LinkedIn | Tue-Thu 8-10am best; Mon and Fri 9-11am; weekends 10am-12pm |
| X | 9am and 1pm |
