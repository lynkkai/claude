# Slack Huddle Notes: How to Record, Transcribe and Summarize a Huddle on Your Mac

*Last updated: October 1, 2026 · Slack details checked against the Slack Help Center on October 1, 2026*

> **Quick answer:** A Slack huddle has 0 Record buttons. On paid Slack plans, AI huddle notes capture key takeaways, action items and a transcript in a canvas, but they do not work in huddles with external people or guests. On a Mac, the Lynkk app records any Slack huddle with no bot, writes a Meeting Brief with owned action items, and Lynkk for Slack answers questions from your notes.

## What is a Slack huddle?

A Slack huddle is a quick audio or video call that starts from a channel or direct message, with no meeting link to schedule. On the free plan, a huddle can have 2 participants; on paid plans, up to 50 people can join, with up to 25 on video at a time.

Huddles are built for short, unplanned conversations: a quick decision, a bug walkthrough, a 10 minute check-in. That is exactly why they are easy to forget: nobody books them, and nobody takes notes.

## Can you record a Slack huddle?

Not with a built-in button: as of October 1, 2026, Slack offers 0 options to save a huddle as an audio or video file. Its built-in way to keep a huddle is AI huddle notes, a written summary with a transcript.

So if you want to record Slack huddle audio, or you need notes in a huddle that Slack's AI notes do not cover, you need a separate app on your computer.

| Option | What you get | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| Slack AI huddle notes | Takeaways, action items, transcript in a canvas | Paid plans; not with external people or guests |
| Lynkk Mac app | Recording, transcript, Meeting Brief, action items | Mac with Apple silicon; 2 hour cap per recording |

## How do you turn on AI huddle notes in Slack?

On a paid Slack plan, you turn on AI huddle notes in 3 clicks on desktop. Everyone in the huddle is notified, and a banner shows until the huddle ends or someone turns notes off.

1. **In the huddle, click "AI notes: Off"** in the top left corner.
2. **Click "Start AI notes & transcription".**
3. **Click "Start notes".**

On mobile, tap the 3 dots at the bottom of the screen, then Start AI Notes, then Start Notes. To start notes automatically, open the channel's settings and turn on "Start AI notes automatically for every huddle".

## Where do you find a Slack huddle transcript?

When the huddle ends, Slack organizes the AI notes into 1 canvas and shares it in the huddle thread. The canvas lists the attendees, topics discussed, action items and an embedded Slack huddle transcript.

That works well for internal huddles on paid plans. It does not help on the free plan, in huddles with people from other companies or guests, or when you want tasks to go somewhere other than a canvas.

## How do you record a Slack huddle on a Mac?

On a Mac, you can record any Slack huddle in 3 steps with the Lynkk Mac app, including huddles where Slack's AI notes are not available. No bot joins the huddle.

1. **Install the [Lynkk Mac app](https://lynkk.ai/download)**, free to install on Apple silicon Macs with macOS 14.2 or later.
2. **Join the huddle in Slack.** About 3 seconds into a steady call, Lynkk shows a card that says "Slack call detected" with **Record** and **Not now**.
3. **Click Record.** Lynkk records both sides on 2 channels, so the transcript labels your side "You", and it stops when the huddle ends.

While recording, a slim column on the edge of your screen shows the clock, live words and a note field. Tell everyone in the huddle that you are recording, just as Slack's own banner does for AI notes.

## What do you get after a recorded huddle?

Every recorded huddle becomes a Meeting Brief with 7 parts: title, abstract, key takeaways with timestamps, topics, action items, decisions with context and open questions. Each timestamp plays the huddle from that moment.

- **[Action items](https://lynkk.ai/features/action-items)** carry an owner, a due date and a priority, and you can send them to Jira from the note, 1 issue per item.
- **Transcripts** cover 17 languages, 4 of them in beta, with auto-detect.
- **[Meeting notes](https://lynkk.ai/features/meeting-notes)** are emailed to you after each recording, and you can share a note with a link.

The same app records Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, WhatsApp, FaceTime and Webex calls, so huddles and meetings sit in 1 place. See [Lynkk pricing](https://lynkk.ai/pricing) for what Free and Pro include.

## Is it legal to record a Slack huddle?

Recording a huddle follows the same rules as any call: US federal law allows recording with 1 participant's consent, but some states require everyone to agree. Federal law ([18 U.S.C. § 2511](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title18-section2511)) sets the 1 party rule, while California ([Penal Code § 632](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=632)) requires all parties to consent.

Your company may also have its own policy on recording internal conversations, so check it before you start. The [NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework/privacy-framework) is a useful checklist for reviewing any recording tool. Lynkk keeps recordings private to your account and never trains on your conversations.

## FAQ

### Can you record a Slack huddle?
Not with Slack itself: as of October 1, 2026, huddles have no Record button. On paid plans, AI huddle notes save takeaways, action items and a transcript to a canvas. To record the audio, or to cover huddles with guests, use a Mac app such as Lynkk, which records both sides on 2 channels.

### Does Slack have a huddle transcript?
Yes, on paid plans. When AI huddle notes are on, Slack shares 1 canvas in the huddle thread after the call, with attendees, topics, action items and an embedded transcript. AI notes are not available in huddles with external people or guests, and not on the free plan.

### How many people can join a Slack huddle?
On the free plan, a Slack huddle can have 2 participants. On paid plans, up to 50 people can join, and up to 25 can have video on at the same time, according to the Slack Help Center on October 1, 2026. Lynkk records the huddle from your Mac on any Slack plan.

### Does Lynkk post huddle summaries to Slack channels?
No. Lynkk for Slack answers questions from your own Lynkk notes when you send a DM, mention @Lynkk or use /lynkk, and it does not read your channels or post summaries. Your Meeting Brief, with 7 parts including action items, stays in your Lynkk account unless you share it.

### Do people know when a Slack huddle is being recorded?
With Slack's AI notes, yes: all participants are notified and a banner shows until notes are turned off. Other tools may not show a banner, so whichever of the 2 options you use, tell everyone at the start of the huddle that you are recording or taking AI notes.
