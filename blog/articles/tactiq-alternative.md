*Disclosure: Lynkk publishes this guide and is one of the tools reviewed. Prices come from vendor pricing pages, checked 17 October 2026.*

Tactiq is a Chrome extension that captures live transcripts in Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams without putting a bot in the call. Still a good tool, too. So why switch? People look for a Tactiq alternative when they hit the free plan's 10 transcripts a month, need meetings outside the browser, or want more done with the notes. Our roundup of [AI note takers](https://lynkk.ai/blog/best-ai-note-takers) covers the wider field.

Below are 7 options, each matched to a reason for switching.

## Why do people look for a Tactiq alternative?

The most common reason is the free plan: 10 transcripts and 5 AI credits a month. Upgrading helps only partly. Pro removes the transcript cap for $8 per user per month billed yearly, yet still includes just 10 AI credits a month. Hit either limit and Tactiq keeps transcribing while AI features pause until the next month.

Other reasons we hear:

- **Browser-only capture.** Tactiq works through Chrome, so desktop meeting apps and in-person conversations need another tool.
- **Limited AI on lower plans.** Unlimited AI credits start on Team at $16.67 per user billed yearly, and 1 credit covers 1 action, such as a summary or a set of action items.
- **No follow-up automation.** Teams want tasks with owners, tickets and prep for the next call.
- **No recording.** Some teams want audio or video to go back to, not just a transcript.

## What is Tactiq, and how does it work?

Tactiq is a Chrome extension that captures live transcripts in Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams without adding a bot to the call. How? By reading the meeting's captions in your browser, saving them as a transcript, and letting you run AI prompts on the text.

That design explains its strengths and limits:

- **Strength: no bot.** Other attendees do not see a recorder join.
- **Strength: fast setup.** Install the extension and join a call.
- **Limit: browser only.** Desktop meeting apps and in-person conversations need another tool.
- **Limit: transcript, not recording.** There is no audio or video to go back to.
- **Limit: caption quality.** The transcript can only be as good as the captions it reads, which is a different trade-off from a [Zoom note taker](https://lynkk.ai/blog/zoom-note-taker) that records audio.

## Which Tactiq alternative is most accurate?

Accuracy depends more on audio quality and language than on the brand. Otter, Fireflies, Fathom and Lynkk record the meeting audio directly and run their own speech recognition on all of it, while caption readers depend on the meeting platform's captions.

To compare accuracy fairly, run 2 tools on the same 3 meetings and check 4 things: names of people and companies, numbers and dates, technical terms, and speaker labels. Those errors take the longest to fix. Hindi speakers, or a team that switches between Hindi and English? Test that case specifically, because [Hinglish support](https://lynkk.ai/features/hinglish-transcription) is rare and Google Meet's notes handle one language per meeting.

## Is Tactiq free, and is it safe to use?

Yes, Tactiq has a free plan with 10 transcripts and 5 AI credits a month, with no credit card and no time limit. On safety, its page lists SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001, Business ($29.17 billed yearly) adds SAML SSO, and Enterprise adds a curated data processing agreement and customizable notifications.

Two practical points:

1. **Browser extensions read the page.** Review the permissions Tactiq requests, and check whether your IT team allows meeting extensions.
2. **Is Tactiq legal?** Transcribing a meeting is legal in the US when you follow consent rules: [federal law](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/2511) needs one party to agree, several states need everyone, so announce the transcript at the start.

## How do you switch from Tactiq without losing transcripts?

Switching takes about 10 minutes. Export first, then remove the extension so it stops capturing.

1. **Export** the transcripts you want to keep from Tactiq (sharing and export are on every plan, including Free).
2. **Install or connect** the new tool and link your calendar if it uses a bot.
3. **Run both for 1 week** on a few meetings and compare the notes.
4. **Remove the Tactiq extension** from Chrome, or pause automatic transcription in its settings (a Pro feature).
5. **Tell your team** which tool now captures meetings, so nobody is surprised, and agree the habit in your next [team standup](https://lynkk.ai/use-cases/team-standups).

## FAQ

### What is the best alternative to Tactiq?

Lynkk is the best Tactiq alternative if you want follow-up work done. Fathom wins if you want free unlimited recording, and Notta if you need cheap transcription minutes and file uploads. Fireflies suits CRM-driven teams, while Google Meet's built-in notes suit English-only Workspace teams.

### Is Tactiq free?

Yes, with no credit card and no time limit: 10 transcripts and 5 AI credits a month. Two useful details from Tactiq's FAQ: 1 credit equals 1 AI action, and when you run out, transcription carries on while AI pauses until the reset. Pro at $8 lifts the transcript cap.

### Is Tactiq safe to use?

Tactiq lists SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR and HIPAA compliance, and says OpenAI does not train on data sent through its enterprise API. Only you see the live transcript, though you can notify the room. Review the extension permissions with IT before a company-wide install.

### Who are Tactiq's main competitors?

Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, Fathom, Notta, Read AI, Google Meet's built-in notes and Lynkk. Entry prices cluster tightly: $8 Tactiq Pro, $8.17 Notta Pro, $8.33 Otter Pro, $10 Fireflies Pro, all billed yearly. The real split is capture method and what happens after the call.

### Is there a Tactiq alternative for Zoom and Microsoft Teams?

Yes. Lynkk, Fathom, Fireflies, Otter and Read AI all cover Zoom and Microsoft Teams as well as Google Meet. One operational difference: a bot joins from your calendar and needs admitting on some platforms, while Tactiq never appears in the participant list because it reads captions locally.

### Does any Tactiq alternative support Hindi?

Yes. Lynkk covers Hindi and adds a Hinglish setting, with Tamil, Telugu, Marathi and Bengali in beta. Google Meet's Take notes for me lists 8 languages, none of them Hindi, and needs the meeting spoken in the chosen one. Test any tool on a real 20-minute call first.
