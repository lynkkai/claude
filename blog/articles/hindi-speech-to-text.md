Hindi speech to text is a type of software that turns spoken Hindi into written text, and it is now free on all 4 of the platforms below. Why bother? Speaking beats pecking out Devanagari on a cramped keyboard, and the same trick handles WhatsApp messages, emails, documents and notes alike.

Below, you'll find how to switch on Hindi voice typing on every common device, what suits long recordings and meetings, and how to sharpen accuracy when results come out messy.

## What is Hindi speech to text?

Hindi speech to text converts spoken Hindi into written text as you speak, usually in Devanagari script, and it comes in 2 forms. Other names: Hindi voice typing, Hindi dictation. Point that same technology at recordings rather than live speech and it becomes Hindi transcription.

Two ways to use it:

| Mode | Best for | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Live voice typing | Messages, emails, short notes | Gboard, iPhone keyboard, Google Docs |
| Recorded or meeting capture | Interviews, lectures, meetings | AI transcription and meeting tools |

## How do you convert Hindi speech to text on Android?

Knowing how to convert Hindi speech to text on Android takes 4 steps through Gboard, Google's keyboard:

1. Open **Gboard settings**, then **Languages**, and add **Hindi**.
2. Open any app with a text box, such as WhatsApp or Gmail.
3. Switch the keyboard to Hindi with the globe or language key.
4. Tap the **microphone** and speak in Hindi.

Gboard's output lands in Devanagari. Prefer Roman-script Hinglish? Add the Hinglish or Hindi (Latin) keyboard layout and dictate there instead. Google warns that talk-to-text does not work in every language it offers as a keyboard, so test yours before relying on it.

## How do you use Hindi voice typing on iPhone?

On iPhone, Hindi voice typing rides on keyboard dictation, which Apple sets up in 3 steps ([Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/dictate-text-iph2c0651d2/ios)):

1. Open **Settings**, then tap **General**, then **Keyboard**.
2. Turn on **Enable Dictation**, and tap Enable Dictation again if a prompt appears.
3. In any app, switch to the Hindi keyboard and tap the **microphone** key.

Dictation follows whichever keyboard is active, which is Apple's own rule: use a keyboard for another language and you dictate in that language. Apple lists Hindi (India) among its dictation languages, and dictation is processed on the iPhone itself, so no internet connection is needed.

## How do you get Hindi speech to text online for free on a computer?

The easiest free Hindi speech to text online is Google Docs voice typing, which lists Hindi and runs in the latest versions of 3 browsers: Chrome, Edge and Safari ([Google Docs Help](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/4492226)). Windows, Mac and Chromebook all qualify:

1. Open a new Google Doc in your browser.
2. Click **Tools**, then **Voice typing**.
3. Choose **Hindi** from the language list above the mic.
4. Click the microphone and speak.

No cost, no time limit. Two catches: the Doc must stay open throughout, and voice commands work only in English, with both account and document language set to English, so Hindi punctuation goes in by hand. An administrator can also switch the feature off for a whole organisation.

## Can you use Hindi speech to text on Windows 10 and Mac?

Yes. Microsoft lists Hindi among the languages that support voice typing in Windows 11, which you start with Windows key + H in any text box; it needs an internet connection and a working microphone ([Microsoft Support](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/use-voice-typing-to-talk-instead-of-type-on-your-pc-fec94565-c4bd-329d-e59a-af033fa5689f)). On Windows 10, check Language & region for Hindi first, or use Google Docs voice typing in Chrome instead.

Macs ship with dictation, and Apple lists Hindi (India) among its macOS dictation languages. Open **System Settings**, then **Keyboard**, and turn on **Dictation**. Want more Mac options? ## How do you make Hindi voice typing more accurate?

Most Hindi speech to text errors come from 5 causes, and all 5 are fixable:

1. **Wrong keyboard language.** Switch to Hindi before tapping the mic.
2. **Noise.** Move away from fans, traffic and TV, or use earphones with a mic.
3. **Speed.** Speak at a normal pace with short pauses between sentences.
4. **Punctuation.** Spoken punctuation support varies by keyboard, so plan to add full stops and commas by hand afterward.
5. **Names and English words.** Check and fix them after dictating, since they are the most common errors.

In meetings, keep the device within 1 metre of the speakers and ask people not to talk over each other during decisions. [Meeting notes](https://lynkk.ai/features/meeting-notes) are only as good as the audio behind them.

## FAQ

### How can I convert Hindi speech to text for free?

Use Gboard voice typing on Android, keyboard dictation on iPhone, or Google Docs voice typing with Hindi selected. All 3 cost nothing. One snag: Google Docs voice commands understand only English, and they need your account and document language set to English, so add Hindi punctuation by hand.

### Which is the best app for Hindi speech to text?

Gboard on Android or the iPhone keyboard for messages, Google Docs voice typing for documents, and Lynkk for Hindi or Hinglish meetings, which it records and summarises automatically. Recorded files need an AI transcription tool that supports Hindi. Test it on 5 minutes of your own audio before relying on it.

### Can I do Hindi voice typing on a laptop?

Yes, 3 ways. Google Docs voice typing works in Chrome, Edge or Safari on any laptop. Windows 11 adds its own voice typing with Windows key + H, and macOS has Dictation in Keyboard settings. Windows voice typing needs internet; Apple's dictation runs on the device.

### Does Hindi speech to text work offline?

Partly. Apple says iPhone dictation is processed on the device in many languages with no internet connection, while Microsoft says Windows voice typing needs you to be connected. Google Docs voice typing also runs through the browser's online service, so plan for connectivity on a laptop.

### Can speech to text write Hindi in English letters?

Yes. Use a Hinglish or Hindi (Latin) keyboard layout on your phone, and dictation will write Hindi words in Roman script. Handy for WhatsApp messages and informal notes. Formal documents are another story: Devanagari output is usually easier for other Hindi readers to scan.
