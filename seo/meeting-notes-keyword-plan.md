## 5. On-page recommendations

### URL
`/features/meeting-notes` works. If changing is easy, `/features/ai-meeting-notes` matches the
primary keyword the way Notion's does. The page has no rankings yet, so a change (with a 301)
costs nothing now and gets harder later.

### Title tag (56 chars)
```
AI Meeting Notes with Action Items in 30 Seconds | Lynkk
```
Alternate (51 chars):
```
AI Meeting Notes, Summaries & Action Items | Lynkk
```

### Meta description (146 chars)
```
Lynkk's AI meeting note taker turns online and in-person meetings into notes, summaries, and action items in 30 seconds. Multilingual. Start free.
```

### H1
```
AI meeting notes, summaries and action items, ready in 30 seconds
```

### Suggested H2 structure (keyword in brackets)
1. **How Lynkk's AI meeting note taker works** [meeting notes taker, ai meeting note taker]
   Record, transcribe, structure: a 3-step visual.
2. **Meeting notes with action items, assigned automatically** [meeting notes with action items]
   Show owners, due dates, and the push to Jira.
3. **An AI meeting summary of every call: decisions, next steps, who said what** [ai meeting summary, meeting summary]
4. **Works for online and in-person meetings** [ai note taker for in person meetings]
   This is the strongest differentiator vs. bot-only tools. Make it a full section.
5. **Meeting notes for your whole team** [team ai meeting notes]
   Shared, searchable notes across every meeting (knowledge graph).
6. **Send notes where the work happens** CRM fields, Jira tickets, Calendar, Calendly, Cal.com, MCP.
7. **Multilingual and private by design** languages, data residency by region.
8. **See a real example** an actual sample of Lynkk's notes output (screenshot plus real HTML text, so Google can read it).
9. **Lynkk vs. other AI meeting notes apps** [ai meeting notes app, meeting notes taking app]
   Short table: before/during/after coverage, in-person, voice assistant, data residency.
10. **FAQ** (see below)

### FAQ (targets People Also Ask; add FAQPage schema)
- What is an AI meeting note taker?
- How do AI meeting notes work?
- Can Lynkk take notes for in-person meetings?
- Does Lynkk work with Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams? *(confirm platforms first)*
- What's the difference between meeting notes and meeting minutes? [meeting notes vs minutes]
- Can AI write meeting minutes? [meeting minutes ai, ai for meeting minutes]
- How accurate are AI meeting notes?
- Is Lynkk free?
- Where is my meeting data stored?

### Other on-page items
- **Length:** aim for 1,200-1,800 words of real, crawlable text (not text baked into images).
- **Schema:** `SoftwareApplication` (name, category, offers: free) + `FAQPage`.
- **Images:** descriptive alt text, e.g. "Lynkk AI meeting notes with action items and summary".
- **Internal links:** link to this page from the homepage, /features/meeting-bot, and every
  blog post below, using anchors like "AI meeting notes" and "meeting notes with action items".
- **Proof points** seen in search snippets for lynkk.ai (confirm before using):
  97% transcription accuracy, 15+ languages with code-switching, unlimited free recording
  and transcription. Specific numbers like these help both conversions and AI Overview citations.
