## Deliberate departures from brag defaults

- **Length 50s, vertical 1080x1920**, as in the approved script (brag default is 15-25s landscape).
- **Voiceover on** (Kokoro): female `af_heart` main cut, male `am_fenrir` alternate, plus a music-only cut.

## Rubric

- **What is it?** Lynkk remembers every customer conversation and turns it into follow-ups, CRM updates and your next best move.
- **Who for?** Salespeople with back-to-back calls.
- **Sets it apart:** it keeps the promises made on the call and drafts the follow-up from what was actually said.
- **Hook:** "Wait!" slams over a Google Meet call where Priya asks "So, what did you promise us last time?"
- **Real UI shown:** the lynkk.ai hero call summary ("Corvane Logistics · Discovery call", key decision "Start with a 15-person pilot")
  and the lynkk.ai Ask Lynkk answer to "Draft a follow-up to Priya from today's call", both captured from lynkk.ai at 2x, unedited.
- **Tone:** `polished`; tense night act, relieved day act.
- **Caption:** `share-copy.txt`.

## Illustrative data

lynkk.ai's demo account: Corvane Logistics, Priya Shah, Jamie Davis. Problem-act UI (tabs, chat, calendar, draft, email) is illustrative story UI.
The toasts reuse lynkk.ai copy ("Nothing slips through. 3 action items · 2 commitments", "Follow-up, ready when you ask. Grounded in what was actually said.").

## Music and grid

120 BPM, beat 0.5s, bar 2s from frame 0; every cut is a whole second, so on the beat. Animation events snapped to 8ths.
Act 1 A minor (clock on quarters, bass on 8ths from 3s, heartbeat from the promise at 16s, sparse evening, accelerating ticks and riser,
1/16 silence before 34s). Act 2 C major I-V-vi-IV, plucks, four-on-the-floor from 36s.
SFX in key on the grid: whoosh into each cut, pops on notes/messages/meetings/reveals/toasts, typing clicks, a ping for Priya's email.
