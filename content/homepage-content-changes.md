## No changes

- Navigation, all buttons, the "Unlimited recording · No credit card required." badge, and every image or product screenshot.
- "Connected Memory" section and its three tabs (connected memory, cross-meeting context, actionable intelligence).
- "Find the answer / See the context / Keep moving" cards and "Capture commitments / Keep ownership clear / Push work forward" cards.
- The Mac feature cards ("It notices the call", "Hold a key and talk, anywhere", "It keeps itself current") and the Simple Pricing block.
- The existing FAQ questions (the two new ones go above them).
- Final CTA H2 and subline.

## Why these spots

- The **H1, hero paragraph and title tag** carry the most weight with Google. "AI note taker" (27,100/mo) and "AI meeting assistant" (4,400/mo) are missing from all three today.
- Each **section paragraph** gets one supporting keyword, placed at the start of an existing sentence so the tone stays the same.
- **"AI meeting notes"** (1,000/mo) is deliberately not added to the homepage; /features/meeting-notes should own it.
- The **FAQ additions** mirror competitors that rank for "what is [brand]" (what is otter 1,900/mo, what is fireflies 1,000/mo).

## Check before publishing

- **Change 7** says Lynkk answers live in the meeting. Brand memory says Lynkk "joins your meeting and speaks when you need it"; confirm this is live in the product today, otherwise drop the second sentence.
- **Change 6** names Zoom, Meet and Teams. The live Mac section already lists them ("Zoom, Teams, Meet, Slack huddles, WhatsApp"), so this matches the site.
- H1 length: the new H1 is 1 character longer than the current one (64 vs 63), so the hero should keep its three-line layout.
