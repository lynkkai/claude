# Lynkk: Free blog submission sites

Free platforms where the Lynkk team can publish articles about meetings, productivity,
sales, and AI note taking. Compiled 2026-10-06. DA figures are approximate (Moz).

**Reality check:** most of these links are nofollow. Treat them as distribution and brand
reach, not a backlink shortcut. Best practice: publish on lynkk.ai/blog first, then
republish here with a canonical link back to the original where the platform supports it.

## Posting checklist

- [ ] Publish the original on lynkk.ai first, then republish with a canonical link
- [ ] Spell it **Lynkk** everywhere, link to https://lynkk.ai/
- [ ] Use the bio line: "Lynkk is one AI for every meeting: before, during and after. Start free at lynkk.ai"
- [ ] One link to lynkk.ai per article in the body, plus the author bio
- [ ] Add UTM tags per platform (e.g. `?utm_source=medium&utm_medium=article`)
- [ ] No em dashes or en dashes in any copy (house style)
- [ ] Track each URL, date, and link type in a sheet
