# Lynkk.ai: Keyword Plan

**Prepared:** 2026-11-03
**Companion to:** `seo/competitor-keyword-analysis.md`
**Data source:** Ubersuggest, US market (Google loc 2840), October 2026 snapshot
**Site state:** 115 live URLs (from lynkk.ai/sitemap.xml), 3 ranking keywords, DA 1, 5 referring domains

---

## 1. The finding that shapes this plan

The previous analysis assumed Lynkk would need to build comparison pages, integration pages and vertical landing pages. **It already has them.** The sitemap shows 115 live URLs:

- 12 comparison pages (`/compare/otter`, `/compare/fireflies`, `/compare/granola`, `/compare/fathom`, `/compare/read-ai`, `/compare/notta`, `/compare/circleback`, `/compare/meetgeek`, `/compare/plaud`, `/compare/gong`, `/compare/avoma`, `/compare/clari-copilot`)
- 10 integration pages (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Attio, Zoho, Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Google Calendar, Jira)
- 10 feature pages, 10 use-case pages
- Roughly 55 blog posts, including five competitor pricing posts and two "best note taker" roundups

So the diagnosis is not a content gap. **It is 115 pages competing for 3 keywords on the strength of 5 referring domains.** The content layer is built; nothing is ranking because there is no authority behind it, and possibly because some of it is not indexed.

That changes the plan in three ways:

1. **This is a keyword assignment plan, not a content production plan.** Most work below is mapping a target keyword to a page that already exists, then optimizing it: 44 keyword assignments across 42 existing URLs, against 14 new pages over 12 months.
2. **Link acquisition is the gating factor, not content volume.** Publishing page 116 before fixing the link profile will not move anything. Section 7 covers this.
3. **Cannibalization is now a real problem.** Six pages target Zoom. Four target otter.ai. Four target general transcription. Section 6 resolves these.

---

## 2. How to read this plan

Every page gets **one primary keyword** (the ranking target, used in title, H1, URL and opening paragraph) and **secondary keywords** (supporting variants covered in body copy and H2s). One keyword is never assigned to two pages.

Waves are assigned by Search Difficulty against what a DA 1 site can realistically win:

| Wave | Timing | SD range | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wave 1** | Months 1 to 3 | SD 6 to 32 | Winnable now, with current authority |
| **Wave 2** | Months 3 to 6 | SD 32 to 40 | Needs 20 to 50 referring domains |
| **Wave 3** | Months 6 to 12 | SD 40 to 55 | Needs DA 15 to 25 and cluster depth |
| **Wave 4** | Months 12 to 24 | SD 48 to 60 | Needs DA 25 to 40 |

Action codes: **OPT** = optimize existing page, **NEW** = create page, **MERGE** = consolidate into another URL, **HOLD** = leave as is, revisit later.

## 7. The gating factor: this plan does not work without links

115 pages and 5 referring domains is the core problem. Keyword assignment alone will not fix it. Three workstreams run in parallel with Wave 1:

**7.1 Indexation audit (week 1).** Verify in Google Search Console how many of the 115 URLs are actually indexed. A DA 1 site with 115 pages and 3 ranking keywords may have a large share excluded as "crawled, not indexed" or "discovered, not indexed". If so, that is the first bottleneck and no amount of on-page work bypasses it. Check: XML sitemap submitted, no stray `noindex`, internal links to every page from somewhere crawlable, and a flat click depth from the homepage.

**7.2 Internal linking (weeks 1 to 4).** With almost no external authority, internal links are the main ranking signal available. Build hub-and-spoke structure: `/features/action-items` links to the action-items blog cluster; `/blog/zoom-note-taker` links to the Zoom how-to and the integration page; every comparison page links to `/pricing` and `/compare`. Target a minimum of 3 internal links into every Wave 1 page.

**7.3 Link acquisition targets.**

| Milestone | Referring domains | Primary tactic |
|---|---|---|
| Now | 5 | baseline |
| Month 3 | 20 to 30 | Directory and tool-listing submissions (the `launch/` folder already holds Product Hunt copy), founder commentary, podcast appearances |
| Month 6 | 50 to 70 | Inclusion in third-party "best AI note taker" listicles, which is higher leverage than publishing another one |
| Month 12 | 120 to 180 | Free tools from Wave 3, original data studies, integration partner listings (Jira, HubSpot, Zoom marketplaces) |

Getting Lynkk **into** existing listicles outperforms ranking its own. Those pages already hold the rankings for "best ai note taker" (1,900) and the whole commercial cluster.

## 10. Caveats

- Volumes, SD and CPC are US (loc 2840), October 2026. India volumes run roughly 7 to 15 percent of US on the same terms with lower difficulty and far lower CPC, so a parallel India track would use the same page map at lower cost.
- "granola alternatives" (320, SD 32) is contaminated by the breakfast-food meaning; Ubersuggest's own suggestion set includes "granola alternatives for yogurt". Target "granola ai note taker" (1,300, SD 30) instead, and check the live SERP before committing.
- Search Difficulty is a model, not a guarantee. Verify the live SERP for any keyword before building a page against it, particularly the SD 6 to 16 outliers, which are the most likely to be mismeasured.
- The 115-URL inventory comes from the sitemap, which lists intended pages. The indexation audit in section 7.1 will confirm how many Google actually holds.
- Page-level traffic data was unavailable for 113 of the 115 URLs because they carry no measurable rankings yet. Wave assignments are therefore based on keyword difficulty and page intent, not on existing page performance.
