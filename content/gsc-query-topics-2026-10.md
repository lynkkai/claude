### Guardrails so these do not compete with existing pages

- **#1** supports /features/meeting-bot. The blog targets the how-to queries ("how to send a meeting bot to a meeting",
  "join zoom call bot", "add a bot to a Zoom meeting"). The feature page keeps "meeting bot" and "zoom meeting bot".
  Skip "how to build a meeting bot" (developer intent; Lynkk is not an API).
- **#2** needs one change on a live page: remove "ai note taker for zoom" from the meta keywords of /blog/best-ai-note-takers
  so the new post owns it (the same split as the existing Teams post).
- **#3** must not target "multilingual meeting transcription"; /features/multilingual-transcription already ranks 13 for it.
- **#4** links to /blog/founders-meeting-notes ("Can AI prepare me for my next meeting?") and does not target "meeting notes".
- **#6** is a TRUTHS.md rewrite of the superseded draft `blog/action-items-examples-and-template.md`. It must not target
  "meeting action items" or "action item tracker ai" (/features/action-items owns them) and must say Jira send is manual.
- **#7** must not target "team meeting notes" (/features/workspaces owns it) and must not claim SSO, team-wide memory or more than 10 seats.
- **#2 and #8 name other products.** Your live listicles already do this, but TRUTHS.md says the team must supply sourced
  facts for named comparisons. Needs your decision.
