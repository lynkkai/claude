## What should you include in meeting notes?

Not sure what to include in meeting notes? Good ones have 5 sections, and if you keep only these, your notes will still be useful a month later:

1. **Meeting details:** title, date, time, location or link.
2. **Attendees:** who was there, and who was absent.
3. **Key points:** 1 to 3 lines per agenda topic.
4. **Decisions:** one clear sentence each.
5. **Action items:** task, owner and due date.

Optional sections include open questions, parking lot items and links to documents. ## What does an action-focused meeting notes template look like?

This layout has 4 blocks and puts actions in the first one, which suits project syncs and standups where the point is moving work along.


Client call notes need 4 extra fields: what you committed to, what the client committed to, risks raised, and whether the recap went out. Promises made on a call become expectations.

## How do you use the Cornell method for meeting notes?

The Cornell method splits the page into 3 parts: a cue column, a notes column and a summary, which suits training sessions and long discussions. [Cornell's Learning Strategies Center](https://lsc.cornell.edu/how-to-study/taking-notes/cornell-note-taking-system/) credits the system to Professor Walter Pauk and notes that research finds handwriting notes more effective than typing them.

| Cues and questions | Notes |
|---|---|
| [Key question or keyword] | [Detailed notes for that point] |
| [Key question or keyword] | [Detailed notes for that point] |
| **Summary** | [3 to 4 sentences on the main takeaways] |

Write notes on the right during the meeting, add cues on the left right after, and finish with the summary.

## How many meeting notes templates does a team need?

Most teams need 3 meeting notes templates at most: one for recurring team meetings, one for one-on-ones and one for client or external calls. Add more? People stop using any of them.

Store them in 1 shared place, such as a team folder or Notion database, and name them clearly, for example "TEMPLATE: Weekly sync notes". Every quarter, prune. Delete sections nobody fills in, because a free meeting notes template only helps if it stays short enough to use in a live meeting.

## How do you structure meeting notes so people read them?

Structure meeting notes with the most important information at the top: decisions and action items first, discussion below. Readers skim the first few lines of anything you send them, so put what they need in those lines. That is how to structure meeting notes people actually finish.

Three formatting rules help:

1. **Bold the owners' names** in action items so people find their tasks.
2. **Use one line per decision**, not paragraphs.
3. **Link documents** instead of summarizing them.

## FAQ

### Is there a free meeting notes template?

Yes, all 5 in this guide are free to copy. Microsoft and Notion both publish free ones too, though read the fine print: Notion's meetings category mixes free templates with paid ones, so the Brainstorm and Standup Meeting Notes pages are free while others in the same list are not.

### What is the best format for meeting notes?

Details and attendees first, then key points, decisions, and action items with owners and due dates. One choice people get wrong: taking the notes on a laptop. Cornell's Learning Strategies Center points to research finding that handwriting beats typing for recall, so consider writing and typing up the 5 sections afterwards.

### How do I make a meeting notes template in Google Docs?

Type "@" in a Doc and choose the meeting notes building block, or paste a template from above. The block fills in 3 things from the Calendar event: title, date and guest list. Google also lets you save your own wording as a custom building block, so your 5 headings are 2 keystrokes away.

### Does Notion have a free meeting notes template?

Yes. Notion's meetings category lists free pages such as Brainstorm Meeting Notes and Standup Meeting Notes, and any of the 5 templates here pastes in. Worth doing once: turn the page into a database template, because a database of notes can be filtered by project, person or date later.

### What is the difference between meeting notes and minutes?

Notes are informal records for your team. Minutes are the official record of attendance, decisions and votes, approved at the next meeting, and a [meeting minutes sample](https://lynkk.ai/blog/meeting-minutes-sample) shows the difference. California HOA boards must go further: the minutes, or a summary, have to be available to members within 30 days of the meeting.

### How soon should meeting notes be shared?

Share them the same day, ideally within 1 hour, while everyone still remembers the context. One habit helps more than speed: send the 3 or 4 action items in the body of the message, not as an attachment, so the people who owe something see it without opening a file.
