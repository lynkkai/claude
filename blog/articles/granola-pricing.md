# Granola AI Pricing in 2026: What US Users Actually Pay

*By [Samarth Wagh](https://lynkk.ai/blog/authors/samarth-wagh), Co-founder, Lynkk.ai · Last updated 17 October 2026 · Pricing checked 17 October 2026*

> **Quick answer:** Granola pricing in 2026 has 3 tiers. **Basic** is free but keeps only 30 days of meeting history. **Business** costs **$14 per user per month** and includes unlimited history, advanced AI models and integrations. **Enterprise** costs **$35 per user per month** and adds SSO, admin controls and org-wide retention rules.

*Disclosure: Lynkk publishes this guide and makes a competing AI meeting assistant. All Granola prices come from [granola.ai/pricing](https://www.granola.ai/pricing), checked 17 October 2026.*

Granola pricing looks simple on the surface: free, $14 or $35. The details matter more. Its free tier is generous on features and strict on history, and the jump to Business is mostly about keeping your notes and wiring them into the other tools your team already uses every day.

This guide explains what each Granola plan includes, what you actually pay as a person or a team, and when a different tool is cheaper for the same job.

## How much does Granola cost in 2026?

Granola pricing in 2026 is $0 on Basic, $14 per user per month on Business and $35 per user per month on Enterprise. Basic and Business are billed monthly; according to the help center, annual billing is available on Enterprise contracts only.

| Plan | Price per user per month | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | $0 | Individuals trying Granola |
| Business | $14 | Professionals and teams who keep notes |
| Enterprise | $35 | Companies that need SSO and admin control |

No paid trial is listed. The company's own pitch is to "try Granola for a few meetings" on the free plan first.

## Is Granola free?

Yes, there is a free Basic plan, and it is fairly complete. You get AI meeting notes, AI chat within and across meetings, shared folders, custom note templates and multi-language support. The catch? History. Basic keeps only 30 days of meetings.

What the Basic plan includes:

- AI meeting notes from your device audio, with no bot joining the call
- Chat with your notes, limited to Granola's "Auto" model choice
- Shared folders and custom templates
- An option to opt out of model training at any time

Older reviews describe a 25-note limit on the free plan. As of October 2026, the limit is 30 days of meeting history. Granola's help center says older notes are stored, not deleted, but you cannot open them in the app until you upgrade. You can export titles and summaries as a CSV from Settings.

## What do you get on Granola Business for $14?

Business at $14 per user per month removes the 30-day limit and adds 3 groups of features: unlimited notes and history, advanced AI thinking models, and integrations.

- **Unlimited notes and history:** every meeting stays searchable.
- **Advanced AI models:** pick stronger models for chat instead of "Auto".
- **Integrations:** Attio, Notion, HubSpot, Affinity and Zapier.
- **Developer access:** an MCP integration and API access.
- **Admin basics:** centralized billing and user management.

For most individuals and small teams, this is the tier that turns Granola from a 30-day scratchpad into a long-term notebook you can search months later, when a client asks what you agreed in the spring.

## When do you need Granola Enterprise at $35?

You need Enterprise ($35 per user per month) when IT requires single sign-on, org-wide controls, or retention rules. It adds everything in Business plus:

- Enterprise-grade security and admin controls
- Single sign-on (SSO)
- Priority support and usage analytics
- Org-wide auto-deletion periods
- Admin controls for sharing and API access
- Team-wide opt-out of model training

At $35 a seat, Enterprise is 2.5 times the Business price. Overkill for most. Teams under 50 people rarely need it unless a security review demands SSO.

## What does Granola cost for a team?

Scale it up and a 10-person team pays $1,680 a year on Business and $4,200 a year on Enterprise. Here is how the cost grows with headcount.

| Team size | Business ($14) per year | Enterprise ($35) per year |
|---|---|---|
| 1 user | $168 | $420 |
| 5 users | $840 | $2,100 |
| 10 users | $1,680 | $4,200 |
| 25 users | $4,200 | $10,500 |

Remember that the app only captures meetings when someone runs it. If only 3 of 10 people take notes, you may need fewer seats than headcount, which can cut the bill by more than half.

## Does Granola offer student or nonprofit pricing?

Yes. Granola's help center lists 2 programs that its pricing page does not show: accepted students and startups get 12 months of Business free, and registered nonprofits get 50% off Business, which works out to $7 per user per month.

- **Students:** apply with an academic email. The student pack cannot move to a personal email later.
- **Startups:** apply and, once accepted, complete checkout to activate the 12 months.
- **Nonprofits:** must be registered to qualify for the 50% discount.

For comparison, Otter offers student pricing for people with a .edu email, at $79.99 a year for Pro, and Read AI has a separate education pricing page.

## How do you upgrade, downgrade or cancel Granola?

Upgrading takes about 2 minutes: open Settings, then Billing, and click Upgrade on the Business or Enterprise card. Payment runs through Stripe. Your workspace upgrades as soon as it clears.

Downgrading works the other way. Cancel or change plan in the Stripe portal, and your paid plan runs until the end of the current billing period before dropping to Basic. Two details catch people out:

1. **Seats follow members.** To pay for fewer seats, remove members or move them to another workspace.
2. **Removed members' cards can stay attached.** Granola's help center warns that removing a member does not remove their payment method, so check who is being charged.

If your workspace admin manages billing, you will see a billing contact email instead of an Upgrade button.

## What happens to your notes if you stop paying for Granola?

Dropping to Basic does not delete your notes. Older ones (beyond 30 days) are stored but hidden in the app until you upgrade again. Safe fallback, then, but a frustrating one if you rely on old meetings.

Before you downgrade, export what you need. A CSV of note titles and summaries can be generated from your profile settings. For full notes, copy the important ones into your docs or CRM first.

## Is Granola worth paying for?

Under current Granola pricing, the tool is worth $14 a month if you take notes in most of your meetings, want no bot on the call, and need notes in Notion, HubSpot or Attio. It is less worth it if you need video playback, Jira tickets, or a tool that runs without you remembering to open it.

Ask yourself 3 questions before upgrading:

1. **Do I go back to notes older than 30 days?** If not, Basic may be enough.
2. **Where does my follow-up work live?** Granola's integrations cover CRMs and Notion, not Jira.
3. **Do I meet in person?** Granola's mobile apps cover this, but in-person notes are separate from desktop capture.

## FAQ

### How much is Granola per month?

Business costs $14 per user per month and Enterprise costs $35 per user per month. The Basic plan is free. For a 10-person team, that comes to $1,680 a year on Business and $4,200 on Enterprise. Students and accepted startups can get 12 months of Business free, and nonprofits get 50% off.

### Is Granola free forever?

Yes. Granola Basic is free with no time limit, but it keeps only 30 days of meeting history. To keep older notes, you need Business at $14 per user per month. Notes older than 30 days are stored, not deleted, but you cannot open them until you upgrade. Export titles and summaries as a CSV before relying on Basic alone.

### Does Granola have annual pricing?

Only on Enterprise. Granola's help center says Basic and Business are billed monthly and annual billing is available on Enterprise contracts only. Business is billed per user each month, so you can add or remove seats as your team changes without a yearly commitment.

### What is the difference between Granola Business and Enterprise?

Business ($14) adds unlimited history, advanced AI models and integrations. Enterprise ($35) adds SSO, admin controls, org-wide auto-deletion, usage analytics and priority support. Most teams under 50 people only need Business unless their security review requires single sign-on, which is the main reason to pay $35.

### Does Granola charge per meeting or per minute?

No. Billing is per user per month, and none of the plans list a minute or meeting cap; on the free plan the limit is 30 days of history. Heavy users get predictable costs as a result. Long meetings and many meetings cost the same as light use on the same plan.

### Is there a cheaper alternative to Granola?

Fireflies Pro costs $10 per seat billed yearly and Fathom's free plan records unlimited calls. Lynkk costs $15 a month and adds prep, live answers and Jira tickets. Which option is cheapest depends on your bottleneck. If it is history, Fathom or Lynkk keep notes longer; if it is privacy, Jamie and Meetily also skip the bot.
