## What is a free AI note taker?

A free AI note taker is an app that records a meeting, turns speech into text and writes a summary with action items, all on a $0 plan. Every free plan has a limit: minutes per month, meetings per month, length per meeting or how long notes are kept.

The limit matters more than the price tag. A plan with 300 minutes a month covers about 10 half-hour calls, while a plan capped at 5 meetings runs out in a single busy day.

2. **You need older notes.** A 30-day history limit hides last quarter's decisions.

## How do you get the most out of a free AI note taker?

You can stretch a free AI note taker plan 2-3 times further with a few habits. These 5 tips work on every plan in this list.

1. **Record only the meetings that matter.** Skip quick check-ins so minutes last until the end of the month.
2. **Name your meetings clearly.** Good titles make notes easy to find later, especially on plans with short history.
3. **Export key notes.** Copy decisions into your project tool before a 30-day history limit hides them.
4. **Use a decent microphone.** Clear audio means fewer transcript errors to fix by hand.
5. **Pick one tool for every meeting.** Splitting calls across 3 free apps scatters your notes. Lynkk covers Zoom, Teams, Google Meet and in-person meetings in 1 account.
