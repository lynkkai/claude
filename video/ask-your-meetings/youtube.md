## Description

Ever had the answer, said in a meeting weeks ago, and nobody can find it?

Ask Your Meetings is the conversational brain of Lynkk. Lynkk captures every meeting, online or in person, turns it into structured notes and action items, and connects everything into one searchable knowledge graph. Then you just ask, in plain language:

- What did Acme say about pricing last quarter?
- Which decisions are still open from the launch review?
- What did I promise Priya on Tuesday?

You get one clear answer, drawn from every call.

Lynkk also works before and during your meetings. Before a call, it shows what was discussed, what's still open, and who said what. During the call, it can join and answer out loud: ask for past context, pull up a document, and get the answer live.

Built for sales teams, product and engineering, founders and managers, new hires, global teams, and teams in regulated industries who need to choose where their data is processed.
