# How to Transcribe Zoom Meetings and Get a Zoom Transcript on Any Zoom Plan

*Last updated: September 30, 2026 · Checked against Zoom's help center on September 30, 2026*

> **Quick answer:** You can transcribe Zoom meetings in 3 ways. Zoom creates an audio transcript for cloud recordings on paid plans, but only if the setting was on before the meeting. ## What are the 3 ways to transcribe Zoom meetings?

There are 3 reliable ways to transcribe Zoom meetings, and the right one depends on your Zoom plan and what you need after the call.

| Method | Zoom plan needed | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Lynkk** | **Free or paid** | **Transcript, notes, action items, Jira tickets** |
| Zoom cloud recording transcript | Paid (Pro and up) | Transcript file with the recording |
| AI Companion meeting summary | Paid Zoom Workplace | Summary, next steps and transcript |

If you are on a free Zoom account, only a third-party tool like Lynkk can give you a transcript, because Zoom does not transcribe local recordings.

## How do you get a Zoom transcript from a cloud recording?

This is how to get Zoom transcript text from a cloud recording in 3 steps on a paid plan. The setting must be on before the meeting, because Zoom cannot transcribe recordings made while it was off.

1. **Turn on audio transcription** for cloud recordings in the Zoom web portal settings, before the meeting.
2. **Record the meeting to the cloud** from the Record button during the call.
3. **Open the recording** in the Recordings page of the Zoom web portal after processing finishes, where the transcript appears with it.

## How do you get a Zoom transcript after the meeting?

You can only get a Zoom transcript after the meeting if 1 of 2 things was set up beforehand: cloud recording with audio transcription on, or a tool like Lynkk recording the call. Zoom does not transcribe past recordings retroactively.

To find it:

- **Cloud recording transcripts** appear on the Recordings page of the Zoom web portal once processing finishes.
- **AI Companion transcripts** come with the meeting summary, if your admin lets users keep them.
- **Lynkk transcripts** appear in your Lynkk workspace about 30 seconds after the call, with notes and tasks.

## Why is my Zoom meeting not transcribed?

A Zoom meeting is usually not transcribed for 1 of 3 reasons, all confirmed in Zoom's help center.

| Problem | Why it happens | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| No transcript on a free account | Zoom does not transcribe local recordings | Use Lynkk, or upgrade Zoom |
| No transcript on a paid account | The audio transcript setting was off before the meeting | Turn it on for future meetings |
| Old recordings have no transcript | Zoom does not transcribe recordings after the fact | Record future meetings with the setting on, or use Lynkk |

## Why do Zoom transcripts matter for accessibility?

Zoom transcripts make meetings accessible to people who are deaf or hard of hearing, and US federal guidance treats transcripts as a baseline for audio content. The [Section 508 guidance on captions and transcripts](https://www.section508.gov/create/captions-transcripts/) explains when text alternatives are needed.

The ADA also lists real-time transcription among the aids for effective communication, as the [ADA effective communication page](https://www.ada.gov/resources/effective-communication/) explains.
