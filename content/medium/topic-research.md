# Medium topic research for Lynkk

Researched 2026-10-06, topics 11-14 added 2026-10-07. Keyword numbers are US monthly search volume, SEO difficulty
(SD, 0-100, lower is easier) and CPC from Ubersuggest. CPC is a rough signal of
commercial value: advertisers pay more for buyers.

Use with the `create-medium-article` skill. Product facts come only from
`memory/lynkk-brand.md`.

> **Claim check (2026-10-07):** `lynkk-post-kit/docs/TRUTHS.md` (checked against
> the product) rules out data residency, an AI that speaks in meetings, CRM
> updates, automatic Jira and "notes in 30 seconds". Topics 1 and 10 (data
> residency angle), 4 (speaking AI) and 5 (CRM) need a new Lynkk angle before
> drafting. The topic ideas themselves still stand.

## What the data says

1. **"AI note taker" is the big head term** (27,100/mo, SD 48), far bigger than
   "AI meeting assistant" (4,400/mo, SD 60). Readers search for the note taker; Lynkk's
   story is that note taking is only one third of the job. Articles should meet
   readers at "note taker" and then widen the frame.
2. **Evergreen "how to take meeting notes" queries are large and easy.**
   "format for meeting notes" 22,200 (SD 51), "how to take good meeting notes" 880
   (SD 16), "how to write meeting notes" 880 (SD 36), "meeting notes with action items"
   210 (SD 12). These are ideal for Medium, which ranks well on how-to content.
3. **Privacy is the live story of 2026.** Otter.ai faces a consolidated federal class
   action over recording without all-party consent (CIPA, federal Wiretap Act and
   Illinois biometric claims survived dismissal), and a July 2026 suit against Granola
   alleges recording without notice and training on meeting data by default. Lynkk's
   data residency feature is a natural, honest angle.
4. **Niche queries carry high buyer value.** "hipaa compliant ai note taker"
   (260/mo, CPC $31), "ai note taker for in person meetings" (480/mo, CPC $14.62),
   "conversation intelligence platform" (880/mo, CPC $43), "ai note taker for
   therapists" (CPC $50).
5. **"Live" and "real time" meeting assistants are an emerging category.**
   "live ai meeting assistant" 170/mo, "real time ai meeting assistant" 110/mo. New
   entrants (Clinq, CoAgentor) launched in 2026 with agents that speak in calls.
   Lynkk's "speaks when needed" feature fits here, and search demand is early, so
   thought leadership can shape the conversation.
6. **"Before the meeting" has almost no search demand** ("ai meeting prep" 10/mo,
   "meeting prep" 390/mo). Good for an opinion piece on Medium, not for SEO.

## Recommended topics (priority order)

Mode refers to which skill reference to use: leadership (argument, decisions) or
technical (implementation, architecture).

### 1. The AI note taker consent problem
- **Working title:** Your AI note taker may be recording people who never agreed to it
- **Deck:** What the Otter and Granola lawsuits allege, and the five questions to ask
  before a bot joins your next call
- **Reader promise:** A practical consent and data checklist for teams using any AI
  note taker.
- **Keywords:** ai note taker (27,100), hipaa compliant ai note taker (260, CPC $31)
- **Lynkk angle:** Data residency (pick the region where every model processes your
  data). Keep it to one paragraph.
- **Mode:** leadership
- **Why now:** Active litigation in 2026, high reader anxiety, timely.
- **Blocked on:** Confirm how Lynkk notifies participants and whether it trains on
  customer data before mentioning either. Add "not legal advice".

### 2. Note takers solve the wrong half of the meeting
- **Working title:** AI note takers fixed the easy part of meetings. Here is the rest.
- **Deck:** Notes after the call are table stakes. The value is in the context before
  it and the work after it.
- **Reader promise:** A before, during and after framework for judging any meeting AI.
- **Keywords:** ai meeting assistant (4,400), best ai meeting assistant (110, SD 32)
- **Lynkk angle:** This is Lynkk's core positioning (one AI across the whole meeting
  lifecycle).
- **Mode:** leadership
- **Blocked on:** Nothing. Can be written now.

### 3. How to take meeting notes people actually act on
- **Status:** Written. See `content/medium/meeting-notes-that-turn-into-action/`.
- **Working title:** How to take meeting notes that turn into action (and what to
  stop writing down)
- **Deck:** A simple format for decisions, owners and deadlines, plus which parts are
  worth automating
- **Reader promise:** A reusable notes format and a rule for what to automate.
- **Keywords:** format for meeting notes (22,200), how to take good meeting notes
  (880, SD 16), how to write meeting notes (880), meeting notes with action items
  (210, SD 12)
- **Lynkk angle:** Light mention: structured notes and assigned action items within
  30 seconds.
- **Mode:** leadership (practical how-to)
- **Why:** Highest volume, lowest difficulty, evergreen. Best SEO bet.
- **Blocked on:** Nothing.

### 4. When the AI in your meeting can talk back
- **Working title:** What changes when your meeting AI can speak
- **Deck:** Voice agents are joining calls in 2026. Here is when that helps, when it
  is awkward, and the etiquette teams need.
- **Reader promise:** Decision rules for when an AI should speak in a meeting.
- **Keywords:** live ai meeting assistant (170), real time ai meeting assistant (110)
- **Lynkk angle:** Lynkk joins and answers by voice when asked (past context,
  documents).
- **Mode:** leadership
- **Why:** Emerging category with little content yet. Good for Medium distribution.
- **Blocked on:** Nothing.

### 5. The 30-second follow-up
- **Working title:** The meeting is not over until the CRM is updated
- **Deck:** Why follow-up work dies after the call, and how to make notes, tasks,
  CRM fields and tickets happen automatically
- **Reader promise:** A follow-up workflow sales and product teams can copy.
- **Keywords:** meeting follow up (480, SD 23), after meeting follow up email (210),
  meeting follow up email examples (170)
- **Lynkk angle:** CRM field updates and Jira tickets created after the call.
- **Mode:** leadership, or technical if it shows a real integration flow
- **Blocked on:** Which CRMs Lynkk supports (HubSpot, Salesforce?) before naming any.

### 6. In-person meetings are the AI blind spot
- **Working title:** Most AI note takers ignore half your meetings: the ones in a room
- **Deck:** Interviews, client visits and hallway decisions rarely get captured. How
  to record in-person conversations responsibly.
- **Reader promise:** How to capture in-person meetings well, consent included.
- **Keywords:** ai note taker for in person meetings (480, CPC $14.62), in person
  meeting ai note taker (90)
- **Lynkk angle:** Records in-person conversations, offline-first, multilingual.
- **Mode:** leadership
- **Blocked on:** iOS/Android availability (affects "how" advice).

### 7. Start every call already caught up
- **Working title:** Stop starting meetings cold
- **Deck:** The five minutes before a call matter more than the hour after. How to

- **Keywords:** meeting prep (390, SD 19). Low SEO value, strong opinion piece.
- **Lynkk angle:** Surfaces what was discussed, what is open and who said what.
- **Mode:** leadership
- **Blocked on:** Nothing.

### 8. Conversation intelligence beyond sales
- **Working title:** Conversation intelligence is not just for sales teams anymore
- **Deck:** What it is, why product, hiring and leadership teams now use it, and how

- **Keywords:** conversation intelligence (1,900, SD 60), conversation intelligence

- **Lynkk angle:** Knowledge graph across all meetings, searchable.
- **Mode:** leadership
- **Blocked on:** Nothing, but SD 60 head term is hard; aim at the "what is" query.

### 9. Your meetings as a knowledge graph (MCP)
- **Working title:** Your meetings are the knowledge base you never built
- **Deck:** How a knowledge graph of conversations, exposed through MCP, lets other

- **Keywords:** low search volume; targets Medium's large developer and AI audience.

- **Mode:** technical
- **Blocked on:** Real MCP docs or examples from the Lynkk team. The skill forbids

### 10. Data residency for meeting AI
- **Working title:** Where does your meeting data go? A data residency checklist for

- **Mode:** leadership
- **Blocked on:** Available regions and certifications (SOC 2, GDPR, HIPAA). Do not

## More topics (added 2026-10-07)

Found with a second Ubersuggest pass on meeting types and Lynkk features the first
list didn't cover: one-on-ones, agendas, standups and Mac dictation.

### 11. One-on-ones that pick up where the last one ended
- **Working title:** One-on-One Meeting Questions That Build on Last Time
- **Deck:** Most question lists treat every one-on-one like the first. Carry open
  threads forward and the conversation gets better.
- **Reader promise:** A short question set organized around what changed since
  the last one-on-one, plus a running notes doc that keeps context between meetings.
- **Keywords:** questions for one on one meeting (1,000, SD 6), one on one meeting
  questions (1,600, SD 27), one on one meeting template (1,600, SD 19), one on one
  meeting (2,400, SD 38), one on one meeting notes template (110)
- **Competition:** The top pages are long lists ("150 questions") with strong
  backlinks. Don't compete on length. Compete on continuity, which none of them
  cover.
- **Lynkk angle:** Surfaces context from past meetings: what was discussed, what
  is still open, who said what.
- **Mode:** leadership
- **Care point:** One-on-ones are personal. Include when *not* to record, and
  ask permission when you do.
- **Blocked on:** Nothing.

### 12. Agendas that end in decisions
- **Working title:** How to Write a Meeting Agenda That Ends in Decisions
- **Deck:** Turn every agenda item into a question the meeting has to answer, and
  start with what the last meeting left open.
- **Reader promise:** A reusable agenda format and a test for which items deserve
  meeting time at all.
- **Keywords:** meeting agenda (5,400, SD 52), how to write meeting agenda (590,
  SD 11), meeting agenda how to write (320, SD 7), how to run effective meetings
  (320, SD 9), team meeting agenda (880, SD 35, CPC $11.89)
- **Note:** The biggest agenda queries ("meeting agenda form" 18,100) are people
  looking for downloadable templates. Medium can't serve those well, so target the
  how-to queries instead.
- **Lynkk angle:** Open action items from past meetings feed the first agenda
  section.
- **Mode:** leadership
- **Pairs with:** Topic 3 (agenda in, notes out). Close to Topic 7, but Topic 7 is
  prep for attendees and this one is for organizers. Write one before the other,
  not both in the same week.
- **Blocked on:** Nothing.

### 13. Voice typing on a Mac
- **Working title:** Talk, Don't Type: A Practical Guide to Voice Typing on a Mac
- **Deck:** How to set up the Mac's built-in dictation, where it falls short, and
  the habits that make dictated drafts usable.
- **Reader promise:** Working setup in five minutes, plus editing habits for text
  written by voice.
- **Keywords:** mac voice to text (1,600, SD 25), voice to text on mac (1,300,
  SD 21), dictation mac shortcut (480, SD 24), voice to text mac app (320, CPC
  $12.97), voice dictation mac (140, CPC $13.75), how to use voice to text on mac (210)
- **Competition:** Weak. The pages ranking now are small sites with very little
  traffic, according to Ubersuggest content ideas. This is the easiest opening in
  either list.
- **Lynkk angle:** The Lynkk Mac app includes dictation: text appears at your
  cursor as you speak, in other apps. This reaches people outside the meeting use
  case.
- **Mode:** technical (how-to)
- **Blocked on:** At drafting time, check the current macOS steps on Apple's
  support page (the shortcut differs between macOS versions and keyboards). Check
  with the team which apps, languages and offline modes Lynkk dictation supports
  before stating anything beyond the brand file.

### 14. Standups that clear blockers
- **Working title:** Your Daily Standup Is a Status Report. Make It a Blocker
  Meeting.
- **Deck:** A tighter standup format, and how to get the follow-ups into Jira before
  people are back at their desks.
- **Reader promise:** A standup format focused on blockers, and a rule for what
  belongs in the standup versus after it.
- **Keywords:** daily stand up meeting (2,400, SD 51), stand up meeting (1,900,
  SD 43), daily standup meeting (1,600, SD 43), what is a stand up meeting (1,000,
  SD 43), stand up meeting agenda (480, SD 35, CPC $13.67), scrum standup meeting
  questions (110, SD 30)
- **Lynkk angle:** Jira tickets created from the meeting.
- **Mode:** leadership with light process detail. Medium's product and engineering
  readership is large.
- **Blocked on:** Nothing, but cite the official Scrum Guide accurately for what
  the Daily Scrum is meant to be.

### Runner-up: meeting transcription in Zoom and Teams
zoom meeting transcription (590, SD 32), teams meeting transcription (390, SD 22),
microsoft teams meeting transcription (390, CPC $16.32). Held back: it needs the
list of meeting platforms Lynkk supports, and it overlaps with Topic 3 and the

3. Topic 12 (agendas): pairs with the published notes article.

## Lower priority or not for Medium

- **"Can Copilot / ChatGPT take meeting notes?"** (390 and 90/mo, SD about 30). Good
  search demand, but needs careful, current verification of Microsoft and OpenAI
  features. Better on the lynkk.ai blog.
- **"Best AI note taker" listicles** (1,900/mo, competition 1.0). Vendor-written
  rankings read as ads on Medium and are hard to rank. Keep for lynkk.ai.
- **Bot vs bot-free note takers** ("bot free ai note taker" 110, SD 59). Lynkk
  joins as a participant, so this invites an unfavorable comparison. Skip for now.
- **Multilingual meetings.** Good fit, but wait until the supported language count
  is confirmed.

## Suggested first three

1. Topic 3 (meeting notes how-to): biggest, easiest search opportunity.
2. Topic 2 (wrong half of the meeting): states Lynkk's positioning.
3. Topic 1 (consent problem): timely, once the two consent questions are answered.
