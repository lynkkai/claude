# Lynkk: 2 Short Video Scripts (Problem, then Fix)

Source page: https://lynkk-revenue-story.imsjwagh99.chatgpt.site/#workflow

- **Format:** Vertical video (Reels, Shorts, TikTok, LinkedIn)
- **Length:** 50 seconds each (about 90-120 spoken words)
- **Focus:** Most of the video is the problem. About 30 seconds on the pain, 15 seconds on the fix, 5 seconds on the call to action.
- **Style:** A salesperson talking to camera, mixed with short acted scenes and screen recordings of the Lynkk app
- **Captions:** Always on (most people watch on mute)
- **Hook rule:** The very first word is the hook. Say it loud, show it big on screen, before anything else.
- **Language rule:** Short, everyday words. If a 15-year-old wouldn't get it, rewrite it.

## Tips for filming

- **First word first:** No logo, no music build-up, no "Hi guys." Open on the hook word, loud and big on screen.
- **Let the problem hurt:** The problem part is the main story. Act it out like a short scene people recognize from their own day. Use quick cuts (3-5 seconds each) and keep the mood tense.
- **Switch the mood at the fix:** At "Here's the fix," change the music and lighting so it feels like relief.
- **Keep it simple:** Say "sales records" out loud. Only show the word "CRM" in the hashtags.
- **Demo data:** Use the sample customer from the page (Acme Corp, Priya). Mark app shots as "Example" if used in ads. The acted scenes are made up to show the problem, not real customer stories.
- **Extra version:** Make a Hindi and English mix version of Video 1 ("Let's close this." / "हाँ, let's do it.") to show Lynkk works in 15+ languages.
- **Stay accurate:** "Follow up in 5 minutes. Not 3 days." and "No credit card" come straight from the page. The Team plan ($39 per person) is "coming soon," so don't show it as available.
