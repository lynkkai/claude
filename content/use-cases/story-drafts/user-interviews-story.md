## Meet Rachel

Rachel designs apps at Greenway Foods LLC. This week she has eight customer interviews. She wants to know why new users
stop using the app.

## Rachel's problem

In each interview, Rachel tries to listen and write at the same time. She looks at her notebook more than at the person.

Her notes only have short phrases, not the customer's real words. After eight interviews, she can't remember who said
what. Finding one good quote means listening to hours of recordings again. She tried transcription software once, but it gave her a
wall of text with no names.

## How Lynkk helps Rachel transcribe interviews

Rachel starts using Lynkk. Here is what changes.

**Before the interview, Rachel presses record.**
She records from her computer, or in the room when she meets someone in person. No bot joins the call.

**During the interview, Rachel just listens.**
She looks at the person, not her notebook. She asks better follow-up questions.

**After the interview, the text is ready.**
Lynkk turns the audio to text and labels who said each line. It also lists the main points and the questions still open.

### Here is the note Lynkk wrote for Rachel (example)

**Customer interview, onboarding · Zoom · 41 min · 2 speakers**

- 08:12 **Rachel:** What made you look for something new?
- 08:15 **Customer:** Setting up took our team two weeks. Half of them gave up.
- 08:31 **Rachel:** Where did they give up?

**Main point:** New teams give up during setup.

**Still open:** Which setup step loses the most people?

Rachel can click any line to hear that moment again.

## So, what is Lynkk?

Lynkk is an AI note taker for interviews. Researchers call this qualitative research transcription. It simply means
turning spoken interviews into text you can read, search and quote.

With Lynkk, you get three things:
- **The full text:** every word, with each speaker labelled.
- **The main points:** each with the time it was said.
- **Memory:** ask Lynkk "What did people say about setup?" across all your interviews (Pro).

## Questions people ask

**Is Lynkk an interview transcription service?**

It's software, not a human service. Lynkk records the interview and writes the text for you, with each speaker labelled.

**Can I record an interview in person?**

Yes. Record the room with the Mac app or the web app.

**Is Lynkk one of those user research tools that sorts themes?**

No. Lynkk doesn't tag themes. Instead, you ask questions across all your interviews and get answers with sources.

**Which languages can the interview be in?**

English, Hindi, Hinglish, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Arabic and
Russian, with Tamil, Telugu, Marathi and Bengali in beta.

**Do I need permission to record?**

Yes. Always ask the person first, and tell them how you will use the recording.
