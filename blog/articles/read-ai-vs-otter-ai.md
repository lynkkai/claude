# Read AI vs Otter AI in 2026: Which Should US Teams Pick?

*By [Samarth Wagh](https://lynkk.ai/blog/authors/samarth-wagh), Co-founder, Lynkk.ai · Last updated 17 October 2026 · Pricing checked 17 October 2026*

> **Quick answer:** In Read AI vs Otter AI, **Otter** wins on live transcription and price: Pro costs $8.33 a month billed yearly and the free plan gives 300 minutes. **Read AI** wins on meeting analytics and coaching: it scores engagement and talk time, and Pro costs $15 a month billed yearly. Read AI's free plan stops at 5 meetings; Otter's stops each call at 30 minutes.

*Disclosure: Lynkk publishes this guide and makes a competing AI meeting assistant. Prices come from [Read AI](https://www.read.ai/plans-pricing) and [Otter](https://otter.ai/pricing), checked 17 October 2026.*

Read AI vs Otter AI is a common shortlist, and from a distance it is easy to see why. Each joins Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, writes summaries and action items, and has a free plan.

Up close? Different problems. Otter is built around the transcript: you read along live and search it later. Read AI is built around the meeting itself, which means it cares about how the call went, who spoke and for how long, how engaged people were, and what you could do better next time.

## What is the difference between Otter AI and Read AI?

The core difference is focus. Otter AI produces a live, searchable transcript, while Read AI produces meeting reports with engagement and talk-time analytics plus a personal meeting coach. Both summarize and list action items.

| | Read AI | Otter.ai |
|---|---|---|
| Main output | Meeting report and analytics | Live transcript |
| Free plan | 5 meetings/month, 1 h each | 300 min/month, 30 min per call |
| Paid from (yearly) | $15 per user/month | $8.33 per user/month |
| Apps | Windows, macOS, Android, iOS | Web, mobile apps |

Ask yourself what you want to know once the call ends. If it is "what exactly did they say?", Otter answers faster. If it is "how did that meeting go, and how do I run the next one better?", Read AI does.

## Is Read AI or Otter AI better?

Otter AI is better for people who need accurate live notes, captions during calls, and the lowest price. Managers and sellers who want feedback on their meetings, plus reports they can share with a team, will get more from Read AI. Neither wins everything.

Choose by role:

- **Students, journalists, researchers:** Otter, for the live transcript and lower cost.
- **Sales managers and team leads:** Read AI, for engagement metrics and coaching.
- **Product and engineering teams:** neither does tickets well by default; see the Lynkk section below.

## How do Read AI and Otter AI compare on price?

On both billing cycles, Otter costs less. Otter Pro costs $16.99 monthly or $8.33 billed yearly, while Read AI Pro costs $19.75 monthly or $15 billed yearly. Team plans follow the same pattern. Otter Business is $19.99 billed yearly; Read AI Enterprise is $22.50 billed yearly and needs at least a 5-license minimum before you can buy it.

| Tier | Read AI (monthly / yearly) | Otter.ai (monthly / yearly) |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 |
| Pro | $19.75 / $15 | $16.99 / $8.33 |
| Team tier | Enterprise $29.75 / $22.50 | Business $30 / $19.99 |
| Top tier | Enterprise+ $39.75 / $29.75 | Enterprise, custom |

Picture a 10-person team on annual billing: Otter Pro comes to $1,000 a year, and Read AI Pro to $1,800. Full breakdown: [Read AI pricing](https://lynkk.ai/blog/read-ai-pricing).

## Why is Otter AI so expensive for teams?

For individuals on annual billing ($8.33 a month), Otter is not expensive at all. The jump comes with the Business plan, which costs $30 per user per month on monthly billing. Teams often end up there because Pro caps each conversation at 90 minutes and 1,200 minutes a month.

There is a similar jump on the other side. The Enterprise tier, which is the one you need for audio and video playback, costs $22.50 to $29.75 per user and requires 5 licenses, so a team of 3 still pays for 5 seats. Same story both ways.

## Which has the better free plan?

Frequent short calls favour Otter's free plan: 300 minutes a month, up to 30 minutes each. A few long calls favour Read AI's: 5 meetings a month, up to 1 hour each.

| Free plan limit | Read AI | Otter.ai |
|---|---|---|
| Meetings or minutes | 5 meetings/month | 300 min/month |
| Length per meeting | 1 hour | 30 minutes |
| History | Not limited on page | 25 most recent conversations |
| File uploads | Not included | 3 lifetime |

Say you run a single weekly hour-long client call. Read AI Free covers it. Otter Free cuts each call in half, which leaves you with notes for the first 30 minutes and nothing for the decisions made at the end.

## Which has better apps and platform coverage?

On apps, Read AI lists native versions on 4 platforms: Windows, macOS, Android and iOS, all available on the free plan. Otter takes a lighter approach, with a browser-based web app plus iOS and Android apps, and a meeting agent that joins Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams.

| Platform | Read AI | Otter.ai |
|---|---|---|
| Windows desktop app | Yes | Web app |
| macOS desktop app | Yes | Web app |
| iOS and Android | Yes | Yes |
| Zoom, Meet, Teams | Yes | Yes |

In-person meetings work too, since both mobile apps can record a conversation in the room. One catch with Otter: those in-person recordings count against the same 300 or 1,200 monthly minutes as your calls.

## Which is more accurate, Read AI or Otter AI?

With clear English audio and 1 speaker at a time, both tools are accurate enough; crosstalk, accents and poor microphones trip up each of them. Where they differ is in fixing mistakes. You get more correction tools in Otter: custom vocabulary (5 terms on Basic) and team vocabulary on Pro.

What you read matters for accuracy too. Otter shows the raw transcript, so errors are visible and easy to fix. With Read AI leading on summaries, a misheard word may be smoothed over while a wrong number quietly slips into the report that goes to your manager. Check names, dates and prices before you share either output.

## How do the integrations compare?

Premium integrations come with Read AI Pro, 7 of them: Notion, Confluence, Salesforce, HubSpot, Jira, Zapier and webhooks. On Otter, Pro adds Salesforce, HubSpot and Zapier. API access and webhooks wait until Enterprise.

Product teams get a slight edge from Read AI's Jira and Confluence connections. Large sales teams may lean the other way. Why? Because Otter's Enterprise plan adds custom CRM and dialer integrations and an Otter Sales Notetaker, which together cover most of what a big sales floor asks for.

## Is Otter.ai safe? Is Read AI private?

Security? Each offers enterprise tiers. On Otter Enterprise you get SSO, SCIM, domain capture and a HIPAA compliance add-on. Read AI Enterprise+ includes HIPAA compliance, SAML, SCIM, domain capture and custom data retention.

Day to day, though, the bigger questions are about control:

1. **Auto-join:** both tools can join meetings from your calendar automatically. Agree internally on when that is allowed.
2. **Visible bots:** attendees see both tools join. Some clients will ask you to remove them.
3. **Consent:** several US states require all parties to consent to recording, so announce the note taker at the start.

## How do Read AI and Otter compare with Fireflies?

Fireflies sits between them. It records like Otter, then adds CRM sync and search across calls, at $10 per seat billed yearly for Pro. For Read AI vs Otter AI vs Fireflies, the short version is simple: pick Otter for transcripts, Read AI for analytics, and Fireflies for CRM-heavy sales.

## FAQ

### Is Read AI or Otter AI better for meetings?

For live transcription and lower cost, Otter is better. For engagement analytics, coaching and shareable reports, choose Read AI. The real question is whether you need a record of what was said, or feedback on how the meeting went and how to improve it. Test both on real calls.

### What is the difference between Otter AI and Read AI?

The split is simple. Otter AI focuses on live, searchable transcripts, while Read AI focuses on meeting reports with engagement, talk-time and coaching insights. Each summarizes meetings and lists action items. People who reread what was said tend to prefer Otter; managers who want feedback on how meetings went lean to Read AI.

### Is Read AI cheaper than Otter?

No. Read AI Pro costs $15 a month billed yearly versus $8.33 for Otter Pro. On monthly billing, Read AI Pro is $19.75 and Otter Pro is $16.99. For a 10-person team on annual billing, that is about $1,800 a year for Read AI Pro versus $1,000 for Otter Pro.

### Does Read AI show a live transcript like Otter?

Yes, partly: it offers live notes during meetings, including on its free plan (5 sessions a month). Otter goes further, since the live transcript is its core feature on every plan. Free limits differ as well. Read AI's free plan caps each meeting at 1 hour, while Otter's free plan cuts each conversation at 30 minutes.

### Which is better for Microsoft Teams, Read AI or Otter?

Yes, each tool joins Microsoft Teams meetings, and Read AI also offers a Microsoft Teams marketplace app. If your company prefers built-in tools, Teams' AI recap requires a Microsoft 365 Copilot license. Whichever you pick, it appears as a visible participant, so tell attendees at the start of the call.

### Do Read AI and Otter support languages other than English?

Some do. Read AI lists support for 20+ languages, whereas Otter focuses mainly on English. Lynkk supports 17 languages plus auto-detect, including Hindi and a Hinglish setting for mixed Hindi and English. Test any tool with a real meeting in your team's main languages before you buy seats for everyone.
