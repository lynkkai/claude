# How to Use Speech to Text on Mac in 2026: 5 Ways Compared

By [Samarth Wagh](https://lynkk.ai/blog/authors/samarth-wagh) · 4 November 2026 · Apple features checked against the macOS User Guide in November 2026

> **Quick answer:** There are **5** ways to do speech to text on a Mac: Apple's built-in Dictation (free, types as you speak), Notes (records and transcribes audio), a third-party dictation app, a meeting transcription tool, and Voice Control for hands-free use. Dictation is the fastest start, and it stops automatically after **30 seconds** of silence.

Speech to text on Mac means turning spoken words into typed text, either live as you talk or afterwards from a recording. Two different jobs. Pick the wrong tool for yours, and that's usually why a speech to text Mac setup seems broken.

## Which speech to text method should you use on Mac?

Pick by what you're starting with: your own voice right now, or audio you already have, such as a 45-minute recording. This table maps the 5 speech to text Mac options to the job each does best.

| Method | Best for | Cost | Works offline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apple Dictation | Typing by voice in any app | Free, built in | Partly, depends on your Mac |
| Notes recording | Recording and transcribing a conversation | Free, built in | Not stated by Apple |
| Third-party dictation app | Accuracy, formatting and commands | Free to paid | Varies by app |
| Meeting transcription tool | Zoom, Google Meet and Teams calls | Free tiers to paid | No |
| Voice Control | Hands-free control, accessibility | Free, built in | Yes, on device |

## How do you turn on speech to text on a Mac?

Turn on Apple's Dictation in System Settings, under Keyboard, then click Enable. Then put your cursor in any text field and start dictating: from the Edit menu, the Microphone key, or whichever keyboard shortcut you picked, [per Apple's macOS User Guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/use-dictation-mh40584/mac).

Apple's guide says you can enter text with Dictation anywhere you can type. 2 details are worth knowing before you start:

- **It stops on silence.** Dictation has no length limit, but it switches off after 30 seconds with no speech detected.
- **Punctuation can be automatic.** In supported languages, Dictation adds commas, periods and question marks for you, and you can turn that off in Keyboard settings.

Our guide to [dictation on Mac](https://lynkk.ai/blog/dictation-on-mac) covers the setup screens step by step, including the keyboard shortcut options.

### What is the speech to text Mac shortcut?

The shortcut is whichever one you select in System Settings under Keyboard, then Dictation. Apple's guide lists pressing the Microphone key, using the Dictation keyboard shortcut, or choosing Edit then Start Dictation, so there's no single shortcut that's true on every Mac.

Got a Microphone key? That's the quickest route. If not, pick a shortcut you won't hit by accident, because Dictation starts listening the moment it fires.

## Is Mac speech to text processed on your device?

Sometimes. Apple's guide says you can check in Keyboard settings whether general text dictation is processed on your device rather than sent to Siri servers, so it comes down to 2 things: your Mac and your language.

Apple also says newer macOS uses an on-device model to improve spelling, punctuation and capitalisation, available in English on Mac models with M3 or later and at least 12GB of unified memory. Asking because of privacy? Check that setting before you dictate anything sensitive.

## Is there free speech to text on Mac, and what does it cost?

Yes. Apple Dictation is free and built into macOS, with no account, subscription or time limit. For most people, speech to text Mac free means Dictation plus the Notes app, which covers typing by voice and transcribing a recording.

Paid apps earn their money on accuracy, custom vocabulary, formatting and commands. Dictate now and then, and the free tools are plenty. Dictate for hours a day, and the time you stop losing to corrections pays for an app.

## How do you transcribe a recorded audio file on a Mac?

To transcribe audio on Mac, use an app built for transcription rather than live dictation, because Apple Dictation only types what it hears through the microphone right now. Apple's Notes can record audio in a note and generate a live transcript that's searchable, [per Apple's Notes User Guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/notes/record-and-transcribe-audio-apd4b3cf2a2f/mac).

Already have the file, say an MP3 from a voice recorder? A transcription service is the simpler path. ## How do you use speech to text in Word, Pages and Google Docs on Mac?

Apple Dictation works in any app where you can type, so it works in Pages, Google Docs in a browser, and Microsoft Word. Both of the latter also have their own option. Google's [voice typing](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/4492226) sits under Tools and needs Chrome, Edge or Safari, while Word's Dictate button on the Home tab is a [subscriber-only feature](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/dictate-your-documents-in-word-3876e05f-3fcc-418f-b8ab-db7ce0d11d3c) that needs Microsoft 365 and an internet connection, with Option plus F1 as its Mac shortcut.

| App | Use Apple Dictation | App's own option |
|---|---|---|
| Pages | Yes, Edit then Start Dictation | None needed |
| Google Docs | Yes, in the browser | Voice typing, under Tools, in Chrome, Edge or Safari |
| Microsoft Word | Yes | Dictate button on the Home tab |

## How do you transcribe a meeting on a Mac?

To transcribe a meeting, use a tool that records the call rather than your microphone alone, so you capture every speaker. Apple Dictation hears only what reaches your mic. That's why it's the wrong tool for a Zoom call.

## Why is speech to text not working on Mac?

A speech to text Mac not working problem usually has 1 of 4 causes. The wrong microphone, a missing permission, a language mismatch, or Dictation switching off after silence. Work through them in order.

1. **Check the microphone:** System Settings, then Sound, then Input, and watch the level move as you speak.
2. **Check permissions:** in Privacy and Security, confirm the app can use the microphone.
3. **Check the language:** Dictation transcribes the language selected in Keyboard settings, not the one you're speaking.
4. **Restart dictation:** it stops after 30 seconds of silence, so press the shortcut again.
5. **Check the cursor:** Dictation types where the cursor is, so click into a text field first.

## What is the difference between Dictation and Voice Control on Mac?

Dictation does 1 job: it types what you say. Voice Control lets you operate the whole Mac by voice, including clicking, scrolling and opening apps, and it's designed as an accessibility feature.

Want to write a message by talking? Use Dictation. Want to run your Mac with no keyboard or mouse at all? That's Voice Control, and dictation comes as part of it.

## FAQ

### Does Mac have built-in speech to text?

Yes. Every modern Mac includes Apple Dictation, which types what you say into any text field, free and with no account. Turn it on in System Settings, under Keyboard, then start it with the Microphone key, your chosen shortcut, or Edit then Start Dictation. The Notes app can also record and transcribe audio.

### How long can you dictate on a Mac?

Apple's guide says Dictation has no timeout for length, so you can keep talking as long as you like. It does stop automatically after 30 seconds with no speech detected, which is why long pauses end a session. Press your shortcut again to carry on where you left off.

### Can Mac speech to text work offline?

It depends on your Mac and language. Apple's guide says you can check in Keyboard settings whether general text dictation is processed on your device rather than sent to Siri servers. Third-party apps vary: some run a local model with no connection, while others stream audio to a server for better accuracy.

### How accurate is speech to text on Mac?

Apple Dictation is accurate for everyday writing in a quiet room with a decent microphone. Accuracy drops with background noise, strong accents, technical vocabulary and names, which is the usual reason people try a voice to text Mac app instead. Apps that let you add a custom dictionary tend to handle jargon better, which matters most if you dictate product names, medical terms or code.

### Can you add words to the Mac dictation dictionary?

Apple's Dictation doesn't offer a general custom dictionary in the way dedicated apps do, though it learns some names from your contacts. If you regularly dictate unusual terms, a third-party dictation app with a custom dictionary saves the most time, because you fix each word once instead of after every session.

### Does speech to text work in every Mac app?

Apple says you can use Dictation anywhere you can type, so it works in Mail, Pages, Notes, browsers and most third-party apps. Some apps with custom text fields, including parts of certain code editors and games, may not accept dictated text. If nothing appears, click into the field first and check the app has microphone permission.
