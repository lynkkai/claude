# Lynkk Dictation: 30-Second Video Script

**Format:** Short-form video (Reels / TikTok / Shorts / LinkedIn), 9:16
**Length:** ~30 seconds (~65 words of voiceover)
**Feature:** Lynkk dictation on the Mac desktop app: speak, and text appears at your cursor in any app you're using
**Tone:** Fast, relatable, a little self-roasting
**Longer cut:** see `dictation-40s.md`

---

## Production notes

- **What was cut from the 40s version:** the separate "There's a better way" line and the "no copy, no paste, no carpal tunnel" line. Problem and funny beat now share one scene.
- **Pacing:** Hold the CEO reply on screen for about a second under the VO. Keep it readable.
- **Sound design:** Frantic keyboard clatter under the hook, record-scratch at 10s, calm lo-fi beat from 12s onward.
- **Captions:** Burn in captions; most viewers watch on mute.
- **Claims check:** Dictation is confirmed on the Mac desktop app only. Don't imply Windows/mobile until confirmed (see `memory/lynkk-brand.md`).
