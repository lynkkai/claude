## Rubric

- **What is it?** Lynkk remembers every customer conversation and turns it into follow-ups, CRM updates and your next best move.
- **Who for?** Sales reps, sales leaders and RevOps.
- **What it does for them:** records that match what the customer actually said, and live help on the next call.
- **What sets it apart:** every item links back to the moment in the call (site copy: "Every item links back to the call."); Cue is visible only to you.
- **Most striking claim:** "The CRM tells one story. Your customer told another. The gap shows up in your forecast." (lynkk.ai)
- **Visual hook:** "Wrong." slams in red over two cards: records say "Looking good", the call says "Our budget is on hold for now."
- **Real UI shown:** the lynkk.ai Sales Call note ("Corvane Logistics · Commercials") and the live Cue demo in Google Meet, both captured from lynkk.ai's own markup at 2x.
- **Tone:** `polished` with a tense first act (dark, ticking) and a relieved second act (day, bright groove).
- **Share caption:** see `share-copy.txt`.
