# Google Meet AI Note Taker Options for Hindi and Hinglish Teams in India in 2026

A Google Meet AI note taker is a tool that listens to a Meet call and writes the summary, decisions and action items for you, either from inside Meet or as an add-on. For Indian teams, the choice comes down to one question most global reviews skip: does it understand how your team actually talks? Many Indian meetings switch between English and Hindi mid-sentence.

This guide explains what Google Meet's own note taker can and cannot do, then compares the add-ons that Indian teams use, with free plans, language support and data hosting.

## Does Google Meet have an AI note taker?

Yes. Google Meet's AI note taker is called **Take notes for me**, powered by Gemini. It writes meeting notes into a Google Doc saved in the organizer's Google Drive, inside a "Google Meet" folder, with a sub-folder per meeting ([Google Meet help](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/14754931)).

Key facts from Google's help center:

- **Plan needed:** an eligible Google Workspace edition or Google AI plan.
- **Languages:** English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish, one language per meeting.
- **Mixed languages:** not supported in the same meeting.
- **Who controls it:** with Host Management on, only the host and co-hosts can start or stop the notes.

For Indian teams, the missing language is the headline. Hindi is not supported, and Hinglish meetings fall under "mixed languages", which Google says are not supported. A tool with [multilingual transcription](https://lynkk.ai/features/multilingual-transcription) fills that gap.

## How do you add an AI note taker to Google Meet?

Knowing how to add an AI note taker to Google Meet comes down to 3 routes, and the first one needs no install at all:

1. **Turn on Take notes for me.** In a meeting, click "Take notes for me" at the top right, then "Start taking notes". Organizers can also tick "Take notes with Gemini" in the calendar event under Video call options, then Meeting records.
2. **Connect a bot-based tool to your calendar.** Tools like Lynkk, Read AI, Otter, Fireflies and Fathom join scheduled Meet calls as a participant. With Lynkk you can also paste a meeting link to send [the meeting bot](https://lynkk.ai/features/meeting-bot) to a single call.
3. **Install a browser extension.** Lynkk's [Chrome extension](https://lynkk.ai/features/chrome-extension) and Tactiq capture Google Meet without a bot joining the call.

Whichever you choose, tell participants the meeting is being recorded or transcribed. Google itself supports this: an admin can require every participant to consent before notes, recording or transcription start. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, transparency about processing personal data matters, and it is basic courtesy anyway.

## Can a Google Meet AI note taker handle Hindi and Hinglish?

Google's own note taker cannot: Hindi is not on its 8-language list, and mixed-language meetings are not supported. Third-party tools vary, so test with a real Hinglish meeting before you commit.

What to check in a 20-minute test:

- **Code-switching:** does the transcript keep both languages, or force everything into English?
- **Names and places:** are Indian names spelled correctly?
- **Numbers:** are lakh, crore and rupee figures captured correctly?
- **Summary language:** can you get the summary in English even when the meeting was in Hinglish?

## Can Google Meet transcribe and record meetings?

Yes, on eligible plans. Google Meet records meetings to Drive and creates transcripts, but recording needs 1 of 10 listed Workspace editions, a Workspace Individual subscription or a Google One plan with 2 TB or more storage. An admin must allow it too ([Google Meet recording help](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/9308681)).

Participants can only record when the host has permission to record, so a guest from outside the organization needs to be promoted to co-host first. For the transcript side, see our [Google Meet transcript guide](https://lynkk.ai/blog/google-meet-transcript).

## FAQ

### Does Google Meet have a free AI note taker?

Take notes for me needs an eligible Workspace edition or Google AI plan, so it is not free on personal Gmail accounts. One detail catches teams out: admins can turn it on automatically for some meetings, so check your calendar invites before assuming nothing is listening.

### Does Google Meet's AI note taker support Hindi?

No. Google lists 8 supported languages, and Hindi is not among them. The language is set per meeting rather than per participant, so one Hindi speaker in an English meeting still gets transcribed into the meeting language. Lynkk's Hinglish setting handles mid-sentence switching instead.

### How do I add an AI note taker to Google Meet?

Turn on Take notes for me inside the meeting, connect a bot-based tool to your calendar, or install a Chrome extension such as Lynkk's or Tactiq. Worth knowing: if your admin requires consent, every participant sees a prompt and must click Start, Join or Continue before capture begins.

### What is the best Google Meet AI note taker for India?

Lynkk suits Indian teams because it supports Hindi, has a Hinglish setting and offers an India data region. Read AI lists Hindi reports too. Test on a real 20-minute Hinglish meeting, and check how each tool spells Indian names and handles lakh and crore figures.

### Is it legal to use an AI note taker in Google Meet in India?

Using an AI note taker is generally allowed when participants know the meeting is being recorded or transcribed. Under the DPDP Act, be clear about how personal data is processed and stored. Note that Google shares its notes with everyone on the invite, not only the people who attended.

### Can a Google Meet AI note taker work without a bot?

Yes. Google's own Take notes for me works without a bot, and Lynkk's Chrome extension and Tactiq capture Meet calls without a bot joining. One trade-off: extensions need the browser tab open for the whole call, while a bot keeps recording after you drop off.
