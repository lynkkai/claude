# How to Remove Fireflies.ai From Teams, Zoom and Meet in 2026

*By [Samarth Wagh](https://lynkk.ai/blog/authors/samarth-wagh), Co-founder, Lynkk.ai · Last updated 17 October 2026*

> **Quick answer:** For how to remove Fireflies AI from Teams in under 30 seconds: open **Participants**, click **(...)** next to Fireflies and choose **Remove from meeting**, or type **/ff leave** in the meeting chat. To stop it joining again, set Fireflies auto-join to **Only when I invite**, remove **fred@fireflies.ai** from calendar invites, and ask your Teams admin to **block the app**. Deleting the Fireflies account stops it completely.

*Disclosure: Lynkk publishes this guide and makes a different AI meeting assistant. Steps come from Fireflies' help center and Microsoft's community answers, checked 17 October 2026.*

Searching for how to remove Fireflies AI from Teams usually means one of two situations. Either the bot just showed up in your meeting and you want it gone, or it keeps coming back no matter what you try. Both are covered below, across Microsoft Teams, Zoom and Google Meet.

Usually someone on the invite connected their calendar to Fireflies, and its auto-join setting sent the bot (named Fred) into every meeting. Kicking it out once fixes today. Changing that setting fixes tomorrow.

## How do you remove Fireflies AI from a Teams meeting right now?

Each of these 3 ways to remove Fireflies from a live Microsoft Teams meeting takes under 30 seconds:

1. **From the participant list:** open **Participants**, click **(...)** next to Fireflies, then select **Remove from meeting**. You need organizer or presenter rights.
2. **From the chat:** type `/ff leave` in the meeting chat. Fireflies leaves the call.
3. **From the Fireflies dashboard:** the person who owns the bot can click the **Stop** icon on the Live meeting banner on their Home dashboard.

Speed matters here. According to Fireflies' help center, removal within the first 3 minutes means no transcript or notes get created ([Fireflies guide](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/7098191513-how-to-remove-fireflies-from-a-meeting-or-stop-it-from-joining)).

## How do you remove Fireflies from Zoom and Google Meet?

Zoom and Google Meet work almost identically, and the steps take about 10 seconds:

| Platform | Steps |
|---|---|
| Zoom | Open the participant list, click **More** next to Fireflies Notetaker, then **Remove** |
| Google Meet | Open the participant list, click the three-dot icon next to Fireflies, then **Remove from the call** |
| Any platform | Type `/ff leave` in the meeting chat |

Zoom only lets a host or co-host remove a participant. Google Meet behaves similarly when Host Management is on: hosts and co-hosts alone can remove people.

## How do you stop Fireflies from joining your meetings automatically?

Stopping auto-join means changing settings in 3 places. Bot keeps returning? Do all 3:

1. **Change auto-join.** In the Fireflies dashboard or settings, find **Auto-join calendar meetings** (or Auto-record meetings) and choose **Only when I invite**, using `fred@fireflies.ai`.
2. **Turn it off for specific meetings.** On the Fireflies home page, open the **Upcoming** tab, find the meeting and toggle Fireflies **off**.
3. **Remove Fred from invites.** Edit the event in Google Calendar or Outlook, delete `fred@fireflies.ai` from the guest list, and save.

There's also **Recording Rules**, which block meetings by keyword, email address or domain. Because rules override auto-join, you could, say, block every external client meeting in one go.

## How do you remove Fireflies AI from Microsoft Teams permanently?

To remove Fireflies AI from Microsoft Teams permanently, 2 people usually need to act: the person whose account sends the bot must delete their Fireflies account or disconnect it, and a Teams admin should block the app. Do just one? The bot often keeps joining.

**If you own the Fireflies account:**

1. Sign in at app.fireflies.ai with the same Microsoft account you connected.
2. Go to **Settings**, scroll down and use the delete option to remove the account.
3. Check Microsoft's app permissions for your account and remove Fireflies if it still has access.

**If someone else's Fireflies keeps joining your meetings:** ask them to change their auto-join setting, or remove `fred@fireflies.ai` from the invite each time. You cannot delete another person's account.

## How does a Teams admin block Fireflies for the whole company?

Blocking the Fireflies app takes a Teams admin 4 steps in the Teams admin center, per a Microsoft community answer ([Microsoft Q&A](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/4429002/removing-fireflies-ai-note-taker-bot-from-microsof)):

1. Open the **Teams admin center** and go to **Teams apps**, then **Manage apps**.
2. Search for **Fireflies**.
3. Select the app and choose **Block**.
4. Confirm the status shows **Blocked**.

That alone isn't always enough. Users in the same thread report the bot still slipping in, since Fireflies can join as a meeting participant through calendar access. Admins often add 2 more controls:

- **Lobby settings:** set meeting options so only organizers and co-organizers bypass the lobby. The bot then waits in the lobby where the organizer can deny it.
- **App consent:** review enterprise applications in Microsoft Entra ID and restrict or remove Fireflies' access to calendars and sign-in.

Menu names drift over time. Check Microsoft's current documentation for your tenant.

## Is Fireflies AI safe, and why does it join meetings uninvited?

Fireflies is a legitimate, widely used product with HIPAA and SSO on its $39 Enterprise plan. Joining "uninvited" is usually a settings issue. Not a security breach. Once one attendee connects their calendar with auto-join on, Fred shows up to every meeting on that calendar, even ones other people organized.

Still, treat any unexpected recorder as a privacy question: who owns the bot, where does the recording go, and did everyone agree? Several US states require all parties to consent before a call is recorded.

## FAQ

### How do I permanently remove Fireflies AI from Microsoft Teams?

Delete or disconnect the Fireflies account that sends the bot, remove fred@fireflies.ai from invites, and have a Teams admin block the app in the Teams admin center under Teams apps, then Manage apps. App blocking alone doesn't always work. Calendar access lets the bot keep joining.

### How do I kick Fireflies out of a meeting?

Open the participant list, click the menu next to Fireflies and choose Remove from meeting, or type /ff leave in the meeting chat. Act fast: within the first 3 minutes, Fireflies creates no transcript or notes. In Teams, removing a participant requires host or presenter rights.

### Why does Fireflies keep joining my Teams meetings?

Someone on the invite connected their calendar to Fireflies with auto-join turned on. Ask the bot's owner to switch auto-join to Only when I invite, or remove fred@fireflies.ai from the invite. With recurring meetings, edit the whole series rather than one occurrence; otherwise Fred will be back the following week.

### Will removing Fireflies delete the transcript?

It depends on the timing. Removed within the first 3 minutes, Fireflies creates no transcript or notes; removed later, the owner may still get notes for the part it recorded. Without attendee agreement, ask the bot's owner to delete those meeting notes.

### Can I block Fireflies without admin rights?

Partly, yes. You can remove it from meetings you organize and set your lobby so only organizers bypass it, which leaves the bot waiting outside for the organizer to simply deny it entry each time. Company-wide blocking, though, still requires a Teams admin.

### Is Fireflies AI safe to use?

Fireflies is a legitimate product with enterprise security options, including HIPAA and SSO on its Enterprise plan. The usual complaint is uninvited joining via auto-join, and settings control that. Ask who owns the bot and where the recording goes, since several US states require all parties to consent to recording.
