# Blog year refresh: 2026 to 2027

Re-checked 2026-11-25 (first version 2026-11-17) from a full scan of the 81 live posts on lynkk.ai/blog: `<title>`, meta, Open Graph and Twitter tags, H1 to H6, paragraphs, lists, tables, FAQ blocks, image alt text, JSON-LD schema and the social images themselves.

## Summary

- **44 posts** have text to change, **232 edits** in total (Part 1).
- **63 social images** show a "2026" badge, and **5** also have 2026 in the headline (Part 2).
- **Real dates stay as they are** (Part 3): "checked September 30, 2026", "June 2026", sample due dates, published and updated dates. Changing them would make the posts wrong.
- URLs contain no year, so no redirects are needed.
- H2 to H6 headings: only the Otter.ai alternatives post has 2026 in a subheading (one H2, one H3). No other post has the year in any subheading, list, table header or FAQ question, apart from the dated table headers listed in Part 3.

Note: an H1, its JSON-LD `headline` and the image alt are often generated from the same field in the CMS or post front matter. Changing the source field once may update several rows below.

## What changed since the first check (Nov 17 to Nov 25)

- **No post uses 2027 yet.** The only "2027" on the blog is a real date in the recording-law post ("notice rules... due to apply from May 2027"). Leave it.
- **17 new posts**, and most went live with 2026: fathom-vs-otter, fireflies-vs-otter, gong-alternatives, granola-alternatives, granola-vs-fathom, granola-vs-otter, jamie-ai-review, meeting-notes-template, read-ai-pricing and teams-transcript (plus three topic hub pages that list other posts' titles and update on their own). They are included in Part 1.
- **dictation-on-mac** was removed and now redirects to speech-to-text-mac.
- **Pricing posts were retitled on Nov 18** to "(Nov 2026)" price-check titles: Fathom, Fireflies, Gong, Granola and Otter. See Part 3.
- **Year dropped from the title** on ai-note-taker-microsoft-teams (H1 still says "(2026)"), zoom-note-taker, how-to-transcribe-zoom-meetings, is-it-legal-to-record-a-meeting, sales-conversation-intelligence and board-meeting-agenda.
- **Retitled but kept 2026**: ai-transcription-software, best-free-ai-note-takers, best-second-brain-apps, call-coaching-software, daily-standup-meeting (year added), hindi-transcription (year added), transcribe-audio-to-text, voice-memo-transcription, voice-to-text-apps and zoom-ai-companion-alternatives (year added).
- **Mixed signals to fix**: the comparison posts have "(2026)" in the SEO title but not in the H1, and the pricing posts say "(Nov 2026)" in the title while their images still say "in 2026".
