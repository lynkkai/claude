# onlinecultus.com: is it worth a free guest post for Lynkk?

Checked 2026-10-09. The site itself was blocked from this environment, so the numbers
below come from Ubersuggest (domain overview, top pages, backlinks, anchor texts).
Open the site in a browser before submitting to confirm the points marked "check".

## Verdict

**Fine to post since it's free, but expect little SEO value. Treat it as a minor
brand mention, not a ranking boost.** Don't pay for it, and don't build a pattern of
links from sites like this.

## The numbers

| Metric | Value | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Domain Authority | 24 | Low to middling |
| Organic traffic | ~217 visits/month (Sep 2026) | Very small. Peak in the last 2 years was ~435/month |
| Ranking keywords | 272 | Almost all ranked on page 2 or lower |
| Top page | /coursera/ (~25 visits/month) | No page brings in real traffic |
| Backlinks / referring domains | 4,849 / 813 | Looks big, but the profile is low quality (see below) |
| Traffic country | US-English data | Not a market signal for Lynkk buyers |

## Red flags

1. **Spammy backlink profile.** The most common anchor texts pointing to the site are
   automated spam ads (expired-domain sellers, "inject your brand into ChatGPT"
   services) and one reading "onlinecultus.com premium seo backlinks for higher
   google rankings". That is typical of a domain used to sell links.
2. **Looks like a revived expired domain.** Posts date back to 2014-2021, then go quiet,
   then new posts appear from late 2025. Old education blogs bought for their
   backlinks and reused for guest posts often follow this pattern.
3. **Off-topic for Lynkk.** It's an education blog (courses, universities, study
   resources, medieval history). A link from an unrelated niche passes little
   relevance to an AI meeting assistant.
4. **No real audience.** ~200 visits a month spread over hundreds of old posts means
   the article will get almost no referral traffic.

## What's still okay

- It's free, so the only cost is the time to write the article (done).
- One link from a low-quality site will not hurt lynkk.ai. Google mostly ignores links
  like this rather than penalising the target.
- An education angle (students, teachers, study groups) does fit some real Lynkk uses:
  recording in-person conversations, multilingual capture, searchable notes.

## How to submit it safely

- Use **one** link to https://lynkk.ai/ with a branded anchor ("Lynkk" or "lynkk.ai").
  No keyword-stuffed anchors like "best AI meeting assistant".
- Accept nofollow or "sponsored" if they add it. It changes nothing for us here.
- Don't pay any fee if one appears later, and don't agree to put a link back to them on lynkk.ai.
- Check before sending: is the site live, do recent posts mention other brands only
  inside casino/CBD/loan content (if so, skip it), and is there a named editor or
  contact page?

## Better uses of the same effort

Sites that would actually move the needle for Lynkk are in the productivity, SaaS,
sales, remote-work and AI-tools niches with real traffic (DA 40+, thousands of
monthly visits, a clean anchor profile). AI tool directories and comparison
articles ("best AI note takers") are also more relevant.
