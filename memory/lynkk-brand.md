  - Online **and** in-person capture in one account (Mac app or browser recorder for the room).

  - Help **before** the call (context from past notes), **during** (Cue, beta) and **after** (notes, tasks, Jira, Slack).

## Features

### Before the call
- Home screen shows today's meetings from Google Calendar with a brief from past notes ("what did we promise last time?").
- Ask Lynkk (Pro) can prep you for upcoming calls from your notes, with numbered sources.

### During the call
- **Cue (beta, Mac app, by invite):** sits at the top of your screen during a call. Ask it anything, or press ⌘↩ and it reads your screen and the conversation. Modes for Sales, Support, Interview prep or custom. Never hidden from a screen share; "only text leaves your Mac".
- **The meeting bot does not speak.** It joins as a visible participant named after you (for example "Maya's Notetaker"), does not post a consent message, and leaves once it's the only one left. Users should announce recording themselves.

### After the call
- A note holds: the recording, a transcript with speaker labels, and an AI Summary (title, short abstract, up to 8 key takeaways, topics, action items with owner, due date and priority, and decisions). Takeaways, action items and decisions carry clickable transcript times.
- **Summary Shapes (Pro):** same meeting, different structure, for example Brief, Sales Call (customer, stakeholders, pain points, budget), General Meeting, Customer Interview.
- Summary emailed to you after every note; optional "Email summary to attendees" (off by default).
- Tasks board; send action items to Jira (one issue per action item, on every plan).
- Search across every note (⌘K / Ctrl+K), by meaning and exact words, on every plan.
- **Ask Lynkk (Pro):** answers across all your notes with numbered sources down to the moment in the recording; can draft a Jira issue or Google Calendar event and acts only after you confirm. Reads only your own notes (workspace admins also cover notes members left visible to admins).
- Share a note by link (works 30 days, can be turned off, recording only if you choose) or invite a viewer by email.

### Capture and platforms
- **Meeting bot:** Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams. Auto-joins calendar meetings with a link when Google Calendar is connected, or paste a link and press Send bot.
- **Mac app:** Apple silicon only, **macOS 14.2 or later** (no Intel). Detects calls in Zoom, Teams, Webex, FaceTime, Slack huddles, WhatsApp, and Meet/Zoom/Teams/Webex in browsers, then asks Record or Not now (setting: Ask first, Automatically, Off). Records your mic and the call audio on two separate channels. No bot in the call.
- **Chrome extension:** Google Meet, Zoom web client, Microsoft Teams (work and personal), tab plus mic on two channels, audio only. Chrome 116 or later; also runs in Edge, Brave and Arc.
- **Browser recorder:** in the web app, "Start recording" under Offline for in-person meetings; can pause, resume and survive a page reload.
- **In person:** Mac app or browser recorder; leave the laptop on the table.
- **Offline resilience:** if the Mac loses connection, the recording is saved locally and uploads when back online.
- **Not available today:** no iPhone, Android or Windows app (Windows/Linux users use the web app, extension and bot); **no audio or video file upload/import**.
- Recording length: up to 2 hours per note on every plan.

### Dictation (Mac app)
- Hold fn, talk, and the words land at your cursor in any app (Slack, Mail, Notion, browser, terminal).
- Cloud dictation hears 18 languages and detects others. Hinglish and Hindi are opt-in; Hinglish comes back in English letters.
- Pro adds cloud polishing and a synced custom dictionary.
- Dictated words are erased from Lynkk's servers after 7 days.

### Languages
- **17 languages plus Hinglish and auto-detect** for meeting transcription: English, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Arabic, Russian, with Tamil, Telugu, Marathi and Bengali in beta. Every language on every plan.
- Note: the home page body says "15+ languages" while the meta description, FAQ, pricing and features pages say 17. **Use "17 languages plus Hinglish"** in copy.

## Privacy and security (live)

- Everything travels over HTTPS (HSTS). Tokens for Google Calendar, Jira and Slack are encrypted with **AES-256-GCM**, keys kept outside the database. Recordings play through signed links that expire after an hour. No public address for the database or the queue.
- **Lynkk does not train AI models on your recordings, transcripts or notes.** Audio goes to a speech-to-text provider and transcripts to AI model providers to write the note.
- **Not end-to-end encrypted** (Lynkk must read the recording to transcribe it).
- **No data region setting today.** Lynkk and its providers may process data outside your country, including in **India and the United States**. Do not claim data residency or region choice.
- Own account: nobody else can open your notes unless you share. Workspace: each note shows whether admins can see it; admins can read summary and transcript (never the recording), can't edit, and every admin read is logged. Any note can be marked **Personal**.
- Export: Settings, Privacy and data, Export my data (one zip, Markdown and JSON plus recordings, link valid 24 hours). Account deletion by emailing admin@lynkk.ai; privacy policy says personal data is deleted or de-identified within 30 days.
- GDPR and India's DPDP Act rights (see, correct, export, delete) are referenced. **No SOC 2 or other certification is claimed on the live site.**
