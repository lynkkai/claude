# Lynkk Dictation: 40-Second Video Script

**Format:** Short-form video (Reels / TikTok / Shorts / LinkedIn), 9:16
**Length:** ~40 seconds (~80 words of voiceover at a relaxed, punchy pace)
**Feature:** Lynkk dictation on the Mac desktop app: speak, and text appears at your cursor in any app you're using
**Tone:** Fast, relatable, a little self-roasting

---

## Script

| Time | Visual | Voiceover (VO) | On-screen text |

## Production notes

- **Pacing:** Let the CEO reply ("What are we buying?") sit on screen for a full second without VO. That pause is the laugh.
- **Sound design:** Frantic keyboard clatter under the hook, record-scratch at 15s, calm lo-fi beat from 19s onward. The contrast sells the "before vs after."
- **Captions:** Burn in captions; most viewers watch on mute.
- **Alt hook options** (A/B test the first 3 seconds):
  - "Typing is just thinking, but slower."
  - "POV: you just autocorrected your CEO."
  - "Your thumbs called. They want a day off."
- **Claims check:** Dictation is confirmed on the Mac desktop app. Show the Mac app on the end card, and don't imply Windows/mobile until confirmed (see `memory/lynkk-brand.md`).
