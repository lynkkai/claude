# Keyword and competitor analysis (November 2026)

Sources: Ubersuggest (pulled 2026-11-11, US location 2840, India 2356 where marked) and the
Google Search Console export for 2026-08-10 to 2026-11-09, plus an on-page audit of all 115
sitemap URLs on lynkk.ai.

Published report: https://claude.ai/artifact/La3kP8arpmahUU5HPJkdrM (private until shared)

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `report.html` | Full report: competitors, funnel split, intent, clusters, difficulty tiers, proposed content changes, 90-day plan |
| `keyword-clusters.csv` | Lynkk target keywords with volume, SD, intent, funnel stage, current GSC position, difficulty tier, target page and action |
| `competitor-funnel-keywords.csv` | Top keywords for each of the 10 competitors, split into TOFU / MOFU / BOFU |
| `competitors.csv` | DA, traffic, keyword and backlink counts for the 10 competitors, with what drives their traffic |

Difficulty tiers for a DA 2 site: Quick win = SD 20 or less, or already ranking top 20 in GSC.
Medium = SD 21-35. Long-term = SD 36+ or rivals with DA 50+.

None of the proposed content changes have been applied to the site.
