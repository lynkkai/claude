# Jamie AI Note Taker Review: A 2026 Look for US Teams

*By Samarth Wagh, Co-founder, Lynkk.ai · Last updated 17 October 2026 · Pricing checked 17 October 2026*

> **Quick answer:** The Jamie AI note taker records meetings from your own device, so **no bot joins the call**. It works with any meeting app and in person, hosts data in the EU, and lists **99+ languages**. The free plan gives **10 AI notes a month up to 30 minutes each**. Paid plans: Plus $24, Pro $46 a month billed yearly, Team $37 per seat billed yearly.

*Disclosure: Lynkk publishes this review and makes a competing AI meeting assistant. Facts come from [Jamie's pricing page](https://www.meetjamie.ai/pricing), checked 17 October 2026.*

The Jamie AI note taker gets searched a lot for a simple reason: it promises meeting notes without the awkward bot. For people who meet with clients, candidates or patients, that promise matters more than any feature list.

This review covers how Jamie works, what each plan costs, how it handles privacy and languages, and which Jamie alternatives fit better for specific teams.

## What is the Jamie AI note taker?

Jamie is an AI note taker that captures meeting audio on your computer or phone rather than sending a bot into the call. After the meeting it writes notes, detects tasks and identifies speakers. It works with "all online platforms" and in-person meetings, per Jamie's pricing page.

What Jamie does on every plan:

- **Bot-free capture** from your device
- **Speaker recognition** and task detection
- **Notes and transcripts in 99+ languages**
- **Ask Jamie**, a chat over your meeting notes
- **Unlimited storage**, EU hosting and GDPR compliance
- **No model training** on your data

## How does Jamie work without a bot?

Jamie records the audio playing on your device and your microphone, the same way a person with a recorder would. That is why it works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex or a phone call, and why nobody sees a participant called "Jamie" join.

The trade-offs of device capture:

1. **You must start it.** If Jamie is not running, the meeting is not captured.
2. **One device, one recording.** Your colleagues need their own Jamie to capture meetings they host.
3. **Consent is still required.** No bot does not mean no recording. Several US states require all parties to agree before a call is recorded, so tell attendees.

## Is the Jamie AI note taker free?

Yes. Jamie's free plan gives 10 AI notes a month, each up to 30 minutes, with 1 custom template, 50 custom vocabulary terms, Ask Jamie chat, and exports to Google Docs, Notion and OneNote.

The 30-minute cap is the real limit. A 45-minute client call gets cut off. The free plan suits short check-ins or a trial; daily users will need Plus or Pro.

## What do you get on Jamie Plus, Pro and Team?

The jump from Free to Plus doubles your notes to 20 a month and raises the meeting cap to 2 hours. Pro removes the note cap, allows 5-hour meetings and adds CRM and AI-tool integrations. Team adds shared workspaces and admin controls.

- **Plus ($24):** 20 notes a month, 2-hour meetings, 3 custom templates.
- **Pro ($46 billed yearly):** unlimited notes, 5-hour meetings, unlimited templates, Salesforce, HubSpot, Asana, Pipedrive and an MCP connection for AI tools.
- **Team ($37 per seat billed yearly):** shared templates, shared workspace and tags, user management, 100 vocabulary terms, centralized billing.
- **Enterprise (custom):** SAML SSO, SCIM, audit logs, IP allowlist, data processing agreements, 250 vocabulary terms and a dedicated account manager.

## Is Jamie safe and private?

Jamie's privacy story is its strongest feature: bot-free capture, EU hosting, GDPR compliance and a stated policy of no model training on your data. Its page lists ISO 27001 and DORA, with SOC 2 Type II marked as pending.

For US teams, 2 questions are worth asking Jamie's sales team:

- **US data residency.** EU hosting suits European clients. Some US companies prefer or require US hosting.
- **SOC 2 status.** Many US security reviews require a completed SOC 2 Type II report.

## Does Jamie have an Android app?

Yes, in beta. [Jamie's documentation](https://docs.meetjamie.ai/enterprise/users/mobile-app) lists an iOS app on the App Store and a beta Android app on Google Play. The mobile apps record in-person meetings, show your meetings and tasks, and let you ask questions on the go. Desktop apps capture online meetings on your computer.

Because the Android app is a beta, features may lag the iPhone version. If in-person capture is your main use, test the mobile app on a real meeting before committing. Room noise and distance from the phone affect any tool's accuracy.
