# Teams Transcription: How to Turn It On, Download It, and Fix It When It Fails

## A plain guide to Microsoft Teams transcription, with or without a recording

Teams transcription turns what people say in a Microsoft Teams meeting into searchable text with speaker names and timestamps. You start it from the meeting controls under More, then Record and transcribe. It can run with or without a recording, and afterwards organizers can download the transcript as a .docx or .vtt file.

That is the short version. The longer version is that transcription in Teams depends on who you are in the meeting, what your admin allows, and whether you remembered to start it. This guide covers how to turn it on, how to transcribe without recording, why the button is sometimes missing, how to download the file, and what to do when the built-in transcript is not enough.

## Transcription, recording and live captions are not the same thing

Teams has 3 features that people mix up:

- **Live captions** show subtitles on your own screen during the meeting. They are not saved, and only you see them.
- **Transcription** writes down the whole meeting as text, saves it, and makes it available after the meeting.
- **Recording** saves the audio and video. It often starts transcription at the same time, but the two can run separately.

If you want a written record, you need transcription, not captions. Captions disappear when the meeting ends.

## How to turn on Teams transcription

Here is how to turn on Teams transcription during a meeting:

1. **Join the meeting** in the Teams desktop app or Teams on the web.
2. **Select More** (the 3 dots) in the meeting controls.
3. **Hover over Record and transcribe.**
4. **Select Start transcription.** In some versions this option is called Show transcript.

Everyone in the meeting sees a notice that transcription has started. A transcript pane opens on the side, with each speaker's name and the time they spoke.

Before you rely on it, check the spoken language. Teams transcribes in the language set for the meeting, and if that does not match what people are actually speaking, the transcript will be nonsense. The setting is in the transcript pane.

To stop, go back to More, then Record and transcribe, and select Stop transcription.

## Teams transcription without recording

Yes, you can run Teams transcription without recording. Start transcription on its own using the steps above, and do not start a recording.

This is useful for meetings where people are uncomfortable being on video, or where your company keeps recordings for a short time but wants text notes for longer. The transcript still lands in the meeting's recap, and you still get speaker names and timestamps.

The opposite is not always true. If you record a meeting but never start transcription, you may not be able to create a transcript from that recording later inside Teams. Start transcription during the meeting if you think you will need text.

## Why the Teams transcription button is greyed out or missing

If you cannot start transcription, it is usually 1 of 4 things:

1. **You are not a presenter.** Organizers can limit who starts transcription, and attendees who are not presenters may not see the option at all.
2. **Your admin has turned it off.** Transcription is controlled by your organization's meeting policy.
3. **You are a guest** or from another organization.
4. **Your license does not include it.**

None of these can be fixed from inside the meeting. Ask the organizer to start it, or ask IT whether the meeting policy allows transcription for your account.

## How to find and download a Teams transcript

The transcript is available shortly after the meeting ends. There are 2 places to find it:

- **The meeting event** in your Teams calendar. Open it and look for the transcript.
- **The meeting chat.** Open the chat, go to **Recap**, then **Transcript**.

To download it, open the transcript and use the **Download** menu. You can pick a .docx file, which opens in Word, or a .vtt file, a caption format that video players and editing tools understand.

By default, organizers and co-organizers can download the transcript. Other attendees may be able to read it but not download it. If you need a copy, ask the organizer.

## How to transcribe a Teams meeting you do not control

Sometimes you are a guest, or the organizer forgets, and Teams transcription is not an option. Here is how to transcribe a Teams meeting in that case:

- **Ask the organizer** to start transcription, and to share the file afterwards.
- **Use a meeting bot** that joins from the link, if the organizer is happy to admit it from the lobby.
- **Record from your own side** with a desktop app or a browser extension, if everyone agrees.

Whatever you choose, tell people. Teams shows a banner for its own transcription, but nobody sees a banner for a tool running on your computer.

## Is it legal to transcribe a meeting?

A transcript is a recording of what people said, so the same rules apply. In the US, federal law allows recording with the consent of at least one participant ([18 U.S.C. § 2511](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title18-section2511)), but several states require everyone to agree. In the EU, a transcript of identifiable people is personal data under the [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj).

The safe habit is simple: say at the start that the meeting is being transcribed, and why. This is general information, not legal advice.

## Getting more out of a Teams transcript

A transcript of a busy 60-minute meeting runs to thousands of words. Nobody reads that. A few habits make it useful:

- **Search, don't scroll.** Look for names, numbers and words like "agreed" or "by Friday".
- **Say decisions out loud.** "So we agreed to ship on the 20th" is easy to find later.
- **Name owners when you assign tasks.** "Priya, can you send that?" beats "someone should send that".
- **Pull out a 5-line summary** the same day: decisions, owners, dates.

## When Teams transcription is not enough

The built-in transcript is a raw record. It does not tell you what was decided, who owns what, or what is still open, unless your company pays for extra AI features in Microsoft 365. It also only exists when someone remembered to start it, and only for people with the right permissions.

## Teams transcription checklist

- Start transcription under More, then Record and transcribe.
- Check the spoken language before people start talking.
- Transcribe without recording when video is not needed.
- Start transcription during the meeting; it may not be possible afterwards.
- Find it in the meeting chat under Recap, then Transcript.
- Download it as .docx or .vtt (organizers and co-organizers by default).
- Tell everyone it is being transcribed.

A transcript is only useful if someone turns it into decisions and next steps.
