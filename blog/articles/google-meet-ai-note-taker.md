For Indian teams, choosing a Google Meet AI note taker comes down to one question most global reviews skip: does it understand how your team actually talks? Many Indian meetings switch between English and Hindi mid-sentence. Many client calls mention rupee figures, Indian names and local places.

This guide explains what Google Meet's own note taker can and cannot do, then compares the add-ons that Indian teams use, with free plans, language support and data hosting.

## Does Google Meet have an AI note taker?

Yes. Google Meet's AI note taker is called **Take notes for me**, powered by Gemini. It writes meeting notes into a Google Doc saved in the organizer's Google Drive, inside a "Google Meet" folder ([Google Meet help](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/14754931)).

Key facts from Google's help center:

- **Plan needed:** an eligible Google Workspace edition or Google AI plan.
- **Languages:** English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish, one language per meeting.
- **Mixed languages:** not supported in the same meeting.
- **Who controls it:** if Host Management is on, only hosts and co-hosts can turn it on or off.

For Indian teams, the missing language is the headline. Hindi is not supported, and Hinglish meetings fall under "mixed languages", which Google says are not supported.

## Is the Google Meet AI note taker free?

No. Take notes for me is not available on free personal Gmail accounts; it needs an eligible Google Workspace edition or a Google AI plan. Free Google Meet AI note takers come from third-party tools with free plans.

## How do you add an AI note taker to Google Meet?

There are 3 ways to add an AI note taker to Google Meet, and each one takes under 5 minutes:

1. **Turn on Take notes for me.** In a meeting, click "Take notes for me" at the top right and then "Start taking notes". Organizers can also enable it in the calendar event under Video call options.
2. **Connect a bot-based tool to your calendar.** Tools like Lynkk, Read AI, Otter, Fireflies and Fathom join scheduled Meet calls as a participant. With Lynkk you can also paste a meeting link to send the bot to a single call.
3. **Install a browser extension.** Lynkk's Chrome extension and Tactiq capture Google Meet without a bot joining the call.

Whichever you choose, tell participants the meeting is being recorded or transcribed. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, transparency about processing personal data matters, and it is basic courtesy anyway.

## Can a Google Meet AI note taker handle Hindi and Hinglish?

Google's own note taker cannot: Hindi is not on its 8-language list, and mixed-language meetings are not supported. Third-party tools vary, so test with a real Hinglish meeting before you commit.

What to check in a 20-minute test:

- **Code-switching:** does the transcript keep both languages, or force everything into English?
- **Names and places:** are Indian names spelled correctly?
- **Numbers:** are lakh, crore and rupee figures captured correctly?
- **Summary language:** can you get the summary in English even when the meeting was in Hinglish?

Lynkk's Hinglish setting is built for exactly this case. Our guides to Hindi transcription and Hindi speech to text go deeper.

## Can Google Meet transcribe and record meetings?

Yes, on eligible plans. Google Meet can record meetings to Drive and create transcripts, but recording needs an eligible Workspace edition or a Google One plan with 2 TB or more storage, and an admin must allow it ([Google Meet recording help](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/9308681)).
