## Positioning
- **One-liner:** Lynkk records your meetings, writes the notes, pulls out the tasks, and lets you ask questions across everything you've recorded.
- **Differentiator:** one app across the whole meeting: a morning briefing email before, live words during, and a Meeting Brief after.

## What Lynkk does (per TRUTHS.md)
- **Ways to record:** Mac app (notices Zoom, Google Meet in a browser, Teams, Slack huddles, WhatsApp, FaceTime and Webex calls; no bot; records both sides; live words and a note field on the screen edge; saves to disk, so bad Wi-Fi does not lose the file; Apple silicon, macOS 14.2+); meeting bot for Meet, Zoom and Teams (free, audio only; video recording is Pro; live transcript panel); Chrome extension (no bot; Meet, Zoom web, Teams in a Chrome tab); web recording at lynkk.ai (works in person).
- **Every note:** transcript with speaker labels in **17 languages (4 in beta)**; Meeting Brief (free): title, abstract, takeaways with timestamps, topics, action items with owner/due date/priority, decisions, open questions; Summary Shapes (Pro, 9 built in); per-note chat (Pro); share by revocable link or email invite; summary emailed after every meeting.
- **Before:** morning briefing email (with Google Calendar): who you're meeting, the last note with them, open action items.
- **Tasks:** kanban or list; **Jira: Send from the note, 1 issue per action item. Manual, not automatic.**
- **Ask Lynkk (Pro):** answers only from **your own notes**, with citation chips that play the recording from the moment.
- **Slack (answers need Pro):** DM, @Lynkk or /lynkk; answers from your notes; does not read channels or post summaries.
- **Dictation (Mac):** hold fn, talk, let go; Instant (on device, English, free), Accurate and Polished (cloud, Pro); cloud dictation hears 18 languages.
- **Workspaces:** up to 10 seats; everyone gets Pro; notes are admin-visible (default) or Personal.
- **Privacy:** recordings are private to your account; Lynkk never trains on your conversations.
- **Plans:** Free (recording and transcription, meeting bot audio only, Meeting Brief, tasks, Jira send, Instant dictation, 2-hour cap per recording). Pro ($15 a month per the pricing page title) adds Ask Lynkk, Summary Shapes, per-note chat, weekly digest, bot video, Slack answers, cloud dictation, custom words. No free trial. Do not state note limits, hours or "unlimited".
