# How to Record Webex Meetings and Get a Webex Transcript on Any Plan

*Last updated: October 1, 2026 · Steps and plan details checked against Webex Help Center on October 1, 2026*

> **Quick answer:** Here is how to record Webex meetings: every Webex plan, including free, can record to your computer from the desktop app, while cloud recording needs a Webex Starter, Plus or Business plan. A Webex transcript is not available on the free plan. On a Mac, the Lynkk app records Webex calls with no bot and gives you a transcript, notes and action items whatever Webex plan you are on.

## Can you record Webex meetings on the free plan?

Yes. According to the Webex Help Center, checked on October 1, 2026, local recording is available on all Webex plans, including the free plan, while cloud recording is available on 3 paid plans: Starter, Plus and Business.

| Feature | Free plan | Starter, Plus, Business |
|---|---|---|
| Record to your computer | Yes, from the desktop app | Yes |
| Record to the cloud | No | Yes |
| Webex transcript of recordings | No | Yes |
| AI summary of cloud recordings | No | On the Webex Suite meeting platform |

So the answer to how to record Webex meetings for free is simple: use the desktop app and record to your computer.

## How to record Webex meetings step by step

Knowing how to record Webex meetings takes about 4 steps once you are in the meeting as the host. These steps follow the Webex Help Center:

1. **Join from the Webex desktop app.** Free users can only record from the desktop app.
2. **Click Record** in the meeting controls.
3. **Choose where to save it:** in the cloud (paid plans) or on this computer (all plans).
4. **Click Record again to start.** Use the same control to pause or stop.

Recordings are saved in MP4 format. If you do not see a Record option, the host may not have given you permission to record.

## Where do Webex recordings go?

Webex saves recordings in 1 of 2 places, depending on the option you picked. Cloud recordings appear in your Webex account's recordings list, and the host gets an email when they are ready.

Local recordings go to your computer. On Windows, they save to the **Documents** folder by default; on a Mac, they save to your **Home** folder, which you can open in Finder.

## How do you get a Webex transcript?

You get a Webex transcript from a recording on a paid plan: Webex can automatically transcribe the audio of meetings you record in MP4 format, and the transcript shows when you play the recording. This feature is not available on the free plan.

On the Webex Suite meeting platform, cloud recordings can also come with an AI-generated summary, action items, a transcript and chapters, which Webex emails to the host to review and share.

If you are on the free plan, or you are not the host, you will not get a Webex transcript from Webex itself. That is where a call recorder on your own computer helps.

## Can you get a Webex transcript without recording in Webex?

Yes, in 1 way: transcribe the call on your own computer instead of using Webex's recording. A Webex transcript without recording in Webex comes from a separate app that captures the call audio on your device, so 0 Webex recording settings are involved.

On a Mac, the [Lynkk Mac app](https://lynkk.ai/download) notices Webex calls about 3 seconds after they start and shows a card that says "Webex call detected" with **Record** and **Not now**. It records both sides on 2 channels, so the transcript labels your side "You", and no bot joins the meeting.

The Mac app needs Apple silicon and macOS 14.2 or later. Tell everyone in the meeting that you are recording, exactly as you would with Webex's own button.

## What are common problems when you record Webex meetings?

If you are stuck on how to record Webex meetings, the problem usually has 1 of 4 causes. Check these before trying another tool.

| Problem | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| No Record button | You are not the host or lack permission | Ask the host to record or grant permission |
| No cloud option | Free plan | Record to your computer, or upgrade |
| No Webex transcript | Free plan, or a local recording | Use a paid plan with cloud recording, or a Mac call recorder |
| Can't find a local file | Default save folder | Check Documents (Windows) or Home (Mac) |

## Is it legal to record a Webex meeting?

Recording a Webex meeting is legal in most of the US with at least 1 participant's consent, but some states require everyone to agree. Federal law ([18 U.S.C. § 2511](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title18-section2511)) sets the 1 party rule, while California ([Penal Code § 632](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=632)) requires all parties to consent.

Announce the recording at the start, whichever tool you use. To review how any recording app handles your data, the [NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework/privacy-framework) is a useful checklist.
