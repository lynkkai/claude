## What is Hindi speech to text?

Hindi speech to text converts spoken Hindi into written text as you speak, usually in Devanagari script. Other names: Hindi voice typing, Hindi dictation. Point that same technology at recordings rather than live speech and it becomes [Hindi transcription](https://lynkk.ai/blog/hindi-transcription).

Two ways to use it:

| Mode | Best for | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Live voice typing | Messages, emails, short notes | Gboard, iPhone keyboard, Google Docs |
| Recorded or meeting capture | Interviews, lectures, meetings | AI transcription and meeting tools |

## How do you convert Hindi speech to text on Android?

On Android, Hindi speech to text works through Gboard, Google's keyboard, in 4 steps:

1. Open **Gboard settings**, then **Languages**, and add **Hindi (हिन्दी)**.
2. Open any app with a text box, such as WhatsApp or Gmail.
3. Switch the keyboard to Hindi with the globe or language key.
4. Tap the **microphone** and speak in Hindi.

Gboard's output lands in Devanagari. Prefer Roman-script Hinglish? Add the Hinglish or Hindi (Latin) keyboard layout and dictate there instead.

## How do you use Hindi voice typing on iPhone?

On iPhone, Hindi voice typing uses the built-in keyboard dictation in 3 steps:

1. Go to **Settings**, then **General**, then **Keyboard**, then **Keyboards**, and add **Hindi**.
2. Make sure **Enable Dictation** is turned on in the same Keyboard settings.
3. In any app, switch to the Hindi keyboard and tap the **microphone** key.

Dictation follows whichever keyboard is active. Words appearing in English? Switch keyboards first, then tap the mic.

## How do you get Hindi speech to text online for free on a computer?

The easiest free Hindi speech to text online is Google Docs voice typing, which lists Hindi as a supported language and runs in Chrome, Edge and Safari ([Google Docs Help](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/4492226)). Windows, Mac and Chromebook all qualify:

1. Open a new Google Doc in your browser.
2. Click **Tools**, then **Voice typing**.
3. Choose **हिन्दी (भारत)** from the language list above the mic.
4. Click the microphone and speak.

No cost, no time limit. The catch is a stable internet connection and a Doc that must stay open throughout, and because spoken punctuation commands understand English only, Hindi punctuation goes in by hand.

## Can you use Hindi speech to text on Windows 10 and Mac?

Yes. On Windows 10 and 11, Google Docs voice typing in Chrome or Edge is the most reliable free route for Hindi. Windows' own voice typing (Windows key + H) varies, since supported languages depend on your Windows version and installed language packs, so check whether Hindi appears in your list.

Macs ship with dictation covering many languages. Open **System Settings**, then **Keyboard**, turn on **Dictation**, and add Hindi under dictation languages if your macOS version offers it. Want more Mac options? Our guide to [dictation on Mac](https://lynkk.ai/blog/dictation-on-mac) covers them.

## How do you make Hindi voice typing more accurate?

Most Hindi speech to text errors come from 5 causes, and all 5 are fixable:

1. **Wrong keyboard language.** Switch to Hindi before tapping the mic.
2. **Noise.** Move away from fans, traffic and TV, or use earphones with a mic.
3. **Speed.** Speak at a normal pace with short pauses between sentences.
4. **Punctuation.** Spoken punctuation support varies by keyboard, so plan to add full stops and commas by hand afterward.
5. **Names and English words.** Check and fix them after dictating, since they are the most common errors.

In meetings, keep the device within 1 metre of the speakers and ask people not to talk over each other during decisions.

## FAQ

### How can I convert Hindi speech to text for free?

Use Gboard voice typing on Android, the iPhone keyboard microphone, or Google Docs voice typing in Chrome with हिन्दी selected. All three cost nothing. One snag: spoken punctuation commands in Google Docs understand only English, so add Hindi full stops and commas by hand after dictating.

### Which is the best app for Hindi speech to text?

Gboard on Android or the iPhone keyboard for messages, Google Docs voice typing for documents, and Lynkk for Hindi or Hinglish meetings, which it records and summarises automatically. Recorded files need an AI transcription tool that supports Hindi. Test it on 5 minutes of your own audio before relying on it.

### Can I do Hindi voice typing on a laptop?

Yes. Open Google Docs in Chrome, Edge or Safari, go to Tools, then Voice typing, and select हिन्दी. Windows, Mac and Chromebook laptops all work. Headphones with a microphone improve accuracy, and keep the Doc open while you speak, because typing stops when the tab closes.

### Does Hindi speech to text work offline?

Partly. Some phone keyboards support offline voice typing after you download the language pack, while Google Docs voice typing needs an internet connection. Offline modes help in low-connectivity areas. Accuracy can dip below online voice typing, though, so check names and numbers afterward.

### Can speech to text write Hindi in English letters?

Yes. Use a Hinglish or Hindi (Latin) keyboard layout on your phone, and dictation will write Hindi words in Roman script. Handy for WhatsApp messages and informal notes. Formal documents are another story: Devanagari output is usually easier for other Hindi readers to scan.

### How do I convert a Hindi meeting recording to text?

Upload the recording to an AI transcription tool that supports Hindi, or use an AI meeting assistant like Lynkk to capture future meetings automatically with transcript, summary and tasks. With older recordings, keep audio clear. Very long files may need splitting into parts if your tool caps file length.
