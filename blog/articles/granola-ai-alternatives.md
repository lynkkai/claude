# Granola AI Alternatives That Work for US Teams in 2026

*By Samarth Wagh, Co-founder, Lynkk.ai · Last updated 17 October 2026 · Pricing checked 17 October 2026*

> **Quick answer:** The best Granola AI alternatives in 2026 are **Lynkk** (bot or bot-free capture plus tasks and Jira, $0 to $15), **Jamie** (bot-free, EU-hosted), **Notion AI Meeting Notes** (inside Notion Business, $20 per member), **Fathom** (unlimited free recordings), **Fireflies** (CRM sync from $10 billed yearly) and **Meetily** (local and open source).

*Disclosure: Lynkk publishes this guide and is one of the tools reviewed. We checked prices on each vendor's site.*

Granola earned its fans by doing one thing well: it listens through your computer, never sends a bot, and turns your scrappy notes into clean ones. So why are people searching for Granola AI alternatives?

Usually because their needs grew. The free plan only keeps 30 days of history. Teams want shared notes, video, or tasks that land in Jira. And some people still believe Granola is Mac-only, which is out of date.

This guide covers 7 alternatives and when each one beats Granola.

## Why do people look for Granola AI alternatives?

The top reason is the free plan: Granola Basic keeps only 30 days of meeting history and limits AI chat to its "Auto" model. Unlimited history starts at $14 per user per month on Business.

Other reasons we see:

- **No recording to go back to.** Granola writes notes but does not keep video. If you need to replay a moment, you need another tool.
- **Team workflows.** Shared folders exist, but call review, coaching and ticketing need other software.
- **Follow-up work.** Notes are great; the Jira ticket and follow-up email still need writing.
- **Habit.** Granola needs the app running during the call. Forget, and nothing is captured.

## Does Granola work on Windows, Android or Linux?

Granola now runs on Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android. Android arrived on 1 July 2026 for in-person conversations, so older "Granola for Android" alternatives lists are out of date. There is no Linux app.

| Platform | Granola support (Oct 2026) |
|---|---|
| Mac | Yes, video calls and in-person |
| Windows | Yes, video calls |
| iPhone | Yes, phone calls and in-person |
| Android | Yes, in-person conversations |

If you are on Linux, a local open-source tool or a browser-based recorder is your best route. Lynkk's web app and Chrome extension work from any desktop browser.

## Is there a free Granola AI alternative?

Yes. Fathom gives unlimited free recordings, Otter gives 300 free minutes a month, Jamie gives 10 free notes and Lynkk gives 5 free notes a month. Meetily is free if you run it yourself.

The best free pick depends on your bottleneck. If you hit Granola's 30-day history limit, Fathom or Lynkk keep your notes longer. If you want no bot at all, Jamie or Meetily match Granola's approach.

## Which Granola alternative is most private?

The most private Granola alternatives are local tools like Meetily, where audio never leaves your machine. Among cloud tools, look for bot-free capture, a data region you choose, and a no-training promise.

- **Lynkk:** bot-free capture via Mac app or Chrome extension, AES-256 encryption, and data pinned to 1 of 6 regions (US, EU, UK, India, Singapore, Australia).
- **Jamie:** bot-free, EU hosting, states no model training on your data.
- **Granola itself:** bot-free and lets every plan opt out of model training. If privacy is your only concern, Granola may already be enough.
