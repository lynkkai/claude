# Notion AI Note Taker Review 2026: How AI Meeting Notes Works for US Teams

*By [Samarth Wagh](https://lynkk.ai/blog/authors/samarth-wagh), Co-founder, Lynkk.ai · Last updated 17 October 2026 · Pricing checked 17 October 2026*

> **Quick answer:** The Notion note taker, called **AI Meeting Notes**, records meetings from the Notion desktop app without a bot and writes the summary into a Notion page. It is included on the **Business plan ($20 per member per month)** and Enterprise. Free and Plus get only a limited trial. Usage is capped at **10 hours per user per day**.

*Disclosure: Lynkk publishes this review and makes a competing AI meeting assistant. Facts come from Notion's [pricing page](https://www.notion.com/pricing) and [help center](https://www.notion.com/help/ai-meeting-notes), checked 17 October 2026.*

The Notion note taker is a built-in feature, AI Meeting Notes, that records and summarizes a meeting straight into a Notion page. If your team already lives in Notion, that sounds ideal: your notes land next to your docs, tasks and wikis, with no new tool to buy. And for many teams it is ideal. Others find a few limits matter more than the convenience, which is why Notion rarely tops a list of [AI note takers](https://lynkk.ai/blog/best-ai-note-takers).

What follows is based on Notion's own pricing page and help center, checked in October 2026: how it works, what Notion AI note taker pricing looks like, and where it stops.

## What is the Notion AI note taker?

Notion's AI note taker is a feature called AI Meeting Notes. From the Notion desktop app, it transcribes a meeting, then writes a summary and action items into the Notion page where you started it. In Notion's words, it automates "your meeting notes and follow-ups, no bot needed."

Because it lives inside Notion, the notes inherit everything Notion does well:

- **Placement:** notes sit in the project page, wiki or database where they belong.
- **Search:** Notion AI can search and answer questions across your workspace, including meeting notes.
- **Sharing:** page permissions decide who sees the notes.

## How does Notion's AI note taker work?

The Notion note taker starts in 3 steps: type `/meet` on a page in the Notion desktop app, click **Start transcribing** when the meeting begins, and click **Stop** when it ends. A summary then appears automatically.

A few details from Notion's help center, which also lists browser and mobile capture:

1. **Desktop app for calls.** The browser and phone apps record only your microphone, so other participants go uncaptured if you wear headphones. Typing `/meet` on the web prompts you to open the desktop app.
2. **Consent is on you.** Starting transcription confirms everyone has agreed to be recorded.
3. **Minimum length.** Notion needs at least 300 transcribed characters, roughly 1 minute of speech, to write a summary.
4. **Daily cap.** Each user can transcribe up to 10 hours a day.

Since no bot joins, it works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams or any app that plays audio on your computer, in the way a [Zoom note taker](https://lynkk.ai/blog/zoom-note-taker) without a bot does. Mac users need macOS 13 or later and desktop app version 4.7.0 or higher.

## Which languages does Notion's note taker support?

Notion's help center lists 16 languages: English, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Thai, Vietnamese, Danish, Finnish, Norwegian, Dutch and Swedish. Speaker labeling works in English only. Hindi does not appear on that list.

## Does Notion's AI note taker work with Zoom and Google Meet?

Yes. The Notion note taker records audio from your computer instead of joining as a bot, so it works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and other meeting apps. Use the desktop app to capture the full conversation.

The trade-offs of this approach:

- **No bot to remove.** Clients will not see a recorder join.
- **You must start it.** If you forget to type `/meet`, nothing is captured.
- **Only your device.** Colleagues who did not start it on their machine have no recording of their own, unlike a shared [Google Meet transcript](https://lynkk.ai/blog/google-meet-transcript).

## Where are Notion AI meeting notes stored, and who can see them?

Notion AI meeting notes are stored in the page where you typed `/meet`, so the page's existing permissions decide who can read them, and notes you create are private to you by default. Workspace owners can set automatic transcript deletion, and the pricing page lists AI Meeting Notes transcript deletion under Enterprise admin controls.

Three practical rules for teams:

1. **Start notes in the right page.** A meeting started in a private page stays private. One started in a team wiki is visible to everyone with wiki access.
2. **Set a retention schedule.** If legal or HR conversations are recorded, agree on how long transcripts stay before you roll the feature out.
3. **Keep consent visible.** Notion can play an audio consent message, and owners can enforce it for every member, so switch that on rather than relying on memory.

## How do you get better results from the Notion note taker?

Most weak notes out of the Notion note taker come from 4 fixable habits: starting transcription late, poor audio, no agenda, and never reviewing the summary. Fix those and the Notion note taker improves noticeably.

- **Start before people talk.** Type `/meet` and click Start transcribing as the call opens, not after the first decision.
- **Use a headset or good mic.** Notion records your computer's audio, so a muffled microphone means a muffled transcript.
- **Add the agenda to the page first.** The summary is easier to scan when it sits under the agenda you planned.
- **Check numbers and names.** AI summaries can misplace a figure. Fix prices, dates and owners before sharing the page.
- **Move action items into your task system.** If you plan in a Notion database, copy each action item into it with an owner and date.

## Where does Notion's note taker fall short?

The Notion note taker falls short in 4 areas. Calls need the desktop app for both sides of the audio, and use is capped at 10 hours a day; speakers are labeled only in English, and nothing preps you before calls or files tickets after them.

Action is the bigger gap for many teams. The notes are good, but turning decisions into Jira tickets or chasing owners still takes manual work. Notion tasks help if your team plans in Notion; teams planning in Jira need another step, which is where [tasks with owners and due dates](https://lynkk.ai/features/action-items) earn their keep.

## FAQ

### Is the Notion AI note taker free?

No. It comes with Business ($20 per member per month) and Enterprise, while Free and Plus get a limited trial. Two details the plan grid hides: eligible mobile subscriptions that bundle Notion AI include it too, and a workspace owner can switch AI Meeting Notes off for everyone.

### How do I use Notion's AI note taker?

Type `/meet` on a page in the desktop app, click Start transcribing, then Stop. Notion writes the summary into the page, and you can click Resume transcription to carry on. Agenda notes typed under Notes before the call feed into the summary, and uploads work too: AAC, M4A, MP3 and WAV, but not MP4 or MOV.

### Does Notion AI take meeting notes without a bot?

Yes, no bot joins: Notion records your system audio and mic through the desktop app. It even notices when another app is using your microphone and offers to take notes, a prompt you can turn off under Settings, Notifications. Nothing is captured unless you start it.

### Can Notion transcribe Zoom and Google Meet calls?

Yes, through the desktop app. Two gotchas: in a browser, conferencing audio is lost if you wear headphones, and on a virtual desktop Notion hears only your mic unless IT installs its local plugin. For Google Meet, a Chrome add-on brings speaker labels to group calls.

### Is Notion's note taker better than Granola?

Notion suits teams already on Business who want notes beside their docs. Granola costs $14 against $20 and adds pre-meeting briefs and follow-up emails on its paid tier, neither of which Notion lists. Granola Basic keeps only 30 days of history, though, while Notion notes stay as pages.

### Does Notion's AI note taker support Hindi?

Hindi is not among the 16 languages Notion's help center lists as of October 2026. Lynkk covers Hindi and adds a Hinglish setting for mid-sentence switching, with Tamil, Telugu, Marathi and Bengali in beta. Notion labels speakers in English only, so test any tool on a real multilingual call.
