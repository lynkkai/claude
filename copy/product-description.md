## Feature blurbs
For a features grid or product tour. Each one stands on its own.

**Context before every call**
Lynkk pulls what matters from your past meetings before the next one starts:
what was discussed, what's still open, and who said what. Show up prepared
without digging through old notes.

**A voice assistant in the meeting**
Lynkk joins the call and speaks when you need it. Ask for past context, pull up
a document, or get an answer out loud, without alt-tabbing or breaking the
conversation.

**Follow-up in 30 seconds**
Structured notes, assigned action items, updated CRM fields and new Jira
tickets, ready about 30 seconds after the meeting ends. The busywork is done
before you've opened your next tab.

**A searchable memory of every meeting**
Every conversation becomes part of a knowledge graph you can search. Find the
decision, the number or the promise, no matter which meeting it came from.

**Online and in person**
Not every meeting has a link. Lynkk captures video calls and in-room
conversations alike, and detects and records meetings automatically.

**Multilingual and offline-first**
Lynkk works in the language your meeting actually happens in, and keeps working
when your connection doesn't.

**Choose where your data lives**
Pick a region and every model behind Lynkk processes and answers from that
region. It's built for teams with legal, privacy and compliance requirements.

**Dictation on Mac**
The Lynkk Mac app includes dictation: speak, and text appears at your cursor in
whatever app you're using.

**Connects to your stack**
Connect your calendar, Jira, Calendly, Cal.com and your CRM, and plug in
anything else via MCP.

> Never walk into a call cold again. Before every meeting, Lynkk reminds you

### Founders, managers and consultants
> Back-to-back meetings leave no time to write anything down, let alone follow
> up. Lynkk does it for you. It preps you with context before each call, answers

### Global and multilingual teams
> Your meetings don't all happen in one language, and neither does Lynkk.
> Capture conversations across languages, get instant action items, and keep a
> single searchable record of everything your team discussed, wherever and
> however it was said.

### Interviews, field work and in-person meetings
> Lynkk isn't limited to video calls. Record interviews, site visits, workshops
