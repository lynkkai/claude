# Follow-Up Email After Meeting a Client or Your Team: 8 Examples to Copy

*Last updated: October 1, 2026*

> **Quick answer:** A good follow up email after meeting someone has 4 parts: a thank you, a 2 to 3 line recap, action items with owners and dates, and the next step. Send it within 24 hours, and the same day after a client meeting. Copy one of the 8 examples below, or let Lynkk write the recap from your recording and email you the summary after every meeting.

## What should a follow-up email after a meeting include?

Every follow up email after meeting a client, a colleague or a new contact needs the same 4 parts, in this order. Anything else makes it longer without making it more useful.

| Part | What to write | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Thanks | 1 line, specific to the conversation | 1 sentence |
| Recap | What you discussed and agreed | 2 to 3 lines |
| Action items | Who does what, by when | 1 line each |
| Next step | The next meeting, call or deliverable | 1 sentence |

The action items are the part people act on, so give each one a name and a date. "We'll send the proposal" is vague; "Maya will send the proposal by Wednesday" gets done.

## When should you send a follow-up email after meeting someone?

Send a follow-up within 24 hours of any meeting, and the same day after a client, sales or interview meeting. After 2 days, people remember the conversation differently and your recap starts to feel like a correction.

| Meeting type | Send within |
|---|---|
| Client or sales meeting | Same day |
| Job interview | 24 hours |
| Internal team meeting | 1 hour, while it is fresh |
| Networking or first meeting | 24 to 48 hours |

## What subject line should a follow-up email use?

Use a subject line that names the meeting and the date, so it is easy to find later: 3 to 8 words is enough. Avoid "Following up" on its own; nobody can search for it.

- Recap: Onboarding sync, Oct 1
- Next steps from today's call with Acme
- Great to meet you at the product meetup
- Thank you, and next steps on the proposal
- Action items from our 1:1

## 8 follow-up email after meeting examples to copy

These 8 follow up email after meeting examples cover the meetings people search for most: team recaps, clients, sales calls, new contacts, managers, employees, interviews and no reply. Replace the bracketed parts and send.

### 1. Meeting recap email after a team meeting

This internal recap takes about 2 minutes to write and saves the "what did we agree?" thread later. It is the format most meeting recap email examples follow.

> **Subject:** Recap: [meeting name], [date]
>
> Hi team,
>
> Thanks for a focused meeting. Quick recap:
>
> **Decided:** [decision], because [reason].
>
> **Action items:** [Name] to [task] by [date]. [Name] to [task] by [date].
>
> **Open question:** [question]. [Name] will follow up by [date].
>
> Next check-in is [date]. Reply here if I missed anything.

### 2. Example of follow-up email after meeting a new client

This example of follow up email after meeting a new client confirms 3 things: what they need, what you will deliver, and when you will talk next.

> **Subject:** Thank you, and next steps for [company]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thank you for your time today. It was helpful to hear how your team handles [their problem].
>
> To confirm what we agreed: we will send [deliverable] by [date], and you will share [what they promised] by [date].
>
> I've put [day and time] on the calendar for our next call. Let me know if another time works better.
>
> Best, [Your name]

### 3. Follow-up email after a sales call or demo

After a sales call, recap the customer's goal in their words and list 2 or 3 next steps with dates. Mirroring their language shows you listened.

> **Subject:** Next steps from today's demo
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for walking us through [goal] today. You mentioned [their main concern], so I've attached [resource] that covers it.
>
> **Next steps:** I'll send pricing for [number] seats by [date]. You'll loop in [their colleague] for a technical call on [date].
>
> Talk soon, [Your name]

For more on sales follow-up, see [AI notes for sales calls with next steps and owners](https://lynkk.ai/use-cases/sales-calls).

### 4. Follow-up email after meeting someone at a networking event

A follow up email after meeting someone at an event should be short: 1 detail from the conversation, 1 reason to stay in touch, and 1 easy next step.

> **Subject:** Great to meet you at [event]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I enjoyed our chat about [topic] at [event]. Your point about [detail] stuck with me.
>
> Would you be open to a 20 minute call in the next 2 weeks? Here's my calendar: [link].
>
> Best, [Your name]

### 5. Follow-up email after a meeting with your manager

After a meeting with your manager, write down what you agreed in 3 to 5 lines so you are both working from the same plan. It also gives you a record for your next review.

> **Subject:** Summary of today's conversation
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for the time today. Here's what I took away:
>
> I'll focus on [priority] this month and deliver [result] by [date]. You'll check on [support you asked for].
>
> If I've missed anything, let me know.

### 6. Follow-up email after a 1:1 with an employee

After a 1:1 with someone on your team, recap 2 lists: what they committed to and what you committed to. Managers forget their own commitments more often than they think.

> **Subject:** Our 1:1 on [date]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for the open conversation. Recap of what we agreed:
>
> **You:** [commitment] by [date].
>
> **Me:** [commitment] by [date].
>
> We'll check in on both at our next 1:1 on [date].

### 7. Follow-up email after a job interview

Send an interview follow-up within 24 hours, thank each interviewer, and mention 1 specific moment from the conversation. Keep it under 120 words.

> **Subject:** Thank you for today's interview
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thank you for meeting with me about the [role] position. I especially enjoyed discussing [specific topic], and it made me more excited about [what the team is doing].
>
> Please let me know if I can share anything else. I look forward to hearing about next steps.
>
> Best, [Your name]

### 8. Follow-up email when there is no reply

If there is no reply after 3 to 5 business days, send 1 short nudge that adds something useful rather than just "bumping" the thread.

> **Subject:** Re: [original subject]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Following up on my note from [day]. In case it's useful, here is [new resource or detail].
>
> Is [proposed next step] still a good next step on your side?
>
> Thanks, [Your name]

## How do you write a meeting recap email?

Write a meeting recap email in 4 steps: open with 1 line of context, list the decisions, list the action items with owners and dates, and close with the next meeting. Use the team recap above as your meeting summary template.

1. **Context:** "Recap of today's [meeting]."
2. **Decisions:** each one with the reason.
3. **Action items:** 1 line each, with a name and a date.
4. **Next step:** the date of the next meeting or check-in.

Put the recap in the body of the email, not only in an attachment. Most people never open the attachment.

## Should you send a follow-up email after every meeting?

No. Send a follow-up when there are 2 or more action items, a decision someone outside the meeting needs to know, or an external person in the room. Skip it for quick syncs where nothing changed.

A simple rule: if someone could later ask "who said they'd do that?", send the follow-up email after meeting with them.

## Do follow-up emails need to follow the CAN-SPAM Act?

It depends on 1 question: what is the email's primary purpose? A follow-up that mainly recaps a meeting is usually a relationship message, but if the email's primary purpose is promoting a product, the CAN-SPAM Act's rules for commercial email apply. Those rules include accurate headers, an honest subject line, a postal address and a working way to opt out, according to the [FTC's CAN-SPAM compliance guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business).

Sales teams sending follow-ups at scale should check that guide with their legal team. This is general information, not legal advice.

## FAQ

### How soon should you send a follow-up email after a meeting?
Send a follow-up within 24 hours of a meeting, and the same day after a client, sales or interview meeting. For internal team meetings, within 1 hour is best, while everyone still remembers the same version of what was agreed. After 2 days, recaps start to feel like corrections.

### What is an example of a follow-up email after a meeting?
A simple example has 4 lines: "Thank you for your time today. To confirm, we agreed to [decision]. [Name] will [task] by [date], and I'll [task] by [date]. Our next call is [date]." The 8 examples above cover clients, teams, managers, interviews and new contacts.

### What should the subject line of a follow-up email be?
Use 3 to 8 words that name the meeting and the date, such as "Recap: Onboarding sync, Oct 1" or "Next steps from today's call with Acme". A specific subject line makes the email easy to find later. Avoid "Following up" on its own, because nobody can search for it.

### How do you follow up after meeting someone new?
Send a short email within 24 to 48 hours that mentions 1 detail from your conversation, gives 1 reason to stay in touch and suggests 1 easy next step, such as a 20 minute call. Keep it under 100 words; a long first follow-up is less likely to get a reply.
