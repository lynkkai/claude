## What is Lynkk?

Lynkk turns every meeting, online or in person, into structured, searchable notes, action items, and a knowledge graph. Unlike tools that only help after the call, Lynkk is **one AI across the entire meeting lifecycle**.

| Before | During | After (within ~30 seconds) |
|---|---|---|
| Context from past meetings: what was discussed, what is still open, and who said what. Start ahead, not cold. | Lynkk joins the call and speaks when you need it. Ask for past context, pull up documents, and get answers live, by voice. | Structured notes, assigned action items, updated CRM fields, created Jira tickets, and everything searchable. |

## Highlights

- **Online and in person:** record any conversation, with automatic meeting detection

- **Knowledge graph:** every meeting connected and searchable

- **Data residency:** pick a region, and every model behind Lynkk processes your data there
