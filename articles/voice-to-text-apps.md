## What is a voice to text app?

A voice to text app is software that listens to your voice and types what you say, usually within 1 second of each phrase. Most modern tools add punctuation automatically and let you format text with spoken commands like "new line" or "period."

There are 2 kinds. Dictation software types your own voice into a document or text box, while transcription apps record a conversation with several people and turn it into a transcript. Lynkk does both in 1 app.

## How accurate are voice to text apps?

Voice to text apps are highly accurate for clear, single-speaker dictation, and meeting tools add speaker labels. Lynkk transcribes meetings in 17 languages, and its cloud dictation hears 18 languages on Pro.

Accuracy drops with 3 common problems: background noise, a microphone far from your mouth and speaking too fast. A headset and a steady pace fix most errors.

## How do you dictate faster and with fewer errors?

You can dictate faster with 5 habits that work in every voice to text app. Most people speak faster than they type, so a few small changes to how you dictate pay off quickly.

1. **Use a headset or close microphone.** It cuts background noise.
2. **Speak in full phrases.** Pausing mid-sentence breaks punctuation.
3. **Say punctuation when needed.** "Comma," "period" and "new paragraph" work in most tools.
4. **Dictate first, edit later.** Fixing each word as you go slows you down.
5. **Keep 1 tool for everything.** Switching apps means relearning shortcuts. Lynkk dictation works in every Mac app.

## Why does voice to text matter for accessibility?

Voice to text tools help people who cannot type easily, and US accessibility law treats speech and text alternatives as part of equal access under the 1990 ADA. The [ADA effective communication guidance](https://www.ada.gov/resources/effective-communication/) lists transcription and captioning among the aids organizations may provide.

Federal agencies also follow [Section 508 guidance on captions and transcripts](https://www.section508.gov/create/captions-transcripts/) so audio content is available as text. Lynkk's meeting transcripts make every call readable, not only large events.

## How should a voice to text app handle your data?

A voice to text app should meet 3 privacy basics: process your speech securely, tell you where it goes and never use it to train models without your consent. The [NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework/privacy-framework) is a useful checklist for judging how a vendor manages privacy risk.

Lynkk keeps recordings private to your account and never trains models on your conversations.
