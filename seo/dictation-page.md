## 1. Strategy in one paragraph

Low-authority sites are already ranking for dictation keywords. On page 1 for these
terms you'll find aidictation.com (DA 18, #4 for "ai dictation"), talk-type.com (DA 17),
getvoibe.com (DA 15), embertype.com (DA 8), carelesswhisper.app (DA 6), VoiceInk (DA 28)
and Spokenly (DA 25). So this cluster is winnable without authority. Target the
**"voice to text / speech to text / talk to text + Mac"** family as the primary topic
(4 near-identical queries, ~6,100 searches combined, SD 14-25) and use **"AI dictation"**
as the category term. Put the high-volume listicle terms ("dictation software",
"best dictation app") and the competitor terms ("Wispr Flow alternative") on separate
supporting pages that link back here, so no one page tries to rank for everything.

## 3. What competitors are doing

**SERP evidence (Ubersuggest, US)**

| Query | Who ranks (DA) | Takeaway |
|---|---|---|
| voice to text mac | reddit (92), Apple support (99), **Willow blog (34)**, **VoiceInk home (28)**, **carelesswhisper.app (6)** | A DA 6 how-to page ranks. Mixed intent: half want Apple's built-in dictation, half want an app |
| dictation app for mac | Wirecutter (95), reddit, Willow blog (34), **Spokenly home (25)**, **embertype blog (8)**, VoiceInk (28) | Homepages with "AI Dictation for Mac" in the title rank |
| ai dictation | Substack (92), Zapier (82), **aidictation.com (18)**, Willow blog (34), laxis.com (43) | Exact-match title + low DA = #4 |
| dictation software | Zapier (82), **Willow blog (34)**, reddit, Wirecutter, **talk-type.com (17)**, **getvoibe.com (15)**, **dictationdaddy (11)** | Listicles win, but low-DA product sites get in |
| wispr flow alternative | reddit, saner.ai (32), **getvoibe.com (15)**, eesel.ai (37), wisprflow.ai own page | Everyone publishes an "alternatives" page, including Wispr itself |

**How the main competitors set up their pages**

- **Wispr Flow** ([wisprflow.ai](https://wisprflow.ai/), [features](https://wisprflow.ai/features)):
  leads with "works in every app" and "4x faster than typing". Feature blocks: removes
  filler words and adds punctuation automatically, understands you when you change your
  mind mid-sentence, 100+ languages, personal dictionary, voice shortcuts/snippets,
  coding support, syncs across Mac, Windows, iPhone, Android. Separate pages for
  pricing, students, `/comparison/superwhisper-alternative`. **Since August 2026 it also
  has a bot-free meeting Notetaker.** Full breakdown in `seo/wispr-flow-teardown.md`.
- **VoiceInk** ([tryvoiceink.com](https://tryvoiceink.com/faq)): title tag is literally
  "VoiceInk: The Best Dictation App for macOS". Sells on privacy (runs locally on your
  Mac), "Power Mode" (different settings per app), AI cleanup, and a one-time price.
  Has a separate FAQ page and a "best Typeless alternatives" page.
- **Superwhisper**: gets most of its non-brand traffic from a `/best-speech-to-text`
  listicle on its own domain (ranks for "voice to text applications", 4,400 vol) and
  `/vs/wispr-flow`, `/vs/macwhisper` comparison pages.
- **Willow Voice**: blog listicles ("Best AI Dictation Software", "best voice dictation
  software mac") that rank on page 1 for "dictation software" at DA 34.

**What this means for Lynkk**

1. Put the platform plus the category in the title tag ("AI Dictation for Mac"), the way
   VoiceInk and Spokenly do.
2. Cover the usual checklist (works in any app, cleans up filler words, punctuation,
   multilingual, privacy) so Google sees a complete page.
3. Win on what a passive notetaker doesn't do. VoiceInk, Superwhisper and Willow don't
   cover meetings at all, but Wispr Flow now does (bot-free notes, summaries, MCP). So
   "dictation plus notes in one app" is not enough on its own. Lead with **context before
   the call, a live voice assistant during it, CRM and Jira updates after it, in-person
   meetings, and data residency**.
4. Include a "Lynkk vs built-in Mac dictation" section. Half the people searching
   "voice to text mac" are looking at Apple's tool, and this section is how you reach them.

**[H3] What is AI dictation?**
AI dictation turns your speech into text and then uses AI to clean it up: it removes
filler words, adds punctuation and formats the result so it reads like you typed it.
Older dictation software only transcribes word for word.

**[H3] What is the best dictation app for Mac?**
It depends on what you need. Apple's built-in dictation is free and good for short
messages. If you write a lot of email, Slack and docs, an AI dictation app like Lynkk
gives you cleaner text in every app. And if you also take meetings, Lynkk covers
dictation and meeting notes in one app.

**[H3] How do I use voice to text on a Mac?**
With Apple's built-in tool, go to System Settings > Keyboard > Dictation and turn it on,
then press the dictation key (or the Fn key twice) in any text field. With Lynkk,
install the Mac app, allow microphone and accessibility access, then press your Lynkk
shortcut and speak.

**[H3] What is the Mac dictation shortcut?**
For Apple's built-in dictation, the default is pressing the Fn (Globe) key twice, or the
microphone key on newer keyboards. You can change it under System Settings > Keyboard >
Dictation. Lynkk uses its own shortcut, **[confirm default]**, which you can customize.

**[H3] Why is Mac dictation not working?**
The usual causes are: dictation is switched off in System Settings > Keyboard, the wrong
microphone is selected, the app doesn't have microphone permission (System Settings >
Privacy & Security > Microphone), or the language isn't supported. If built-in
dictation keeps failing in certain apps, a system-wide dictation app like Lynkk is
an alternative.
