# Lynkk: Meta Ads Plan (USA, goal: sign-ups)

Prepared 2026-10-23 for the drafted site at
https://lynkk-revenue-story.imsjwagh99.chatgpt.site/ (CTA "Start free" goes to lynkk.ai/sign-up).

Cost figures below are planning assumptions taken from public benchmarks (listed under Sources at the end).
Replace them with your own account data after week 2.

---

## 1. Summary

- **Who to target first:** people whose income depends on conversations, who buy their own tools,
  and who meet clients **in person** as well as on Zoom. Meta is good at reaching these people and poor at
  reaching enterprise buyers. In-person capture is also where Lynkk differs from Fathom, Fireflies and Gong,
  which mostly work as meeting bots on video calls.
- **Priority niches:** (1) real estate agents, (2) insurance agents, (3) startup and SMB sellers and
  founders (the audience the drafted site is written for), plus one cheap test: (4) Hindi/English (Hinglish)
  speaking professionals in the US.
- **Budget:** $100/day for 4 weeks to test (about $3,000), then $150-250/day on the winners for weeks 5-8.
  Total for 60 days: about **$7,500-$10,000**.
- **Expected result:** about 60-120 sign-ups in month 1 (at $25-50 per sign-up), aiming for 200-350 in
  month 2 once cost per sign-up is under $25.
- **Main risk:** with a $19/month Pro plan and a free tier, a sign-up is only worth a few dollars unless a good
  share of sign-ups upgrade. Track sign-up to Pro from day one (section 7).

phone, because agents will ask.

## 3. Target audience research

### Why these niches

Meta works best for products that **one person can sign up for and use alone**, the same day. The best
niches for Lynkk have all of these:

1. Many meetings a week, so they hit the 5-meeting free limit quickly
2. A real cost when something said in a meeting is forgotten (a lost deal, a missed follow-up)
3. Meetings **in person and on the phone**, not only on Zoom
4. They pay for their own tools (independent contractors, small teams)
5. They are active on Facebook and Instagram and reachable with job, employer or interest signals

| Niche | Size (US) | Meetings | In-person | Pays own tools | Meta reach | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Real estate agents | Very large, mostly self-employed (NAR is the largest trade group) | Showings, listing appointments, calls daily | Very high | Yes | Very high | **1** |
| Insurance agents (independent and captive) | ~572,600 jobs (BLS 2025) | Daily client calls and kitchen-table meetings | High | Often | High | **2** |
| Startup and SMB sellers, founders | Large, spread out | Many video calls | Low-medium | Founders yes, reps sometimes | Medium | **3** (matches current site) |
| Hinglish-speaking professionals | Smaller, easy to isolate with language targeting | Mixed | Mixed | Often (small business owners, agents, consultants) | High via language filter | **Test** |
| Financial advisors (solo/RIA) | ~299,400 jobs (BLS 2025) | Client reviews | Medium | Yes | Medium | Later: they need compliance features and recordkeeping that should be confirmed first |
| Field and home-services sales (solar, roofing, HVAC estimators) | Large | In-home consultations | Very high | Company often pays | Medium | Later test |
| Consultants, coaches, recruiters | Large | Many client calls | Low-medium | Yes | Medium | Later test |

### Niche 1: Real estate agents (start here)

- **Demographics:** median age 57, about 66% women (NAR 2026 Member Profile). Newer members (2 years or less in
  the business) have a median age of 45 and are more open to new tools.
- **Ad targeting:** ages 28-65, all genders (the data will shift delivery toward women on its own, so don't force it).
- **Pain in their words:** "What did the buyer say about the kitchen?", "I forgot to send the comps",
  "I spend my evenings writing up notes and updating my CRM."
- **Why Lynkk fits:** records showings and listing appointments in person, remembers what each client said
  across every visit, drafts the follow-up text or email, and preps you before the next meeting.
- **Proof the niche buys this:** Plaud (AI voice recorder) runs a dedicated real estate page around
  showing notes, buyer preferences and CRM updates. Lynkk can say the same thing without a $169 device.
- **Compliance note:** this is software for agents, not a housing ad. Keep homes for sale, prices and
  listings out of the creative, so the ads are not treated as Special Ad Category (Housing), which would
  remove age and targeting controls.

### Niche 2: Insurance agents

- **Demographics:** about 53% women, average age 45, most workers between 25 and 44 (Data USA, 2024).
- **Ad targeting:** ages 25-60, all genders.
- **Pain:** quoting calls and policy reviews where the details (dependents, current coverage, renewal dates)
  get lost; slow follow-up loses the sale to the next quote.
- **Why Lynkk fits:** phone and in-person capture, "who said what" for every household, follow-up drafts,
  reminders before renewal conversations.
- **Angle:** "Your follow-up is your commission."

### Niche 3: Startup and SMB sellers, founders (the site's current audience)

- **Demographics:** sales reps average age about 43, about 69% men (Data USA, services sales reps, 2024).
  SDRs skew younger and male (about 63% men).
- **Ad targeting:** ages 24-50, all genders.
- **Pain:** CRM is always out of date, follow-ups slip, walking into a call without the context from the last one.
- **Why Lynkk fits:** this is exactly the drafted site: Account Memory, CUE live coaching, auto follow-ups, CRM updates.
- **Competition:** Fathom (generous free plan), Fireflies, Otter, Gong at the high end. Lead with what they lack:
  in-person capture, the private live assistant (CUE), and memory across the whole account.

### Test niche: Hinglish-speaking professionals in the US

- There are close to 5 million Indian Americans (US Census estimates), heavily represented in sales, real estate,
  insurance, consulting and small business ownership. Many switch between Hindi and English in client meetings
  and with teams in India.
- Meta's **language** setting is a hard filter (unlike interests), so this audience can be isolated cleanly:
  Location US + Language Hindi (and optionally Gujarati, Punjabi, Telugu, Tamil if Lynkk supports them).
- **Angle:** "Finally, a notetaker that understands Hinglish." Almost no competitor says this.
- Keep it to $10/day. If it wins, it is cheap and defensible.

## 4. Meta targeting setup

Meta now treats job titles and interests as **suggestions** (Advantage+ audience can deliver beyond them),
and many interests were removed in 2025-2026. Detailed-targeting exclusions were also removed in 2025.
So: keep audiences broad, let the creative do the targeting, and **check each interest below exists in Ads
Manager before using it.**

### Settings that apply to every ad set

- **Location:** United States, "People living in this location"
- **Language:** leave blank (except the Hinglish ad set)
- **Placements:** Advantage+ placements, but exclude Audience Network (it tends to bring low-quality sign-ups)
- **Exclusions (custom audiences):** existing users (customer list upload), people who completed sign-up (pixel)
- **Attribution:** 7-day click, 1-day view; judge results on 7-day click

### Audience suggestions per ad set

| Ad set | Age | Job titles / employers (suggestions) | Interests (suggestions, verify in Ads Manager) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 Real estate agents | 28-65 | Real estate agent, Realtor, Real estate broker, Listing agent; employers Keller Williams, eXp Realty, RE/MAX, Coldwell Banker, Compass, Century 21 | National Association of Realtors, Zillow Premier Agent, Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, real estate brokerage |
| A2 Insurance agents | 25-60 | Insurance agent, Insurance broker, Agency owner, Financial representative; employers State Farm, Allstate, Farmers Insurance, New York Life, Northwestern Mutual, Primerica | Life insurance, insurance agency, NAIFA |
| A3 Startup and SMB sellers | 24-50 | Account executive, Sales representative, Sales development representative, Founder, Business development manager | Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Apollo.io, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Gong |
| A4 Broad control | 25-55 | None | None (Advantage+ audience with no suggestions; the creative does the targeting) |
| B1 Hinglish test | 22-55 | None | Language = Hindi (hard filter); optional interests: entrepreneurship, small business, real estate |
| C1 Retargeting | 18-65 | Website visitors 30 days who did not sign up; video viewers 50%+; Instagram/Facebook page engagers 90 days | |

**Lookalikes, later:** once there are 100+ sign-ups from ads (better 500+), build a 1% lookalike of
"sign-ups who recorded a meeting" and test it against A4.

## 5. Campaign structure and budget

### Tracking setup (week 0, before spending anything)

1. Install the Meta Pixel on the site and lynkk.ai/sign-up, plus the Conversions API (server side).
2. Fire `CompleteRegistration` when an account is created. This is the event campaigns optimize for.
3. Also send two custom events from the server: `FirstMeetingRecorded` (activation) and `SubscribePro`
   (paid). These are what you will optimize toward later.
4. Verify the domain in Business Manager.
5. UTMs on every ad: `utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign={{campaign.name}}&utm_content={{ad.name}}`
6. Upload the existing user list as a custom audience to exclude.

### Phase 1: test (weeks 1-4), $100/day, about $3,000

Objective: **Leads**, conversion location Website, optimize for `CompleteRegistration`, CTA button **Sign Up**.

| Campaign | Budget type | Ad set | Daily |
|---|---|---|---|
| LYNKK_US_Prospecting_NicheTest | Ad set budgets (ABO) so each niche gets a fair test | A1 Real estate | $25 |
| | | A2 Insurance | $20 |
| | | A3 Startup/SMB sellers | $20 |
| | | A4 Broad control | $15 |
| LYNKK_US_Hinglish_Test | ABO | B1 Hinglish | $10 |
| LYNKK_US_Retargeting | ABO | C1 Retargeting | $10 |
| **Total** | | | **$100** |

Each prospecting ad set gets 3-4 ads (section 6). Don't touch anything for the first 5-7 days.

**Note on the learning phase:** Meta wants about 50 conversions per ad set per week to leave learning.
At $25-50 per sign-up that would need $175-350/day per ad set, so in phase 1 the ad sets will stay "in
learning". That's expected: phase 1 is about finding which niche and message win, not about efficiency.

### Phase 1 decision rules

- After an ad set spends **$150 with 0 sign-ups**, pause it.
- After **$300**, pause any ad set whose cost per sign-up is more than 1.5x the best ad set.
- Pause individual ads with link CTR under 0.7% after 2,000 impressions.
- At the end of week 2: move the paused budget into the best niche. At the end of week 4: pick 1-2 winning niches.

### Phase 2: scale (weeks 5-8), $150-250/day, about $4,500-7,000

- Merge the winning niche ad sets into one campaign with **campaign budget (Advantage+ campaign budget)**,
  so Meta can push spend to the best ad set.
- Raise budget by no more than 20% every 3-4 days to avoid resetting learning.
- Retargeting rises to 15-20% of spend.
- Add the 1% lookalike once the source audience is big enough.
- Once `FirstMeetingRecorded` reaches about 50 a week, test optimizing for it instead of sign-ups. Cost per
  result will go up, but you will get users who actually use the product.

### Budget options

| Plan | Daily | 60-day total | Use when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lean | $50 | about $3,000 | Cash is tight. Run only A1, A3 and retargeting. Results take twice as long. |
| **Recommended** | $100, then $150-250 | **$7,500-10,000** | Enough to compare niches and scale one. |
| Growth | $250, then $500 | $22,000+ | Only after phase 1 shows sign-up to Pro of 5% or more. |

## 6. Creative and copy

**Formats, in order of priority:**
1. **9:16 vertical video (Reels/Stories), 15-30 seconds**, phone-filmed, a real person talking. Example:
   an agent in their car after a showing, opening Lynkk to see the buyer's notes and the drafted follow-up.
2. **Screen-recorded demo** with captions: a meeting goes in, notes, action items and a follow-up draft come out.
3. **Static before/after:** "What your CRM says" vs "What the customer actually said" (from the site).
4. **Carousel:** the site's day-in-the-life: 9:00 AM prep, 2:00 PM call with CUE, 2:52 PM follow-up ready.

Always add captions (most people watch with sound off) and put the hook in the first 2 seconds.

### Hooks and copy by niche

Copy follows Meta's personal attributes policy: talk about the problem, don't tell the viewer who they are
("Are you a realtor?" style lines get ads rejected or limited).

**Real estate**
- Hook: "The buyer told you exactly what they want. Three showings later, do you still remember?"
- Primary text: Lynkk records your showings and client calls, remembers what every buyer said, and drafts
  your follow-up before you reach the car. Works in person and on the phone. Start free, no credit card.
- Headline: Never forget what a client said

**Insurance**
- Hook: "Your follow-up is your commission."
- Primary text: Lynkk captures every quote call and kitchen-table meeting, remembers each household's details,
  and drafts the follow-up while the client is still deciding. 5 meetings a month free, no credit card.
- Headline: Follow up faster, close more policies

**Startup and SMB sellers**
- Hook: "Your CRM says 'budget approved'. Your customer said 'budget is on hold'."
- Primary text: Lynkk remembers every customer conversation and turns it into follow-ups, CRM updates and next
  steps. Private live coaching during the call. Online, in person, 15+ languages. Start free.
- Headline: Your revenue lives in your conversations

**Hinglish**
- Hook: "Finally, a meeting notetaker that understands Hinglish."
- Primary text: Half the meeting in Hindi, half in English? Lynkk gets all of it and gives you clean notes,
  action items and follow-ups in English. Start free.
- Headline: Notes for how you actually talk

**Retargeting**
- "Still writing meeting notes by hand? Your first 5 meetings a month are free."
- Use the demo video or a feature-by-feature carousel.

**Creative guardrails**
- Don't show or imply secret recording. Many US states require everyone's consent to record (for example
  California, Florida, Illinois, Pennsylvania, Washington). Show the user telling the client they're taking AI notes.
- Only name integrations that work today. The site lists Pipedrive as "coming soon", so don't name it in ads.
  Confirm which CRMs work before naming any (Salesforce, HubSpot, Follow Up Boss).
- Don't promise things that aren't on the landing page. Ad and page must say the same thing.

## 7. Landing pages and the numbers that matter

### Where to send traffic

The drafted site is written for B2B sellers ("pipeline review", "RevOps"). A real estate or insurance agent
landing there will feel it's not for them. Options, from best to quickest:

1. **Best:** one landing page per niche (`/real-estate`, `/insurance`) with the same layout and a swapped hero,
   example and FAQ.
2. **Quick:** change the hero headline and subheadline based on `utm_campaign`.
3. **Test:** for retargeting, send people straight to `lynkk.ai/sign-up`.

### KPIs and targets

| Metric | Week 1-2 target | Week 5-8 target |
|---|---|---|
| Link CTR | 0.8%+ | 1.2%+ |
| Cost per link click | under $2.50 | under $1.50 |
| Landing page to sign-up | 10%+ | 15%+ |
| **Cost per sign-up** | **$25-50** | **under $25** |
| Sign-up to first meeting recorded (7 days) | 40%+ | 50%+ |
| Sign-up to Pro (60 days) | measure | 5-10% |

### Unit economics check

A sign-up is worth (share who upgrade) x $19 x (months they stay). For example:

| Sign-up to Pro | Months on Pro | Value per sign-up |
|---|---|---|
| 5% | 12 | $11.40 |
| 10% | 12 | $22.80 |
| 10% | 24 | $45.60 |

So at $30 per sign-up, you need roughly 10% of sign-ups to upgrade and stay 1-2 years for ads to pay back.
Freemium tools on average convert around 5%, so the onboarding and the 5-meeting limit need to do real work.
For agents who meet clients every day, the limit is hit in the first week, which helps. Review this table at
the end of month 2 before raising spend.

## 8. Pre-launch checklist

- [ ] Pixel + Conversions API installed; `CompleteRegistration`, `FirstMeetingRecorded`, `SubscribePro` firing
- [ ] Domain verified; existing users uploaded as exclusion audience
- [ ] Sign-up works on mobile with Google sign-in; welcome email explains how to record the first meeting
- [ ] At least one form of social proof on the page (beta user quotes, number of meetings processed, a short founder video)
- [ ] Niche hero variants for real estate and insurance (or UTM-based headline swap)
- [ ] Confirm: mobile app for in-person recording, which CRMs work today, whether Spanish is supported
      (if yes, a Spanish-language ad set is worth testing next), privacy and consent wording
- [ ] 3-4 ads per niche ready, captions on all videos
- [ ] Facebook page and Instagram account branded "Lynkk" (double k) with a few posts, since people check before signing up
- [ ] Meta Ad Library checked for current ads from Fathom, Fireflies, Otter, Granola and Plaud to avoid copying their angles

## 9. Week-by-week timeline

| Week | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0 | Tracking, landing page fixes, creative production |
| 1 | Launch phase 1. Don't change anything for 5-7 days. |
| 2 | First cuts using the decision rules. Move budget to the best niche. Add 2 new ads to the best ad set. |
| 3 | Refresh creative on anything with rising cost. Check sign-up to activation by niche, not just cost per sign-up. |
| 4 | Pick 1-2 winning niches. Build the lookalike if there's enough data. |
| 5-6 | Phase 2: merge winners into one campaign budget, raise spend 20% every 3-4 days. |
| 7-8 | Test optimizing for `FirstMeetingRecorded`. Review unit economics before going beyond $250/day. |
