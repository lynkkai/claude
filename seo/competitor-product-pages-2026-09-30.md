## How competitors were selected

A page is listed only if it came from a tool result in this session:

- **[SERP]**: appeared in an Ubersuggest `serp_analysis` result (US, English) for a keyword that matches the
  Lynkk feature. The position and keyword are shown so you can see how strong the evidence is.
- **[WEB]**: found through a web search when Ubersuggest SERPs had no product pages for that feature
  (knowledge-graph and workspaces). These pages exist and describe a matching feature, but there is **no
  evidence they rank** for anything.

Only product, feature, integration or store pages are listed. Blogs, listicles, reddit and help articles are
left out unless no product page was found.

**Not verified:** none of these pages could be opened (competitor sites are blocked by this environment's
network policy). Titles are the search result titles. What each page actually says is **[UNVERIFIED]**. Dashes in
competitor titles were changed to hyphens per the repo writing rule.

## Patterns across features

- **Otter, Read AI, Fireflies and Fellow** show up for 4 or more of the 6 features (listed above). They are the
  competitors with a matching page for almost every Lynkk feature. [SERP + WEB]
- **Platforms compete with you directly** on three features: Google Meet (chrome-extension), Zoom (meeting
  notes/bot-free), Slack (slack). Their built-in AI notes rank above independent tools. [SERP]
- **Chrome Web Store listings rank** as their own results (Scribbl #1, Fireflies #4 for "ai note taker chrome
  extension"). [SERP]
- **Wispr Flow** competes on dictation *and* sells a note taker (its title says "AI Notetaker"), so it overlaps
  with two Lynkk features. [SERP]
