# YouTube Competitor Research: What Ranks for Lynkk's P1 Topics

> Pulled 2026-10-26 with yt-dlp YouTube search (top 8 results per topic, US). Metadata only: titles, lengths, view counts, channels.
> Transcripts were not downloaded. YouTube asked for a sign-in from this cloud environment, so the `youtube-transcript` skill can't run here. Run it on your own computer to pull transcripts (see the end of this file).
> Topic numbers match `video/lynkk-youtube-longform-seo-plan.md`.

## Takeaways

1. **Competitors are already doing this.** Tactiq shows up on four of the ten topics (Zoom AI Companion, Teams, ChatGPT minutes, recording Zoom) with short tutorials. Bluedot, Fellow, Noota, Equal Time and VoiceToNotes also publish on these topics. This confirms the plan, and it means Lynkk needs a clear angle to stand out.
2. **Under 5 minutes is competitive for how-to topics.** Most ranking videos for Zoom AI Companion (16), Teams (17), ChatGPT minutes (19) and recording Zoom (21) run 1:30-6:45. Our 3-4.5 minute cuts fit right in.
3. **Roundups and comparisons run longer.** For "best AI note taker" (1), "best free" (2) and "Fireflies vs Otter vs Fathom" (13), the top videos mostly run 6-20 minutes. The 3:13 VoiceToNotes roundup proves short can rank, but our 4-5 minute roundups need a tight format: one strong verdict, about 30 seconds per tool, and the full table in the description.
4. **Big, old videos own "meeting minutes" (20).** The top results have 130K-770K views and are years old. Views don't drive AI citations, but this will be the slowest to rank on YouTube itself. Lean on the template download and the AI angle to differ.
5. **In-person (3) is wide open.** The ranking videos have 557-4,861 views, and one is a 1-minute clip. A solid 4-minute demo can compete quickly.
6. **Nobody leads with the sales angle.** No ranking video for these topics is built around account memory, follow-ups or sales calls. That's Lynkk's lane: say it in titles and thumbnails.
7. **Title patterns that rank:**
   - Year in the title: "(2026)", "in 2026", "2026 Guide".
   - Proof words: "Ranked & Tested", "I Tested 5 Tools in a Real Meeting", "Honest Review", "No Marketing Hype, Just Testing".
   - Format words for how-tos: "Step-by-Step", "Beginner Tutorial", "As Participant or Host".
   - Capture-method words: "Bot-Free", "Botless", "Privacy-First".
   - Curiosity: "90% of People Choose WRONG", "Stop Paying for Otter.ai".
   - Keep titles honest. Only claim "tested" if the video shows a real test.

## Getting the transcripts (run on your own computer)

The `youtube-transcript` skill is installed in this repo at `.claude/skills/youtube-transcript/`. YouTube blocks caption downloads from cloud servers, so run it from Claude Code on your own computer:

1. Open this repo in Claude Code on your laptop.
2. Ask: "Get the transcript of https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID". Start with the links in "Videos to study".
3. Save the `.txt` transcripts in a `research/transcripts/` folder and push, or upload them here.

With transcripts, we can map each top video's hook, structure and talking points, and tighten the Lynkk scripts against them.
