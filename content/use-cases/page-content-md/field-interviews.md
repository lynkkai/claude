#### What is a field interview?

A field interview is a conversation you record out in the world: on a farm, in a shop, or in someone's home. These places often have no internet, and people may switch between languages. Lynkk records on your Mac without internet and writes the note when you're back online.

**Who it's for**
- Researchers and NGOs doing field studies
- Journalists reporting on the ground
- Market research teams visiting towns and villages

#### A voice recorder for Mac that turns interviews into notes

**Record: no signal needed.** The Mac app records the room and saves the recording on your Mac.

**Upload: when you're back online.** The recording uploads on its own, and the note follows.

**Read: transcript and takeaways.** The transcript in the language of the interview, with speaker labels, key takeaways and open questions.

#### Questions about recording field interviews

**Can I record with no internet connection?**

On a Mac, yes. The Mac app saves the recording and uploads it when you're back online. Recording in the browser needs a connection.

**Does Lynkk do Hindi transcription?**

Yes. Lynkk turns Hindi audio to text, and also transcribes Hinglish, the mix of Hindi and English, with speaker labels.

**Which other languages does Lynkk transcribe?**

English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Arabic and Russian, with Tamil,
Telugu, Marathi and Bengali in beta.

**How long can an interview recording be?**

Up to 2 hours per recording. For a longer session, start a new recording.
