## Retitles for existing pages (the low hanging fruit)

These are not new posts. They are title tag and meta rewrites on pages that already rank in the top
10 and earn zero clicks. Higher return per hour than anything above.

| Page | Current performance | New title |
|---|---|---|

| `/blog/hindi-transcription` | 371 impr, pos 8.15, 0 clicks | **Hindi and Hinglish Transcription: How to Get Accurate Text From Mixed-Language Audio** |
| `/blog/board-meeting-agenda` | 428 impr, pos 23.0 | **Board Meeting Agenda Templates (Free, AI-Ready)** |
| `/blog/daily-standup-meeting` | 674 impr, pos 26.6 | **Daily Standup Meeting Agenda: Format, Template and Examples** |
| `/blog/action-items-examples-template` | 1,717 impr, pos 29.9 | **Action Items: Examples, Templates and How to Track Them** |
| `/blog/voice-to-text-apps` | 2,001 impr, pos 35.5, 0.10% CTR | **Best Voice to Text Apps in 2026: 9 Tested for Accuracy and Accents** |

### Why each retitle

- **Google Meet:** thirteen of the top queries say "where", not "how". The title must say "where".
- **Voice Memos:** five of the top queries are yes/no ("does voice memos transcribe", "can iphone
  voice memos be transcribed"). Answer yes in the title.
- **Zoom:** fix the `/blog/` vs `/articles/` duplicate before retitling, or the rewrite is wasted.
- **Standup and board agenda:** every query in those clusters wants a template, so the title must
  contain the word.
