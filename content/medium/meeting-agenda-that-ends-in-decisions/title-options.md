# Title and subtitle options

## A. Direct how-to (current choice)

- **Title:** How to Write a Meeting Agenda That Ends in Decisions
- **Subtitle:** Turn every agenda item into a question the meeting has to answer,
  and start with what the last meeting left open.
- **Why:** Contains the target search phrase ("how to write meeting agenda") and
  states the payoff. Best for Google traffic to the Medium post.

## B. Consequential claim

- **Title:** Your Meeting Agenda Lists Topics. It Should List Questions.
- **Subtitle:** A topic has no finish line. A question does. Here is how to rewrite
  an agenda so meetings end with answers.
- **Why:** Stronger in the Medium feed because it names a habit almost every reader
  has. Weaker for search.

## C. Recognizable failure

- **Title:** Why Your Meetings End With "Let's Take This Offline"
- **Subtitle:** The fix starts before the meeting: an agenda built from questions,
  decision owners and last week's open items.
- **Why:** Curiosity hook built on the opening scene. Least search value.

## D. Question

- **Title:** What Should a Meeting Agenda Actually Include?
- **Subtitle:** Questions instead of topics, a label for each item, and five minutes
  for what the last meeting left open.
- **Why:** Matches how people phrase the search. Plainer, and a little generic in a
  feed.

## Recommendation

Keep **A** for search. Use **B** if the piece goes to a Medium publication or is
mainly promoted in the feed.
