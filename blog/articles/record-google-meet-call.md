# How to Record a Google Meet Call for Free in 2026

*By [Samarth Wagh](https://lynkk.ai/blog/authors/samarth-wagh), Co-founder, Lynkk.ai · Last updated 17 October 2026*

> **Quick answer:** Here is how to record a Google Meet call in 4 clicks: click **Meeting tools**, then **Recording**, then **Start recording**, and confirm. The recording saves to the organizer's Google Drive. Built-in recording needs an **eligible Google Workspace plan** or **Google One with 2 TB or more**, and only works on a **computer or Android**. On a free Gmail account, use a free screen recorder or an AI meeting assistant.

*Disclosure: Lynkk publishes this guide and makes an AI meeting assistant mentioned below. Google Meet steps come from [Google's help center](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/9308681), checked 17 October 2026.*

Knowing how to record a Google Meet call sounds simple until the record button is missing. Whether it appears depends on three things (your plan, your admin's settings and your device), and plenty of personal Gmail users never see the option at all.

Below: the built-in method step by step, why recording might be unavailable, and free alternatives for laptops, Android phones and iPhones.

## How do you record a Google Meet call with the built-in recorder?

With an eligible plan, capturing a Google Meet call takes 5 clicks on a computer:

1. Start or join the meeting in Google Meet.
2. At the bottom right, click **Meeting tools**, then **Recording**.
3. Optionally, choose a language for captions.
4. Click **Start recording**, then **Start** in the pop-up.
5. To stop, click **Meeting tools**, then **Recording**, then **Stop recording**, and confirm.

Capture stops automatically after 8 hours or once everyone leaves, and attendees see a notice that the session is being recorded. Only need notes, not video? On eligible plans, Google's [Take notes for me](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/14754931) writes them into a Doc.

## Where do Google Meet recordings go?

Files land in the organizer's Google Drive, inside a "Google Meet" folder with one sub-folder per meeting, and a link also arrives by email, both to the organizer and to whoever pressed record.

Started it but not the organizer? Look in your inbox for that link, not your own Drive.

## Which Google plans can record Google Meet calls?

According to Google's help center, built-in recording comes with these plans:

| Plan type | Can record? |
|---|---|
| Free personal Gmail | No |
| Google One with 2 TB or more storage | Yes |
| Workspace Individual | Yes |
| Business Standard and Business Plus | Yes |
| Enterprise Starter, Standard and Plus | Yes |
| Essentials and Enterprise Essentials | Yes |
| Education Plus (Staff license) and Teaching and Learning Upgrade | Yes |

Workspace accounts need an administrator to switch recording on first. Business Starter? Absent from Google's list.

## How do you record a Google Meet call for free?

Account can't record? Here are 3 free ways to record a Google Meet call on a laptop:

1. **Windows Game Bar.** Press **Windows + G**, then use the capture widget, or press **Windows + Alt + R** to record the active Chrome window with its audio.
2. **OBS Studio.** A free, open-source recorder for Windows and Mac that captures the screen, system audio and your microphone. It takes 10 minutes to set up.
3. **Mac screen recording.** Press **Command + Shift + 5** to record the screen. macOS does not capture other people's call audio by default, so pair it with a tool that records system audio.

Tell people before you hit record. Meet displays a notice whenever its own recorder runs, but third-party screen recorders stay silent, which leaves the announcement to you, and several US states require every participant's consent.

## How do you record a Google Meet call on Android and iPhone?

Android users get built-in recording through the same Meeting tools menu, provided the plan allows it, but iPhone is different: Google's help center says recording only works on a computer or Android device. So iOS lacks the option entirely.

| Device | Built-in Meet recording | Free workaround |
|---|---|---|
| Laptop (Windows or Mac) | Yes, on eligible plans | Game Bar, OBS Studio |
| Android phone | Yes, on eligible plans | Android screen recorder |
| iPhone or iPad | No | Join from a laptop, or use an AI note taker |

iPhone's screen recorder may not reliably pick up other participants' audio. Test it before anything important.

## How do you record a Google Meet call with audio?

Meet's native recorder always captures every participant's audio. Screen recorders are where sound goes missing, since they often grab your microphone yet skip everyone else on the call. Before relying on one, check these 3 settings:

1. **Windows Game Bar:** in Game Bar settings, choose to record all audio, not just the game or app, if you also want your microphone.
2. **OBS Studio:** add both a "Desktop Audio" (or application audio) source and a "Mic/Aux" source, then run a 30-second test call.
3. **Mac:** macOS screen recording records your microphone but not system audio by default. Use OBS with application audio capture, or an AI note taker, to capture other speakers.

Play back the first minute of a test clip ahead of any important call. Silence is the most common screen-recorder failure.

## Why can't I record a Google Meet call?

Usually it comes down to 5 common reasons the option is missing or greyed out:

1. **Your plan does not include recording**, for example a free Gmail account or Business Starter.
2. **Your admin turned recording off** for your organization or group.
3. **You are not the host** and the host has not allowed participants to record.
4. **You are on iPhone or iPad**, where built-in recording is not available.
5. **Education restrictions** apply to some school accounts and roles.

Fixes: switch to a laptop, ask the host for permission, or get your Workspace admin to enable recording.

## Does Google Meet record calls automatically?

No. Google Meet does not record calls automatically unless someone starts the recording or an admin or organizer has set it up for that meeting. Gemini's "Take notes for me" can be switched on from the calendar event too. That produces notes, though, not a recording.

## FAQ

### How do I record a Google Meet call for free?

Use a free screen recorder such as Windows Game Bar or OBS Studio, or an AI meeting assistant with a free plan like Lynkk, if your plan does not include recording. Tell participants first. Built-in recording needs an eligible Workspace plan or Google One with 2 TB or more storage.

### Can I record Google Meet on a personal Gmail account?

Not with the built-in recorder, unless you have Google One with 2 TB or more storage. Free Gmail accounts can turn to screen recorders or AI note takers instead. Worth checking: Google One plans at 2 TB or above bundle other Google benefits, so you may already pay for one.

### Where are Google Meet recordings saved?

In the meeting organizer's Google Drive, inside a "Google Meet" folder with a sub-folder for each meeting. An email with the link goes to the organizer and to whoever started recording, and capture ends automatically after 8 hours or when everyone leaves.

### Why is the record button missing in Google Meet?

Usually one of four causes: your plan lacks recording, your admin disabled it, the host hasn't granted permission, or you're on an iPhone, where built-in recording is unavailable. Most cases clear up after switching to a laptop and asking the host for permission. Otherwise, ask your Workspace admin to enable recording for your account.

### Can I record a Google Meet call on my phone?

On Android, yes, if your plan allows it. iPhone is the exception: Google Meet's built-in recording isn't available there, so join from a laptop or use an AI note taker. Android screen recorders also work. Just confirm other people's audio gets captured before an important call.

### Do participants know when a Google Meet call is recorded?

Yes, when you use Google Meet's built-in recorder, participants are notified. Screen recorders alert nobody, so the announcement falls to you. And because several US states require every participant's consent before a call is recorded, say it at the start either way.
