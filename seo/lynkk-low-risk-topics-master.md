### N1, podcast transcription, 2,500 at entry SD 13

The head term `podcast transcription` is 2,900 at SD 56, too hard. But the cluster underneath it is
unusually soft: `podcast transcription example` **880 at SD 13**, `podcast transcription services`
**590 at SD 15**, `spotify podcast transcription` 720 (SD 29), `podcast transcription service` 720
(SD 32), `free podcast transcription` 140 (SD 25), `podcast transcription tool` 90 (SD 28),
`podcast transcription software` 50 (SD 21), `podcast transcription app` 30 (SD 22). Zero
cannibalization: no page on lynkk.ai mentions podcasts. Fit is medium rather than strong, since
podcasters are not Lynkk's stated ICP, but the capability is identical to what the product does.

### N2, Windows note taking app, 1,500 at SD 34, with one condition

`note taking app for windows` 480 (SD 34), `best note taking app for windows` 720 (SD 36), plus
stylus, free and Windows-11 variants. No existing page, and it is the obvious counterpart to the Mac
direction you picked.

**The condition:** your brand memory lists Web plus Mac as confirmed and **Windows availability as
"to confirm"**. If Lynkk does not run on Windows, do not write this as a product pitch. It can still
work as an honest comparison listicle that positions the web app, but that is a weaker page and you
should decide which before commissioning. Confirm Windows support first.

### N3, live captions, 1,400 usable at entry SD 16, with a warning about intent

The viable subset: `live captions app` 590 (SD 23), `live captions translator` 390 (SD 30),
`live captions extension` 170 (SD 20), `live captions chrome extension` 110 (SD 28),
`live captions and translation` 90 (**SD 16**), `team live captions` 90 (SD 23), `live captions mac`
70 (SD 17), `mac live captions` 50 (SD 13).

**The warning:** the full `live captions` cluster is 3,600 and I am only counting 1,400 of it,
because the majority of the volume is people trying to **turn captions off**: `how to turn off live
captions` 1,000, `turn off live captions` 880, `how to turn off live captions on chrome` 880, and
roughly thirty more "turn off", "remove", "disable" variants. That is the opposite of buying intent.
Target only the "app", "extension" and "translation" terms, and ignore the headline cluster size that
any tool will report.

### N4, speaker diarization, 930 at SD 19, and I am not recommending it

Included for completeness because the CPC is striking (`speaker diarization` $23.86,
`speaker diarization meaning` **$109.20**, `whisper speaker diarization` $36.40). But the cluster is
`pyannote`, `whisper`, `python`, `github`, `nemo`, `assemblyai`, `pytorch`, `arxiv`. This is machine
learning engineers choosing a library, not managers choosing a meeting tool. It would bring traffic
that cannot convert. "Who said what" is a genuine Lynkk feature, so if you want this angle, express
it in plain language inside another post rather than targeting the technical term.
