Patterns worth copying:

1. **Primary keyword first in the title and in the URL slug.** Notion, Krisp, Tactiq and
   Zoom all use an exact-match slug and lead the title with the keyword.
2. **Stack 2-3 keyword families in the title.** Read.ai, Summary.ai and Notta combine
   "notes / note taker", "transcription" and "summaries" in one title, and each ranks for
   all three families.
3. **One page per cluster.** Krisp and Tactiq keep a separate "AI meeting summary" page
   rather than folding it into a general notes page.
4. **Listicles and Reddit threads own the "best" queries.** "best ai note taker", "free ai
   note taker" and "in person" SERPs are full of reviews (zapier, simular.ai, tldv, plaud,
   Reddit). A product page will not rank there; getting **listed in** those reviews is the
   play.

## 4. Recommended page

### URL
`/features/ai-meeting-notes`

Matches the existing `/features/*` pattern and Notion's exact-match slug. If a meeting
notes page already exists at another URL, keep that URL and 301 nothing.

### Title tag (pick one, all under 60 characters)
1. `AI Meeting Notes & Action Items in 30 Seconds | Lynkk` (52)
2. `AI Meeting Notes Taker: Notes, Summaries, Tasks | Lynkk` (55)
3. `AI Meeting Notes for Online & In-Person Meetings | Lynkk` (56)

Recommendation: **#1**. Leads with the primary keyword, adds the lowest-SD secondary
("action items") and a concrete promise.

### Meta description (under 155 characters)
```
Lynkk takes AI meeting notes for online and in-person meetings. Get a summary, action items, CRM updates and Jira tickets within 30 seconds. Start free.
```

### H1
```
AI meeting notes that do the work after the meeting
```
Alternate: `AI meeting notes for every meeting, online or in person`

### Intro (first 100 words must include "AI meeting notes", "meeting notes" and "action items")
```
Lynkk takes AI meeting notes for you, in any meeting, online or in person. Within
30 seconds of hanging up you get structured meeting notes, a short summary, action
items assigned to the right people, CRM fields updated and Jira tickets created.
Every conversation lands in one searchable knowledge graph, so nothing said in a
meeting gets lost.
```
CTA: `Start free`

### On-page checklist
- [ ] Primary keyword in: URL, title, H1, first 100 words, one H2, one image alt, meta description
- [ ] Each secondary keyword used once or twice, naturally; no stuffing
- [ ] 1,200-1,800 words of visible copy (product pages ranking here combine features, comparison and FAQ)
- [ ] Image alt text, e.g. "AI meeting notes with action items generated by Lynkk"
- [ ] Schema: `SoftwareApplication` (with `offers` price 0 for free tier), `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList`
- [ ] Internal links in: homepage, `/features/knowledge-graph`, `/features/meeting-bot`, nav, footer, all with anchor "AI meeting notes"
- [ ] Internal links out: knowledge graph, integrations, pricing, signup
- [ ] Page is indexable, in `sitemap.xml`, submitted in Search Console
- [ ] No em or en dashes in any copy (house rule)

## 6. Authority: the actual bottleneck

With DA 1 and one nofollow link, on-page work alone will move the page onto pages 2-3.
Getting to page 1 needs links:

1. **Directory submissions** (Product Hunt copy is ready in `launch/`). Aim for 20-30 AI
   tool directories in the first month.
2. **Get added to the listicles that rank.** Contact the authors of: simular.ai "Best AI
   Meeting Note Takers in 2026", perfectwikiforteams.com "10 Best AI Note Takers for
   Microsoft Teams", charitydigital.org.uk "The best AI tools for meeting notes",
   plaud.ai "5 Best AI Note Takers for In-Person Meetings", zapier "best AI meeting assistants".
3. **Reddit.** r/ProductivityApps "Tested 5 AI note taking apps for in-person meetings"
   ranks for 3 of our keywords. Answer honestly there and in similar threads.
4. **Competitor alternative pages** later (Jamie 1,300 / SD 17, Granola 1,300 / SD 30,
   Tactiq 1,000 / SD 35 for "[brand] ai note taker"; check "[brand] alternative" volumes first).
