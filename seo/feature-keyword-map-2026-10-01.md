## High-priority opportunities in detail

### 1. Meeting notes template (Meeting Brief + Summary Shapes)
The Meeting Brief is a ready-made notes template: title, abstract, key takeaways with timestamps, topics,
action items (owner, due date, priority), decisions with context, open questions. Summary Shapes (Pro) add
9 meeting types: General Meeting, Daily Stand-up, Product Review, Engineering Design, Sales Call,
Customer Interview, 1:1, Board Meeting, Brainstorm.

second, and the web recorder at lynkk.ai records your mic in person. Searchers hit the same wall our
FaceTime and WhatsApp posts describe: QuickTime and the screenshot toolbar do not capture internal audio.
