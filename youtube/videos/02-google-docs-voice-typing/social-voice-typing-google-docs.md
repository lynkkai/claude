Voice typing in Google Docs is free and fast, until it stops mid-sentence.

How to turn it on: open the doc in Chrome, then Tools, Voice typing (or Command Shift S). Click the mic and talk. Say "comma", "period" and "new line" for punctuation.

When it stops, check six things:
1. You're in Chrome
2. Your Mac allows the microphone for Chrome (System Settings, Privacy & Security)
3. Chrome allows the microphone for the doc
4. The right input is selected (System Settings, Sound)
5. You stayed in the doc. Clicking away stops it
6. The language above the mic matches yours, and you're online
