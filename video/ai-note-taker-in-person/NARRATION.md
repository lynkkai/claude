# Narration sheet

The presenter reads these to picture. Each line has a window on the 2:00 timeline and a budget: the window
length, and how long the line runs at an unhurried pace. Every line has room to spare, so being a little slower or
more expressive than the budget is fine. Being faster is fine too: the gaps are B-roll and graphics, not dead air.

Record each line as its own take, with a couple of seconds of room tone either side, so they can be slid onto the
grid in the edit. Lines marked **beat** land on a music bar line, so a short breath before them helps the cut.

- The disclosure at 0:22 is deliberate and should stay in the presenter's own words if they prefer different ones.
- Prices and the 17 languages figure are checked against the companion page. If a price changes before the shoot,
  change the board in `film/src/scenes/Boards.tsx` as well as the line.
