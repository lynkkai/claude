# Fathom AI Review: A Free Note Taker or a Full Meeting Assistant Like Lynkk?

*Last updated: October 1, 2026 · Fathom details checked on Fathom's own pricing and product pages on September 30, 2026*

> **Quick answer:** Fathom AI is a free note taker that records, transcribes and summarizes Zoom, Google Meet and Teams calls, with advanced summaries for your first 5 calls a month and paid plans from $15 per user per month. **Lynkk is the better full meeting assistant**: it records with no bot on the Mac, writes a Meeting Brief with owners and due dates, sends action items to Jira and briefs you each morning before your meetings.

> **Disclosure:** This Fathom AI review is published by Lynkk, and Lynkk is one of the 2 tools compared. Fathom details come from Fathom's own pricing and product pages, cited in plain text with the date we checked them.

## What is Fathom AI and how does it work?

Fathom AI is a meeting note taker that joins 3 platforms, Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, to record, transcribe and summarize your calls. When the call ends, you get a transcript, a summary, action items and a draft follow-up email.

Fathom built its name on a generous free plan with unlimited recordings. You can highlight moments during the call, share clips afterward and ask questions about past meetings with its built-in assistant.

Its paid plans add sales features such as CRM sync and call scoring, which is why the Fathom AI note taker is popular with sales teams.

## Is the Fathom AI note taker free?

Yes, the Fathom AI note taker has a free plan with unlimited recordings, transcripts and AI summaries. The catch is that advanced summaries are limited to your first 5 calls a month, after which you get simpler summaries unless you upgrade.

Lynkk also starts free. The Free plan includes recording and transcription, the meeting bot (audio only), the Meeting Brief, tasks and Jira send, with a 2 hour cap per recording. See [Lynkk pricing](https://lynkk.ai/pricing) for what Pro adds.

## How much does Fathom AI cost for paid plans?

Fathom AI paid plans start at $15 per user per month, as checked on September 30, 2026. New Team and Business plans no longer include free seats, so every member of a team plan needs a paid seat.

| Plan | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|

| Paid plans | From $15 per user per month | More AI features, CRM sync on higher tiers |

For a 10-person team, that means at least $150 a month on paid Fathom plans. A Lynkk workspace gives up to 10 people Pro, and each person chooses which notes stay personal.

### How do they record meetings?

Lynkk gives you 4 ways to record, and 2 of them use no bot. The [Lynkk Mac app](https://lynkk.ai/download) notices calls in Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Slack huddles, WhatsApp, FaceTime and Webex and records both sides on 2 channels.

The [Lynkk Chrome extension](https://lynkk.ai/features/chrome-extension) records Meet, Zoom (web) and Teams in a browser tab, and the [free Lynkk meeting bot](https://lynkk.ai/features/meeting-bot) joins from your calendar when a bot is fine. For in-person meetings, you can record your microphone in the browser at lynkk.ai.

Fathom adds its notetaker to the call, which works well for internal meetings but is visible to clients and guests.

### What do the notes contain?

Every Lynkk recording becomes a Meeting Brief with 7 parts: title, abstract, key takeaways with timestamps, topics, action items, decisions with context and open questions. Each action item has an owner, a due date and a priority, and every timestamp plays the recording from that moment.

Fathom summaries are clear and quick to share, with action items and a follow-up email draft. The difference is structure: Lynkk separates decisions and open questions, which is what people look for a week later.

### What happens before and after the call?

Lynkk sends a morning briefing email on days with meetings, covering 3 things per meeting once Google Calendar is connected: who you are meeting, your latest note with them and how many action items are still open. After the call, tasks go to a board and you can send them to Jira from the note.

On Pro, [Ask Lynkk](https://lynkk.ai/features/knowledge-graph) answers questions from your own notes with citations that open the note at the right moment. Fathom's assistant also answers questions about past calls.

## Where is Fathom AI stronger than Lynkk?

Fathom AI is stronger in 4 areas, and it is fair to say so. If one of these is your top need, Fathom is a good choice.

1. **CRM sync.** Paid Fathom plans write notes to CRMs such as HubSpot and Salesforce. Lynkk does not sync with a CRM.
2. **Sales coaching.** Fathom adds call scoring and talk-time metrics. Lynkk focuses on notes, tasks and prep.
3. **Clips and highlights.** Fathom makes it easy to share short clips of key moments.
4. **Platforms.** Fathom runs beyond the Mac. Lynkk records from a Mac, a Chrome tab or a browser.

## Is Fathom AI safe to use on client calls?

Fathom AI is used by many businesses, but any AI meeting assistant is safe only if you check 2 things: how the vendor handles your data and whether everyone on the call agreed to be recorded. The [NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework/privacy-framework) is a practical checklist for the first.

For consent, federal law ([18 U.S.C. § 2511](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title18-section2511)) allows 1 party consent, while California ([Penal Code § 632](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=632)) requires all parties to agree. Lynkk keeps recordings private to your account and never trains on your conversations.

*This is general information, not legal advice.*
