Easy to rank

  • SD (Ubersuggest SEO difficulty) sets the realistic target: SD 15 or less → #3, 16-25 → #5, 26-35 → #8, 36+ → long-term.
  • Pages already in the top 15 get a #3 target: Google has accepted them.
  • SERP checks confirmed low-DA sites on page 1: DA 2 for "scrum stand up meeting", DA 13 for "ai note taker for teams", DA 9 and 22 for "granola vs otter ai".

Gives clicks

  • Projected clicks use Ubersuggest's click model: #3 gets about 8-10% of searches, #5 about 4.5%, #8 about 2%.
  • Most of these SERPs show an AI Overview, which takes some clicks. Templates and "vs" searches still click through well. In India, sites with DA 35-37 at #2 and #5 get 5,019 and 904 clicks for "template for meeting minutes".
  • "google meet transcript" is click-light: an AI Overview plus Chrome extension listings take most clicks. That makes a Chrome Web Store listing worth doing.

Recommended site structure

Seven hubs: six blog hubs and the product section. Every blog post links up to its hub page, across to 2-3 sibling posts, and to one product page, so traffic and authority flow toward sign-up. The blog hub pages are new, short pages (300-500 words plus a list of posts) at /blog/topic/[hub].

Linking rules

  • TOFU post → MOFU or product page: one CTA in context, after the step or template. For example: "No Zoom transcript on your plan? Lynkk records free Zoom."
  • MOFU post → /compare/[tool] and /pricing: a comparison table with Lynkk as a row, linking to its compare page.
  • CTA wording: Lynkk has no free plan. Use "Try 5 hours on us" or "Start with 5 free hours", never "Start free" or "free AI note taker" for Lynkk itself. "Free template" is fine.
  • Hub pages ← homepage: a "Free meeting templates and guides" block on the homepage links to all 6 hubs.
  • Anchor text: use the target keyword of the page you link to ("AI note taker for Teams", not "click here").

Effect on the keyword plan

  • "free ai note taker" (2,400/mo) moves from P1 to P2. People looking for a free tool are less likely to pay after 5 hours. Keep best-free-ai-note-takers as an honest comparison of free plans, with Lynkk positioned as 5 free hours then the lowest paid price.
