## What is the Otter AI note taker, and why look for an alternative?

The Otter AI note taker records, transcribes and summarizes meetings, and its bot, OtterPilot, joins Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams calls from your calendar. It has been around for more than 5 years and is best known for its live transcript.

People look for an Otter.ai alternative for 3 common reasons, based on the questions they search most:

1. **The bot joins too often.** "How to remove Otter AI note taker" is one of the most searched Otter questions.
2. **The free minutes run out.** The free plan includes 300 minutes a month, which a busy week can use up.
3. **Follow-up is still manual.** People copy action items into Jira or their task tool by hand.

Every tool in this list fixes at least 1 of those. Lynkk addresses all 3 with bot-free recording on the Mac, a free plan that includes a meeting bot, and Jira send from each note.

## 1. Lynkk: best Otter AI note taker alternative overall

Lynkk is the best Otter AI note taker alternative because it helps at 3 stages of a meeting: it briefs you before, records with or without a bot during, and turns the call into owned tasks after. It starts free.

### How does Lynkk record meetings?

Lynkk records in 4 ways, and 2 of them use no bot. The [Lynkk Mac app](https://lynkk.ai/download) notices calls in Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Slack huddles, WhatsApp, FaceTime and Webex and records both sides on 2 channels, so your side is labeled "You".

The [Chrome extension](https://lynkk.ai/features/chrome-extension) records Meet, Zoom (web) and Teams in a browser tab with no extra participant. When a bot is fine, the [free Lynkk meeting bot](https://lynkk.ai/features/meeting-bot) joins from your calendar, audio only. For in-person meetings, you record your microphone in the browser at lynkk.ai.

### What does every Lynkk note include?

Every recording becomes a Meeting Brief with 7 parts: title, abstract, key takeaways with timestamps, topics, action items, decisions with context and open questions. Each [action item](https://lynkk.ai/features/action-items) has an owner, a due date and a priority, and you can send it to Jira from the note, 1 issue per item.

With Google Calendar connected, a morning briefing email shows who you are meeting, your latest note with them and how many action items are still open. On Pro, [Ask Lynkk](https://lynkk.ai/features/knowledge-graph) answers questions from your own notes with citations.

### What could be better?

Lynkk has 3 limits worth knowing. There is no phone app, so recording happens on a Mac, in Chrome or in a browser; there is no CRM sync; and each recording has a 2 hour cap.

### How much does Lynkk cost?

Lynkk starts free, with recording, transcription in 17 languages, the meeting bot (audio only), the Meeting Brief, tasks and Jira send. Pro adds Ask Lynkk, Summary Shapes and Slack answers; see [Lynkk pricing](https://lynkk.ai/pricing).

## 2. Fathom: best free Otter.ai alternative for unlimited recordings

Fathom's free plan includes unlimited recordings, transcripts and AI summaries, with advanced summaries for your first 5 calls a month. Paid plans start at $15 per user per month.

**Best for:** individuals who record many calls and want a summary after each one without paying.

**What we liked:** the free plan is the most generous in this list for recording volume, and summaries come with a draft follow-up email.

**What could be better:** Fathom adds a notetaker to your calls, and new Team and Business plans need a paid seat for every member.

**Where Lynkk goes further:** bot-free recording on the Mac, owners and due dates on every action item, and a morning brief before meetings.

## 3. Fireflies: best for a searchable call library

Fireflies includes unlimited transcription and AI summaries on all 4 plans, and Pro costs $10 per seat per month billed annually. Its bot, Fred, joins Zoom, Google Meet and Teams from your calendar.

**Best for:** sales teams that want to search every past call and push notes into a CRM.

**What we liked:** AskFred answers questions across your meetings, and the integration list is long.

**What could be better:** free storage is limited to 400 minutes per team, and recordings are capped at 2 hours on Free and Pro.

**Where Lynkk goes further:** no bot needed on the Mac, a brief before each meeting, and decisions and open questions kept separate in every note.

*Source: Fireflies pricing page, checked September 30, 2026.*

## 4. Granola: best bot-free notepad

Granola is a notepad that listens to your microphone and computer audio with 0 bots in the call, then fills in your rough notes after the meeting. It is free with 30 days of history, and Business costs $14 per user per month.

**Best for:** people who like typing their own notes and want AI to complete them.

**What we liked:** no bot ever joins, 29 built-in templates, and apps for Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android.

**What could be better:** the notes depend on what you type, and free users only see the last 30 days.

**Where Lynkk goes further:** Lynkk writes the notes for you from the recording, and it has a free meeting bot for calls you cannot attend.

*Source: Granola pricing page and help center, checked October 1, 2026.*

**Best for:** European teams and people who record on Windows or a phone.

**What we liked:** it records system audio with no bot and works in person on its mobile apps.

**What could be better:** the free plan's 30 minute limit is short for most meetings, and prices are in euros.

**Where Lynkk goes further:** Jira send, a morning brief before meetings, and a free meeting bot when you want one.

*Source: Jamie website and pricing page, checked September 30 and October 1, 2026.*

**Best for:** managers who want data on meeting habits as well as notes.

**What we liked:** paid plans store an unlimited number of meetings, and Enterprise+ adds data retention policies and SAML.

## Is it legal to record meetings with an AI note taker?

Recording is legal in most of the US when at least 1 person on the call agrees, but some states require every participant to consent. Federal law ([18 U.S.C. § 2511](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title18-section2511)) allows 1 party consent, while California ([Penal Code § 632](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=632)) requires all parties to agree.

Tell everyone at the start of the call, whichever tool you use. To review any vendor's data practices, the [NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework/privacy-framework) is a practical checklist. Lynkk keeps recordings private to your account and never trains on your conversations.
