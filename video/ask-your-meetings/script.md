# Voiceover script: Ask Your Meetings

Female voice (Kokoro TTS, voice `af_heart`). Lynkk is spelled "Link" in the TTS input only, so it is pronounced correctly.


## 1. Hook: the problem

You had the answer.
It was said in a meeting, three weeks ago.
Someone promised a deadline. A customer named their budget. The team agreed on a decision.
And now, nobody can find it.

## 2. The problem: meeting overload

Most teams sit through dozens of meetings every week.
What gets said there is some of the most valuable knowledge your company has.
And most of it disappears the moment the call ends.

## 3. The problem: scattered knowledge

Notes end up scattered across docs, chats, and inboxes.
Recordings pile up that nobody will ever rewatch.
So you scroll through transcripts, message colleagues, or book another meeting just to recap the last one.
Decisions get debated twice. Commitments slip. New teammates start from zero.

## 4. The turn

The real problem isn't that nobody takes notes.
It's that your meetings can't answer questions.

## 5. Solution reveal

Until now. Meet Ask Your Meetings, the conversational brain of Lynkk.

## 6. How it works

Lynkk captures every meeting, online or in person.
It turns each one into structured notes and action items.
Then it connects everything into one searchable knowledge graph: the people, the topics, the decisions, and the commitments, across every conversation.

## 7. Ask in plain language

So instead of searching, you just ask, in plain language.
What did Acme say about pricing last quarter?
Which decisions are still open from the launch review?
What did I promise Priya on Tuesday?
No folders to dig through. Just one clear answer, drawn from every call.

## 8. Before and during meetings

It works before the meeting, too. Lynkk shows you what was discussed, what's still open, and who said what, so you start ahead, not cold.
And during the call, Lynkk can join and answer out loud. Ask for past context, pull up a document, and get the answer live, without leaving the conversation.

## 9. Use cases

Here's how teams use it.
Sales teams prep every call with the full customer history, and keep the CRM up to date.
Product and engineering teams trace why a decision was made, and turn requests into Jira tickets.
Founders and managers keep track of every commitment, even across ten calls a day.
New hires get up to speed by asking questions, instead of watching hours of recordings.
Global teams capture conversations in many languages.
And teams in regulated industries choose the region where their data is processed.

Scattered notes become one source of truth.
Hours of searching become one question.
Forgotten promises become tracked action items.
