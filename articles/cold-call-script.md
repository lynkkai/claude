# Cold Call Script: 12 Templates That Book Meetings, Plus What the Data Says Works

*Last updated: 24 November 2026*

> **Quick answer:** The best cold call script follows 5 steps: a short opener, the reason for your call, one problem you solve, an open question, and a specific ask for a meeting. In Gong's analysis of cold calls, stating the reason for the call raised success 2.1x, while "Did I catch you at a bad time?" made a meeting 40% less likely.

A **cold call script** is a short, flexible outline of what to say when you call a prospect who is not expecting you. It is not a speech to read word for word. It is a structure that keeps you on track for the first 30 seconds, which decide whether you get the next 5 minutes.

Below you will find the 5-part structure, what call data says about openers, 12 copyable scripts for different industries and situations, voicemail and follow-up scripts, and how to improve your script after every call.

## What does a cold call script look like?

A cold call script looks like a 5-part outline, about 100 to 150 words if read aloud, with room to adapt to what the prospect says. Here is the basic cold call script example most of the 12 templates below build on:

> **1. Opener:** "Hi [Name], this is [Your name] from [Company]. How've you been?"
>
> **2. Reason for the call:** "The reason for my call is that we work with [role] at [type of company] who are dealing with [problem]."
>
> **3. Relevance:** "[Similar company] was spending [time or money] on [problem] before they [result]."
>
> **4. Question:** "How are you handling [problem] today?"
>
> **5. Ask:** "Would it be crazy to grab 20 minutes on Thursday to see if it's worth a deeper look?"

Keep each part to 1 or 2 sentences. The goal of the first call is a meeting, not a sale.

### How long should a cold call script be?

A cold call script should take 30 to 45 seconds to say before your first question. Gong found that [successful cold calls are nearly twice as long as unsuccessful ones](https://www.gong.io/blog/cold-call-stats), about 5 minutes 50 seconds, so the script only needs to earn the conversation, not fill it.

## Does cold calling still work?

Yes, cold calling still works when the call is relevant and short on fluff. Gong's research shows successful cold calls last about 5 minutes 50 seconds, nearly twice as long as failed ones, which means prospects will stay on the line when the first 30 seconds earn it.

What has changed is tolerance for generic pitches. Buyers screen unknown numbers, and many calls go to voicemail. That makes 3 things more important than before:

- **Targeting.** Calling 50 well-chosen accounts beats 300 random ones.
- **Timing.** Calls tied to a trigger event, such as a new hire, funding or a product launch, get more real conversations.
- **Multi-channel follow-up.** A call plus a short email and a LinkedIn touch is stronger than any single channel.

### What is the purpose of a cold call?

The purpose of a cold call is to book 1 next step, usually a 15 to 30 minute meeting, not to close a sale. A cold call qualifies whether the prospect has the problem you solve and whether now is a reasonable time to talk about it.

If you try to pitch the whole product on a cold call, you lose the prospect's attention. If you ask for a small, specific next step, you give them an easy yes.

## What does the data say about the best cold call openers?

Call data from Gong points to 3 opener rules: skip permission-based openers, try "How've you been?", and state the reason for your call early. These are patterns from large call samples, not guarantees, but they are a better starting point than guesswork.

| Opener or habit | What Gong found |
|---|---|
| "Did I catch you at a bad time?" | 40% less likely to book a meeting |
| "How've you been?" | 6.6x higher success than calls without it |
| "The reason for my call is..." | 2.1x higher success rate |
| Rep talks 55% of the time | Linked with successful calls |
| Longest monologue | 53 seconds in successful calls vs 25 seconds |
| Best days to call | Wednesday and Thursday |

The pattern: give the prospect a clear, honest reason to keep listening, then do more of the talking early, because every question you ask costs them effort before they trust you.

## How do you write a cold call script?

You write a cold call script in 6 steps: pick 1 persona, pick 1 problem, write the 5-part outline, add proof, prepare 3 objection answers, and test it on 20 calls before changing it.

1. **Pick 1 persona.** A script for a VP of Sales is different from one for an IT manager.
2. **Pick 1 problem.** The most common, costly pain that persona has. One problem per script.
3. **Write the 5 parts.** Opener, reason, relevance, question, ask.
4. **Add proof.** A customer name, a number or a short result from a similar company.
5. **Prepare 3 objection answers.** "Not interested," "Send me an email," and "We already use X."
6. **Test, then adjust.** Make 20 calls before rewriting so you are judging the script, not 1 bad call.

For deeper objection answers, see our guide to [objection handling scripts](https://lynkk.ai/blog/objection-handling).

### What should you research before a cold call?

Spend 2 to 3 minutes researching each prospect before you dial. That is enough to make the call relevant without turning prospecting into a research project.

- [ ] Their role and how long they have been in it
- [ ] 1 recent company event: funding, hiring, a launch or news
- [ ] The tools they likely use (from job posts or their website)
- [ ] A similar customer you can mention
- [ ] The 1 problem you will lead with

Write the trigger and the problem at the top of your script before you dial. If you cannot find either in 3 minutes, move the prospect down your list and call someone with a clearer reason.

## What are the best cold call script templates for B2B and SaaS?

The best B2B and SaaS cold call scripts lead with a specific problem and a peer example, then ask for a short meeting. Use these 4 sales cold call script templates as starting points.

### 1. B2B cold call script to get a meeting

> "Hi [Name], this is [Your name] from [Company]. How've you been?
>
> The reason for my call is that we help [role]s at [industry] companies cut [problem] without adding headcount. [Peer company] went from [before] to [after] in [time].
>
> How are you handling [problem] right now?
>
> [Listen.]
>
> It sounds like it could be worth a closer look. Are you open to 20 minutes on Wednesday or Thursday?"

### 2. SaaS or software sales cold call script

> "Hi [Name], [Your name] from [Company]. How've you been?
>
> I'm calling because teams using [their current tool] often tell us [specific frustration]. We built [product] to fix exactly that, and [customer] saved [hours or money] in the first month.
>
> Is [frustration] something your team runs into?
>
> [Listen.]
>
> Would a 15-minute demo next week be useful, or is the timing wrong?"

### 3. SDR cold call script with a trigger event

> "Hi [Name], it's [Your name] with [Company]. I saw [trigger: new funding / new role / hiring for X].
>
> The reason for my call is that when companies [trigger], [problem] usually follows. We help with that by [one-line value].
>
> How are you planning to handle [problem] as you [grow / change]?"

### 4. Referral cold call script

> "Hi [Name], this is [Your name] from [Company]. [Referrer] suggested I give you a call.
>
> We helped [Referrer's team] with [result], and they thought you might be dealing with something similar.
>
> Would it make sense to compare notes for 20 minutes this week?"

## What is the best cold call script for real estate?

The best real estate cold call script is short, local and specific about the property or neighborhood. Real estate searches are among the most common for this topic, so here are 3 versions for agents and investors.

### 5. Real estate agent cold call script (expired listing)

> "Hi, is this [Name]? This is [Your name] with [Brokerage]. I noticed your home on [Street] came off the market.
>
> I'm calling because I've sold [number] homes in [neighborhood] this year, and I had a few thoughts on why it may not have sold.
>
> Are you still thinking about selling?"

### 6. For sale by owner (FSBO) cold call script

> "Hi [Name], I'm [Your name], a local agent with [Brokerage]. I saw your home on [Street] is for sale by owner.
>
> I'm not calling to list it today. I'm wondering, if a buyer of mine were interested, would you work with an agent?"

### 7. Wholesale real estate cold call script

> "Hi, is this [Name]? My name is [Your name]. I'm a local investor, and I'm calling about the property on [Street].
>
> Would you consider an offer on it if the price and timing worked for you?
>
> [If yes:] What would you need to make a sale worth it?"

## What are good cold call scripts for insurance and financial advisors?

Insurance and financial advisor cold call scripts work best when they lead with a life event or a review, not a product. Always follow the calling rules in your market, including [Do Not Call registry](https://www.donotcall.gov/) rules for consumer calls in the US.

### 8. Insurance cold call script

> "Hi [Name], this is [Your name] with [Agency]. How've you been?
>
> The reason for my call is that many [homeowners / business owners] in [area] are seeing premiums go up at renewal. I do free 15-minute policy reviews to check for gaps and savings.
>
> When does your current policy renew?"

### 9. Cold call script for financial advisor

> "Hi [Name], I'm [Your name], a financial advisor with [Firm]. I work with [professionals / business owners] in [area] who are planning for [retirement / a sale / college costs].
>
> I'm not calling with a product. I'm calling to offer a 20-minute review of where things stand.
>
> Is [goal] something you're actively planning for?"

## How do you handle gatekeepers, voicemail and follow-ups?

Handle gatekeepers with a clear name and reason, keep voicemails under 30 seconds, and follow up within 2 business days. These 3 scripts cover the moments between the first dial and the first conversation.

### 10. Gatekeeper script

> "Hi, this is [Your name] from [Company]. Could you help me? I'm trying to reach whoever handles [area] for the team. Would that be [Name]?"

Be polite and specific. Gatekeepers often decide who gets through based on whether the caller sounds like a real business contact.

### 11. Cold call voicemail script

> "Hi [Name], it's [Your name] from [Company]. The reason for my call is [one-line problem and result]. I'll send you a short email with details. My number is [number]. Again, [Your name], [number]."

### 12. Follow-up cold call script

> "Hi [Name], [Your name] from [Company]. I left you a message on [day] about [problem]. I'll be quick: does [problem] matter to you this quarter, or should I check back later?"

After a good call, confirm the meeting in writing. Our [sales follow-up email templates](https://lynkk.ai/blog/sales-follow-up-email) cover what to send.

## How do you respond to common cold call objections?

Respond to cold call objections by acknowledging them, asking 1 question, and offering a smaller next step. The 3 most common objections have simple answers:

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I'm not interested." | "Fair enough. Is that because [problem] isn't a priority, or because you've already solved it?" |
| "Send me an email." | "Happy to. So it's relevant, what's the biggest issue with [area] right now?" |
| "We already use [competitor]." | "Makes sense. What would you change about it if you could?" |

## What should you do after the cold call?

After the call, log the outcome in your CRM within 5 minutes and note the exact words the prospect used about their problem. Those words make your next script better than any template.

Then review your calls weekly. Listen for 3 things: where prospects hung up, which opener got a real answer, and which objection came up most. Change 1 part of the script at a time.

## FAQ

### What is the best script for a cold call?

The best script is the one that fits your buyer, but most strong scripts share 5 parts: a short opener, the reason for the call, a relevant problem, an open question and a clear meeting ask. Test any script on at least 20 calls before judging it.

### Should you read a cold call script word for word?

No. Use the script as an outline and learn it well enough to sound natural. Prospects can tell within 5 seconds when someone is reading.

### Are cold calls legal?

B2B cold calls are generally legal in the US, but calls to consumers must follow federal rules, including the national Do Not Call registry, plus any state rules. Other countries have their own rules, so check before you call.

### What is the best time to make cold calls?

Gong's data shows Wednesday and Thursday are the 2 best days for cold calls. Within the day, test early morning and late afternoon for your market, since results vary by industry.

### Why is cold calling hard?

Cold calling is hard because you interrupt someone who did not ask to hear from you, and most calls end in voicemail or a quick "no." A clear reason for calling in the first 10 seconds is the biggest single fix.
