# How to Record FaceTime Calls on iPhone and Mac, With Audio From Both Sides

*Last updated: October 1, 2026 · Steps checked against Apple Support on October 1, 2026*

> **Quick answer:** You can record FaceTime calls with Apple's built-in Call Recording, but only 1-to-1 FaceTime audio calls in supported regions: during the call, tap or click More, then Call Recording, and both people hear a notice. The recording and its transcript are saved to the Notes app. For FaceTime video calls or group calls, a Mac app such as Lynkk records both sides of the conversation.

## Can you record FaceTime calls?

Yes, you can record FaceTime calls, with 2 limits on Apple's own tool. Call Recording works only for audio calls with 1 other person, and only in countries, regions and languages where Apple offers it.

That leaves out the calls people most often want to keep: FaceTime video calls and group FaceTime. For those, you need either a screen recording (which often misses the other person's voice) or a separate app.

| Call type | Apple Call Recording | Other option |
|---|---|---|
| 1-to-1 FaceTime audio | Yes, in supported regions | Mac app |
| 1-to-1 FaceTime video | No | Mac app or screen recording |
| Group FaceTime | No | Mac app |

## How do you record FaceTime calls on iPhone?

You record a FaceTime audio call on iPhone in 3 taps, once Call Recording is available in your region. Apple's steps, checked on October 1, 2026, are:

1. **Start a 1-to-1 FaceTime audio call** in the FaceTime app.
2. **Tap the More button** in the call controls.
3. **Tap Call Recording.** Both of you hear a notice that the call is being recorded.
4. **Tap Stop** to end the recording, or simply end the call.

The recording is saved to a **Call Recordings** folder in the Notes app. Open the note and tap **Transcript** to read what was said, in supported languages.

## How to record FaceTime call audio on a Mac

The Mac version works the same way for 1-to-1 FaceTime audio calls. Here is how to record FaceTime call audio on a Mac in 3 steps:

1. **During a FaceTime audio call**, click the **More** button.
2. **Click Call Recording.** Both participants hear the recording notice.
3. **Click More, then Stop** when you are done.

Open the Notes app and go to the **Call Recordings** folder to play the recording or read the transcript. With Apple Intelligence, you can also get a summary of the recorded call.

## Why does screen recording miss the other person's audio?

Screen recording usually captures only 1 side of a FaceTime call: yours. On iPhone, turning on the microphone in Screen Recording records your voice, but privacy restrictions mean the other person's audio is often missing.

On a Mac, QuickTime and the Screenshot toolbar (Shift-Command-5) do not capture the Mac's internal audio on their own. People work around this with audio routing utilities, which takes setup and is easy to get wrong in the middle of a call.

If you need a clean recording of both voices, use Apple's Call Recording for audio calls, or an app built to record calls.

## Can I record FaceTime calls without the other person knowing?

No, and you should not try. Apple's Call Recording plays a notice to both people, and recording someone secretly can break the law in many US states.

Federal law ([18 U.S.C. § 2511](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title18-section2511)) allows recording when 1 party to the call consents, but California ([Penal Code § 632](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=632)) and several other states require every party to agree. The safe habit is to say "I'm recording this call" at the start, whichever tool you use. To check how any app handles your recordings, the [NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework/privacy-framework) is a useful checklist.

*This is general information, not legal advice.*

## What are common problems when you record FaceTime calls?

Most FaceTime recording problems come down to 4 causes. Check these before you try another tool.

| Problem | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| No Call Recording option | Not available in your region or language, or it is a video or group call | Check Apple's feature availability page, or use a Mac app |
| Only your voice recorded | Screen recording does not capture call audio | Use Call Recording or a call recording app |
| Can't find the recording | Recordings go to Notes, not Photos | Open Notes, then the Call Recordings folder |
| No transcript | Language not supported for transcripts | Try a tool that supports your language |
