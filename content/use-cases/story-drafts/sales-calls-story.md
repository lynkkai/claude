## A sales call story you might know

*Meet Maya. She's an example, but you may know someone just like her.*

Maya is an account executive. On Tuesday she has four sales calls back to back. At 2 pm she's on Zoom with Dana from
Acme. Dana has questions about pricing, wants to try the product with two teams first, and asks who on Acme's side
needs to sign off.

Maya is doing three jobs at once. She's selling, she's listening, and she's trying to type notes. She promises a
revised quote by Friday. Her colleague Dev says he'll book a security review. Dana mentions, almost in passing, that
she isn't sure who signs the annual order.

At 6 pm Maya opens her notes. They say: *"pricing?? pilot 2 teams. sec review. Fri."* Who promised the quote? Was it
Friday the 2nd or the 9th? And what was that thing about the annual order? The follow-up email waits until 9 pm, and
the open question about who signs never makes it in.

A week later, the deal slows down. Not because Maya sold badly, but because a few small things slipped.

## How an AI sales assistant changed Maya's next call

Before her next call with Acme, Maya starts using Lynkk.

### Before the call: she starts ahead

Ten minutes before the call, Maya asks Lynkk: *"What's still open with Acme?"* Lynkk answers from her past calls:
Dana still hasn't said who signs the annual order, and it links to the exact moment she raised it. Maya opens the
call with that question.

### During the call: she just listens

When the Zoom call starts, the Lynkk Mac app offers to record. Nothing new joins the call, so the conversation feels
normal. Halfway through, Dana asks: *"What did we agree on the pilot size?"* Instead of scrolling through old notes,
Maya gets the answer from Cue during the call: a pilot for two teams, agreed on the first call.

### After the call: the note is already written

By the time Maya is back in her inbox, the note is ready. Every next step has an owner and a date. She writes the
follow-up email straight from the note, and sends the action items to Jira so the security review isn't forgotten.

*Asking Lynkk across calls and Cue during calls are part of Pro.*

### Maya's note from the Acme call (example)

**Acme pricing call · Zoom · 32 min**

**Next steps**
- Send the revised quote to Dana. Owner: Maya. Due: Fri 2 Oct.
- Book the security review. Owner: Dev. Due: Tue 6 Oct.
- Share the pilot plan for two teams. Owner: Maya. Due: Fri 9 Oct.

**Decision:** Start with a pilot for two teams. Agreed at 14:22.

**Open question:** Who signs the annual order on Acme's side? Raised at 21:37.

**Key takeaways:** 08:12 Pricing is the main question for Acme. 17:50 They want two teams on it before a wider rollout.

Every time in the note plays the recording from that moment, so Maya can hear exactly how Dana said it.

## Questions about AI for sales calls

**Is Lynkk call recording software?**

Yes, and more. Lynkk records your sales calls, then writes the notes, next steps and open questions for you.

**Can Lynkk record without a bot joining?**

Yes. The Mac app and Chrome extension record from your computer. The bot is optional.

**Does Lynkk sync with my CRM?**

No. You can send action items to Jira and share notes with a link.

**How is Lynkk different from other AI sales tools?**

Lynkk focuses on the conversation: notes, next steps and answers across calls, online or in person. Revenue platforms
add CRM sync and forecasting.

**Do I need to tell the buyer I'm recording?**

Yes. Recording rules differ by country and state, so tell the buyer at the start and record only with their agreement.
