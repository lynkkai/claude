# Free plan removed: copy changes (15 November 2026)

Lynkk no longer has a free plan. New users start on **Starter** (₹0, first 5 hours of AI notes),
then **Pro at $4 a month (₹250 in India)**. The live site (crawled 15 Nov) still sells a free plan.

`free-claims-copy-changes.csv` lists 183 changes on 71 pages (titles, meta descriptions, H1/H2s
and body sentences), each with replacement text. Not included: the "Start for free" button in the
site header and footer (477 times on all 126 pages), which is a single template change to
"Try 5 hours on us".

Rows marked `[CONFIRM]` depend on facts the team must confirm: whether dictation and the audio
meeting bot stay on Starter, and whether Starter needs a credit card. Rows marked
"No change" mention a free Zoom or Gmail account, not Lynkk.

"Free" stays correct for free templates, competitors' free plans and Zoom's free plan.

The pricing rules now live in `memory/lynkk-brand.md`.
