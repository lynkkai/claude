## What is Granola AI, and why look for an alternative?

Granola AI is an AI notepad that listens to 2 audio sources, your microphone and your computer's audio, with no bot joining the call. You type rough bullets during the meeting, and Granola merges them with the transcript afterward.

Our Granola AI review found 3 common reasons people look for Granola AI alternatives, based on the questions they search most:

1. **They want the notes written for them.** Granola AI notes depend on what you type during the call.
2. **The free history is short.** Free users only see notes from the last 30 days in the app.
3. **They need follow-up or a bot.** Granola cannot attend a meeting you are not in, and tasks still need moving into Jira.

Lynkk is the best Granola AI alternative because it keeps what people like about Granola, recording with 0 bots on the Mac, and adds the parts Granola leaves to you: a full note, owned tasks, Jira send and prep before the next call.

### How does Lynkk record without a bot?

The [Lynkk Mac app](https://lynkk.ai/download) notices calls in 7 apps, Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Slack huddles, WhatsApp, FaceTime and Webex, and records both sides on 2 channels with no bot. A slim column on the edge of your screen shows the clock and live words while you talk.

The [Lynkk Chrome extension](https://lynkk.ai/features/chrome-extension) records Meet, Zoom (web) and Teams in a browser tab with no extra participant. When a bot is fine, the [free Lynkk meeting bot](https://lynkk.ai/features/meeting-bot) can join meetings you cannot attend.

### What does Lynkk write for you?

Every recording becomes a Meeting Brief with 7 parts: title, abstract, key takeaways with timestamps, topics, action items, decisions with context and open questions. You type nothing during the call, and each [action item](https://lynkk.ai/features/action-items) has an owner, a due date and a priority.

You can send action items to Jira from the note, 1 issue per item. With Google Calendar connected, a morning briefing email shows who you are meeting and what is still open. On Pro, [Ask Lynkk](https://lynkk.ai/features/knowledge-graph) answers from your own notes with citations.

### What could be better?

Lynkk has 3 limits compared with Granola. It has no Windows or phone app, it connects to fewer tools (Jira, Google Calendar and Slack), and Pro offers 9 built-in Summary Shapes rather than custom templates.

## Is Granola AI legal to use without a bot in the call?

Recording with no bot is still recording, so the same consent rules apply to Granola AI, Lynkk and every tool here. Federal law ([18 U.S.C. § 2511](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title18-section2511)) allows 1 party consent, while California ([Penal Code § 632](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=632)) requires all parties to agree.

Say at the start of each call that you are taking AI notes. The [NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework/privacy-framework) is a useful checklist for reviewing any vendor. Lynkk keeps recordings private to your account and never trains on your conversations.
