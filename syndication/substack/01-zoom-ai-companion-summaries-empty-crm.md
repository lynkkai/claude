# Zoom AI Companion Gave Us Summaries. Our CRM Stayed Empty.

*A summary is not a record. Why sales teams still retype every call, and three ways to close the gap.*

Hi, and welcome to **After the Call**, a newsletter from the Lynkk team about what happens after meetings end: the notes, the follow-ups and the work that falls through the cracks.

This week's issue starts with a question someone typed into Google, almost word for word:

> "Zoom AI Companion gives us summaries but nothing in our CRM. What other tools could close that gap for reps?"

It's a very specific complaint, and a very common one. Let's unpack it.

## The summary arrived. Then what?

Zoom AI Companion (on the paid Zoom plans that include it) does a decent job of writing a meeting summary. The rep gets an email or a doc. It's readable. It's even accurate.

And then the rep opens Salesforce or HubSpot and types the same information again: next steps, budget, timeline, who the decision maker is. Or, more often, doesn't.

The problem isn't the summary's quality. It's that **a summary is written for a human to read, and a CRM record is structured for a system to use.** They're different shapes.

A summary says: *"Priya mentioned that their budget has grown since discovery and they want to start with a smaller group."*

A CRM needs: *Budget: $50K. Pilot size: 15. Next step: security review. Decision maker: IT lead (not yet named).*

Converting one into the other is exactly the admin reps hate most, so it's exactly what gets skipped.

## Three gaps, not one

When teams say "nothing gets into our CRM," they usually mean one of three different things:

**1. Structure.** The notes don't separate budget, timeline, stakeholders and next steps. Fix: use a note format built for sales calls, with fixed sections.

**2. Transport.** The structured notes exist but live in a different app. Fix: a real CRM sync, or a one-click copy into the right fields.

**3. Coverage.** Half the calls aren't on Zoom. They're on Teams, Google Meet, a phone, or in person at the customer's office. Fix: a tool that captures every channel your reps actually sell through.

The second searcher's version of the question shows the third gap clearly: *"alternatives to Zoom AI Companion for sales reps who also take customer calls on Teams and Google Meet."*

## What to look for in a replacement (or a supplement)

If you're evaluating tools to close this gap, ask:

- **Does it write sales-shaped notes?** Look for sections like customer, stakeholders, pain points, budget and next steps, not just "summary".
- **What exactly does "CRM integration" mean?** Automatic field sync, a push button, or "copy to clipboard"? All three exist, and vendors use the same word for all three. Ask for a demo of the actual workflow.
- **Does it cover every call type your reps use?** Zoom, Teams, Meet and in-person, in one archive.
- **Can reps ask questions across past calls?** "What did Acme say about pricing in Q2?" is the question that prepares a rep for a renewal.

We wrote a longer comparison of [Zoom AI Companion alternatives](https://lynkk.ai/blog/zoom-ai-companion-alternatives), with pricing and bot or bot-free capture for each.

A note on honesty, since we make one of these tools: **Lynkk doesn't sync to CRMs yet.** On Pro, its Sales Call summary shape writes notes with customer, stakeholders, pain points and budget sections that a rep can copy into Salesforce or HubSpot, and on every plan it records Zoom, Meet, Teams and in-person calls and sends action items to Jira. If automatic field sync is your must-have, test the tools that offer it, and check that the sync does what the demo shows.

## The bigger idea: conversation intelligence

Zoom's summaries are one step on a longer path. Larger sales teams often move to "conversation intelligence" platforms that analyze calls across the whole pipeline: talk ratios, competitor mentions, deal risk. Those are powerful and expensive, and many small teams don't need all of it. Here's a plain-English overview of [conversation intelligence for sales teams](https://lynkk.ai/blog/sales-conversation-intelligence), including when it's worth it and when a good notetaker is enough.

## The fastest fix you can make today

While you're evaluating tools, there's one change that helps immediately: **send a structured follow-up email after every customer call, and paste it into the CRM.**

Five lines: what we agreed, what you'll do, what we'll do, the date, and one open question. The customer gets clarity, the rep gets a record, and the CRM gets *something*. If you need templates, here are [12 follow-up email examples](https://lynkk.ai/blog/follow-up-email-after-meeting) for sales, client and internal calls.

## This week's takeaway

The gap between "AI summarized the call" and "the CRM knows what happened" is structure plus transport plus coverage. Most tools solve one. Ask which ones yours solves.
