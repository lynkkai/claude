## What are meeting minutes?

Meeting minutes are a short written record of a meeting that captures 3 core things: decisions, action items and the people responsible. Their purpose is to give everyone, including people who missed the meeting, 1 agreed version of what happened.

Good minutes are brief. They record outcomes, not every comment, so a reader can see in 1 minute what changed and who owns the next step.

## What should be recorded in meeting minutes?

Minutes should record 7 items, whatever the meeting type. Use this list as a checklist before you send them.

| Item | Example |
|---|---|
| Date, time and meeting name | Sprint planning, Tuesday 10:00 |
| Attendees and absentees | 6 attended, 1 absent |
| Agenda topics | Q4 roadmap, bug backlog |
| Decisions made | Ship search filters in sprint 14 |
| Action items | Write filter spec |
| Owner and due date for each action | Priya, Friday |
| Open questions | Pricing for the team plan |

For product teams, the last 3 rows matter most, because they become tickets, owners and follow-up.

## Do meeting minutes have to be verbatim?

No. Minutes do not need to be word for word, and 1 short paragraph per topic is normal; they summarize decisions and actions. A full transcript is a different document, useful for checking exact wording later.

AI meeting minutes give you both. Lynkk keeps the full transcript with speaker labels and writes the short minutes on top, and every action item links back to the exact moment it was agreed. You send the short version and keep the long one for reference.

## Who takes meeting minutes?

Traditionally, 1 attendee takes the minutes, often a project manager or a rotating note-taker. That person listens less and speaks less, because they are busy writing.

With a meeting minutes app like Lynkk, nobody has to. Lynkk joins or records the meeting, writes the minutes and assigns action items, so every attendee can take part in the discussion.

## What do product teams need from meeting minutes?

Product teams need minutes that turn into work, and that means 3 things: clear decisions, action items with owners and dates, and tickets in Jira before the next standup. Minutes that sit in a document nobody opens do not move the backlog.

The manual way looks like this: someone types notes during sprint planning, cleans them up afterward, then copies each task into Jira by hand. Details get lost at every step, and tickets often appear a day later, or not at all.

After the meeting, do 4 things with the minutes within 24 hours: send them, turn tasks into tickets, confirm owners and link them to the next agenda. Minutes that nobody acts on are only a record.

## FAQ

### Can meeting minutes be bullet points?
Yes, and for most team meetings they should be. Use 1 bullet per decision and 1 per action item, with the owner and due date on each. Formal boards may follow a set format, but product and engineering teams benefit most from short, scannable bullets.

### Why are meeting minutes important?
They matter because they give everyone 1 agreed record of decisions and responsibilities. Without them, people remember different outcomes, tasks slip and decisions get made twice. For product teams, minutes are also the bridge to the backlog, which is why Lynkk turns them straight into Jira tickets.

### Can meeting minutes be recorded?
Yes. Recording works in 2 steps: record the meeting, then let AI write the minutes from the recording, which keeps a transcript for checking exact wording. Get consent from everyone first, because some US states require all parties to agree. Lynkk records online and in-person meetings and writes the minutes about 30 seconds after.

### Who should meeting minutes be distributed to?
Send them to every attendee, anyone who owns an action item and anyone who missed the meeting but is affected by its decisions. Send them within 24 hours so owners can start. Lynkk shares notes with your workspace and can post answers about meetings in Slack channels.

It is software that does 3 jobs: it records a meeting, transcribes it and writes the minutes for you, including decisions, action items, owners and dates. The best tools also send tasks to your work tools. Lynkk writes minutes about 30 seconds after the meeting and creates Jira tickets from the action items.
