## 1. How to read the numbers

- **Volume** = monthly searches for the **exact primary keyword** (not the title). Ubersuggest rounds into buckets, so read 1,600 as roughly 1,300-1,900.
- **Cluster volume** = primary + other keywords the post covers, added together. It is an upper bound, not a traffic forecast.
- **Real difficulty** comes from the weakest sites in today's top 10, which matters more than SD for a new domain.
- **(IN)** = India volume. India sends most of Lynkk's clicks today, so India keywords are worth more to you than their volume suggests.
