# Competitor facts (verified, for accuracy across articles)

Checked 2026-09-30 via search results restricted to each vendor's own site. Never link these sources in articles (no-competitor-links rule); cite in plain text.

## Watch-outs (claims we must NOT make)
- **Zoom AI Companion is not Zoom-only.** Licensed users can invite it to Microsoft Teams and Google Meet meetings (Zoom help: "Using AI Companion in third-party meetings"). Zoom's "My Notes" works across Zoom, Teams, Google Meet and in-person conversations. Do not write "only works inside Zoom".
- **Microsoft Copilot has a "Capture" feature** (Copilot Notebooks on iPhone and Windows) for recording conversations. Do not claim Copilot cannot record in person. Safe: "Copilot's meeting recap is built into Teams and needs a Copilot license."
- Do not claim Lynkk is "the only" tool with a feature unless verified against every competitor named in the article.

## Verified facts
- Zoom: Basic accounts record locally only (desktop app on Windows/macOS/Linux); cloud recording on paid plans; Zoom transcribes cloud recordings only, and only if the setting was on before recording; AI Companion is $0 extra on paid Zoom Workplace plans; hosts can allow participant recording or require requests; automatic recording setting exists; admins can enable/disable meeting summary with AI.
- Microsoft: Teams recording + live transcription included; transcript in calendar event, downloadable as .docx/.vtt; channel recordings in SharePoint, others in the recorder's OneDrive; intelligent recap needs Teams Premium or Copilot license; Copilot $30/user/month add-on (annual); Word Transcribe 300 min/month (M365), 30,000 with Copilot, .wav/.mp4/.m4a/.mp3; Word Dictate for M365 subscribers (Home > Dictate, Option+F1 on Mac); Windows voice typing Win+H, needs internet, auto punctuation.
- Apple: Mac Dictation, press Fn twice by default.
- Google: Docs voice typing via Tools > Voice typing in Chrome, Edge, Safari; editing commands English only. Gemini "Take notes for me" in Meet, included in Workspace Business and Enterprise since Jan 15, 2025.
- Otter: Basic free 300 min/month, 20 AI chat queries; Pro $8.33/user/month annual (1,200 min); Business $19.99 annual (6,000 min, unlimited imports); Enterprise custom.
- Fireflies: Free/Pro/Business/Enterprise; Pro $10 annual ($18 monthly); Business $19 ($29); Enterprise $39 annual only; free storage 400 min/team; 2 h cap Free/Pro, 3 h Business.
- Fathom: free unlimited recordings; advanced summaries first 5 calls/month; paid from $15/user/month (tiers $19-$34); new Team/Business plans need paid seats.
- Granola: free (30-day history); Business $14/user/month; Enterprise from $35; Mac, Windows, iPhone, Android; no bot.
- Jamie: free 10 meetings/month, 30 min; Plus €25; Pro €47; Team €39/seat; EU hosting.
- Read AI: free 5 meetings/month; Pro from $15/user/month annual.
- Rev: AI $0.25/min (96%+ claimed), human $1.99/min (99%+, 12 h or less), AI delivery under 5 min, subscribers save up to 15%.
- Descript: free 1 h/month; paid from $16/month; Creator 10 h, Pro 30 h; extra hours $2/h.
- Plaud Note Pro: $189; 4 MEMS mics; 16.4 ft pickup; 50 h recording; 60 days standby; Starter 300 min free; Pro $99.99/yr or $17.99/mo; Unlimited $239.99/yr or $29.99/mo; 112 languages.
- Pocket: 52 g MagSafe clip-on; core features with no mandatory subscription; Pocket Pro optional $19.99/month (first 30 days free). Device price not verified.
- Dragon Professional v16: Windows 11 (compatible with 10); dictation + audio file transcription. Price not verified.
