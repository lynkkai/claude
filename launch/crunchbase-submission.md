# Lynkk: Crunchbase profile submission

Field-by-field, ready to paste into Crunchbase's "Add a company" / profile editor.
Anything marked TODO needs a real value from the team before the profile goes live.
Crunchbase is a company database, not a launch channel, so accuracy and completeness
matter more than punchy copy: profiles get rejected or left unverified when the basics
(founded date, HQ, employee count, founders) are missing.

---

## Before you start

1. **Search first.** Look up "Lynkk", "lynkk.ai", "Lynk", "Lynk AI" and "Linnk" on Crunchbase.
   If a profile for Lynkk already exists, **claim** it instead of creating a duplicate.
   Duplicates are hard to merge later and they split the profile's SEO value.
2. **Use a work email.** Crunchbase matches the email domain against the website domain,
   so submit and claim from an @lynkk.ai address (marketing@lynkk.ai works).
3. **Expect a review queue.** New organization profiles are moderated. Turnaround is
   usually a few days, and thin profiles are the ones that stall.
4. **Assign an admin.** Whoever claims the profile becomes the point of contact for
   future edits and for Crunchbase sales outreach.

## Full Description

```
Lynkk is an AI meeting assistant that covers the entire meeting lifecycle: before, during and after the conversation.

Before a call, Lynkk surfaces context from past meetings: what was discussed, what is still open, and who said what, so teams start ahead instead of cold. During the meeting, Lynkk joins and speaks when needed, answering questions by voice, pulling up documents and retrieving past context live. Within about 30 seconds after the meeting ends, it delivers structured notes, assigned action items, updated CRM fields and created Jira tickets, with every meeting searchable through a knowledge graph.

Lynkk records both online meetings and in-person conversations, is multilingual and offline-first, and is available as a web app and a Mac desktop app with dictation that types at the cursor inside other applications. Customers choose the region where their data is processed, and every model behind Lynkk answers from that region, which supports legal and compliance requirements.

Lynkk integrates with calendars, Jira, Calendly, Cal.com and CRM systems, and connects to other tools via MCP. The product is free to start.
```

Word on tone: Crunchbase profiles are read by investors, analysts and journalists, so this
version is deliberately flatter than the Product Hunt copy in `launch/producthunt-submission.md`.
Keep the two consistent on facts (30 seconds, offline-first, data residency, integrations).
