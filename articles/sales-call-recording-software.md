## What is sales call recording software?

Call recording software for sales teams records, stores and transcribes customer calls. It then turns each one into something usable: a summary, a searchable transcript, a CRM entry or a coaching review. Plain recording is the easy part. Every tool here does it.

What separates them is 3 things: whether a bot joins the call, what AI call recording features come after, and whether the price is published.

## What is the best sales call recording software?

The best choice depends on team size and whether you need coaching and forecasting attached. Here's each tool, and where it stops making sense.

### 1. Gong: best for large teams

Gong is the best-known revenue intelligence platform, and at scale its call analysis and deal intelligence are hard to match. No published prices, though. No free plan either. Buyer reports put seats at roughly $1,200 to $2,400 a year with a platform fee on top, on contracts of 1 to 3 years, per our [Lynkk vs Gong comparison](https://lynkk.ai/compare/gong).

- **Pick it if:** you have RevOps to run it and want recording tied to forecasting.
- **Look elsewhere if:** you need a published price or want to start this week.

### 2. Clari Copilot: best for Clari and Salesloft teams

Clari Copilot records calls, flags deal risk and runs coaching scorecards. Mention a competitor, and a battlecard appears. Clari and Salesloft merged in December 2025, and buyer reports put standalone Copilot at about $120 to $160 a user a month on a 12-month minimum, per our [Clari Copilot comparison](https://lynkk.ai/compare/clari-copilot).

- **Pick it if:** deal risk from calls feeds your forecast.
- **Look elsewhere if:** you sell mostly in meetings rather than on the phone.

### 3. Avoma: best for mid-market teams

Avoma bundles a lot into one subscription: recording, notes, conversation intelligence, CRM updates and scheduling. Startup is $19 a seat a month billed yearly, with coaching and forecasting as $29 add-ons, per [Avoma's pricing](https://www.avoma.com/pricing). There's a 14-day trial rather than a free plan.

- **Pick it if:** you want recording and coaching in one published price.
- **Look elsewhere if:** you need a free tier that keeps working.

### 4. Fathom: best free sales call recorder

Fathom has a free-forever plan for recording, with sales features on paid tiers. AI scorecards, coaching metrics and CRM field sync sit on Business at $34 a user a month, or $25 billed yearly with a 2-user minimum, per [Fathom's pricing](https://fathom.ai/pricing).

- **Pick it if:** you want to record every call at no cost and upgrade later.
- **Look elsewhere if:** your meetings happen in a room as often as on Zoom.

### 5. Zoom: best if you already pay for Zoom

Zoom records calls natively, locally or to the cloud, and its paid plans add AI summaries. If everything you do is on Zoom, nothing is simpler. For calls on Google Meet, Teams or across a table, it does nothing at all.

- **Pick it if:** your whole team lives in Zoom.
- **Look elsewhere if:** you sell across several platforms.

### 6. Lynkk: best for calls and in-person meetings

Lynkk records Zoom, Google Meet and Teams with a [meeting bot](https://lynkk.ai/features/meeting-bot), or without one from the Mac app, which also records a conversation across a table. Each call becomes a transcript, summary and action items, and on Pro, Ask Lynkk answers questions across every call with the minute behind each answer. Free includes unlimited recordings and the first 5 hours of AI notes, with Pro at $15 a month, per [Lynkk's pricing](https://lynkk.ai/pricing).

- **Pick it if:** you want one tool for calls and in-person sales meetings.
- **Look elsewhere if:** you need CRM field sync, deal scoring or forecasting. Lynkk doesn't do those.

## Is it legal to record sales calls?

It depends where everyone on the call is. Call recording consent under US federal law needs one party's agreement, but about a dozen states, including California, Florida and Pennsylvania, require consent from everyone, as our guide to [whether it's legal to record a meeting](https://lynkk.ai/blog/is-it-legal-to-record-a-meeting) sets out in detail.

The safe habit is the same everywhere. Say you're recording at the start, and get a clear yes. 3 practical rules:

1. **Plan for the strictest rule** among the participants' locations, not your own.
2. **Say it out loud**, don't rely on a bot's name in the participant list.
3. **Write the one-liner** into your call script so every rep uses the same wording.

Our [sales calls](https://lynkk.ai/use-cases/sales-calls) page has consent wording you can copy.

## Do you need a bot to join the call?

No. There are 2 ways to record: a bot that joins as a visible participant, or a desktop app that records on your own machine. Buyers who dislike bots on their calls tend to prefer the second.

| | Bot joins the call | No bot |
|---|---|---|
| Visible to everyone | Yes, in the participant list | No, so tell people yourself |
| Works when you're not host | Usually | Yes |
| In-person meetings | No | Yes, from the laptop |
| Common objection | "Who is that in our call?" | None, but disclosure is on you |

Some companies ban recording bots outright. Worth checking before you roll a tool out to the whole team.

## How much does sales call recording software cost?

Prices run from free to about $160 a user a month among these tools. The published ones are Avoma at $19 a seat a month yearly, Fathom's Business at $34, and Lynkk Pro at $15 a month. Gong and Clari Copilot are quote-only.

Three costs people forget. Contract length. The add-ons carrying the features you actually wanted, since Avoma's coaching is a separate $29. And seat minimums.

Ask for the total first-year number, not the per-seat sticker price.

## What should you look for in a sales call recorder?

Judge it on what happens after the recording stops. Every tool records. These 6 checks sort a shortlist quickly.

- **Platforms:** Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, phone calls, and in-person if you need it.
- **Bot or no bot:** does it have to join the call?
- **Transcript quality:** speaker labels, search, and the languages your team sells in.
- **CRM:** does it write back to your CRM, and on which plan?
- **Retention and access:** who can hear the recording, and how long is it kept?
- **Price transparency:** published price, or a sales cycle?

## How do you use recorded sales calls?

Recording is worthless if nobody listens. Teams that get value from it build a small weekly habit instead of a big archive: one call per rep, reviewed together.

Use the recordings for 4 jobs: coaching one skill a week, writing accurate follow-up emails, updating the CRM from what was actually said, and settling disagreements about what a customer asked for. ## FAQ

### Does Zoom record sales calls automatically?

Zoom can record automatically if the host enables it, either locally or to the cloud, depending on the plan and admin settings. Automatic recording applies to Zoom meetings only, so calls on Google Meet, Microsoft Teams or in person need something else. Participants see a recording indicator when it starts.

### Can you record a sales call without the other person knowing?

Legally, often no, and commercially it's a bad idea even where it's allowed. About a dozen US states require consent from every participant, and other countries add their own rules. Announcing it costs you a sentence, and buyers rarely object once they know the recording is for notes.

### What is the best free sales call recording software?

Fathom is the strongest free option, with a free-forever recording plan. Lynkk's free plan covers unlimited recordings and the first 5 hours of AI notes. Zoom includes local recording on its free tier with limits. Free plans usually restrict AI summaries, CRM sync or scorecards rather than the recording itself.

### Do sales call recorders integrate with a CRM?

The paid ones usually do, but the plan matters. Fathom puts CRM field sync on its Business plan, Avoma includes CRM updates in its platform, and Gong and Clari Copilot build around CRM integration. Lynkk does not sync to a CRM today, so check this before buying if it's a requirement.

### How long should you keep sales call recordings?

Long enough to be useful and no longer, which for most teams means 6 to 12 months. Set a retention period deliberately rather than keeping everything forever, and check whether your tool lets admins configure it. Regulated industries often have specific rules that override your preference.

### Can you record in-person sales meetings?

Yes, with an app that records from your laptop or phone rather than joining a call. Lynkk's Mac app records a conversation in the room, and dedicated voice recorders do the same. The consent rules are identical to phone calls, so say you're recording before you start.
