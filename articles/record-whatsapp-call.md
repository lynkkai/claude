# How to Record WhatsApp Calls on iPhone, Android and Mac, and Get Notes Afterward

*Last updated: October 1, 2026 · Steps checked against current iOS, Android and WhatsApp behavior on October 1, 2026*

> **Quick answer:** WhatsApp has 0 built-in tools to record calls, and screen recording on a phone usually captures only your own voice. The most reliable way to record WhatsApp calls with both voices is to take the call in the WhatsApp app for Mac and use a call recording app, such as Lynkk, which records both sides and then writes notes and action items for you.

## Can you record WhatsApp calls?

Yes, you can record WhatsApp calls, but WhatsApp itself does not offer a record button for voice or video calls as of October 1, 2026. Every method relies on your phone, your computer or a separate app.

Here is what works on each device, at a glance:

| Device | Built-in option | Captures both voices? |
|---|---|---|
| iPhone | Screen Recording with microphone | Usually only your voice |
| Android | Screen recorder or call recording app | Depends on phone and Android version |
| Mac | WhatsApp for Mac plus a call recording app | Yes, with the right app |

If you need both people clearly, a Mac is the most dependable place to record WhatsApp calls.

## Can I record WhatsApp calls on iPhone?

You can try, but iPhone screen recording rarely captures the other person's voice on a WhatsApp call. Here are the 4 steps if you want to test it:

1. **Open Control Center** by swiping down from the top-right corner.
2. **Press and hold the Screen Recording button.** If it is missing, add it in Control Center settings.
3. **Tap Microphone** to turn it on, then start recording.
4. **Start or answer the WhatsApp call**, and stop the recording from Control Center when you are done.

Because of iOS privacy restrictions, the saved video often contains only your side of the call. Apple's own Call Recording feature works for phone calls and FaceTime audio calls, not for WhatsApp.

## How do you record WhatsApp calls on Android?

On Android, results depend on your phone maker and Android version, so test with a friend first. Most phones offer a built-in screen recorder in Quick Settings with 2 audio options: media sounds and microphone.

1. **Swipe down twice** to open Quick Settings.
2. **Tap Screen recorder** (add it with the edit button if it is missing).
3. **Choose to record media sounds and microphone**, if your phone offers both.
4. **Start the WhatsApp call**, then stop the recording from the notification.

Many Android versions block apps from capturing the other person's call audio, so you may again hear only yourself. Some third-party call recording apps work around this through accessibility permissions; read their privacy terms before granting that access.

## How to record WhatsApp call audio on a Mac

A Mac is the most reliable of the 3 devices to record WhatsApp calls, because the call runs in the WhatsApp desktop app and a call recording app can capture both your microphone and the call audio. Here is how to record WhatsApp call audio on a Mac in 4 steps:

1. **Install WhatsApp for Mac** and link it to your phone.
2. **Install the [Lynkk Mac app](https://lynkk.ai/download)**, which is free to install on Apple silicon Macs with macOS 14.2 or later.
3. **Start or answer the WhatsApp call on your Mac.** About 3 seconds in, Lynkk shows a card that says "WhatsApp call detected" with Record and Not now.
4. **Click Record.** Lynkk records both sides on 2 channels, so the transcript labels your side "You", and it stops when the call ends.

QuickTime and the Screenshot toolbar (Shift-Command-5) do not capture the Mac's internal audio on their own, which is why a screen recording of a WhatsApp call often has only your voice.

## Why does my WhatsApp call recording have no sound from the other person?

Your recording is missing the other voice because phones and computers protect call audio by default. In most cases only 1 source, your microphone, is captured.

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Only your voice on iPhone | iOS blocks call audio in screen recordings | Take the call on a Mac with a call recorder |
| Only your voice on Android | Android version blocks call audio capture | Test another recorder, or use a Mac |
| Silent recording on Mac | QuickTime cannot capture internal audio | Use an app that records call audio |
| Echo or doubled voices | Speakers feeding into the microphone | Wear headphones during the call |

## Is it legal to record WhatsApp calls?

It depends on where you and the other person are: US federal law allows recording with 1 party's consent, but some states require everyone on the call to agree. Federal law ([18 U.S.C. § 2511](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title18-section2511)) sets the 1 party rule, while California ([Penal Code § 632](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=632)) requires all parties to consent.

WhatsApp calls often cross borders, where stricter rules may apply. Do not record anyone secretly: say at the start that you are recording. The [NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework/privacy-framework) is a useful checklist for judging how any recording app handles your data.

## FAQ

### Can I record WhatsApp calls with the built-in WhatsApp app?
No. As of October 1, 2026, WhatsApp has no built-in button to record voice or video calls on iPhone, Android or desktop. You need your device's screen recorder or a separate call recording app. On a Mac, the Lynkk app records both sides of a WhatsApp call and writes notes afterward.

### Can you record WhatsApp calls on iPhone with sound?
Usually only 1 side, yours. iPhone Screen Recording with the microphone on captures your voice, but iOS privacy restrictions often block the other person's audio. Apple's Call Recording covers phone calls and FaceTime audio, not WhatsApp. Taking the call on a Mac with a call recorder captures both voices on 2 channels.

### Does WhatsApp notify the other person when you record a call?
WhatsApp shows 0 recording notices, because it has no recording feature. That makes it your job to tell people, and in some US states, such as California, every party must consent. Say "I'm recording this call" at the start, whichever device you use.
