  1. Visibility is growing fast, but clicks are not keeping up. Weekly impressions went from 775 to 16,467 in six weeks. Clicks peaked at 104 and fell to 78 last week, because new impressions arrive at position 15-40.
  2. Most lost clicks are on pages that already rank on page 1. 11 pages (Google Meet transcript, Granola pricing, Zoom AI Companion alternatives, voice memos, Hindi) average #6-12 but convert far below normal CTR. Titles and meta descriptions are the cheapest fix on the site.
  3. Topical authority is strong only in a few topics: Google Meet transcripts, competitor comparisons, meeting bots, and meeting prep and knowledge. The core category (AI note taker, meeting assistant, and Zoom/Teams searches) has 4,566 impressions with only 8-16% in the top 10.
  4. The funnel is top-heavy. TOFU queries bring half the impressions at a 0.15% CTR. MOFU gets 0.60%. Non-brand BOFU gets 0%. Compare pages convert best (2.6% CTR) but get only 832 impressions.
  5. Google now answers many Lynkk queries itself. 112 long, question-style queries (2,629 impressions, median position #7) produced 1 click. These show Lynkk being cited in AI answers. They grow brand searches rather than direct clicks.
  6. India is the best market. India gives 96 clicks at a 2.25% CTR vs 59 at 0.21% for the US. Ubersuggest rank tracking in India improved from an average of #65.7 to #29.8 in four weeks.
  7. Four blog posts and two integration pages have zero impressions. They include "best free AI note takers" (2,400/mo keyword) and "Zoom meeting recorders" (880/mo in India). They are indexable and linked, so Google has most likely chosen not to index them yet.

Since the 11 Nov report: 11 new blog posts went live, and Pro dropped from $8 to $4 a month. Price figures in this report use $4. Swap them into any copy taken from the earlier report.

Structure problems found

  • Sales is the biggest hub (15 posts) with the weakest return (4 clicks). 4 of the 11 new posts went into it.
  • The blog has no hub pages. /blog is one list. /features (87 words, per the Ubersuggest audit) and /use-cases get 0 links from any blog post.
  • Product pages are under-linked: HubSpot and Salesforce 0 blog links, Teams integration 2, Hinglish 5, team standups 2.
  • Overlap in Mac & dictation: dictation-on-mac, speech-to-text-mac and dictate-in-microsoft-word cover the same ground.
  • 52 URLs share one sitemap lastmod (30 Oct 12:00). Google ignores lastmod it cannot trust. Set it to the real edit date.
  • The old /articles/zoom-note-taker URL still gets 984 impressions. Its 308 redirect is correct. Google will consolidate it, so no action is needed.

What this means for sign-ups

  • TOFU (templates, how-tos) brings reach but few clicks, and visitors arrive with a task, not a buying need. Every TOFU post needs one specific CTA tied to its task. For example: "No Zoom transcript? Lynkk records free Zoom."
  • MOFU is where Lynkk is thinnest and where low-DA sites win (vs pages, "AI note taker for [platform]"). This is the focus of the plan below.
  • BOFU: the pages that convert (/compare at 2.6%, /download at 5.6%) get few impressions. Feed them from MOFU posts with comparison tables and links.

Mid-funnel (MOFU) keyword plan

Built from Google autocomplete (via Ubersuggest) on "ai note taker for", "ai meeting assistant for" and "otter ai vs", then checked for volume and SD in the US and India. MOFU searchers already know they want a tool and are choosing which one. These pages turn into sign-ups most directly.

How to learn MOFU keywords on Google

  • Autocomplete patterns. Type a category plus a modifier and read the suggestions: "ai note taker for ...", "[competitor] vs ...", "is [competitor] worth it", "[category] without bot", "best [category] for [role]".
  • People Also Ask on a MOFU search ("best ai note taker for teams") gives the H2s to answer.
  • GSC filter. In Performance, filter queries containing "best", "vs", "for", "alternative" or "software". That list is your own MOFU demand.
  • Ubersuggest. Google suggestions gives the phrases. Keyword ideas or match keywords give volume and SD. Keep SD 25 or lower for now.
  • MOFU page format. Comparison table in the first screen, a clear verdict for each type of buyer, Lynkk labelled "our product", honest limits, price for a team of 10, and a link to /compare.
