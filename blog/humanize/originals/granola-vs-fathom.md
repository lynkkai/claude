# Granola vs Fathom in 2026: Which Fits US Teams Better?

*By [Samarth Wagh](https://lynkk.ai/blog/authors/samarth-wagh), Co-founder, Lynkk.ai · Last updated 17 October 2026 · Pricing checked 17 October 2026*

> **Quick answer:** In the Granola vs Fathom choice, **Fathom** wins on free recording (unlimited recordings and transcripts at $0) and sales features like CRM sync. **Granola** wins on privacy and focus: no bot joins the call, and it builds notes from what you type. Paid plans start at $14 for Granola and $16 (billed yearly) for Fathom.

*Disclosure: Lynkk publishes this guide and also makes an AI meeting assistant. Prices come from each vendor's pricing page.*

Granola vs Fathom is one of the most common head-to-head questions for US teams picking an AI note taker. Both tools write meeting notes for you. They do it in very different ways, and that difference matters more than any feature list.

Fathom sends a recorder to your Zoom, Google Meet or Teams call and captures video, transcript and summary. Granola runs on your device, listens to your computer's audio, and turns your rough notes into clean ones. One is built for recording calls, the other for thinking during them.

## What is the difference between Granola and Fathom?

The core difference is capture: Fathom records the call (usually with a bot, with a bot-free beta), while Granola never joins the call and keeps no video. That one choice shapes price, privacy and who each tool suits.

| | Granola | Fathom |
|---|---|---|
| How it captures | Device audio, no bot | Meeting bot, plus bot-free beta |
| Video recording | No | Yes, with clips and playlists |
| Free plan | 30 days of meeting history | Unlimited recordings and transcripts |
| Cheapest paid plan | $14 per user per month | $16 per user per month billed yearly |

Granola's notes start from your own typing. You jot "pricing pushback, follow up Tuesday" and Granola expands it with the transcript. Fathom's notes start from the recording, then generate a summary and action items.

## How do Granola and Fathom compare on pricing in 2026?

Granola costs $0 on Basic and $14 per user per month on Business. Fathom costs $0 on Free, $20 a month for Premium on monthly billing ($16 yearly), and $34 a month for Business ($25 yearly).

| Plan | Granola | Fathom |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Basic, 30-day history | Free, unlimited recordings |
| Individual | Business $14 | Premium $20 monthly / $16 yearly |
| Team | Business $14 | Team $19 monthly / $15 yearly (2+ users) |
| Advanced | Enterprise $35 | Business $34 monthly / $25 yearly |

Sources: [Granola pricing](https://www.granola.ai/pricing) and [Fathom pricing](https://www.fathom.ai/pricing), checked 17 October 2026. For the full breakdown see our [Granola pricing guide](https://lynkk.ai/blog/granola-pricing).

For a 5-person team on annual billing, Granola Business costs $840 a year and Fathom Team costs $900 a year. The gap is small, so pick on workflow, not price.

## Does Granola or Fathom use a meeting bot?

Granola uses 0 bots: it captures audio on your device, so nobody on the call sees a recorder join. Fathom uses a bot by default and offers bot-free capture in beta, including on the free plan.

This matters most in client-facing work. Some US companies now ask vendors to keep bots out of their calls. With Granola you can still take AI-assisted notes. With Fathom you would switch to the beta bot-free mode or skip recording. Either way, tell people you are taking notes. Many US states require all-party consent to record a call.

## Which is better for sales teams, Granola or Fathom?

For most sales teams, Fathom is the better pick in the Granola vs Fathom decision, because its Business plan ($25 per user per month billed yearly) adds CRM field sync, deal views, coaching metrics and AI scorecards. Managers can also review call clips.

Granola Business connects to HubSpot, Attio and Affinity, so notes can land in your CRM. It does not record video, so there is no call library for coaching. Founders doing their own sales often prefer Granola for that reason: it feels like a notebook, not surveillance.

## Is Granola or Fathom better for privacy?

Granola is the more private choice: no bot, no stored video, and a toggle to opt out of model training on every plan. Fathom stores full call recordings, which is useful for coaching but means more sensitive data held by a vendor.

Check 3 things before you roll either out:

1. **Retention.** Granola's free plan keeps 30 days. Fathom's Enterprise plan adds custom data retention.
2. **Admin control.** Both reserve SSO for Enterprise.
3. **Consent.** Announce recording at the start of each call, whichever tool you use.

## Which integrations do Granola and Fathom support?

Granola Business ($14) connects to 5 named tools: Attio, Notion, HubSpot, Affinity and Zapier, plus an API and an MCP connection for AI assistants. Fathom puts its CRM field sync, deal views and custom summaries on the Business plan ($25 per user billed yearly).

The practical difference:

- **Granola** pushes notes into the tools where you already write, such as Notion pages or CRM records. It suits teams that treat notes as documents.
- **Fathom** pushes structured call data into the CRM and keeps the recording linked. It suits teams that review calls and coach reps.
- **Both** keep enterprise controls such as SSO on their top plan.

If your follow-up work happens in Jira, neither tool creates tickets for you out of the box. That gap is one reason product teams look at Lynkk, which sends action items to Jira in one click.

## Which devices and meeting apps does each tool cover?

Granola runs on 4 platforms: Mac and Windows for video calls, iPhone for phone calls and in-person conversations, and Android for in-person conversations since July 2026. Fathom covers Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams through its recorder.

| Need | Granola | Fathom |
|---|---|---|
| Zoom and Google Meet | Yes, via desktop app | Yes |
| Microsoft Teams | Yes, via desktop app | Yes |
| In-person meetings | Yes, via mobile apps | Not the focus |
| Phone calls | iPhone only | No |

Because Granola listens to your device, it works with almost any meeting app that plays through your speakers. Because Fathom joins as a participant, it works the same way for every person on your team, with nothing to install.

## How long does it take to set up Granola or Fathom?

Both tools take about 5 minutes to set up: sign in with Google or Microsoft, connect your calendar, and join your next meeting. The difference shows up on day 2.

- **Fathom** joins scheduled meetings automatically once your calendar is connected. You do not have to remember anything.
- **Granola** needs the app open on your device during the call. If you forget, there is no recording to fall back on.

For teams, Fathom is easier to standardize. For individuals who want control over what gets captured, Granola's manual start is a feature, not a bug.

## How do Granola and Fathom compare with Fireflies and Otter?

Fireflies ($10 per seat billed yearly) and Otter ($8.33 billed yearly) sit closer to Fathom: bot-based recorders with transcripts and summaries.

- **Granola vs Fathom vs Fireflies:** Fireflies has the deepest integrations and search across calls. Fathom has the best free plan. Granola is the only one with no bot at all.
- **Granola vs Fathom vs Otter:** Otter's free plan caps conversations at 30 minutes. Fathom's does not. Granola's limit is history, not minutes.

## FAQ

### Is Granola better than Fathom?

In the Granola vs Fathom debate, Granola is better for private, bot-free notes that start from what you type. Fathom is better for recording calls, sharing clips and syncing to a CRM. Neither is better for every team. Run both on 3 real meetings before deciding.

### Is Fathom free?

Yes. Fathom's free plan includes unlimited recordings and transcriptions plus instant AI summaries. Some AI features carry a "limited use" tag, and Premium costs $16 a month billed yearly. Fathom works with Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, which makes the free plan useful for most US teams from day one.

### Is Granola free?

Granola has a free Basic plan with AI notes and chat, but it keeps only 30 days of meeting history. Business costs $14 per user per month and removes the history limit. Students and accepted startups can get 12 months of Business free, and registered nonprofits get 50% off, through Granola's help center programs.

### Does Granola record video like Fathom?

No. Granola transcribes your computer's audio and writes notes, but it does not record video. Fathom records video and lets you clip and share moments. If you need to replay a moment, such as a customer's exact wording, Fathom is the better fit. Granola suits people who only need the notes.

### Which works better for Google Meet and Zoom?

Both work with Zoom and Google Meet. Fathom joins as a recorder, while Granola captures audio from your desktop app on Mac or Windows. Fathom also supports Microsoft Teams. Granola needs its app open during the call, while Fathom joins automatically from your calendar. That makes Fathom easier to standardize across a team.
