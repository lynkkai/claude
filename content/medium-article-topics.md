# Lynkk: 5 easy-to-rank Medium article topics

Keyword research pulled from Ubersuggest on 2026-10-08 (location: United States, English).
SD = Ubersuggest SEO difficulty (0-100, lower is easier). Volume = US monthly searches.

## How these were picked

1. Seeded Ubersuggest with Lynkk's core themes: AI meeting assistant, AI note taker, meeting
   notes, meeting transcription, action items, sales call notes, meeting follow-up email,
   meeting preparation.
2. Pulled organic SEO difficulty for about 20 candidates. Rejected anything above SD 35 and
   anything that is mostly a competitor brand term (Otter, Fireflies, Read AI, Copilot).
3. Checked the live SERP for each finalist. Every pick has low-authority sites (DA 7 to 36)
   on page one, and two already have Medium publications ranking, which proves Medium can win.
4. Kept only topics where Lynkk is a natural fit, and covered every stage of Lynkk's story:
   before, during and after the meeting.

Rejected but worth knowing:

| Keyword | US vol | SD | Why not now |
|---|---|---|---|
| how to write meeting notes | 880 | 36 | Good volume, but harder. Best "next" article once the 5 below are live. |
| how to take meeting notes | 590 | 44 | Too competitive for a first wave. |
| ai powered meeting notes | 260 | 49 | Commercial, harder SERP. |
| real time ai meeting assistant | 110 | 59 | Perfect fit for Lynkk, but hard. Revisit later. |
| meeting transcription software | 260 | 67 | Hard. |
| ai note taker that doesn't join meetings | 90 | 30 | Easy, but Lynkk's pitch is that it *does* join and speak. Wrong fit. |

## The 5 topics (easiest first)

### 1. Meeting follow-up email (after stage)

| | |
|---|---|
| Primary keyword | after meeting follow up email (210/mo, **SD 22**) |
| Secondary | meeting notes email sample (110, SD 22), meeting follow up email (50, SD 29), meeting follow up email example, meeting follow up email with action items |
| SERP proof | A Medium publication (betterhumans.pub) is the **#1 organic result**. Others include noota.io (DA 34), biscred.com (DA 20), koalendar.com (DA 43). |

**Title:** How to Write a Follow-Up Email After a Meeting (7 Samples You Can Copy)

**Subtitle:** Short templates for clients, teams and sales calls, plus how to send a recap with notes and action items.

**Outline**
- Why the follow-up email matters (decisions get lost within a day)
- The 4 parts of a good follow-up: thanks, summary, action items with owners and dates, next step
- 7 samples: client meeting, internal team sync, sales discovery call, interview, networking, board/committee, "sharing the meeting notes" email
- Subject lines that get opened
- Timing: send within 24 hours
- Soft tie-in: AI assistants such as Lynkk can draft the notes and action items about 30 seconds after the call, so the email takes a minute instead of twenty

### 2. Meeting preparation checklist (before stage)

| | |
|---|---|
| Primary keyword | meeting preparation (390/mo, **SD 24**) |
| Secondary | checklist for meeting preparation (50, **SD 14**), meeting preparation checklist, pre meeting preparation, sales meeting preparation |
| SERP proof | Page one has formeret.fr (DA 25), ksl-training.co.uk (DA 29), flowtrace.co (DA 30), boardwise.io (DA 19), squer.io (DA 19). Very beatable. |

**Title:** Meeting Preparation: A 10-Minute Checklist So You Never Walk In Cold

**Subtitle:** What to review, what to decide, and what to bring before any client call, 1:1 or team meeting.

**Outline**
- What meeting preparation is and why most people skip it
- The checklist: goal, agenda, attendees, what happened last time, open action items, documents, questions to ask
- The step everyone misses: reviewing what was promised in the *previous* meeting
- Variations: sales call, 1:1, board meeting, interview
- Soft tie-in: this is Lynkk's main differentiator. It surfaces past context (what was discussed, what's still open, who said what) before every call

## Guidelines every article must follow

### Medium's rules (affect distribution and ranking)

- **AI disclosure.** If any text or images are AI-generated, say so in the first two paragraphs
  (and caption AI images). Grammar checkers, outlining help and fact checks don't need disclosure.
- **Fully AI-written stories get no distribution** beyond your own followers, and AI-generated
  writing can't be paywalled in the Partner Program. Write these by hand; use AI only for
  outlining and editing.
- **No AI-written product reviews made to rank and sell.** Medium bans this outright. Topic 5 must
  be a genuine, human-tested comparison.
- **Promotion limits.** Stories whose main point is selling, collecting signups or driving traffic
  don't get General Distribution. Keep Lynkk to one short, useful mention plus a single link to
  https://lynkk.ai near the end. Teach first.
- **Disclose affiliation.** When Lynkk is mentioned in a comparison, say you work on it.
- **No copied or closely rewritten content**, and no unverified facts.

### Lynkk house rules (from this repo)

- Spell it **Lynkk** (double k) and link to **lynkk.ai**. Never Lynk, Linnk or Link AI.
- **No em dashes or en dashes.** Use commas, colons, parentheses or a new sentence. Plain hyphens
  are fine for compound words and ranges.
- Only claim confirmed features (see `memory/lynkk-brand.md`). Do **not** state pricing, Zoom /
  Meet / Teams support, specific CRMs, number of languages, data regions or certifications until
  they are confirmed.

### On-page SEO checklist (for Medium)

- Put the exact primary keyword in the title, the subtitle and the first 100 words.
- In story settings, set a custom **SEO title** (under 60 characters) and **SEO description**
  (under 156 characters) that use the keyword.
- Use the keyword or a secondary in at least two H2 headings.
- Use all **5 tags**. Suggested set: Meetings, Productivity, Remote Work, Artificial Intelligence,
  plus one topic-specific tag (Email Writing, Business, Sales, Project Management, Note Taking).
- Aim for 1,200 to 1,800 words, with a table, a template or samples readers can copy.
- Answer the question in the first paragraph (featured snippet bait).
- Submit to an established publication where possible (Better Humans and Towards AI already rank
  for these keywords). Publication domains rank far better than a new personal profile.
- If you also publish on lynkk.ai's blog, set Medium's canonical link to the original.
- Interlink the 5 articles to each other once they're live.

### Suggested publishing order

1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5. The first four build topical authority with low-risk educational posts;
the comparison piece lands last when the profile already has credible articles behind it.
