# Notion AI Meeting Notes Review: How It Works, What It Costs and Its Real Limits

*Last updated: 24 November 2026*

> **Quick answer:** Notion AI Meeting Notes records, transcribes and summarizes meetings inside a Notion page, with no bot joining the call. It needs the Business or Enterprise plan for full use (Business is listed at $20 per member per month), and Free and Plus users get a limited trial. It works best for teams that already run their work in Notion.

**Notion AI Meeting Notes** is Notion's built-in note taker, launched as part of Notion AI. You start it from a page, it listens through your computer, and when the call ends it writes a summary, action items and a transcript right where your project docs already live.

This review covers how it works, setup in 4 steps, what it costs, 6 limits that matter in practice, and when a dedicated meeting assistant is the better choice. All product details are taken from [Notion's help center](https://www.notion.com/help/ai-meeting-notes) and [pricing page](https://www.notion.com/pricing) as of November 2026.

## How does Notion AI Meeting Notes work?

It captures audio from your device, transcribes it, and writes a summary into a meeting block on a Notion page, using 3 capture modes. It does not send a bot into Zoom, Google Meet or Teams. Notion's pricing page describes it as "no bot needed."

What gets captured depends on where you run it:

| Where you use it | What it records |
|---|---|
| Notion desktop app | System audio and your mic, so both sides of the call |
| Browser | Your mic only; system audio is not captured |
| Mobile app | Whatever your phone's microphone picks up |

After the call you get 3 things in the meeting block: an AI summary, action items, and a transcript with speaker labels in supported languages. You can also upload an existing recording in AAC, M4A, MP3 or WAV format.

### Can Notion AI take meeting notes in Zoom, Google Meet and Teams?

Yes. Because it records system audio through the desktop app, it works with any call app, including Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams. Notion's help center adds 2 extras: Zoom speaker names on a Mac, and Google Meet speaker labels through a Chrome extension.

It can also detect when you leave a call and stop transcribing automatically.

## How do you use Notion AI Meeting Notes?

To start, open a Notion page, type /meet, and begin transcribing before or during your call. Setup takes 4 steps once your plan and desktop app are ready.

1. **Check requirements.** You need a Business or Enterprise plan (or a trial), and the desktop app version 4.7.0 or later. On a Mac, Notion requires macOS 13 or later.
2. **Start the notes.** On any page, type /meet to insert an AI Meeting Notes block, then click **Start transcribing**. You can also start from an event in Notion Calendar.
3. **Share consent.** Notion shows a consent message you can send to attendees so they know the meeting is transcribed.
4. **End and review.** When the call ends, stop transcribing (or let Notion detect it). The summary and action items appear in the block.

### How do you customize the summary?

You can change the summary format with custom instructions, such as "list decisions first" or "group action items by owner." Set 1 default instruction so every meeting follows the same structure.

### How do you connect it to Notion Calendar?

Connect your Google Calendar to Notion Calendar, and each event gets an option to start AI Meeting Notes. The notes are linked to the event, so you can find them later from the 1 place you already check before meetings.

### How do you share and control access to meeting notes?

Meeting notes follow normal Notion page permissions, so access depends on 2 things: the page or database they live in, and who you share it with. You can also choose 1 default database for AI Meeting Notes so every meeting is filed in the same place with the right access.

For sensitive calls, file notes in a private teamspace and share only the summary. Our guide to [AI notetaker security](https://lynkk.ai/blog/ai-notetaker-security) covers what to check before rolling any note taker out across a company.

### How do you turn Notion AI Meeting Notes on or off?

There are 2 levels of control: workspace owners manage AI features in workspace settings, and individual users can stop or skip a meeting block. If a block is already transcribing, click **Stop** in the block to end it.

## Is Notion AI Meeting Notes free?

It is not fully free. Notion's help center says you "must be on Notion's Business or Enterprise Plan to use AI Meeting Notes," and the pricing page lists Meeting notes as a "Limited Trial" on lower plans.

| Plan | Listed price (per member, per month) | AI Meeting Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Limited trial |
| Plus | $10 | Limited trial |
| Business | $20 | Included |
| Enterprise | Custom | Included |

Notion's pricing page notes you can save up to 20% with yearly billing. Prices and trial limits change, so check the [pricing page](https://www.notion.com/pricing) before you budget.

### How much does Notion AI Meeting Notes cost for a team?

For a 10-person team, the Business plan at the listed $20 per member per month comes to about $200 per month, or $2,400 per year before any annual discount. You pay for the whole workspace plan, not just meeting notes.

## What are the limits of Notion AI Meeting Notes?

The main limits are plan cost, a desktop-app dependency for full audio, and a summary that stays inside Notion. Here are 6 limits worth checking against your workflow:

1. **Browser and mobile capture is partial.** In the browser, only your mic is transcribed. On mobile, only what your phone's microphone hears.
2. **Desktop requirements.** Full capture needs the desktop app (4.7.0 or later) and, on a Mac, macOS 13 or later.
3. **Minimum length.** Notion needs at least 1 minute of recorded audio to generate a summary.
4. **Speaker labels need a supported language.** Notion lists 23 languages for speaker labels.
5. **Outputs stay in Notion.** Action items land in Notion pages and databases. Pushing them into Jira or a CRM needs other tools or manual work.
6. **No live help during the call.** It records and summarizes. It does not answer questions or pull up past context while the meeting is happening.

### Does Notion AI Meeting Notes notify other participants?

Notion adds 0 bots to the call, so nothing announces itself. Notion provides a consent message you can share, and you are responsible for telling attendees they are being recorded. Check the [rules on recording meetings](https://lynkk.ai/blog/is-it-legal-to-record-a-meeting) where you work. US federal law ([18 U.S.C. § 2511](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/2511)) permits recording with 1 party's consent, but several states require all parties to agree.

### Does Notion AI Meeting Notes work with headphones?

Yes, in the desktop app (version 4.7.0 or later), because it captures system audio rather than relying on your speakers. In the browser, it captures only your microphone, so with headphones it will miss the other side of the call.

## Is Notion AI Meeting Notes worth it?

It is worth it if your team already pays the $20 per member Business plan and keeps meeting notes, projects and docs in Notion. It is less compelling if you only want a meeting tool, since you pay for the full workspace plan.

| Good fit | Weaker fit |
|---|---|
| Team already on Notion Business or Enterprise | Team on Free or Plus that only needs meeting notes |
| Notes should live next to project docs | Actions need to reach Jira or a CRM automatically |
| Calls happen on a desktop computer | Many in-person or phone conversations |
| English or another supported language | Mixed-language meetings that switch mid-sentence |

## How does Notion AI Meeting Notes compare with dedicated AI note takers?

Notion's tool is a feature inside a workspace, while dedicated AI note takers are built only for meetings. That difference shows up in 5 areas.

| Area | Notion AI Meeting Notes | Dedicated AI meeting assistant |
|---|---|---|
| Capture | Desktop system audio, browser mic, phone mic | Often a bot, desktop app or both |
| Where notes live | Notion pages and databases | Its own app, synced to other tools |
| After the call | Summary, action items, transcript | Often also CRM updates and tickets |
| Cost model | Part of the Notion plan | Separate subscription, often with a free tier |
| Best for | Teams already living in Notion | Teams that meet across many tools |

If most of your follow-up work happens in Notion, the built-in option keeps everything in 1 place. If it happens in Jira, a CRM or email, a dedicated assistant usually saves more time.

## FAQ

### Is Notion AI Meeting Notes free for students?

Notion's help center does not list a separate student plan for AI Meeting Notes. Free plan users get a limited trial, and full access needs 1 of the 2 higher plans: Business or Enterprise.

### Can I upload a recording to Notion AI Meeting Notes?

Yes. You can upload an existing audio recording in 1 of 4 formats: AAC, M4A, MP3 or WAV. Notion then transcribes and summarizes it like a live meeting.

### What languages does Notion AI Meeting Notes support?

Notion lists 23 languages for speaker labels, including English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Chinese. Check the help center for the current list before relying on it for a specific language.

### Does Notion AI Meeting Notes use a bot?

No. Notion AI Meeting Notes does not add a bot to your call. The desktop app captures system audio and your microphone directly, which Notion describes as "no bot needed."

### Where are Notion AI Meeting Notes saved?

Notes are saved in the Notion page where you started the meeting block, and you can set 1 default database for AI Meeting Notes so every meeting is filed in the same place.
