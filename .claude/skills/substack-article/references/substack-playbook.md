# Substack playbook: research notes for the Lynkk publication

Compiled 2026-10-07 from web research. Substack-hosted pages were not directly fetchable from the
research environment, so Substack-specific findings come from search-result summaries of the
sources listed; treat creator-reported numbers as directional, not laws.

## 1. What grows a Substack

| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| Publish at least one post a week; each post is another chance to be discovered. Consistency compounds. | [Darius Bashar, 23 Rules to Grow on Substack in 2026](https://artistmorning.substack.com/p/23-rules-for-a-breakout-year-on-substack) |
| Know the one reader you write for. Finish: "Every time I land in your inbox, you get ___." | same |
| Posts build depth, Notes build visibility: aim for 1 post a week and 1-3 Notes a day. | [Thrive with Carrie, Notes Strategy 2026](https://thrivewithcarrie.substack.com/p/substack-notes-strategy-2026) |
| Recommendations from other publications are the biggest on-platform growth lever. Comment thoughtfully on adjacent writers' posts, collaborate, swap recommendations. | [I Asked 32 Writers for Their Best Growth Advice](https://2hourblogger.substack.com/p/writers-best-substack-growth-advice) |
| Growth runs on four lanes at once: a newsletter worth subscribing to, daily Notes, recommendations, and distribution off Substack (LinkedIn, X, Threads). | [Eden, Substack growth strategy](https://eden.so/blog/substack-growth-strategy/) |
| Track subscribers gained per post, not likes. | [Write Build Scale](https://writebuildscale.substack.com/p/start-and-grow-your-substack) |

## 2. Titles, subtitles, open rate

| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| Title = email subject, subtitle = preview text. Together they decide open rate. The subtitle should add to the title, not repeat it. | [How to Make Your Substack Posts Perform Better](https://pubstacksuccess.substack.com/p/how-to-make-your-substack-posts-perform) |
| Around 7 words / 30-50 characters fits mobile inboxes. | same family of guides |
| Creator analysis of ~94,000 Substack posts: first-person titles beat neutral how-to titles; titles with "mistake / wrong / stop / failed / don't" framing drew notably more reactions. | [I Analyzed 94,000 Substack Posts](https://ceators.substack.com/p/i-analyzed-94000-substack-posts-heres) |
| Open rate guide: under 20% poor, 30-50% average, over 50% good. | same |
| Substack can A/B test two titles on part of the list and send the winner to the rest (for publications above a subscriber threshold). | same |

## 3. Notes (Substack's social feed)

| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| In an analysis of ~473k Notes, short Notes broke out far more often; under ~100 characters broke out at nearly twice the rate of 600-word Notes. Posting 3+ times a week beat occasional posting. | [Eden, How to grow on Substack Notes](https://eden.so/blog/how-to-grow-on-substack-notes/) |
| Story-opening Notes get more engagement than advice-opening Notes; tactical "quick fix" and personal-story-with-data Notes convert to subscribers best. | [Thrive with Carrie](https://thrivewithcarrie.substack.com/p/substack-notes-strategy-2026) |
| Formats that work: one-line insight, micro-story (<300 words), list/countdown. | [Eden](https://eden.so/blog/how-to-grow-on-substack-notes/) |

## 4. SEO on Substack

| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| Each post has three SEO fields: SEO title, SEO description, URL slug. They don't change how the email or on-site post looks. | [Substack: A guide to SEO on Substack](https://on.substack.com/p/substack-seo-guide) |
| Put the brand at the end of the SEO title; use the description to add context and secondary keywords the title couldn't fit; keep slugs short and keyword-bearing. | [Sprout Social, Substack SEO](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/substack-seo/) |

## 5. Brands and companies on Substack

| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| Substack is editorial, not ecommerce. Branded publications that work read like a magazine: perspective, behind the scenes, founder thinking. | [Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/marketing/brands-briefing-what-brands-should-know-before-jumping-into-substack/), [Meltwater](https://www.meltwater.com/en/blog/substack-brand-marketing-examples) |
| Founder-led voice usually beats a faceless logo; readers follow a mind. | [What we actually want from brands on Substack](https://itsgpf.substack.com/p/what-we-actually-want-from-brands) |
| Many successful brand newsletters don't mention the product in every post. | [Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/marketing/why-selena-gomezs-rare-beauty-is-betting-on-substack-as-brands-embrace-long-form-content/) |
| B2B newsletters fail when they broadcast company news instead of being useful. Answer: who is it for, what recurring problem does it solve, why trust us? | [Growthspree, Newsletter strategy for B2B SaaS](https://www.growthspreeofficial.com/blogs/newsletter-strategy-b2b-saas.md) |

**Implication for Lynkk:** publish under a named person (founder or team lead) with a "we" voice
for company views. Keep posts 80%+ useful on their own. Use the product as the worked example,
not the subject.

## 7. Content pillars for the Lynkk Substack

1. **Meeting lifecycle:** before / during / after thinking (Lynkk's core differentiator).
2. **Follow-through:** action items, follow-up emails, CRM and ticket hygiene.
3. **Preparation and context:** walking in warm, institutional memory.
4. **Trust:** consent, bots, privacy, data residency, in-person recording.
5. **Team playbooks:** sales, product/engineering, founders, consultants, multilingual teams.
6. **Behind the build:** what we learned building Lynkk (founder voice, honest tradeoffs).
