## 8. On-page recommendations for /features/meeting-notes

### URL
`/features/meeting-notes` works. If changing is easy, `/features/ai-note-taker` or
`/features/ai-meeting-notes` matches the target keywords better. The page has no rankings yet, so
a change (with a 301 redirect) costs nothing now and gets harder later.

### Title tag (60 chars)
```
AI Note Taking App for Meetings: Notes in 30 Seconds | Lynkk
```
Alternate (51 chars):
```
AI Meeting Notes, Summaries & Action Items | Lynkk
```

### Meta description (148 chars)
```
Lynkk's AI note taker turns online and in-person meetings into AI meeting notes, summaries, and action items in 30 seconds. Free AI note taking app.
```

### H1
```
The AI note taker that writes your meeting notes in 30 seconds
```

### Suggested H2 structure (keyword in brackets)
1. **AI meeting notes, summaries and action items, automatically** [ai meeting notes, ai notes]
2. **How Lynkk's AI note taking app works** [ai note taking app, meeting notes taker]
   Record, transcribe, structure: a 3-step visual.
3. **Meeting notes with action items, assigned automatically** [meeting notes with action items, minutes of meeting action items]
   Show owners, due dates, and the push to Jira.
4. **An AI meeting summary and recap of every call: decisions, next steps, who said what** [ai meeting summary, meeting summary, summarize meeting, meeting recap]
5. **AI meeting minutes, generated automatically** [meeting minutes app, meeting minutes software, ai meeting minutes generator, minutes taker]
   Formal minutes format (attendees, decisions, motions, action items) for board and corporate meetings.
6. **Works for online and in-person meetings** [ai note taker for in person meetings]
   This is the strongest differentiator against bot-only tools. Make it a full section.
7. **Meeting notes for your whole team** [team ai meeting notes]
   Shared, searchable notes across every meeting (knowledge graph).
8. **Send notes where the work happens** CRM fields, Jira tickets, Calendar, Calendly, Cal.com, MCP.
9. **Multilingual and private by design** languages, data residency by region.
10. **See a real example** an actual sample of Lynkk's notes output (screenshot plus real HTML text, so Google can read it).
11. **Lynkk vs. other AI note taking apps** [ai note taking app, meeting notes taking app]
    Short table: before/during/after coverage, in-person, live voice assistant, data residency.
12. **FAQ** (see below)

Link from the page to the homepage with anchor text "AI meeting assistant", and to the live
voice assistant page. That tells Google which page owns which keyword.

### FAQ (targets People Also Ask; add FAQPage schema)
- What is an AI note taker?
- How do AI meeting notes work?
- Is there a free AI note taking app for meetings? [free ai note taking app]
- Can Lynkk take notes for in-person meetings?
- Does Lynkk work with Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams? *(confirm platforms first)*
- What's the difference between meeting notes and meeting minutes?
- Can AI write meeting minutes? [meeting minutes ai, ai for meeting minutes]
- How accurate are AI meeting notes?
- Where is my meeting data stored?

### Other on-page items
- **Length:** aim for 1,200-1,800 words of real, crawlable text (not text baked into images).
- **Schema:** `SoftwareApplication` (name, category, offers: free) + `FAQPage`.
- **Images:** descriptive alt text, e.g. "Lynkk AI meeting notes with action items and summary".
- **Internal links:** link to this page from the homepage, /features/meeting-bot, and every
  blog post below, using anchors like "AI note taker", "AI meeting notes" and "AI note taking app".
- **Proof points** seen in search snippets for lynkk.ai (confirm before using):
  97% transcription accuracy, 15+ languages with code-switching, unlimited free recording
  and transcription. Specific numbers like these help both conversions and AI Overview citations.
