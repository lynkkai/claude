## How to use the Reddit queries

1. Add a "What Reddit users say" or FAQ section to each post answering the listed queries in plain language.
2. Search each query on Reddit, note the top recurring complaints (price, privacy, bots joining calls, accuracy) and address them directly.
3. Do not paste Reddit usernames or full quotes without permission; summarize the sentiment instead.
