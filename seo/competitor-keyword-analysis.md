# Lynkk.ai: Competitor and Keyword Strategy Analysis

**Prepared:** 2026-11-03
**Data source:** Ubersuggest (Neil Patel Digital) via API, account marketing@lynkk.ai
**Primary market:** United States (Google loc 2840). India (2356) sampled for comparison.
**Snapshot month:** October 2026

---

## 1. Executive summary

Five things matter in this report:

1. **Lynkk is starting from zero.** Domain Authority 1, three ranking keywords, nine backlinks from five referring domains. Every recommendation below is filtered through what a DA 1 site can actually win.
2. **The category head terms are out of reach for now.** "ai note taker" (27,100 US searches/mo, SD 48) and "ai meeting assistant" (4,400, SD 60) are held by domains at DA 42 to 66 with 3,800 to 30,000 referring domains. Chasing these first would waste two quarters.
3. **The fastest wins are competitor comparison and alternative keywords.** "fireflies alternatives" is SD 14. "otter ai competitors" is SD 16. "fireflies vs otter" is SD 21 with a $42 CPC. These are low volume but bottom-funnel, cheap to rank for, and they convert.
4. **Lynkk's differentiators have almost no search demand.** "meeting notes to jira" returns 0 volume, "crm meeting notes" returns 10, "ai crm meeting notes integration" returns 0. The knowledge graph, Jira push, CRM sync, MCP and data residency are conversion assets and comparison-page ammunition, not standalone SEO targets.
5. **The whole category is losing organic clicks.** Every single competitor shows a steep traffic decline over the last 24 months (see section 5.3). Part of that is Ubersuggest re-indexing, but the consistency across eleven unrelated domains points to real AI Overview and AI search cannibalization. Brand presence and AI search visibility need to run alongside classic SEO, not after it.

### 2.3 What each competitor actually wins on

This is the most useful part of the competitive picture, because the headline traffic numbers are misleading.

- **read.ai's 454,139 visits are essentially one accident.** The keyword "read" (301,000 searches/mo) at position 2 delivers 145,503 of them. That is a brand-name coincidence, not an SEO capability. Strip it out and read.ai is a mid-sized player. Do not benchmark against their traffic.
- **tactiq.io built its traffic on free tools, not product pages.** Their `/tools/youtube-transcript` page alone ranks for "youtube video transcript" (22,200), "youtube transcript download" (3,600) and dozens of long-tail variants. This is the single most replicable playbook in the set.
- **notta.ai wins on programmatic scale.** 46,137 keywords from templated pages: language-pair translation pages, file-format converters, tool pages. High volume, low relevance to a meeting-intelligence buyer.
- **fireflies.ai wins on off-topic TOFU.** Their best performers include "unique team names work" (390), "team names for groups" (320), "morning meeting questions" (5,400) and "meeting takeaways". Lots of traffic, weak purchase intent. They also own their own brand terms completely ("fireflies pricing", "fireflies ai price", "is fireflies ai free").
- **avoma.com wins on legal and sales-methodology content.** Their `/blog/call-recording-laws` page ranks for an entire cluster of "one party consent states" queries. They also own "conversation intelligence software" at position 1, and they run explicit comparison pages ("fathom vs otter", "gong alternative").
- **otter.ai owns the transcription job-to-be-done,** not the meeting-assistant one: "transcribe audio to text" (22,200), "voice recording transcription" (27,100), "convert audio to text" (2,900).
- **smartnoter.ai is the proof of concept for Lynkk.** DA 8, only 2,110 backlinks, yet 108,481 estimated visits. A near-zero-authority domain can generate real traffic in this category within a short window.

## 4. Intent analysis

Ubersuggest classifies intent into four types. The distribution across this data set is strategically revealing.

| Intent type | What the searcher wants | Share of sampled keywords | Conversion value | Where it clusters |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Informational** | To understand or learn | ~45% | Low | TOFU: meeting minutes, action items definitions, recording how-tos, legality |
| **Transactional** | To get or use a tool now | ~30% | **Highest** | "ai note taker" + platform/feature modifiers, "free", pricing |
| **Commercial** | To compare before buying | ~20% | **High** | "best", "vs", "alternative", "competitors", conversation intelligence |
| **Navigational** | To reach a specific product | ~5% | Zero for Lynkk | Competitor brand terms |

Three observations worth acting on:

1. **The category head term is Transactional, not Informational.** Ubersuggest labels "ai note taker" (27,100) as Transactional with competition 1.0. People searching it want to start using something immediately. That means the page that eventually targets it must be a product or free-tool page, not a blog post.
2. **"ai meeting assistant" is Commercial while "ai note taker" is Transactional.** Same category, different buying stage. "Assistant" searchers are still comparing; "note taker" searchers are ready. Lynkk's positioning language ("your AI teammate for every meeting") sits on the Commercial side, which is the softer, slower side. Worth testing "note taker" language on conversion pages.
3. **Transactional intent concentrates in the platform modifiers.** "ai note taker for teams", "for zoom", "for google meet", "for in person meetings", "for therapists" are all Transactional and all SD 26 to 40. This is the single best intersection of winnable and valuable in the entire data set.

## 6. Competitor analysis: what ranks easily and what takes time

### 6.1 The mechanism

Ubersuggest Search Difficulty is driven mainly by the backlink profile of the pages currently ranking. Lynkk's position is stark: **DA 1, nine backlinks, five referring domains.** The competitors in this category sit at 3,800 to 30,000 referring domains.

Mapping Lynkk's capability against SD gives this realistic ceiling:

| SD band | Winnable for Lynkk today? | Realistic time to page 1 | What it requires |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SD 0 to 20** | Yes, now | **1 to 3 months** | One good page, basic on-page SEO, internal links |
| **SD 21 to 35** | Yes | **3 to 6 months** | Good page plus 5 to 15 referring domains |
| **SD 36 to 45** | Stretch | **6 to 12 months** | Strong page, 20 to 50 referring domains, topical depth |
| **SD 46 to 60** | Not yet | **12 to 18 months** | DA 25+, cluster authority, sustained digital PR |
| **SD 61+** | No | **18 to 24+ months** | DA 40+, hundreds of referring domains |

### 6.2 Keyword types that rank fast (and why)

**1. Competitor comparison and alternative terms. Fastest of all.**
SD 11 to 35. The pages competing are blog posts, not fortified product pages, and the link profiles behind them are thin. "best ai notetaker for in person meetings" is SD 11. "fireflies alternatives" is SD 14. "otter ai competitors" is SD 16. A single well-built comparison page can rank in 4 to 8 weeks.

**2. Question-format long tail. Very fast.**
"does teams have an ai note taker" (260, SD 14), "does microsoft teams have an ai note taker" (170, SD 29). Google rewards a direct answer; authority barely matters. These also win AI Overview and featured-snippet placements, which matters more every quarter.

**3. Platform-plus-feature long tail. Fast.**
SD 26 to 35 consistently. "microsoft teams ai note taker" SD 26, "ai note taker for microsoft teams" SD 26, "ms teams ai note taker" SD 26. Narrow enough that incumbents have not built dedicated pages, valuable enough to convert.

**4. Template and tool pages. Fast, with effort.**
"tracking action items in excel" SD 12, "action items excel template" SD 25. Low difficulty, and a downloadable asset attracts links naturally. tactiq.io's entire traffic base was built this way.

**5. Vertical niches. Fast and lucrative.**
"simple practice ai note taker" SD 15, "hipaa compliant ai note taker" SD 31, "ai note taker for therapists" SD 35 at $50.63 CPC. Almost no competition, real budget behind it.

### 6.3 Keyword types that take a long time (and why)

**1. Category head terms. 18 to 24 months.**
"ai note taker" (SD 48, competition 1.0) has otter.ai, fireflies.ai, notta.ai and a wall of listicles on page 1, backed by 9,500 to 30,762 referring domains. "ai assistant meetings" is SD 84. No amount of content quality substitutes for that link gap.

**2. Single-word and generic terms. Effectively never.**
"meeting transcription" SD 62, "transcribe audio to text" SD 46 with otter.ai entrenched, "summarizer" SD 49 (49,500 volume, notta sits at position 13). These require authority Lynkk will not have for two years, and most are the wrong job-to-be-done anyway.

**3. Competitor brand terms. Never directly.**
"otter ai note taker" SD 57, "fireflies ai note taker" SD 63. The brand owns its own SERP. Reach these only via comparison pages positioned adjacent to the brand result.

**4. "Free" modifiers. 12 to 18 months.**
Counterintuitively hard: "ai note taker free" SD 58, "free ai meeting assistant" SD 65. Every vendor with a free tier competes here, so the SERP is saturated with high-authority product pages.

**5. Conversation intelligence. 12 to 18 months.**
SD 37 to 60, with avoma.com (DA 56) holding positions 1 and 2 on dedicated, well-linked pages. The $126 CPC guarantees continued investment from incumbents.

**6. High-volume meeting-minutes informational terms. 12+ months, low payoff.**
"form for meeting minutes" SD 51, "meeting minutes how to take" SD 51. Microsoft and the template libraries own these with enormous domain authority, and the traffic does not convert.

### 6.4 Category-wide trend: a warning

Every competitor shows a steep decline in Ubersuggest estimated organic traffic over the last 24 months:

| Domain | Nov 2024 | Oct 2026 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| notta.ai | 2,878,031 | 181,816 | **-94%** |
| fireflies.ai | 339,498 | 66,144 | **-81%** |

**Implication:** classic blue-link SEO alone is a declining channel in this category. Lynkk's advantage is that it has no legacy traffic to lose and can build for the new surface from the start. Two consequences:
- Prioritize content formats that win AI Overview citations: direct answers, comparison tables, clearly structured specifications.
- Use the AI Search Visibility tracking already provisioned on this Ubersuggest account (1 brand slot, 10 prompts configured) as a first-class KPI, not an afterthought.

## 7. Final analysis and recommended plan

### 7.1 The strategic read

Lynkk cannot win this category by attacking it head-on. otter.ai has 30,762 referring domains; Lynkk has five. But the data shows three exploitable weaknesses:

1. **Incumbents have not fortified the bottom of the funnel.** The highest-intent keywords in the category sit at SD 11 to 35. read.ai and avoma.com have noticed, and they are the only two doing comparison content seriously. The field is still open.
2. **Lynkk's genuine differentiators map onto uncontested, low-difficulty keyword space.** In-person capture, bot-free recording, data residency and compliance verticals are all SD 11 to 44, all Transactional, and all poorly served.
3. **Authority matters less every quarter.** As AI-mediated search grows, a precise and well-structured answer competes with a well-linked one more effectively than it did two years ago.

### 7.2 The plan

**Phase 1, months 1 to 3. Capture intent, prove the model.**
Target: SD under 25, BOFU. Expected: first page-1 rankings within 6 to 10 weeks.
- Build 8 to 10 comparison and alternative pages: Lynkk vs Fireflies, vs Otter, vs Fathom, vs Read.ai, vs Granola, vs tl;dv, plus "Fireflies alternatives" and "Otter.ai competitors" roundups. Lead with in-person capture, in-meeting voice, knowledge graph and data residency.
- Fix the two existing near-misses: "ai note taker for zoom" (position 39, 480 volume) and "microsoft teams meeting note taker" (position 63). These pages exist and need optimization, not creation.
- Publish the question-format set: "does Teams have an AI note taker", "does Google Meet have an AI note taker".
- Begin digital PR aimed at inclusion in existing third-party listicles. This is higher leverage than publishing your own.

**Phase 2, months 3 to 6. Own the platform and vertical long tail.**
Target: SD 25 to 40, MOFU Transactional.
- A dedicated page per platform: Teams, Zoom, Google Meet, Slack, WhatsApp, in-person.
- Vertical landing pages: therapists and HIPAA, recruiters, financial advisors, consultants. Highest CPC, lowest competition.
- The in-person and bot-free cluster, which nobody owns.
- Action-items template hub with downloadable assets, as a link magnet.
- Target: 25 to 50 referring domains by month 6.

**Phase 3, months 6 to 12. Build category authority.**
Target: SD 40 to 55.
- Free-tool pages following the tactiq.io playbook: transcript formatter, meeting-minutes generator, action-item extractor. This is the most proven link-acquisition mechanism in the category.
- The recording how-to cluster, led by "how to record a meeting on zoom" (9,900 volume at SD 13, the best ratio available).
- Conversation intelligence cluster, to contest avoma.com.
- "Best AI note taker" and the commercial listicle set.

**Phase 4, months 12 to 24. Contest the head.**
Only now: "ai note taker" (27,100), "ai meeting assistant" (4,400), "ai meeting note taker" (3,600). These require the DA 25 to 40 that Phases 1 through 3 produce.

| Domain Authority | 1 | 8 to 12 | 15 to 20 | 25 to 32 |
| Referring domains | 5 | 20 to 30 | 50 to 70 | 120 to 180 |

### 7.4 Three things not to do

1. **Do not target "ai note taker" or "ai meeting assistant" in the first six months.** SD 48 and 60 against DA 1 produces nothing. The content budget is better spent on 20 comparison pages.
2. **Do not build an SEO programme around the knowledge graph, Jira or CRM integrations.** The searches do not exist: "meeting notes to jira" is 0, "crm meeting notes" is 10, "ai crm meeting notes integration" is 0. These are conversion and comparison assets. Feature them on landing pages, not as keyword targets.
3. **Do not copy notta.ai's programmatic model.** 46,137 keywords from templated translation and file-converter pages brought a 94 percent traffic collapse. The pages had no topical relationship to the product, and that is exactly what AI-mediated search punishes hardest.

## 8. Data caveats

- Ubersuggest could not auto-detect competitors for lynkk.ai; the top-10 list was triangulated from three established players' competitor reports and manually filtered. It reflects keyword overlap, not market share.
- fathom.video's organic record (9 keywords, 34 visits) is a tracking artifact. Its DA 47 and 42,584 backlinks are the reliable figures.
- read.ai's 454,139 estimated visits are inflated by the generic term "read" (301,000 volume at position 2). Treat its effective category traffic as roughly 60,000 to 80,000.
- Volumes and difficulty are US (loc 2840). India (loc 2356) runs roughly 7 to 15 percent of US volume on the same terms ("ai note taker" 1,900 vs 27,100) but with notably lower difficulty on several and far lower CPCs ($1.46 vs $13.60). If India is a priority market, the same cluster structure applies at materially lower cost.
- The 24-month traffic declines in section 6.4 mix genuine SERP erosion with Ubersuggest model revisions. Use them as directional evidence, not precise measurement.
- 150 Ubersuggest reports per day are available on this plan; 40 were used before this analysis and roughly 20 during it.
