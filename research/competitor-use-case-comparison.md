## 3. Use case by use case

| Use case | Competitor example | What they lead with | What Lynkk leads with | Where Lynkk is different |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sales calls | Fireflies, Otter | CRM autofill, AI coaching, voice agents | Next steps with owners, a real sample note, ask across calls | No bot needed, in-person and offline calls. Honest: no CRM sync |

## 4. What we changed on every page

1. **New "In simple words" section** after the headline: what the use case is, in 2-3 short sentences, plus a "Who it's for" list.
2. **New "Before and after" table**: three rows of life without Lynkk vs with Lynkk, plus a "Works with" line.
3. **Simpler words**: replaced "cite" and "citations" with "shows where the answer came from", and "note shape" with "note format".

Each page is now about 650 words (up from about 500), and 9 blocks instead of 7.

## 5. What would make the pages even stronger (needs real data)

- **One real customer quote per page**, from someone in that role (competitors all have these).
- **One or two real impact numbers**, such as hours saved per week, once you can measure them.
- **Security certifications** (for example SOC 2), if and when Lynkk gets them. This matters most for lawyers and financial advisors.
