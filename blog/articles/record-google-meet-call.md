# How to Record a Google Meet Call for Free in 2026

*By Samarth Wagh, Co-founder, Lynkk.ai · Last updated 17 October 2026*

> **Quick answer:** Here is how to record a Google Meet call: click **Meeting tools**, then **Recording**, then **Start recording**, and confirm. The recording saves to the organizer's Google Drive. Built-in recording needs an **eligible Google Workspace plan** or **Google One with 2 TB or more**, and only works on a **computer or Android**. On a free Gmail account, use a free screen recorder or an AI meeting assistant.

*Disclosure: Lynkk publishes this guide and makes an AI meeting assistant mentioned below. Google Meet steps come from [Google's help center](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/9308681), checked 17 October 2026.*

Knowing how to record a Google Meet call sounds simple until the record button is missing. Google Meet's built-in recording depends on your plan, your admin's settings and your device. Many people on personal Gmail accounts never see the option at all.

This guide shows the built-in method step by step, explains why recording might not be available, and gives free alternatives for laptops, Android phones and iPhones.

## How do you record a Google Meet call with the built-in recorder?

If your plan allows it, recording a Google Meet call takes 5 clicks on a computer:

1. Start or join the meeting in Google Meet.
2. At the bottom right, click **Meeting tools**, then **Recording**.
3. Optionally, choose a language for captions.
4. Click **Start recording**, then **Start** in the pop-up.
5. To stop, click **Meeting tools**, then **Recording**, then **Stop recording**, and confirm.

Recordings stop automatically after 8 hours or when everyone leaves. Participants see that the meeting is being recorded. If you only need notes rather than video, Google's [Take notes for me](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/14754931) writes them into a Google Doc on eligible plans.

## Where do Google Meet recordings go?

Google Meet recordings save to the meeting organizer's Google Drive, in a "Google Meet" folder with a sub-folder for each meeting. Google emails the link to the organizer and to the person who started the recording.

If you started the recording but are not the organizer, check your email for the link rather than your own Drive.

## Which Google plans can record Google Meet calls?

Built-in Google Meet recording is available on these plans, according to Google's help center:

| Plan type | Can record? |
|---|---|
| Free personal Gmail | No |
| Google One with 2 TB or more storage | Yes |
| Workspace Individual | Yes |
| Business Standard and Business Plus | Yes |
| Enterprise Starter, Standard and Plus | Yes |
| Essentials and Enterprise Essentials | Yes |
| Education Plus (Staff license) and Teaching and Learning Upgrade | Yes |

On Workspace plans, an administrator must turn recording on for your account. Business Starter is not on Google's list.

## How do you record a Google Meet call for free?

If your account cannot record, there are 3 free ways to record a Google Meet call on a laptop:

1. **Windows Game Bar.** Press **Windows + G**, then use the capture widget, or press **Windows + Alt + R** to record the active Chrome window with its audio.
2. **OBS Studio.** A free, open-source recorder for Windows and Mac that captures the screen, system audio and your microphone. It takes 10 minutes to set up.
3. **Mac screen recording.** Press **Command + Shift + 5** to record the screen. macOS does not capture other people's call audio by default, so pair it with a tool that records system audio.

Always tell participants before you record. Google Meet shows a notice when its own recorder runs, but a screen recorder does not, so the announcement is up to you. Several US states require every participant's consent.

## How do you record a Google Meet call on Android and iPhone?

On Android, Google Meet's built-in recording works if your plan allows it, using the same Meeting tools menu. On iPhone, Google's help center says recording is only available on a computer or Android device, so the built-in option is not there.

| Device | Built-in Meet recording | Free workaround |
|---|---|---|
| Laptop (Windows or Mac) | Yes, on eligible plans | Game Bar, OBS Studio |
| Android phone | Yes, on eligible plans | Android screen recorder |
| iPhone or iPad | No | Join from a laptop, or use an AI note taker |

iPhone's screen recorder may not capture call audio from other participants reliably, so test it before an important call.

## How do you record a Google Meet call with audio?

Google Meet's built-in recorder always captures audio from every participant. Screen recorders are where audio goes missing: they often capture your microphone but not the other people on the call. Check these 3 settings before you rely on a screen recording:

1. **Windows Game Bar:** in Game Bar settings, choose to record all audio, not just the game or app, if you also want your microphone.
2. **OBS Studio:** add both a "Desktop Audio" (or application audio) source and a "Mic/Aux" source, then run a 30-second test call.
3. **Mac:** macOS screen recording records your microphone but not system audio by default. Use OBS with application audio capture, or an AI note taker, to capture other speakers.

Always play back the first minute of a test recording before an important call. A silent recording is the most common screen-recorder failure.

## Why can't I record a Google Meet call?

There are 5 common reasons the record option is missing or greyed out:

1. **Your plan does not include recording**, for example a free Gmail account or Business Starter.
2. **Your admin turned recording off** for your organization or group.
3. **You are not the host** and the host has not allowed participants to record.
4. **You are on iPhone or iPad**, where built-in recording is not available.
5. **Education restrictions** apply to some school accounts and roles.

Fix it by switching to a laptop, asking the host for permission, or asking your Workspace admin to enable recording.

## Does Google Meet record calls automatically?

No. Google Meet does not record calls automatically unless someone starts the recording or an admin or organizer has set it up for that meeting. Gemini's "Take notes for me" can also be turned on from the calendar event, but that creates notes, not a recording.

If you want every meeting captured without remembering to press record, an AI meeting assistant that joins from your calendar is the practical option.

## FAQ

### How do I record a Google Meet call for free?

If your plan does not include recording, use a free screen recorder such as Windows Game Bar or OBS Studio, or an AI meeting assistant with a free plan like Lynkk. Always tell participants first.

### Can I record Google Meet on a personal Gmail account?

Not with the built-in recorder, unless you have Google One with 2 TB or more storage. Free Gmail accounts can use screen recorders or AI note takers instead.

### Where are Google Meet recordings saved?

In the meeting organizer's Google Drive, inside a "Google Meet" folder with a sub-folder for each meeting. Google also emails the link to the organizer and the person who started the recording.

### Why is the record button missing in Google Meet?

Your plan may not include recording, your admin may have disabled it, the host may not have given you permission, or you may be on an iPhone, where built-in recording is not available.

On Android, yes, if your plan allows it. On iPhone, Google Meet's built-in recording is not available, so join from a laptop or use an AI note taker.

### Do participants know when a Google Meet call is recorded?

Yes, when you use Google Meet's built-in recorder, participants are notified. Screen recorders do not notify anyone, so announce the recording yourself.
