# How to Get a Google Meet Transcript and What Gemini AI Notes Can Do

*Last updated: September 30, 2026 · Steps checked against Google Meet Help on September 30, 2026*

> **Quick answer:** To get a Google Meet transcript, click Meeting tools > Transcribe > Start transcription during the call, on 1 of 8 supported Workspace editions. The transcript saves as a Google Doc in the organizer's Google Drive and arrives by email within a few hours, up to 24. **For a transcript plus notes and action items after the call, Lynkk is the best AI note taker for Google Meet.**

> **Disclosure:** This guide is published by Lynkk. Google Meet facts come from Google Meet Help, checked on the date above.

## Does Google Meet have transcription?

Yes. Google Meet has a built-in Transcripts feature on 8 Google Workspace editions, and it writes a Google Meet transcript as a Google Doc. It is not included with a free personal Google account.

| Workspace editions with Transcripts |
|---|
| Business Standard and Business Plus |
| Enterprise Starter, Enterprise Standard and Enterprise Plus |
| Teaching and Learning Upgrade and Education Plus |
| Workspace Individual |

Google says Transcripts is on by default for these editions, except Education accounts with a student license, where it starts off. *Source: Google Meet Help, checked September 30, 2026.*

## How do you get a transcript from Google Meet?

You can start a Google Meet transcript in 4 steps on a computer, according to Google Meet Help. Transcripts work on a computer or an Android device, not on iPhone.

1. **Join or start the meeting** in Google Meet.
2. **Click Meeting tools**, then Transcribe.
3. **Click Start transcription**, then Start in the pop-up.
4. **Stop transcription** from the same Transcribe menu when you are done.

If Host management is on, only the host and co-hosts can turn Transcripts on. Everyone else sees the Transcripts icon at the top right once it starts.

## Where are Google Meet transcripts saved?

Every Google Meet transcript saves to 1 place: the meeting organizer's Google Drive, inside the "Google Meet" folder, with a sub-folder for each meeting. Google says older transcripts move to a "Legacy Meet Recordings" folder inside it from July 2026.

The organizer, the hosts and the person who started transcription each get an email with a link to the file. If the meeting had fewer than 200 invitees, all invitees also get edit access. Expect the file within a few hours; Google says it can take up to 24 hours.

## Does Google Meet have an AI note taker?

Yes. Google Meet's AI note taker is Gemini's "Take notes for me", which writes Google Meet AI notes into a Google Doc. It is included in Workspace Business and Enterprise plans since January 15, 2025.

Here is what Google lists for it:

- **Notes location:** a Google Doc in the organizer's Drive, attached to the Calendar event.
- **Outputs:** meeting outcomes with a status, plus action items and assigned tasks.
- **Languages:** 8 spoken languages, 1 at a time; mixed languages in 1 meeting are not supported.
- **In person:** a "Take notes for me" option for in-person meetings on Android.

So the answer to "does Google Meet have an AI note taker" is yes, on the right plan. *Source: Google Meet Help, checked September 30, 2026.*

## Why is Google Meet transcription not working?

Google Meet transcription fails for 1 of 4 reasons in most cases, all tied to rules in Google Meet Help. Check them in this order.

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| No Transcribe option | Use 1 of the 8 supported Workspace editions |
| Option is greyed out | Host management is on; ask the host or a co-host |
| On iPhone | Transcripts work on a computer or Android device only |
| Transcript not arriving | Wait; files can take up to 24 hours |

If you are the admin, Google also lets admins turn meeting transcription on or off for the organization. Check that setting if nobody in your domain can start a transcript.

## FAQ

### Can Google Meet transcripts be edited?
Yes. A Google Meet transcript is saved as a Google Doc, so anyone with edit access can change it. Google says that when a meeting has fewer than 200 invitees, all invitees get edit access to the transcript. The organizer can also share it with others from Google Drive, like any other document.

### How long does it take to get a Google Meet transcript?
Google says transcripts are usually ready within a few hours of the meeting, but it can take up to 24 hours. The organizer, the hosts and the person who started transcription get an email with a link. Lynkk delivers its transcript, notes and action items after the call.

### Can I get a Google Meet transcript on my phone?
Only on 1 phone platform, Android. Google Meet Help says the Transcripts feature works on a computer, laptop or Android device, so you cannot start one from an iPhone. If you join from iPhone, ask the host to start it from a computer, or use Lynkk, which records the whole meeting for 1 set of notes.

### Which Google Workspace plans include Gemini notes?
Gemini's "Take notes for me" in Google Meet is included in Workspace Business and Enterprise plans since January 15, 2025. Free personal Google accounts do not include it. The separate Transcripts feature is on 8 editions, from Business Standard to Workspace Individual, as Google Meet Help lists.

### What languages do Google Meet AI notes support?
Gemini's "Take notes for me" supports 8 spoken languages: English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish. Google says it handles 1 language at a time, so a meeting that mixes languages is not supported. Lynkk transcribes 17 languages and handles people who switch mid-sentence.

### Can Google Meet transcribe a meeting if nobody starts it?
Not with the Transcripts feature, which needs 1 person in the meeting to click Start transcription. To make sure every meeting is transcribed without anyone remembering, connect Lynkk to your calendar so its meeting bot joins each call on its own and sends notes afterward.
