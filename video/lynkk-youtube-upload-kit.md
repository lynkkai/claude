## Channel-wide standards (from the skill)

- **Titles:** under 60 characters, primary keyword in the first five words. Use YouTube's A/B title test on the recommended title plus one alternative for at least two weeks.
- **Thumbnails:** 3-5 words, host's face on the left third, text on the right, high contrast, readable at 120px wide. Use Lynkk brand colors consistently [CONFIRM BRAND PALETTE]. Show tool names as text, not other companies' logos.
- **Descriptions:** keyword in the first 200 characters, chapters from 0:00, 200-500 words, max 3 hashtags.
- **Tags:** 15-25, primary keyword first, under 500 characters total, include "lynkk".
- **Retention:** each script now previews what's coming and saves one payoff for the last chapter (an open loop). Change the visual every 5-8 seconds with B-roll, screen recordings or on-screen text. Add a pattern interrupt (zoom, graphic, sound) every 30-60 seconds.
- **End screen:** last 20 seconds: subscribe button plus the next video named in the script. Until that video is live, point to the playlist instead.
- **Upload:** keyword-named video file, corrected captions (spell "Lynkk" right), add to the playlist, reply to every comment in the first 24 hours.
