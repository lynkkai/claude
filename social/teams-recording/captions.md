Captions for the Teams recording videos, one section per channel. Each has a hook that fits before the platform cuts the caption off with "more", three hook options to A/B test, a caption ready to paste, and the keywords people search on that channel.

| Channel | Video | File |

**Rules these follow** (from `lynkk-post-kit/docs/COPY.md` and `TRUTHS.md`): every claim is in TRUTHS.md, no prices, no competitor names, no speed or accuracy claims, no exclamation marks, no emoji, no dashes. **Core keywords** (use the exact phrase in the first lines where it reads naturally): how to record a Teams meeting, record Microsoft Teams meeting, Teams meeting recording, record Teams without a bot, Teams meeting notes, Teams transcript, meeting notes, AI note taker.
