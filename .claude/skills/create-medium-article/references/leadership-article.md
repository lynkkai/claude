# Leadership Article

Use this reference when the article centers on leadership, communication,
strategy, organizational design, or decision-making.

- Put a consequential decision, tension, or observed failure at the center.
- Separate evidence, inference, experience-based advice, and opinion.
- Do not fabricate personal anecdotes or imply experience not present in the
  authorized sources.
- Use examples to expose a trade-off, constraint, or behavior, not merely to
  decorate the argument.
- Offer concrete questions, decision rules, or actions readers can apply.
- Avoid presenting a situational practice as a universal law.
- End with what should change in the reader's decision or behavior rather than
  summarizing every section.
