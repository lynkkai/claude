# Tactiq Alternatives for US Teams in 2026

*By Samarth Wagh, Co-founder, Lynkk.ai · Last updated 17 October 2026 · Pricing checked 17 October 2026*

> **Quick answer:** The best Tactiq alternative depends on what you outgrew. **Lynkk** adds prep, live answers and Jira tickets (free for 5 notes, $15 Pro). **Fathom** records unlimited calls free. **Notta** is cheapest for minutes ($8.17 billed yearly). **Fireflies** syncs to CRMs ($10 billed yearly). **Google Meet's Take notes for me** suits Workspace users who stay in English.

*Disclosure: Lynkk publishes this guide and is one of the tools reviewed. Prices come from vendor pricing pages, checked 17 October 2026.*

Tactiq became popular as a simple Chrome extension that captures live transcripts in Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams without a bot. It is still a good tool. People look for a Tactiq alternative when they hit the free plan's 10 transcripts a month, need meetings outside the browser, or want more done with the notes.

This guide compares 7 options and explains which one fits which reason for switching.

## Why do people look for a Tactiq alternative?

The most common reason is the free plan: 10 transcripts and 5 AI credits a month. Pro removes the transcript cap for $8 per user per month billed yearly, but still includes only 10 AI credits a month.

Other reasons we hear:

- **Browser-only capture.** Tactiq works through Chrome, so desktop meeting apps and in-person conversations need another tool.
- **Limited AI on lower plans.** Unlimited AI credits start on Team at $16.67 per user billed yearly.
- **No follow-up automation.** Teams want tasks with owners, tickets and prep for the next call.
- **No recording.** Some teams want audio or video to go back to, not just a transcript.

### 2. Fathom: best free Tactiq alternative

Fathom records unlimited calls with video and transcripts for free on Zoom, Google Meet and Teams. It suits anyone whose main complaint is Tactiq's 10-transcript cap. Premium costs $16 a month billed yearly.

### 3. Notta: best for cheap transcription minutes

Notta gives 1,800 minutes a month on Pro for $8.17 billed yearly, and supports file uploads as well as live meetings. Its free plan caps each conversation at 3 minutes, so treat it as a trial. See [Notta pricing](https://lynkk.ai/blog/notta-pricing).

Fireflies joins calls as a bot, transcribes them and pushes notes into CRMs. Pro costs $10 per seat billed yearly. It also has a desktop app that records system audio without a bot.

Otter shows a live transcript during calls, like Tactiq, but through its own agent and apps. Free gives 300 minutes a month with a 30-minute cap per conversation. See [Otter.ai alternatives](https://lynkk.ai/blog/otter-ai-alternatives) for its limits.

Read AI adds engagement and talk-time analytics to summaries. Free covers 5 meetings a month; Pro costs $15 billed yearly. Compare it in [Read AI alternatives](https://lynkk.ai/blog/read-ai-alternative).

## How does Tactiq compare with Notta?

Tactiq vs Notta comes down to capture and limits. Tactiq captures live captions from your browser with no bot, with unlimited transcripts on Pro ($8 billed yearly). Notta joins meetings and transcribes uploads, with 1,800 minutes a month on Pro ($8.17 billed yearly).

| | Tactiq | Notta |
|---|---|---|
| Free plan | 10 transcripts, 5 AI credits | 120 min, 3 min per conversation |
| Pro price (yearly) | $8 | $8.17 |
| Pro limit | Unlimited transcripts, 10 AI credits | 1,800 min, 100 AI summaries |
| File uploads | No | Yes, 100 a month on Pro |

Choose Tactiq for lightweight browser transcripts. Choose Notta if you also transcribe recorded files.

## Is Tactiq free, and is it safe to use?

Yes, Tactiq has a free plan with 10 transcripts and 5 AI credits a month, with no credit card. On safety, Tactiq's Business plan ($29.17 billed yearly) adds advanced data retention and SAML SSO, and Enterprise adds a curated data processing agreement.

Two practical points:

1. **Browser extensions read the page.** Review the permissions Tactiq requests, and check whether your IT team allows meeting extensions.
2. **Is Tactiq legal?** Transcribing a meeting is legal in the US when you follow consent rules. Several states require every participant to agree, so announce the transcript at the start.

## Which Tactiq alternative works without a bot?

Lynkk (Chrome extension and Mac app), Fireflies (desktop app), Fathom (beta) and Google Meet's built-in notes all work without a third-party bot. Granola and Jamie are also bot-free note takers that run on your device.

Bot-free capture matters most for client calls, recruiting and healthcare, where a visible bot can change how people speak.

All 3 save you from Tactiq's 10-transcript free limit in different ways.

## How do you switch from Tactiq without losing transcripts?

Switching takes about 10 minutes. Export first, then remove the extension so it stops capturing.

1. **Export** the transcripts you want to keep from Tactiq (sharing and export are on every plan, including Free).
2. **Install or connect** the new tool and link your calendar if it uses a bot.
3. **Run both for 1 week** on a few meetings and compare the notes.
4. **Remove the Tactiq extension** from Chrome, or pause automatic transcription in its settings (a Pro feature).
5. **Tell your team** which tool now captures meetings, so nobody is surprised.

## FAQ

### What is the best alternative to Tactiq?

Lynkk is the best alternative if you want follow-up work done, Fathom if you want free unlimited recording, and Notta if you want cheap transcription minutes and file uploads.

Yes. Tactiq's free plan includes 10 transcripts and 5 AI credits a month. Pro costs $8 per user per month billed yearly and removes the transcript limit.

Tactiq is a widely used extension, and its Business plan adds SAML SSO and advanced data retention. Review the extension's permissions and your company's policy on browser extensions before installing.

### Who are Tactiq's main competitors?

Tactiq's main competitors are Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, Fathom, Notta, Read AI, Google Meet's built-in notes and Lynkk. Each takes a different approach to capture and follow-up.

Yes. Lynkk, Fathom, Fireflies, Otter and Read AI all work with Zoom and Microsoft Teams as well as Google Meet.

Lynkk supports Hindi and a Hinglish setting for mixed Hindi and English meetings. Google Meet's Take notes for me does not list Hindi among its 8 supported languages.
