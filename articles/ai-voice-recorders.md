## What is an AI voice recorder?

An AI voice recorder is a device or app that records speech and uses AI to transcribe it, summarize it and pull out action items. Unlike a classic recorder that gives you a 60-minute audio file, it gives you text you can search and a summary you can read in 2 minutes.

There are 2 kinds. A device is a small piece of hardware with its own microphones, while an app runs on the phone or laptop you already own.

## How does an AI voice recorder work?

If you are asking how does AI voice recorder work, the answer is 3 stages: it captures audio, sends it to speech recognition, then asks an AI model to write a summary and tasks. Most devices record offline and sync to a phone app later, while apps like Lynkk can process the conversation about 30 seconds after it ends.

1. **Capture:** microphones record the conversation. Devices use built-in mics; apps use your laptop or phone.
2. **Transcribe:** speech recognition turns audio into text, with speaker labels on better tools.
3. **Summarize:** AI writes a summary, decisions and action items, and some tools create tickets or CRM updates.

## How do you get clear recordings in a meeting room?

You get clear recordings in a meeting room by putting the microphone within a few feet of the speakers and cutting background noise. These 5 habits work with any device or app.

1. **Place the device in the middle** of the table, not at 1 end.
2. **Close doors and windows** and turn off fans near the table.
3. **Ask people not to talk over each other,** especially when decisions are made.
4. **Say names at the start** so speaker labels are easy to check.
5. **Check the first minute** of the recording before a long session continues.

Lynkk captures both the room and people joining remotely, so hybrid meetings stay in 1 transcript.

## Is it legal to use an AI voice recorder in person?

Using an AI voice recorder is legal in the US when you follow consent rules, and in-person recording is where people most often forget them. Federal law ([18 U.S.C. § 2511](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title18-section2511)) allows recording when 1 party consents. California requires every party's consent to record a confidential conversation under [California Penal Code § 632](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=632).

A small wearable can be easy to miss, so say out loud that you are recording at the start of every conversation.
