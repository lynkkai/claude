## Features by meeting stage

### Before the meeting: context
- Ask Lynkk across past meetings: what was discussed, what's still open, who promised what
  (Ask Lynkk is a **Pro** feature).
- Account briefs before a call (homepage).

### During the meeting
- **Cue:** sits at the top of the screen during a call; ask it anything, or press ⌘↩ and it reads
  your screen and the conversation. **In beta in the Mac app, by invite.**
- Live transcript in the web app while the meeting bot is in the call.
- Automatic call detection in the Mac app (asks "Record or Not now", or records automatically).

### After the meeting
- A note with: the recording, a transcript with speaker labels, and an AI summary (title, short
  abstract, up to eight key takeaways, topics, action items with owner, due date and priority,
  and decisions linked to the moment they were said).
- Tasks board; action items to **Jira** (one issue per action item).
- Follow-up email drafts (homepage "Draft a follow-up").
- Answers in **Slack** (Pro).
- Recap email; weekly digest every Monday (Pro).
- Share a note with a link: summary, tasks and decisions; recording only if included; link
  expires after 30 days and can be turned off.
- Export any note as Markdown; export all data as one zip.

## Platforms and capture

Four ways to record, all landing in one account:

| Method | What it does | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Meeting bot** | Joins Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams from a pasted link or from Google Calendar (auto-join switch per meeting) | Visible participant named after you; does not speak or post a consent message. Audio on every plan, video on Pro |
| **Mac app** (Beta, v2.4.1) | Detects calls in Zoom, Zoom Phone, Teams, Webex, FaceTime, Slack huddles, WhatsApp, and Meet/Zoom/Teams/FaceTime/Webex in 9 browsers. "Record now" for in-person. Also dictation | Apple silicon, macOS 14.2+. Records mic and call audio on two channels; your side labelled "You". Records offline and uploads later |
| **Chrome extension** | Records Google Meet, Zoom web client and Teams from the tab, no bot | Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc. Tab and mic on two channels, audio only |
| **Browser recorder** (web app) | Records a room conversation from the laptop mic ("Offline: capture a room conversation on this device") | Any computer, including Windows and Linux. Needs internet. Pause, countdown, survives reload |

- **Not available:** no iPhone, Android or Windows app; no upload or import of audio/video files.
- **Recording limit:** up to 2 hours per note on every plan; unlimited notes.
- **Dictation (Mac app):** text appears at your cursor in any app; cloud dictation hears
  18 languages (Pro for the accurate mode).

## Privacy and security (from /security)

- Never trains AI on recordings, transcripts or notes.
- HTTPS everywhere; signed upload links (10 min) and playback links (1 hour).
- Connected tools' tokens encrypted with AES-256-GCM, keys kept outside the database.
- Data region: a workspace can pin processing to the **US, EU, UK, India, Singapore or
  Australia** (source: blog; /security says "the region you choose").
- Mac app keeps its local copy 7 days after upload; dictation audio is not stored, and dictated
  words are erased after 7 days.
- Admin reads of a member's note are logged.
- Rights under GDPR and India's DPDP Act (see, correct, export, delete).
- No SOC 2, ISO 27001 or HIPAA claim anywhere on the site.
