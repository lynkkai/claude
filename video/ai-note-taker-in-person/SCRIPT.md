**The idea:** "Your best meetings don't have a link." A presenter runs a real conference-room test on camera, and
Remi (the Lynkk robot) does the graphics: chapter cards, the comparison boards, the mic placement map, and close-ups
of the real Mac UI that are too small to read in a room shot.

**Edit style:** this is a 4 minute video, not a 32 s teaser, so the presenter's A-roll carries the pace. The Remi
inserts still sit on the kit's grid (120 BPM at 30 fps: beat 15 frames, bar 2 s) so every graphic, chapter card and
cut lands on a bar line, and the bed music runs under the whole video without a break. No inserts longer than 4 bars;
the camera is never still on a graphic for more than a bar.

**Music:** one bed track, 120 BPM, low under speech (ducked about 12 dB under the voice), up to full on the cold open,
the six chapter cards and the end card. Final chord on 4:06.0, the last picture beat (Remi lands on Download for Mac).

## Shoot list (for the team)

- **Room:** a real conference room with a glass wall or hard surfaces (so the echo is honest), seats for 4 to 6.
- **People:** 4 participants. **Written consent from everyone before recording**, and a line on camera (1:50).
- **Devices on camera:** a MacBook (Apple silicon), a phone, a small recorder. No competitor branding or app UI
  visible on the phone or recorder.
- **Screen recordings** on the recording Mac (or a second Mac mirroring the account): menu bar Record now, the
  recording column, Stop, the finished note, the transcript, Wi-Fi off and on mid-recording, the upload resuming.
- **Test protocol:** a 6 to 8 minute real meeting with an agenda, two decisions and three action items with owners
  and dates spoken out loud, so the note has something to show. Run it twice: laptop in the middle, then laptop at the
  far end by the projector, and keep both transcripts for chapter 4 and the mic tips.
- **B-roll:** room wide, hands on the table, the laptop in the middle (top-down if possible, it doubles as the plate
  for G4), a client office, a site visit, Wi-Fi being turned off.
- **Audio for the video itself:** a lav on the presenter. The Mac's recording is for the test, not the soundtrack.

## Truth checks

Every claim on screen or in VO, and where it comes from (`docs/TRUTHS.md` / `docs/TRUTHS-web.md` in the video kit):

- "Record now in the Mac app's menu bar", "same note as a Google Meet or Zoom call" → TRUTHS-web, Meeting notes FAQ:
  "Press Record now in the Mac app's menu bar, or record your mic in the browser. You get the same note as for a
  Google Meet, Zoom or Teams call."
- "records your mic in the browser" → same line.
- "nothing joins anything", "no calendar" → TRUTHS, Calls: "Nothing joins the call. No bot in the participant list.
  No calendar."
- Recording column: clock, Pause, Stop, live words → TRUTHS, Calls: "a slim column on the right edge: clock, source
  app, Pause, Stop, two levels (You, Others), live words, a note field."
- Note contents → TRUTHS, The note: "Title, abstract, key takeaways with timestamps, topics, action items (owner, due,
  priority), decisions, open questions, transcript with speaker labels."
- "split by speaker" → TRUTHS-web, Hinglish: "Every transcript is split by speaker."
- "written to the Mac's disk from the first second", "upload picks up where it stopped" → TRUTHS, Calls: "Audio is
  written to disk from the first second; upload waits for a connection and resumes."
- "up to two hours", "stops after five minutes of silence" → TRUTHS, Calls: "Also stops after 5 minutes of silence,
  and at the 2 hour cap per recording."
- "free to download", Apple silicon, macOS 14.2 or later → TRUTHS, Requirements.
- "no phone app", "Mac and browser" → the plan's production notes; TRUTHS lists only the Mac app, the browser, the
  Chrome extension and the meeting bot.
- Ask permission before recording → TRUTHS-web, Chrome extension FAQ: "only if you tell them. Please do."

**Changed from the plan, because TRUTHS does not back it:**
- "labels each speaker in **17 languages**": no count of 17 anywhere. Transcription languages are listed without a
  count (English, Auto-detect, Hinglish, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Japanese and more, four in beta); the only
  number is 18 languages for cloud *dictation*, which is a different feature. The script says "split by speaker" and
  makes no language-count claim.
- "keeps recording offline, then uploads later": kept, worded as TRUTHS words it.
- No "within 30 seconds" timing for the note (in the brand memory, not in TRUTHS).

## Open questions (answers needed before build)

1. **Competitor names.** The plan's chapter 6 names Otter, Granola and Plaud. The video kit's hard rule is "no
   competitor names" (it was written for launch teasers). This draft says "a note taker with a phone app" and "a
   separate recorder". Name them, as the plan says, or keep them generic?
2. **Speaker labels with one mic.** The Mac app labels your mic as "You" and the call audio as "Speaker A" and so on.
   In a room there is no call audio, so all four people come in on the mic. Does the transcript split them into
   separate speakers, or label everyone "You"? Chapter 4 depends on it; the test footage will answer it.
3. **External mic.** If a USB conference mic is picked as the Mac's input, does Lynkk record from it? If yes, it's a
   good tip for big rooms; it's not in TRUTHS yet, so it's left out.
4. **Who presents** on camera, and the room and date for the shoot.
5. **Music for YouTube.** The kit's soundtrack process uses CC0, CC BY and Mixkit recordings. Mixkit music may not be
   registered with Content ID, and CC BY credits must go in the YouTube description. OK?
