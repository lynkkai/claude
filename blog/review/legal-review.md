# Legal review sheet: posts #24, #34 and #35

For: the company secretary (India) or attorney (US) who signs off these posts before publishing.
Prepared 18 October 2026. Every legal statement in the 3 posts is listed with its source and how it was
verified, so the reviewer only needs to confirm or correct each row. Nothing here is legal advice.

Status key: **Statute** = checked against the text of the law. **Standard** = checked against the issuing
body's text as quoted in search results (the PDF itself could not be downloaded). **Secondary** = commentary only.
**General** = deliberately general wording, no specific rule claimed.
