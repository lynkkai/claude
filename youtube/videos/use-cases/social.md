# Use-case videos: titles and captions for every channel

Copy for posting the three use-case videos (sales, HR, consulting) on YouTube, LinkedIn,
Instagram, Facebook, X, Threads and TikTok. Held to `lynkk-post-kit/docs/TRUTHS.md` and
`COPY.md`: every claim is in TRUTHS.md, Pro features are marked, no prices, no competitor names.

## What to upload where

| Channel | File | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube | `<video>/out/lynkk-for-<video>-teams.mp4`, thumbnail from `out/`, captions from `<video>/out/captions.srt` | Full description, chapters and tags are in `README.md` |
| LinkedIn | the MP4, with `captions.srt` as the caption file | Native video. Add the title in the video title field |
| Facebook | the MP4, with `captions.srt` | Title goes in the video title field |
| Instagram | the MP4 as a Reel or feed video | The videos are 16:9; Reels show them with bars top and bottom. Pick the cover from the CTA frame or upload the thumbnail |
| X | the MP4 (under 2:20, fits) | Each post is under 280 characters with the link |
| Threads | the MP4 | Under 500 characters |
| TikTok | the MP4 | 16:9 plays letterboxed. Links in captions are not clickable, so the caption says the site in words |

Always tell everyone in a meeting when you are recording it. The HR posts say so.
