## What is the Plaud Note AI voice recorder, and why look for an alternative?

The Plaud Note AI voice recorder is a card-sized device that records conversations and sends the audio to Plaud's app for transcripts and AI summaries. The current Note Pro has 4 MEMS microphones, 16.4 feet of pickup range, up to 50 hours of recording and 60 days of standby.

People look for Plaud alternatives for 3 main reasons, based on what they search most:

1. **The subscription.** Many Plaud AI review threads focus on the yearly plan more than the $189 device.
2. **Another thing to carry.** A device must be charged, synced and remembered every day.
3. **Most meetings are online.** If most conversations happen on Zoom or Teams, a laptop app does the same job.

Lynkk is the best Plaud alternative if most of your important conversations happen on calls or around a laptop, because it records them with 0 hardware and turns each one into owned tasks. It starts free.

The [Lynkk Mac app](https://lynkk.ai/download) notices calls in 7 apps, Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Slack huddles, WhatsApp, FaceTime and Webex, and records both sides on 2 channels with no bot. It writes audio to disk from the first second, so bad Wi-Fi or sleep does not lose the file.

For in-person meetings, you record your microphone in the browser at lynkk.ai, with a countdown, pause and a silence warning. Every recording becomes a Meeting Brief with 7 parts: title, abstract, key takeaways with timestamps, topics, action items, decisions with context and open questions. ## Is it legal to record in-person conversations with an AI voice recorder?

It depends on the state: 1 party consent is enough under federal law, but some states require everyone in the conversation to agree. Federal law ([18 U.S.C. § 2511](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title18-section2511)) allows 1 party consent, while California ([Penal Code § 632](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=632)) requires all parties to consent to recording a confidential conversation.

A small device on a table is easy to miss, so say out loud that you are recording. The [NIST Privacy Framework](https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework/privacy-framework) is a useful checklist for reviewing any vendor. Lynkk keeps recordings private to your account and never trains on your conversations.
