# Multilingual meeting transcription in 17 languages

A lot of teams work in more than one language: a sales call in Spanish, a standup in English,
a customer interview in Japanese. Lynkk transcribes 17 languages plus auto-detect, labels who
said what in every one of them, and turns each meeting into a note you can search.

## Cards

### 17 languages, one setting

English, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Japanese, Chinese, Korean,
Arabic and Russian, plus Tamil, Telugu, Marathi and Bengali in beta. Pick one, or let
auto-detect work out the language of each recording.

### Two engines, one transcript

Lynkk runs two transcription providers. If one has an outage, the recording goes to the other
on its own, so a bad moment on one side does not cost you the meeting.

### Mixed Hindi and English calls

Hindi and English in the same sentence? Lynkk has a dedicated Hinglish mode for that.

## How it works

You choose a language once and every recording uses it. Here is what happens to a meeting from
there.

1. Open Settings, then Transcription, and pick one of the 17 languages or Auto-detect. New
   accounts start on Hinglish mode, so if your calls are in another language, change it once
   here.
2. Record the way you like: the meeting bot, the Mac app, the Chrome extension or the browser.
3. When the recording is uploaded, Lynkk sends it for transcription with your language setting
   and speaker labels turned on.
4. The transcript comes back split by speaker, and Lynkk writes the summary, action items and
   decisions from it.
5. The finished note is searchable by meaning or exact words, in the language it was spoken.

---

## Pick a language or let it detect

Each setting suits a different kind of call. Picking a single language tells the engine
exactly what to expect, which is the best choice when every call is in that language.
Auto-detect works out the main language of a recording, which suits a team whose calls are
each in one language but not always the same one. If people mix Hindi and English inside
sentences, use [Hinglish mode](/features/hinglish-transcription) instead.

---

## Speaker-aware across languages

Speaker labels work in every language, so action items and decisions are attributed to the
right person even when the room is multilingual. When the meeting bot records a call, Lynkk
matches the labels to the names the meeting itself shows. On the Mac app your own voice is
recorded on its own channel and labelled You.

---

## What to expect, and the limits

Clear audio gives the best transcript in any language. A few things are worth knowing before
you rely on it.

- Tamil, Telugu, Marathi and Bengali are in beta, so expect more mistakes than in the other
  languages.
- Auto-detect picks one main language per recording. It suits calls that stay in one language,
  not calls that switch mid-sentence.
- Mid-sentence switching is handled for Hindi and English, through Hinglish mode. For any other
  mix, pick the main language of the call.
- Rare names and product terms can come out spelled the way they sound. The dictionary in
  Settings helps dictation on the Mac, not meeting transcripts.
- Each recording can run up to 2 hours.
- Transcription runs in the cloud, so the Mac app keeps recording through a dropped connection
  and uploads when you are back online.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which languages can it transcribe?

17, plus auto-detect: English, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Arabic and Russian, with Tamil, Telugu, Marathi and Bengali in beta. There is also a Hinglish mode for Hindi and English mixed in one sentence. Speaker labels come with every transcript.

### What is the default language?

New accounts start on Hinglish mode, which suits teams that mix Hindi and English. If your calls are in another language, change it once in Settings, under Transcription, and every new recording uses it.

### Can it handle people switching languages mid-sentence?

For Hindi and English, yes: that is what Hinglish mode is for. For other pairs, pick the main language of the call or use auto-detect, which works out the main language of the recording.

### Can I change the language later?

Yes, at any time, in Settings under Transcription. New recordings use the new setting, and recordings you already made keep the language they were transcribed in.

### Does my language setting apply to bot recordings too?

Yes. One setting covers every recording on your account: the meeting bot, the Mac app, the Chrome extension and the browser.

### Which setting should I pick for a mixed team?

If every call is in one language, pick that language for the most accurate transcript. If each call is in one language but it changes from call to call, use auto-detect. If people mix Hindi and English, use Hinglish mode.

### Are speakers labelled in every language?

Yes. Every transcript is split by speaker. Bot recordings use the names shown in the meeting, and the Mac app labels your own voice as You.

### Does it need internet to transcribe?

Transcription happens in the cloud. The Mac app keeps recording if your connection drops and uploads the recording when you are back online; the transcript follows.
