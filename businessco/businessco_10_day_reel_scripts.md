# 10-Day Launch Reel Scripts: Businessco

As of 20 Nov 2026. Plan runs Mon 23 Nov to Wed 2 Dec 2026.

## Research summary

Businessco should launch with face-led, question-answering reels. In UAE business setup, the content that gets saved and shared answers one specific question (mainland or free zone? what are the steps? what documents?). Generic stock-footage promos do not.

**Brand (from the website draft):** Businessco is a UAE corporate service provider. Its five service areas are Formation/Registration, Corporate Support, Administrative Services, Document Verification and Commercial Information. Its line is "We lead you to success". The draft has no pricing, client numbers or founding year, so no reel claims any of these.

**Competitors checked**

| Competitor | What they publish | Format takeaway for Businessco |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Virtuzone](https://virtuzone.com/mainland-company-formation-dubai) (about 74K Instagram followers, per a [third-party mirror](https://imginn.com/virtuzone/)) | Mainland vs free zone explainers, "which of the 49 free zones" guides, e-commerce setup packages | Comparison and "which one is right for you" topics are their core content |
| [Shuraa](https://www.shuraa.com/how-to-start-social-media-business-in-dubai) | Activity-specific setup guides (e.g. a social media business), multilingual blog content | Niche "how to set up X business" angles |
| [Creative Zone](https://www.creativezone.ae/?p=62674) | Mainland vs free zone, licence for selling on Instagram | Practical rules a founder must know before starting |
| [DMCC](https://dmcc.ae/blog/difference-between-dubai-mainland-freezone-companies) and [SPC Free Zone](https://spcfz.ae/differences-between-free-zone-company-and-mainland-company-spc-free-zone) (authorities) | Mainland vs free zone vs offshore comparisons | Even the authorities lead with the same comparison question |

**Formats that earn engagement in this niche**

1. Talking-head explainer with bold on-screen text, 20-40 seconds, one question per reel.
2. Numbered steps ("6 steps in the right order") that people save to use later.
3. Myth vs fact, which drives comments and shares.
4. Behind the scenes and meet-the-team reels, which build trust for a new brand.
5. Reply-to-a-question reels, which turn DMs and comments into the next post.

Reels of 15-30 seconds get the most likes and replays, according to [OneStream Live data cited by SocialBu](https://socialbu.com/blog/instagram-reels-views). Instagram is also favouring reels over still posts ([Meydan Free Zone](https://www.meydanfz.ae/?p=14674)).

**Information gain:** competitors all say "mainland vs free zone". Businessco's edge is order and specifics: the exact sequence of steps, the attestation stamp order, the 25% UBO threshold. Each reel gives one fact a viewer did not have before.

**Limit:** per-reel view counts are not available through search. Before filming, open each competitor's Instagram profile and sort reels by views to confirm the top 3 formats.

## Reel scripts: Day 1-5

All reels are 9:16, 20-40 seconds, with captions burned in. VO = voiceover or spoken to camera.

### Day 1 (Mon 23 Nov): We are Businessco (Launch)

**Hook (0-3s):** Office door opens, person walks to camera. Text: "Starting a business in the UAE? Start here."

| Time | Shot | VO / on-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-3s | Door opens, presenter walks in | "Starting a business in the UAE? Start here." |
| 3-10s | Presenter at desk, mid shot | "We're Businessco, a corporate service provider based in the UAE." |
| 10-22s | Quick cuts: forms, stamp, meeting, laptop. One icon per service | "We set up companies, support them as they grow, handle admin and secretarial work, verify documents and provide market information." Text lists the 5 services |
| 22-28s | Presenter close-up | "Entrepreneur, startup, franchise or a foreign company entering the UAE: we guide you through every step." |
| 28-32s | Logo end card | "We lead you to success. Follow for practical UAE business tips." |

### Day 2 (Tue 24 Nov): Mainland or free zone? 3 questions decide it (Information gain)

**Hook:** "Mainland or free zone? Don't choose until you've answered these 3 questions."

1. "Who are your customers? If you plan to sell directly to the UAE market, a free zone licence alone may not let you do that. Check before you sign." Text: Q1 Where are your customers?
2. "Do you want government contracts? These generally need a mainland company." Text: Q2 Government work?
3. "Do you need a physical office, or will a flexi desk do? This changes your cost a lot." Text: Q3 Office needs?

**Close:** "Answer these three, and the right jurisdiction becomes much clearer. Comment your business activity and we'll tell you what to check first."

**Shots:** presenter to camera, number graphic per question, end card. Avoid quoting prices: costs vary by activity, jurisdiction and visas.

### Day 3 (Wed 25 Nov): Meet the people behind your paperwork (Launch, behind the scenes)

**Hook:** "You'll never meet a robot here. Meet the team."

| Time | Shot | VO / on-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-3s | Quick pan across team at work | "Meet the team behind your company setup." |
| 3-20s | 3-4 team members, 4 seconds each, saying name + one line | e.g. "I handle registrations." "I look after your documents." "I prepare your board minutes." Text: name + role |
| 20-27s | Team together | "When you call Businessco, you talk to one of us." |
| 27-30s | End card | "Say hello in the comments." |

Use real names and roles only, with each person's consent.

### Day 4 (Thu 26 Nov): Setting up a UAE company, in the right order (Formation)

**Hook:** "Most people do company setup in the wrong order. Here's the right one."

1. Choose your business activity. It decides everything else.
2. Choose the jurisdiction: mainland or a free zone that allows that activity.
3. Reserve your trade name.
4. Get initial approval.
5. Sign the documents and secure your office or desk.
6. Get your licence, then start visas and bank account.

**VO close:** "Skip step one and you may end up paying for the wrong licence. We map all six steps for you." Text per step appears as a numbered checklist. Shot: presenter with a whiteboard or on-screen list. Note: exact steps vary by authority, so say "typical order" in the caption.

### Day 5 (Fri 27 Nov): 4 UAE setup myths (Education, myth vs fact)

**Hook:** "Myth or fact? Most new founders get number 2 wrong."

Format: presenter holds up a red "MYTH" card, then a green "FACT" card.

1. Myth: "Foreigners can't own 100% of a mainland company." Fact: "Many activities now allow 100% foreign ownership on the mainland. Check your activity."
2. Myth: "A free zone licence lets you trade anywhere in the UAE." Fact: "Free zone companies have limits on trading directly in the mainland."
3. Myth: "Once I have my licence, I'm done." Fact: "Renewals, registers and records continue every year."
4. Myth: "All free zones are the same." Fact: "Each has its own permitted activities, office options and costs."

**Close:** "Which one surprised you? Tell us below."

## Reel scripts: Day 6-10

### Day 6 (Sat 28 Nov): Getting your degree attested for the UAE (Document Verification)

**Hook:** "Your degree needs 4 stamps before the UAE accepts it. Here's the order."

1. "Verification in your home country, from the university or education ministry."
2. "Your home country's foreign ministry."
3. "The UAE embassy or consulate in that country."
4. "Final attestation by the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA)."

**VO close:** "Miss one and you start again. Some countries have their own rules, so check before you send anything. We liaise with the departments for you."

**Shots:** hands placing a certificate on a desk; 4 stamp graphics appear one at a time; presenter close. Facts from [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/ask-us/how-to-attest-foreign-certificates-in-the-uae-through-mofa-1.500413612) and [MOFA](https://www.mofa.gov.ae/en/missions/london/services/attestation-service). Do not quote fees on screen: check them on the MOFA site first.

### Day 7 (Sun 29 Nov): Your company changed. Did your paperwork? (Corporate Support)

**Hook:** "Sold shares? Renamed your company? Your registers need to change too."

| Time | Shot | VO / on-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-4s | Presenter, text pops in | Hook line |
| 4-14s | Icons: handshake, name tag, door | "Share transfers, name changes, new partners or closing a company all need formal steps with your licensing authority." |
| 14-24s | Presenter, text "25%" large | "UAE companies must keep a register of beneficial owners: anyone who owns or controls 25% or more." |
| 24-30s | Presenter close | "If your ownership changed, update it. We handle the paperwork end to end." |

Source: [CMS, Cabinet Decision No. 109 of 2023](https://cms.law/en/are/legal-updates/shareholder-and-ultimate-beneficial-owner-registers-for-uae-businesses-new-obligations-in-the-uae). Do not mention fine amounts: sources disagree.

### Day 8 (Mon 30 Nov): A day with your company secretary (Administrative Services)

**Hook:** "What does a company secretary actually do all day?"

Format: "day in the life" montage, 5 clips of 4-5 seconds with text labels.

1. 9:00 "Arranging a board meeting" (calendar, calls)
2. 11:00 "Drafting the minutes" (typing)
3. 13:00 "Updating the statutory registers" (binder or screen, blurred)
4. 15:00 "Filing documents so they're found in seconds" (organised folders)
5. 17:00 "Reminders for what's due next" (checklist)

**VO close:** "Running a business is enough work. Let us keep the records." Blur any real client names or data in every shot.

### Day 9 (Tue 1 Dec): Before you sign with a local partner, check these 3 things (Commercial Information)

**Hook:** "Signing with a local partner or agent in the UAE? Check these 3 things first."

1. "Is their company active and properly registered?"
2. "Who really owns and runs it?"
3. "What is their track record in your sector?"

**VO close:** "We provide information on companies, business leaders and market opportunities, so you decide with facts." Shots: presenter to camera, magnifying-glass graphic per point, end card.

| Time | Shot | VO / on-screen text |

## Production and posting notes

- **Post time:** 6-8 pm UAE time on Instagram. Cross-post the education reels (Days 2, 4, 6, 7, 9) to LinkedIn, the channel where corporate decision makers are.
- **No spam:** 3-5 relevant hashtags per reel (e.g. #UAEbusiness #DubaiBusinessSetup #CompanyFormation). No follow-for-follow, no bought engagement, no fake urgency.
- **Accuracy:** no prices, fines, timelines or client numbers on screen unless the team confirms them. Rules change, so add "Rules can change. Check with us for your case." to education captions.
- **Reply to every comment** in the first hour. Save good questions as ideas for the next reels.
- **Before Day 1:** the draft's contact email (email@businessco.ae) looks like a placeholder. Confirm the real email, phone and social handles before captions go live.
- **Arabic:** add Arabic subtitles to at least Days 1, 2 and 10 for local reach.
