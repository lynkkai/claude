# How to Transcribe a Zoom Meeting: 5 Ways, Free and Paid

Zoom can transcribe your meetings, but whether you can actually **keep** that transcript depends on your plan, your role in the meeting, and a few settings most people never open.

Zoom also changed the rules in 2026: since **May 18, 2026**, free Basic accounts can no longer save or download captions. Live captions still show during the call, but once the meeting ends, the text is gone.

Here is every practical way to transcribe Zoom today, what each one needs, and which to pick.

## The short version

| Method | Works on free Zoom? | Need to be host? | You get |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Zoom cloud recording transcript | No (paid plan) | Host or co-host starts recording | Downloadable transcript file after the meeting |
| 2. Live captions and in-meeting transcript | View only | Host controls settings | Live text; saving depends on plan and host settings |
| 3. Zoom AI Companion summary | No (paid) | Host enables | A summary, not a full transcript |
| 4. Local recording plus a transcription tool | Yes | Host, or host gives permission | An audio/video file you transcribe yourself |
| 5. An AI meeting assistant | Yes | No | Transcript, summary and action items |

## 1. Zoom's built-in transcript (cloud recording)

Zoom's own transcript is tied to **cloud recording**, which requires a paid plan. On some plans, audio transcripts need a higher tier, so check what your plan includes before you count on it.

**Turn it on (one time):**

1. Sign in to the Zoom web portal.
2. Go to **Settings** > **Recording**.
3. Make sure **Cloud recording** is on.
4. Under cloud recording options, turn on **Create audio transcript** (it may be labelled **Audio transcript** in your account).
5. Save.

**In each meeting:**

1. As the host or co-host, select **Record** > **Record to the Cloud**.
2. Run the meeting as normal.
3. After the meeting, Zoom processes the recording. When it is ready, you get an email.
4. Go to **Recordings & Transcripts** in the web portal, open the meeting, and download the transcript (a **.vtt** file) alongside the video and audio.

**Good for:** teams that already pay for Zoom and want a transcript filed next to the recording.

**Watch out for:** processing time (often a while for long meetings), the transcript only exists if someone remembered to hit Record to the Cloud, and a .vtt file is a raw transcript. You still have to read it to find the decisions.

## 2. Live captions and the in-meeting transcript

Zoom can show **automated captions** while you talk, and a **full transcript panel** that scrolls with the meeting.

1. In the meeting, select **Captions** (or **More** > **Captions**, depending on your window size).
2. Choose **Show captions**, or **View full transcript** to open the side panel.

Whether anyone can **save** that transcript depends on the host's settings and the account plan. As of May 2026, Zoom has separated captions and transcripts in its settings, and only the meeting transcript can be downloaded, if the host allows it. On free Basic accounts, captions are view only.

**Good for:** accessibility and following along in real time.

**Watch out for:** if saving is not enabled, nothing remains once the meeting ends.

## 3. Zoom AI Companion

On eligible paid plans, Zoom AI Companion can produce a **meeting summary** and suggested next steps after the call, and answer questions during the meeting.

It is useful, but it is a summary, not a word-for-word record. It also lives inside Zoom, so it does not help with your Google Meet or Teams calls, or with conversations that happen in person.

## 4. Local recording plus a transcription tool

Free Zoom accounts can still **record locally** on the desktop app. You can then run that file through a transcription tool.

1. As the host (or with the host's permission), select **Record** > **Record on this Computer**.
2. When the meeting ends, Zoom converts the recording and saves it to your Zoom folder (usually **Documents** > **Zoom**), including an audio-only **.m4a** file.
3. Upload the audio file to a transcription service.

**Good for:** occasional meetings on a free plan.

**Watch out for:** it is manual every time, you need recording permission from the host, and you end up with a transcript but no summary or task list unless you build one yourself. Uploading meeting audio to random free websites is also a privacy question worth asking before you do it.

## Which method should you use?

- **You host meetings on a paid Zoom plan and just need a file for the record:** Zoom cloud recording with audio transcripts.
- **You need captions for accessibility during the call:** Zoom live captions.
- **You are on free Zoom and transcribe once in a while:** local recording plus a transcription tool.
- **You are not the host, or you need notes and next steps, not just text:** an AI meeting assistant like Lynkk.
- **Your meetings happen on Zoom, Teams, Meet and in person:** an AI meeting assistant, so everything lands in one place.

## Tips for a more accurate Zoom transcript

1. **Use a headset or a decent mic.** Laptop mics in echoey rooms are the number one cause of bad transcripts.
2. **One person per audio feed.** If several people share one room mic, speaker labels suffer. Ask in-room participants to also join from their own laptops, muted, or use a tool that identifies speakers by voice.
3. **Rename yourself properly in Zoom.** "iPhone (2)" ends up in your transcript.
4. **Say names and key numbers clearly.** Product names, acronyms and figures are where transcription errors cost the most.
5. **Skim the summary right after the call.** Correcting an action item takes ten seconds while the meeting is fresh.

## Always ask before you record

Zoom shows a recording notice to participants when its own recording starts. Whatever method you use, tell people you are recording and transcribing, and follow your company's policy and the law where you and your participants are. This article is not legal advice.

## Quick answers

**Can I transcribe a Zoom meeting for free?**
Free Zoom accounts can view live captions but, since May 2026, cannot save them. To get a saved transcript on a free plan, record locally and transcribe the file, or use an AI meeting assistant with a free tier, such as Lynkk.

**Can I transcribe a Zoom meeting if I am not the host?**
Zoom's built-in recording needs the host's permission. An AI meeting assistant that records from your side, like Lynkk's Chrome extension or Mac app, does not require you to be the host. You should still tell the other participants.

**How do I transcribe an old Zoom recording?**
If you have the cloud recording on a plan with audio transcripts, the transcript may already be in Recordings & Transcripts. Otherwise, download the audio file and upload it to a transcription tool.

**What format is a Zoom transcript?**
Zoom cloud recording transcripts download as **.vtt** files, a plain text caption format with timestamps that opens in any text editor.
