# Avoma Alternatives for US Sales Teams in 2026

*By [Samarth Wagh](https://lynkk.ai/blog/authors/samarth-wagh), Co-founder, Lynkk.ai · Last updated 17 October 2026 · Pricing checked 17 October 2026*

> **Quick answer:** The best Avoma alternatives depend on what you use Avoma for. For enterprise revenue intelligence, **Gong** (quote-based). For CRM sync and coaching at a lower price, **Fireflies** ($19 per seat billed yearly on Business) or **Fathom Business** ($25). For call prep, live answers and follow-up tasks, **Lynkk** ($15). For transcripts, **Otter**; for analytics, **Read AI**.

*Disclosure: Lynkk publishes this guide and is one of the tools reviewed. Prices come from vendor pricing pages, checked 17 October 2026.*

Avoma bundles a lot into one product: an AI meeting assistant, a scheduler, conversation intelligence and revenue intelligence. That bundle is its strength, and the reason sales teams start searching for Avoma alternatives. Once you add the Conversation Intelligence and Revenue Intelligence add-ons, the per-seat cost climbs fast.

This guide compares 6 Avoma alternatives for US sales teams, with prices, strengths and the trade-off you take with each one.

## How much does Avoma cost in 2026?

Avoma costs $19 per recorder seat per month on Startup and $29 on Organization, billed yearly, or $29 and $39 billed monthly. Enterprise is $39 billed yearly with a 10-seat minimum. View-only seats are free ([Avoma pricing](https://www.avoma.com/pricing)).

| Avoma item | Billed yearly | Billed monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Startup plan | $19 per seat | $29 per seat |
| Organization plan | $29 per seat | $39 per seat |
| Enterprise plan | $39 per seat | Not offered |
| Conversation Intelligence add-on | $29 per seat | $35 per seat |
| Revenue Intelligence add-on | $29 per seat | $35 per seat |
| Lead Router add-on | $19 per seat | $25 per seat |

A rep on Organization with both intelligence add-ons costs $87 a month billed yearly. For a 10-rep team, that is $10,440 a year. That number is usually the trigger to compare Avoma competitors.

## Why do sales teams look for Avoma alternatives?

The top reason is cost at scale: coaching and forecasting features sit in add-ons that roughly triple the base seat price. Other common reasons:

- **Feature overlap.** Teams already pay for a scheduler (Calendly or Cal.com) or a CRM with forecasting.
- **Seat caps.** Startup allows up to 25 paid seats and Organization up to 100.
- **Complexity.** Smaller teams use the note taker and ignore the rest.
- **Enterprise minimums.** Enterprise needs at least 10 paid seats.

## What is Avoma, and what does it offer?

Avoma is an AI meeting assistant built for revenue teams. It combines 4 products in one platform: an AI note taker, a scheduler with lead routing, conversation intelligence for coaching, and revenue intelligence for deals and forecasting. It records Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams calls and syncs notes to CRMs.

| Avoma module | What it does | How it is priced |
|---|---|---|
| Meeting assistant | Recording, transcripts, AI notes | Startup or Organization plan |
| Scheduler and Lead Router | Booking pages, round-robin routing | Included in part, Lead Router add-on |
| Conversation Intelligence | Call scoring, coaching, scorecards | $29 add-on billed yearly |
| Revenue Intelligence | Deal risk, forecasting, CRM updates | $29 add-on billed yearly |

The bundle works well when a team uses most of it. It becomes expensive when a team uses only the note taker.

## When is Avoma enough, and when have you outgrown it?

Avoma is enough when your team uses at least 2 of its 4 modules and stays under its seat caps (25 paid seats on Startup, 100 on Organization). It is a good fit for mid-sized sales teams that want one vendor for notes, scheduling and coaching.

Signs you have outgrown Avoma, or bought more than you need:

- **You only use notes.** A focused note taker costs less.
- **You need deeper revenue intelligence.** Large teams often move to Gong or Clari.
- **You already pay for scheduling.** Calendly or Cal.com duplicates Avoma's scheduler.
- **Reps do not adopt it.** Low usage is the most expensive problem of all.

## How do you test Avoma alternatives safely?

Run a 2-week pilot before you switch, with 3 to 5 reps and real calls. A short, structured pilot avoids a painful migration later.

1. **List what you use today:** which Avoma features, which CRM fields, which reports.
2. **Pick 2 alternatives** that cover that list.
3. **Record the same types of calls** in both tools for 2 weeks.
4. **Score the output:** note quality, CRM accuracy, time saved per rep.
5. **Check contracts:** align the switch with your Avoma renewal date.

## What do Chorus and Clari Copilot offer?

Chorus (now part of ZoomInfo) and Clari Copilot (formerly Wingman) are conversation intelligence tools aimed at sales teams, often bought alongside a wider platform. Both focus on call recording, coaching and deal insight, and both use quote-based pricing rather than published per-seat prices.

Consider them if you already buy ZoomInfo or Clari for data or forecasting, since bundling can lower the effective cost. If you do not, the published prices of Fireflies, Fathom and Lynkk make them easier to compare and trial.

## How does Avoma compare with Microsoft Copilot?

Avoma vs Copilot comes down to scope. Microsoft 365 Copilot summarizes Teams meetings and works across Microsoft apps, but it is not a sales tool: no deal boards, scorecards or scheduler. Avoma is built for revenue teams and works across Zoom, Meet and Teams.

If your sellers only use Teams and your company already licenses Copilot, Copilot covers basic call notes. For coaching and pipeline insight, you need a sales-specific tool.

## What should you check before switching from Avoma?

Check 4 things before you cancel:

1. **Recording history.** Export important calls and notes, or confirm how long Avoma keeps them after cancellation.
2. **CRM fields.** List which fields Avoma updates today, and confirm the new tool can update them.
3. **Scheduling links.** If reps share Avoma scheduler links, move them to Calendly or Cal.com first.
4. **Contract terms.** Annual contracts may not refund unused months; plan the switch near renewal.

## Is Avoma worth it in 2026?

Avoma is worth it for sales teams of roughly 10 to 100 reps that use its note taker, scheduler and at least 1 intelligence add-on, because one vendor replaces 2 or 3 tools. It is rarely worth it for teams that only need meeting notes, or for very large revenue organizations that need enterprise forecasting.

Run the numbers on your own usage. A rep on Organization with Conversation Intelligence costs $58 a month billed yearly. If the coaching features change how managers coach, that is cheap. If nobody opens the scorecards, the same $58 buys a note taker plus a separate scheduler with money left over.

Three questions settle it:

1. **How many modules does each rep actually use?** Fewer than 2 suggests a focused tool.
2. **Do managers coach from Avoma every week?** If not, the coaching add-on is shelfware.
3. **Is forecasting done in Avoma or in your CRM?** If it lives in the CRM, the Revenue Intelligence add-on duplicates it.

## FAQ

### What is the best alternative to Avoma?

Fireflies and Fathom Business are the best lower-cost Avoma alternatives with CRM sync. Gong is the best for enterprise revenue intelligence. Lynkk is the best for call prep, live answers and follow-up tasks. If you mainly use Avoma as a note taker, a cheaper tool usually covers it. If you rely on its forecasting, compare Gong instead.

### Who are Avoma's main competitors?

Avoma's main competitors are Gong, Clari Copilot, Fireflies.ai, Fathom, Otter.ai, Read AI and Lynkk. They range from full revenue platforms to focused note takers. The right choice depends on whether you need forecasting and coaching, which Gong covers, or mainly notes and CRM updates, which Fireflies and Fathom handle at a lower price per seat.

### How much does Avoma cost per user?

Avoma costs $19 per recorder seat per month on Startup and $29 on Organization, billed yearly. Conversation Intelligence and Revenue Intelligence add-ons cost $29 each per seat billed yearly. On monthly billing, the plans cost $29 and $39 per seat. View-only seats are free, so managers who only review calls do not need a paid recorder seat.

### Is Avoma better than Otter?

Avoma is better for sales teams that want scheduling, coaching and revenue insight in one tool. Otter is better and cheaper if you mainly need transcripts and summaries. For a 10-person team on annual billing, Otter Pro costs about $1,000 a year, while Avoma Organization costs $3,480 before any add-ons.

### Is there a free Avoma alternative?

Yes. Fathom's free plan records unlimited calls, and Lynkk, Otter, Read AI and Fireflies have free plans with limits. Avoma offers a 14-day free trial of its Organization plan. Free plans suit testing on real calls before you commit. Run the same 3 sales calls through 2 tools and compare the notes your reps actually reuse.

### Does Avoma have a free trial?

Yes. Avoma offers a 14-day free trial of the Organization plan with no credit card required. Use the trial on live sales calls, not demos, and decide before day 14 which add-ons you would actually need, because those drive most of the final cost.
