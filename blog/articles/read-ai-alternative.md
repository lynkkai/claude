# Read AI Alternatives US Teams Are Switching To in 2026

*By [Samarth Wagh](https://lynkk.ai/blog/authors/samarth-wagh), Co-founder, Lynkk.ai · Last updated 17 October 2026 · Pricing checked 17 October 2026*

> **Quick answer:** The best Read AI alternative depends on what you need after the call. **Lynkk** turns meetings into tasks and Jira tickets (free for 5 notes, $15 Pro). **Fathom** gives unlimited free recordings. **Fireflies** syncs to CRMs from $10 a seat billed yearly. **Granola** and **Jamie** skip the bot. **Teams Copilot** suits Microsoft-only companies.

*Disclosure: Lynkk publishes this guide and is one of the tools reviewed. Prices come from vendor pricing pages.*

Why do teams go looking for a Read AI alternative? Three reasons, mostly. The free plan stops at 5 meetings a month, the bot joins calls people did not expect it in, or the summaries are good but nobody acts on them.

Read AI is a capable product. Meetings on Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams get summarized, engagement and talk time get scored, and you get coached on how you ran the call. Value those metrics? Stay. Want something else from your meetings? The seven tools below are where US teams are moving.

## Why do teams look for a Read AI alternative?

The most common trigger is the free plan limit: 5 meeting transcripts, 5 reports and 5 live-note sessions a month, each capped at 1 hour. Past that, you pay. Pro costs $19.75 per user per month, or $15 billed yearly.

Other reasons we hear from US teams:

- **Auto-join surprises.** Once one person connects a calendar, the notetaker can show up in meetings where other attendees did not expect it. IT teams often ask how to control this.
- **Metrics over action.** Engagement scores are interesting, but teams want the follow-up email written and the ticket filed.
- **Seat minimums.** Read AI's Enterprise tier requires 5 or more licenses.
- **One place for online and in-person.** Many teams want the same tool in the conference room and on Zoom.

## Which Read AI alternatives are worth switching to?

Each Read AI alternative was judged on 4 things: free limits, price per user, privacy controls, and what happens once the meeting ends.

### 1. Lynkk (our product): best for turning meetings into work

Online meetings on Zoom, Google Meet and Teams get a Lynkk bot; in-person conversations and bot-free calls go through the Mac app and Chrome extension. Read AI measures the meeting. Lynkk acts on it.

- **Meeting Brief** before each call, with what is still open from past meetings.
- **Cue** answers questions during the call (Pro).
- **Tasks with owners and dates**, a decision log and one-click Jira tickets after the call.
- **Ask Lynkk** searches all your meetings and shows the exact source.

Lynkk supports 17 languages plus auto-detect, including Hindi and Hinglish, and lets teams pin their data to the US, EU, UK, India, Singapore or Australia. Free covers 5 notes a month. Pro? $15 a month.

**Trade-off:** no Windows or mobile apps yet, and CRM sync is listed as coming soon.

### 2. Otter.ai: best for live English transcription

Otter shows a live transcript during the call and answers questions about it afterward. On the free plan you get 300 minutes a month, but each conversation stops at 30 minutes, and Pro costs $8.33 a month billed yearly. Full comparison: [Read AI vs Otter AI](https://lynkk.ai/blog/read-ai-vs-otter-ai).

### 3. Fireflies.ai: best for sales teams on a CRM

Fireflies pushes call notes into CRMs and offers search across every call, at $10 per seat billed yearly for Pro and $19 for Business. Watch for storage limits on lower tiers. Some AI features also use credits.

### 4. Fathom: best free Read AI alternative

Unlimited recordings and transcripts on the free plan make Fathom the strongest free Read AI alternative. Premium is $16 a month billed yearly. Need CRM field sync? Business adds it at $25.

### 5. Granola: best for notes without a bot

No bot ever joins: Granola captures audio from your device and turns your rough typed notes into full ones, with Business at $14 per user per month and its limits covered in our [Granola AI alternatives guide](https://lynkk.ai/blog/granola-ai-alternatives).

### 6. Jamie: best for EU data hosting

Jamie records without a bot, hosts data in the EU and lists 99+ languages, while its free tier allows 10 notes a month at up to 30 minutes each. Team? $37 per seat billed yearly.

### 7. Microsoft Teams Copilot: the Microsoft alternative to Read AI

Running on Microsoft 365? Then Teams' AI recap features come with a Microsoft 365 Copilot license, so you may already pay for them. The catch: they stay inside Teams. Zoom calls and in-person meetings are not covered. Options are compared in our [Teams AI note takers guide](https://lynkk.ai/blog/ai-note-taker-microsoft-teams).

## What does Read AI do well?

Read AI does 4 things well, and it helps to know them before switching. Engagement and talk-time analytics. A personal meeting coach. Native apps on Windows, Mac, Android and iOS. And premium integrations on Pro, including Notion, Confluence, Salesforce, HubSpot, Jira and Zapier.

Chose Read AI for those reasons? Then compare alternatives on the same points: Fathom Business offers coaching metrics and scorecards, Otter and Fireflies offer broad integrations, and Lynkk focuses on prep before meetings and tasks after them rather than engagement scoring.

## Is there a free Read AI alternative?

Yes. Fathom is the most generous free Read AI alternative, with unlimited recordings and transcripts. Smaller allowances: Otter gives 300 free minutes a month, Jamie 10 notes, and Lynkk 5 notes a month with tasks and Jira send included.

Since Read AI's own free plan stops at 5 meetings, even the smallest free plans above are comparable. What matters more is meeting length. Otter and Jamie both cap free recordings at 30 minutes, which rules out hour-long calls, while Lynkk allows up to 2 hours per note.

## Is Read AI safe and private?

Read AI's top tier, Enterprise+ ($29.75 billed yearly), adds SAML, SCIM, domain capture, HIPAA compliance and custom retention, yet most concerns are less about security than about control. Who can send the notetaker into a meeting?

Switching for privacy? Compare these 3 controls:

1. **Bot-free capture:** Granola and Jamie never use a bot. Lynkk's Mac app and Chrome extension record without one.
2. **Data location:** Lynkk pins processing to one of 6 regions per workspace. Jamie hosts in the EU.
3. **Training on your data:** Granola lets every plan opt out of model training. Jamie states it does not train on your data.

## Which Read AI alternative is best for teams?

For teams of 5 to 50, the best Read AI alternatives are Fireflies (CRM sync), Fathom Team ($15 per user billed yearly, 2-user minimum) and Lynkk (Jira tasks and shared meeting memory). None of them carries the 5-seat minimum of Read AI's Enterprise tier.

Match the tool to the team:

- **Sales:** Fireflies or Fathom Business, for CRM fields and call review.
- **Product and engineering:** Lynkk, for decisions and Jira tickets. See [how product teams turn minutes into Jira issues](https://lynkk.ai/blog/meeting-minutes-to-jira).
- **Consultants and founders:** Granola or Lynkk, for private notes and prep before back-to-back calls.

## How do you switch from Read AI cleanly?

Switching takes about 15 minutes. Follow these 4 steps so the old bot stops joining calls:

1. **Export** the reports and transcripts you need from Read AI.
2. **Disconnect** Read AI from your calendar and turn off auto-join in its settings.
3. **Connect** your calendar to the new tool and test it on 2 or 3 internal meetings.
4. **Tell your team** which tool now records, so nobody is surprised by a new bot.

## FAQ

### What is the best Read AI alternative?

The best Read AI alternative for most teams is the one that matches the follow-up work they do after calls. Want tasks and Jira tickets? Lynkk suits you. Fireflies suits CRM-driven sales teams. Anyone who wants free unlimited recording should start with Fathom.

### Is there a free alternative to Read AI?

Yes. Fathom offers unlimited free recordings and transcripts. Otter, Jamie and Lynkk have free plans too, capped at 300 minutes, 10 notes and 5 notes a month respectively. For light use, even the smallest of these is comparable, because Read AI's own free plan stops at 5 meetings a month.

### What is the Microsoft alternative to Read AI?

Microsoft's alternative is the AI recap in Teams, which comes with a Microsoft 365 Copilot license. Inside Teams it works well. Zoom, Google Meet and in-person meetings are not covered, though. No Copilot license at your company? Then a third-party tool that joins Teams calls is usually the more affordable route.

### Who are Read AI's main competitors?

Read AI's main competitors are Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, Fathom, Granola, tl;dv, Jamie and Lynkk, and their approaches differ, from live transcription to bot-free notes to automated follow-up. Compare on free limits, price per user, privacy controls and what happens after the meeting.

### Can I use a Read AI alternative without a meeting bot?

Yes. Granola and Jamie are bot-free by design, and Lynkk gives you both options: a bot for Zoom, Meet and Teams, or bot-free recording through its Mac app and Chrome extension. Remember that bot-free capture still records. Announce it at the start of each call, especially with clients.

### Does any Read AI alternative support Hindi?

Yes, Lynkk supports Hindi and has a Hinglish setting for conversations that mix Hindi and English. Read AI lists 20+ languages. Check that list against what your team speaks, then test any tool on a real meeting in each of those languages before you roll it out.
