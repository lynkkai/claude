# Granola vs Fathom in 2026: which AI note taker fits US teams better

*By [Samarth Wagh](https://lynkk.ai/blog/authors/samarth-wagh), Co-founder, Lynkk.ai · Last updated 17 October 2026 · Pricing checked 17 October 2026*

> **Quick answer:** In the Granola vs Fathom choice, **Fathom** wins on free recording (unlimited recordings and transcripts at $0) and sales features like CRM sync. **Granola** wins on privacy and focus: no bot joins the call, and it builds notes from what you type. Paid plans start at $14 for Granola and $16 (billed yearly) for Fathom.

*Disclosure: Lynkk publishes this guide and also makes an AI meeting assistant. Prices come from each vendor's pricing page.*

Granola vs Fathom is one of the most common head-to-head questions for US teams picking an AI note taker. Granola is a bot-free AI notepad that listens through your own computer, while Fathom is a call recorder that joins the meeting and keeps the video. Both write meeting notes for you, and that one difference in how they listen matters more than any feature list on either pricing page.

Fathom sends a recorder to your Zoom, Google Meet, Teams or Slack huddle and captures video, transcript and summary. Granola runs on your device instead. It listens to your computer's audio and turns your rough notes into clean ones. One is built for recording calls; the other is built for thinking during them.

## What is the difference between Granola and Fathom?

Capture is the core difference. Fathom records the call (usually with a bot, with a bot-free beta), while Granola never joins the call and keeps no video. That one choice shapes price, privacy and who each tool suits.

| | Granola | Fathom |
|---|---|---|
| How it captures | Device audio, no bot | Meeting bot, plus bot-free beta |
| Video recording | No | Yes, with clips and playlists |
| Free plan | 30 days of meeting history | Unlimited recordings and transcripts |
| Cheapest paid plan | $14 per user per month | $16 per user per month billed yearly |

With Granola, notes start from your own typing. You jot "pricing pushback, follow up Tuesday" and the app expands that shorthand using the transcript. The recorder works the other way round, starting from the full recording and then generating a summary plus action items.

On price, Granola is $0 on Basic and $14 per user per month on Business. Fathom costs $0 on Free, $20 a month for Premium on monthly billing ($16 yearly), and $34 a month for Business ($25 yearly).

| Individual | Business $14 | Premium $20 monthly / $16 yearly |
| Team | Business $14 | Team $19 monthly / $15 yearly (2+ users) |

Take a 5-person team on annual billing. Granola Business costs $840 a year and Fathom Team costs $900 a year. The gap is small. Pick on workflow, not price.

## Does Granola or Fathom use a meeting bot?

Granola uses 0 bots. It captures audio on your device, so nobody on the call sees a recorder join. By default Fathom uses a bot, though it offers bot-free capture in beta, including on the free plan.

Client-facing work is where this matters most, since some US companies now ask vendors to keep bots out of their calls. You can still take AI-assisted notes with Granola. With Fathom you would switch to the beta bot-free mode or skip recording. Either way, tell people you are taking notes. Some US states require consent from everyone, California among them, where the law covers [any confidential communication](https://www.rcfp.org/reporters-recording-guide/california/).

## Which is better for sales teams, Granola or Fathom?

For most sales teams, Fathom is the better pick in the Granola vs Fathom decision, because its Business plan ($25 per user per month billed yearly) adds CRM field sync, deal views, coaching metrics and AI scorecards. Managers can also review call clips.

Notes can still land in your CRM with Granola Business, which connects to HubSpot, Attio and Affinity. What you do not get is video, so there is no call library for coaching. Founders doing their own sales often prefer it for exactly that reason. It feels like a notebook, not surveillance.

## Is Granola or Fathom better for privacy?

Privacy goes to Granola: no bot, no stored video, and a toggle to opt out of model training on every plan. Full call recordings live with Fathom, which is useful for coaching but means more sensitive data held by a vendor.

Both vendors publish their controls, and Lynkk sets out [where each workspace's data is processed](https://lynkk.ai/security). Check 3 things before you roll either out:

1. **Retention.** Granola's free plan keeps 30 days. Fathom's Enterprise plan adds custom data retention.
2. **Admin control.** Both reserve SSO for Enterprise.
3. **Consent.** Announce recording at the start of each call, whichever tool you use.

## Which integrations do Granola and Fathom support?

Granola Business ($14) connects to 5 named tools: Attio, Notion, HubSpot, Affinity and Zapier, plus an API and an MCP connection for AI assistants. On the other side, CRM field sync, deal views and custom summaries sit on Fathom's Business plan ($25 per user billed yearly).

The practical difference:

- **Granola** pushes notes into the tools where you already write, such as Notion pages or CRM records. It suits teams that treat notes as documents.
- **Fathom** pushes structured call data into the CRM and keeps the recording linked. It suits teams that review calls and coach reps.
- **Both** keep enterprise controls such as SSO on their top plan.

If your follow-up work happens in Jira, neither tool creates tickets for you out of the box, which means someone still copies decisions from the notes into the backlog by hand after every planning call. Granola runs on 5 platforms: Mac and Windows for video calls, iPhone for phone calls and in-person conversations, Android for in-person conversations since July 2026, and an Apple Watch app. Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and Slack huddles are covered by Fathom's recorder, and Lynkk's [bot covers Zoom, Meet and Teams](https://lynkk.ai/features/meeting-bot) while its [web app](https://lynkk.ai/web-app) records in person.

Because Granola listens to your device, it works with almost any meeting app that plays through your speakers. Fathom joins as a participant instead. So it behaves the same way for every person on your team, with nothing to install.

## How long does it take to set up Granola or Fathom?

Setup takes about 5 minutes either way: sign in with Google or Microsoft, connect your calendar, and join your next meeting. The difference shows up on day 2.

- **Fathom** joins scheduled meetings automatically once your calendar is connected. You do not have to remember anything.
- **Granola** needs the app open on your device during the call. If you forget, there is no recording to fall back on.

Teams will find Fathom easier to standardize. Individuals who want control over what gets captured may see Granola's manual start as a feature, not a bug. Searching Fathom vs Granola on setup alone? Fathom's bot-free mode is Mac-only for now, with [Windows support on the way](https://www.fathom.ai/overview).

- **Granola vs Fathom vs Otter:** Otter's free plan caps conversations at 30 minutes. Fathom's does not. Granola's limit is history, not minutes.

## FAQ

### Is Granola better than Fathom?

In the Granola vs Fathom debate, Granola is better for private, bot-free notes that start from what you type. For recording calls, sharing clips and syncing to a CRM, Fathom wins. Neither is better for every team, so run both on 3 real meetings before deciding.

### Is Fathom free?

Yes. The free plan includes unlimited recordings and transcriptions plus instant AI summaries. Some AI features carry a "limited use" tag, and Premium costs $16 a month billed yearly. Since it works with Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, the free plan is useful for most US teams from day one.

### Is Granola free?

Partly. The free Basic plan includes AI notes and chat, but it keeps only 30 days of meeting history. Business costs $14 per user per month and removes the history limit. Students and accepted startups can get 12 months of Business free, and registered nonprofits get 50% off, through Granola's help center programs.

### Does Granola record video like Fathom?

No. Granola transcribes your computer's audio and writes notes, but it does not record video. Clipping and sharing moments is a Fathom feature. If you need to replay a moment, such as a customer's exact wording, that makes Fathom the better fit; people who only need the notes are fine with Granola.

### Which works better for Google Meet and Zoom?

Zoom and Google Meet work with both, as does Microsoft Teams: Fathom joins as a recorder, Granola captures the audio through its Mac or Windows app. Fathom also records Slack huddles. Granola needs its app open during the call, whereas Fathom joins from your calendar, which is easier to standardize across a team.
