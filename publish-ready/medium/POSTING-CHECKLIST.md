# Medium posting checklist

The four `.md` files here are paste-ready: no frontmatter, no production notes, and the inline
cover is removed because Medium takes it as the feature image instead.

## Before you post anything

- [ ] **The affiliation is no longer stated in the text.** The top disclosure line was removed on
      2026-11-02 at your request. The product section still says "I work on Lynkk". If a post goes
      out from an account or publication that does not make the Lynkk connection obvious, make it
      evident in the bio or the publication name.
- [ ] **A named human edits each draft.** Medium's Distribution Guidelines class AI-generated
      content and content marketing as low-value. These are AI-assisted drafts. Edit them and add
      at least one thing only you know: a real room that went badly, a real blocked bot, a real
      CRM note that cost a deal.
- [ ] **Decide the byline.** The drafts carry `[Author Name]` placeholders in their frontmatter.
      Whoever posts needs the "I work on Lynkk" disclosure kept in, at the top, regardless.
- [ ] **The Hinglish post needs a Hindi speaker** to check the example sentences and the consent
      line before it goes out.
- [ ] **Settle the language count.** The site says 17 in the homepage FAQ, "every language" on
      /pricing, and 13 named plus 4 beta on /use-cases/field-interviews. The drafts use the
      specific list. Fix the site or fix the drafts, but do not publish all three versions.

## What to expect

Expect **Network Distribution**, meaning your followers and little else. Medium's curators class
content marketing as low-value, so these will most likely not get General Distribution or Boost.
That is fine and was always the plan: the bet is Google, not Medium's feed. A Medium post
currently ranks position 3 for `ai note taker for in person meetings`, which is why this works.

## Spacing

Do not publish all four on one day from one account. Space them a week apart. Four
product-adjacent posts landing together reads as a content-marketing dump to both readers and
curators.
