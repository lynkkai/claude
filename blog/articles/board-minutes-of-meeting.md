# How to write board minutes of meeting in India in 2026, with format and sample

*By [Samarth Wagh](https://lynkk.ai/blog/authors/samarth-wagh), Co-founder, Lynkk.ai · Last updated 17 October 2026*

> **Quick answer:** Board minutes of meeting in India follow **Section 118 of the Companies Act, 2013** and **Secretarial Standard 1 (SS-1)**. Circulate the draft to directors **within 15 days**, allow **7 days** for comments, and have the minutes **signed and entered in the minutes book within 30 days**. Board minutes are preserved **permanently** at the registered office.

Board minutes of meeting are the signed, dated record of what a board decided, not ordinary meeting notes. Indian company law treats properly kept minutes as evidence of what the board decided, and they get checked in audits, due diligence and disputes. One missing resolution or unsigned page can cause real headaches years later.

## What are board minutes of meeting?

Board minutes of meeting are the official written record of a meeting of a company's board of directors, and Section 118(1) of the Companies Act, 2013 allows 30 days to prepare, sign and keep them. Attendance, quorum, business transacted, resolutions passed and any dissent all go in. Section 118(2) requires them to contain a fair and correct summary of the proceedings, and under Section 118(7) minutes kept properly are evidence of the proceedings recorded in them.

General meeting minutes cover shareholder meetings, while committee minutes cover board committees such as the audit committee. Board minutes differ from both.

## What does the Companies Act 2013 require for board minutes?

[Section 118 of the Companies Act, 2013](https://lawx.in/acts/section/24/309/2964) and SS-1, issued by the [Institute of Company Secretaries of India](https://www.icsi.edu/), set 6 core requirements:

| Requirement | Rule |
|---|---|
| Draft circulation | To all directors within 15 days of the meeting |
| Director comments | Within 7 days of circulation |
| Preparation and signing | Prepared, signed and kept within 30 days (Section 118(1)) |
| Certified copy | To all directors within 15 days of signing (SS-1) |
| Minutes book | Consecutively numbered pages; board minute books kept at the registered office or wherever the board decides |
| Preservation | Board and committee minutes preserved permanently |

Who signs? The chairperson of that meeting, or of the next one, under Rule 25(1)(d) of the Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 2014. Since 1 April 2024 the revised SS-1 has applied, so check yours is current.

Keeping minutes of board meeting under Companies Act 2013 rules is not optional. Under Section 118(11), a default costs the company ₹25,000 and every officer in default ₹5,000. Tampering is treated far more seriously: Section 118(12) allows imprisonment up to 2 years, plus a fine of ₹25,000 to ₹1,00,000.

## What should board minutes of meeting include?

Drawn from SS-1 practice, these items should go into board minutes of meeting in India:

1. Name of the company, serial number of the meeting, and day, date, time and venue
2. Name of the chairperson
3. Directors present in person and through video conferencing
4. Company secretary and invitees present, with the reason for invitation
5. Leave of absence granted to directors
6. Confirmation of quorum
7. Confirmation of the previous meeting's minutes
8. Each item of business, with a summary of discussion where needed
9. Resolutions passed, in full text
10. Names of directors who were interested and did not participate
11. Names of directors who dissented or abstained, if any
12. Time the meeting concluded

## Board minutes of meeting format (copy and paste)

Use this board minutes of meeting format for a private limited company. Replace anything in brackets. The same minutes of board meeting format suits committee meetings, and Section 118(1) wants the pages of the book consecutively numbered.

```
[COMPANY NAME] PRIVATE LIMITED
CIN: [CIN]
Registered Office: [Address]

MINUTES OF THE [5th] MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
held on [Day], [Date] at [Time] at [Venue / through video conferencing]

DIRECTORS PRESENT
1. [Name], Chairperson, in person
2. [Name], Director, through video conferencing
3. [Name], Director, in person

IN ATTENDANCE
[Name], Company Secretary
[Name], [Designation], invitee for Item [4]

LEAVE OF ABSENCE
[Name], Director, was granted leave of absence.

1. CHAIRPERSON
[Name] took the chair.

2. QUORUM
The requisite quorum being present, the meeting was called to order.

3. CONFIRMATION OF MINUTES
The minutes of the previous Board meeting held on [date] were
circulated, noted and signed by the Chairperson.

4. [ITEM OF BUSINESS, e.g. APPROVAL OF ACCOUNTS]
[Brief summary of discussion.]
"RESOLVED THAT [full text of resolution]."
"RESOLVED FURTHER THAT [authorisation text]."

5. [INTERESTED DIRECTOR DISCLOSURE]
[Name] being interested, did not participate in Item [5].

6. VOTE OF THANKS
The meeting concluded at [time] with a vote of thanks to the Chair.

Place: [City]                         [Signature]
Date of entry: [Date]                 Chairperson
```

## Board minutes of meeting sample

A short sample entry for 1 item of business, a resolution to open a new bank account:

```
4. OPENING OF A CURRENT ACCOUNT
The Board discussed the need for a new current account for
collections from the Bengaluru branch.

"RESOLVED THAT a current account be opened in the name of the
Company with [Bank Name], [Branch], and that Mr [Name], Director,
and Ms [Name], CFO, be and are hereby severally authorised to sign
all documents and operate the said account."

"RESOLVED FURTHER THAT a certified copy of this resolution be
furnished to the bank under the signature of any Director."
```

Keep discussion summaries short and resolution text exact. Why? Banks, auditors and registrars will ask for the resolution.

## How do you write minutes of a board meeting step by step?

Writing minutes of a board meeting takes 7 steps, agenda to minutes book:

1. **Prepare from the agenda.** Draft headings for each item before the meeting.
2. **Record attendance and quorum** at the start, including directors on video.
3. **Note decisions and resolution text** during the meeting, plus any dissent.
4. **Draft within days.** Turn notes into formal minutes in the past tense.
5. **Circulate within 15 days** to all directors for comments.
6. **Finalise after 7 days** of comments, and get the chairperson's signature; send directors a certified copy within 15 days of signing.
7. **Enter in the minutes book within 30 days** and preserve permanently.

## How often must a board meet in India?

A company must hold its first board meeting within 30 days of incorporation and at least 4 board meetings a year, with no more than 120 days between 2 consecutive meetings, under [Section 173](https://lawx.in/acts/section/24/314/3020). One person companies (other than those with a single director), small companies and dormant companies need at least 1 meeting in each half of the calendar year, at least 90 days apart (Section 173(5)).

At least 7 days' written notice must go to directors. Quorum, under Section 174(1), is one-third of the total strength of the board or 2 directors, whichever is higher, and directors joining by video conferencing count toward it. Each meeting needs its own minutes.

## Are board minutes of meeting legally binding?

Are minutes of meeting legally binding? Not as a contract. They work as evidence instead, in 2 ways under Section 118. Minutes kept in line with the Act are evidence of the proceedings recorded (Section 118(7)). Until the contrary is proved, Section 118(8) adds, the meeting is deemed duly called and held, and appointments of directors, key managerial personnel and auditors recorded in the minutes are deemed valid.

Hence accuracy matters. Under SS-1 practice, once signed, minutes should not be altered; any correction should be approved at a later meeting and recorded in that meeting's minutes.

## Can board meetings and minutes be done through video conferencing?

Yes. Directors may take part in person or through video conferencing, under Section 173(2). Rule 4 of the Companies (Meetings of Board and its Powers) Rules, 2014 used to bar some items from such meetings, and it was omitted on 15 June 2021. Minutes must record which directors attended through video conferencing, and the chairperson must confirm attendance and quorum.

A recording helps the company secretary draft accurate minutes, and a [bot that joins Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams](https://lynkk.ai/features/meeting-bot) can capture the call without anyone taking notes. Still, the minutes remain the legal record. Not the recording.

## What are common mistakes in board minutes?

These 6 mistakes cause most trouble in audits and due diligence:

- **Vague resolutions** such as "the board approved the proposal" without the resolution text
- **Missing quorum** confirmation or incorrect attendance
- **Not recording interested directors** who should have abstained
- **Late signing**, beyond the 30-day window
- **Loose sheets** instead of a minutes book with numbered pages
- **Editing after signing** without a later board approval

## FAQ

### What is the time limit for board minutes in India?

SS-1 sets 15 days for draft minutes to reach directors and 7 days for their comments. Section 118 requires minutes to be prepared, signed and kept within 30 days. A certified copy of the signed minutes goes to directors within 15 days of signing.

### Who signs board minutes of meeting?

Rule 25(1)(d) of the Companies (Management and Administration) Rules, 2014 puts the last page in the hands of the chairperson of that meeting, or of the next one. General meeting minutes differ: the same chairperson signs within 30 days, or a director the board authorises if that chairperson dies or cannot act.

### How long must board minutes be preserved?

Rule 25(1)(f) says board and committee minute books are preserved permanently, in the custody of the company secretary or a director the board authorises, at the registered office or wherever the board decides. SS-1 treats the supporting papers differently: notices and agenda notes are kept for 8 financial years.

### Can board minutes be changed after signing?

Signed minutes should not be altered. Any correction belongs in a later board meeting's minutes, approved and recorded there. The cost of editing instead is set out in Section 118(12): tampering with minutes can mean imprisonment up to 2 years, plus a fine of ₹25,000 to ₹1,00,000.

### Are board minutes confidential?

Board minutes are internal records, not public filings. Section 119 gives members an inspection right over general meeting minute books only, with a copy due within 7 working days of a request. Board minutes stay with directors, auditors and authorities, and some resolutions are still filed with the Registrar of Companies.

### Can minutes be kept electronically?

Yes. Under Section 120, records including minutes may be kept and inspected in electronic form in the prescribed manner, and SS-1 adds that electronic minutes carry a timestamp. Content, the 30-day timeline and the signatures stay the same as on paper. Keep secure backups and an audit trail.
