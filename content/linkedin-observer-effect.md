# LinkedIn post: the observer effect

**Angle:** AI notetakers change the meeting they are recording. Contrarian, coming from
a company that sells meeting AI, which is the reason it travels.
**Audience:** founders, sales leaders, heads of product, anyone with back to back calls.
**Length:** ~270 words.
**Sourcing note:** the 84% figure is from a Fellow survey, widely cited in 2026 coverage
but not independently verified. It is attributed in the post, not asserted as fact. If you
want zero risk, swap it for the softer version at the bottom.

---

## Primary draft

The most useful thing anyone said in your last meeting probably never made it into the notes.

Not because the AI missed it. Because nobody said it out loud.

Fellow surveyed people on this and found 84% change how they speak when an AI notetaker is in the room. Fewer half formed ideas. Fewer "honestly, this isn't working." More careful, quotable, useless sentences.

That is an awkward thing for me to post, because we build meeting AI.

But here is what I think the category got wrong. We all optimised for capture: more accurate transcripts, faster summaries, better speaker labels. Nobody asked what the act of recording does to the conversation itself. So we shipped tools that produce beautiful notes about meetings that got measurably blander the moment we joined.

You cannot fix that with a better summary. You fix it by removing the reasons people tense up.

Where does the recording live? Pick your region, and every model behind Lynkk processes and answers from there. Your legal team gets a real answer instead of a shrug.

Does it work when there is no dial-in link? The honest conversations happen in the hallway, over dinner, in the car on the way back. Lynkk records those too, and works offline.

Is it a stranger in the call? It joins because you asked it to, it speaks when you want it to, and it stops when you say so.

Trust is a product feature. Treat it like one and people go back to talking like people.

What is the last thing you held back because something was recording?

The most useful thing anyone said in your last meeting probably never made it into the notes.

Not because the AI missed it. Because nobody said it out loud.

Watch a room when a notetaker joins. The half formed ideas stop. The "honestly, this isn't working" stops. People get careful, and careful meetings are worse meetings.
