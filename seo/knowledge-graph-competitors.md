# Knowledge Graph: Competitor Research

For: https://lynkk.ai/features/knowledge-graph
Data: Ubersuggest (United States, locId 2840) and web search, pulled 2026-10-01.
DA = domain authority. Traffic = estimated monthly US organic visits (Ubersuggest).
Companion doc: `seo/knowledge-graph-page.md` (keyword plan).

Competitors fall into two groups:

- **A. Product competitors:** meeting or work tools that market a knowledge graph built
  from meetings. They compete for customers.
- **B. SEO competitors:** sites that rank for the knowledge graph keywords we want, but
  mostly don't sell a meeting tool. They compete for search positions.

For reference, lynkk.ai: DA 1, 1 referring domain, 1 ranking keyword, 0 traffic.

### What this means for Lynkk

1. **The biggest players market a knowledge graph but don't rank for it.** Otter and Read AI
   put the knowledge graph in press releases and launch posts, not in content built around
   a keyword. Searches like "ai knowledge graph", "knowledge graph software" and
   "personal knowledge graph" have no meeting tool in the top 10.
2. **Glean owns "knowledge graph for AI" for enterprises.** Don't fight it head-on. Our
   angle is meetings and individuals and small teams, not enterprise search.
3. **Tana is the real race.** Same keywords, same audience, content just published.
4. **How to position against each one** (based on `memory/lynkk-brand.md`):

| vs | Their angle | Lynkk's angle |
|---|---|---|
| Otter, Read AI | Knowledge graph as an add-on to a notetaker; aimed at enterprises | One AI across the whole meeting lifecycle: context before, live voice answers during, graph + CRM/Jira after |
| Tana, Mem | Notes app where the graph comes from what you write | Graph builds itself from conversations, including in-person meetings, multilingual |
| Glean | Company-wide search over every app | Starts free, built around meetings, choose your data region |

Things to confirm before using these as claims: whether Lynkk's graph is queryable by voice
during a live meeting, and whether MCP clients can query it.

### Two benchmarks worth copying

- **atlasworkspace.ai** went from 0 visits (Jan 2026) to about 6,700/month (Sep 2026) and
  4,456 ranking keywords in 8 months, with DA 24 and 209 referring domains. It did this
  almost entirely with "alternatives", "X vs Y" and "best X for Y" posts (Evernote
  alternatives pos 2, Miro alternatives pos 2, NotebookLM vs Claude pos 3). That's the
  closest model for a new site like Lynkk.
- **puppygraph.com** (DA 33, 14.3K visits) ranks with deep glossary posts on graph
  concepts (relationship graphs, social network graphs, graph AI). It shows that
  "what is X / X examples" posts on knowledge graph topics can rank with mid DA.

## Actions this research points to

   Tools for Teams in 2026" and "Best Second Brain Apps in 2026", before Tana's new

2. **Ask to be added to existing lists** (backlinks plus referral traffic). Pitch Lynkk as

3. **If Lynkk exposes the graph over MCP, list it** on pulsemcp.com and mcpservers.org.
   Both rank on page 1 for "knowledge graph mcp" and accept listings.
4. **Write comparison pages** while competitors leave the knowledge graph angle unclaimed:
   "Lynkk vs Otter: knowledge graph", "Lynkk vs Read AI", "Tana vs Lynkk for meeting notes".
5. **Re-check these SERPs monthly**, especially Tana's three new posts and anything Otter
   publishes about its knowledge engine.
