# Read AI vs Otter AI in 2026: Which Should US Teams Pick?

*By Samarth Wagh, Co-founder, Lynkk.ai · Last updated 17 October 2026 · Pricing checked 17 October 2026*

> **Quick answer:** In Read AI vs Otter AI, **Otter** wins on live transcription and price: Pro costs $8.33 a month billed yearly and the free plan gives 300 minutes. **Read AI** wins on meeting analytics and coaching: it scores engagement and talk time, and Pro costs $15 a month billed yearly. Read AI's free plan stops at 5 meetings; Otter's stops each call at 30 minutes.

*Disclosure: Lynkk publishes this guide and makes a competing AI meeting assistant. Prices come from [Read AI](https://www.read.ai/plans-pricing) and [Otter](https://otter.ai/pricing), checked 17 October 2026.*

Read AI vs Otter AI is a common shortlist because the two tools look alike from a distance. Both join Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams. Both write summaries and action items. Both have free plans.

Up close they solve different problems. Otter is built around the transcript: you read along live and search it later. Read AI is built around the meeting itself: how it went, who spoke, how engaged people were, and what to do better next time.

## What is the difference between Otter AI and Read AI?

The core difference is focus: Otter AI produces a live, searchable transcript, while Read AI produces meeting reports with engagement and talk-time analytics plus a personal meeting coach. Both summarize and list action items.

| | Read AI | Otter.ai |
|---|---|---|
| Main output | Meeting report and analytics | Live transcript |
| Free plan | 5 meetings/month, 1 h each | 300 min/month, 30 min per call |
| Paid from (yearly) | $15 per user/month | $8.33 per user/month |
| Apps | Windows, macOS, Android, iOS | Web, mobile apps |

If your question after a meeting is "what exactly did they say?", Otter answers it faster. If your question is "how did that meeting go, and how do I run the next one better?", Read AI answers it better.

## Is Read AI or Otter AI better?

Otter AI is better for people who need accurate live notes, captions during calls, and the lowest price. Read AI is better for managers and sellers who want feedback on their meetings and reports they can share. Neither is better at everything.

Choose by role:

- **Students, journalists, researchers:** Otter, for the live transcript and lower cost.
- **Sales managers and team leads:** Read AI, for engagement metrics and coaching.
- **Product and engineering teams:** neither does tickets well by default; see the Lynkk section below.

## How do Read AI and Otter AI compare on price?

Otter is cheaper on both billing cycles. Otter Pro costs $16.99 monthly or $8.33 billed yearly. Read AI Pro costs $19.75 monthly or $15 billed yearly. On team plans, Otter Business is $19.99 billed yearly and Read AI Enterprise is $22.50 billed yearly with a 5-license minimum.

| Tier | Read AI (monthly / yearly) | Otter.ai (monthly / yearly) |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 |
| Pro | $19.75 / $15 | $16.99 / $8.33 |
| Team tier | Enterprise $29.75 / $22.50 | Business $30 / $19.99 |
| Top tier | Enterprise+ $39.75 / $29.75 | Enterprise, custom |

For a 10-person team on annual billing, Otter Pro costs $1,000 a year and Read AI Pro costs $1,800. Full breakdown: [Read AI pricing](https://lynkk.ai/blog/read-ai-pricing).

## Why is Otter AI so expensive for teams?

Otter is not expensive for individuals on annual billing ($8.33 a month), but the Business plan costs $30 per user per month on monthly billing. Teams often need Business because Pro caps each conversation at 90 minutes and 1,200 minutes a month.

Read AI has a similar jump. Its Enterprise tier, needed for audio and video playback, costs $22.50 to $29.75 per user and requires 5 licenses. In both cases, the real cost appears when a team outgrows the entry plan.

## Which has the better free plan?

Otter's free plan is better for frequent short calls: 300 minutes a month, up to 30 minutes each. Read AI's free plan is better for a few long calls: 5 meetings a month, up to 1 hour each.

| Free plan limit | Read AI | Otter.ai |
|---|---|---|
| Meetings or minutes | 5 meetings/month | 300 min/month |
| Length per meeting | 1 hour | 30 minutes |
| History | Not limited on page | 25 most recent conversations |
| File uploads | Not included | 3 lifetime |

If you run a single weekly hour-long client call, Read AI Free covers it fully. Otter Free would cut each call in half.

## Which has better apps and platform coverage?

Read AI lists native apps on 4 platforms: Windows, macOS, Android and iOS, all available on the free plan. Otter offers a browser-based web app plus iOS and Android apps, and its meeting agent joins Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams.

| Platform | Read AI | Otter.ai |
|---|---|---|
| Windows desktop app | Yes | Web app |
| macOS desktop app | Yes | Web app |
| iOS and Android | Yes | Yes |
| Zoom, Meet, Teams | Yes | Yes |

For in-person meetings, both mobile apps can record a conversation in the room. Otter's in-person recordings count against the same 300 or 1,200 monthly minutes as your calls.

## Which is more accurate, Read AI or Otter AI?

Both tools are accurate enough for clear English audio with 1 speaker at a time, and both slip on crosstalk, accents and poor microphones. Otter gives you more tools to correct errors: custom vocabulary (5 terms on Basic) and team vocabulary on Pro.

Accuracy also depends on what you read. Otter shows the raw transcript, so errors are visible and easy to fix. Read AI leads with summaries, so a misheard word may be smoothed over, but a wrong number can slip into the report. Check names, dates and prices before you share either output.

## How do the integrations compare?

Read AI Pro unlocks 7 premium integrations: Notion, Confluence, Salesforce, HubSpot, Jira, Zapier and webhooks. Otter Pro adds Salesforce, HubSpot and Zapier, with an API and webhooks on Enterprise.

Read AI's Jira and Confluence connections make it slightly more useful for product teams. Otter's Enterprise plan adds custom CRM and dialer integrations and an Otter Sales Notetaker, which suits large sales teams.

## Is Otter.ai safe? Is Read AI private?

Both offer enterprise security tiers. Otter Enterprise includes SSO, SCIM, domain capture and a HIPAA compliance add-on. Read AI Enterprise+ includes HIPAA compliance, SAML, SCIM, domain capture and custom data retention.

Day to day, the bigger questions are about control:

1. **Auto-join:** both tools can join meetings from your calendar automatically. Agree internally on when that is allowed.
2. **Visible bots:** attendees see both tools join. Some clients will ask you to remove them.
3. **Consent:** several US states require all parties to consent to recording, so announce the note taker at the start.

## How do Read AI and Otter compare with Fireflies?

Fireflies sits between them: it records like Otter and adds CRM sync and search across calls, at $10 per seat billed yearly for Pro. For Read AI vs Otter AI vs Fireflies, pick Otter for transcripts, Read AI for analytics, and Fireflies for CRM-heavy sales.

## FAQ

### Is Read AI or Otter AI better for meetings?

Otter is better for live transcription and lower cost. Read AI is better for engagement analytics, coaching and shareable reports. Pick based on whether you need a record of what was said or feedback on how the meeting went. Test both on real calls.

### What is the difference between Otter AI and Read AI?

Otter AI focuses on live, searchable transcripts. Read AI focuses on meeting reports with engagement, talk-time and coaching insights. Both summarize meetings and list action items. Otter suits people who reread what was said; Read AI suits managers who want feedback on how meetings went and how to run the next one better.

### Is Read AI cheaper than Otter?

No. Read AI Pro costs $15 a month billed yearly versus $8.33 for Otter Pro. On monthly billing, Read AI Pro is $19.75 and Otter Pro is $16.99. For a 10-person team on annual billing, that is about $1,800 a year for Read AI Pro versus $1,000 for Otter Pro.

### Does Read AI show a live transcript like Otter?

Read AI offers live notes during meetings, including on its free plan (5 sessions a month). Otter's live transcript is its core feature and is available on every plan. Read AI's free plan caps each meeting at 1 hour, while Otter's free plan cuts each conversation at 30 minutes.

### Which is better for Microsoft Teams, Read AI or Otter?

Both join Microsoft Teams meetings. Read AI also offers a Microsoft Teams marketplace app. If your company prefers built-in tools, Teams' AI recap requires a Microsoft 365 Copilot license. Both join as a visible participant, so tell attendees at the start of the call.

### Do Read AI and Otter support languages other than English?

Read AI lists support for 20+ languages. Otter focuses mainly on English. Lynkk supports 17 languages plus auto-detect, including Hindi and a Hinglish setting for mixed Hindi and English. Test any tool with a real meeting in your team's main languages before you buy seats for everyone.
